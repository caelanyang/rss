1. 📈 Broadcom: AI Target Jumps

Broadcom Q3 revenue (July quarter) surged 86% Y/Y to $29.6 billion ($160 million beat), with non-GAAP EPS of $3.32 ($0.08 beat).

> **译文：** Broadcom第三财季（7月季度）营收同比增长86%至296亿美元（超出预期1.6亿美元），非GAAP每股收益为3.32美元（超出预期0.08美元）。

Semiconductor solutions as a whole grew 127% to $20.8 billion, driven by AI semiconductor revenue, which jumped 221% Y/Y and 54% Q/Q to $16.7 billion, exceeding the $16 billion outlook from last quarter.

> **译文：** 半导体解决方案整体增长127%至208亿美元，由AI半导体收入驱动，该收入同比增长221%、环比增长54%至167亿美元，超出上季度给出的160亿美元展望。

Infrastructure software also accelerated to 29% growth at $8.8 billion.

> **译文：** 基础设施软件也加速至29%增长，达到88亿美元。

The bigger news was the forward reset for next year. Broadcom now expects $115 billion of AI semiconductor revenue in FY27, up from the $100 billion target that disappointed investors in Q2, followed by an extraordinary $230 billion in FY28. Q4 AI revenue is expected to reach $21.7 billion, up 236% Y/Y, another acceleration.

> **译文：** 更大的消息是明年的前瞻调整。Broadcom现在预计FY27
> AI半导体收入将达到1,150亿美元，高于第二季度令投资者失望的1,000亿美元目标，随后FY28将达到非凡的2,300亿美元。第四季度AI收入预计将达到217亿美元，同比增长236%，再次加速。

The customer mix includes the usual suspects. Anthropic is expected to become Broadcom’s largest custom XPU customer in FY27, with OpenAI potentially second, while Google remains a multi-tens-of-billions annual business. Meta is also ramping across multiple chip generations. Management says demand from its four largest AI customers continues to exceed what Broadcom can physically supply.

> **译文：** 客户组合包括常见的那些名字。Anthropic预计将在FY27成为Broadcom最大的定制XPU客户，OpenAI可能位居第二，而Google仍然是一个每年数百亿美元规模的业务。Meta也在多个芯片代际上加速推进。管理层表示，其四大AI客户的需求持续超过Broadcom的物理供应能力。

That bottleneck is prompting more vertical integration. Broadcom is bringing substrate production online in Singapore in FY27 while scaling networking alongside custom compute, including its new 200 Tbps Tomahawk 7 Ethernet switch.

> **译文：** 这一瓶颈正在推动更多垂直整合。Broadcom将在FY27于新加坡将基板生产上线，同时在定制计算旁边扩展网络业务，包括其新的200 Tbps Tomahawk 7以太网交换机。

Bottom Line: Q2 left investors wondering whether management was sandbagging with its $100 billion FY27 AI target. Now we know, with a $15 billion raise after just one quarter. The $230 billion FY28 outlook implies AI semiconductor revenue nearly quadrupling from FY26 in just two years. Now it’s all about how quickly Broadcom can secure enough capacity to satisfy that demand.

> **译文：** 底线：第二季度让投资者怀疑管理层是否在1,000亿美元FY27
> AI目标上有所保留。现在我们知道答案了——仅一个季度后就上调了150亿美元。2,300亿美元的FY28展望意味着AI半导体收入将在短短两年内从FY26水平增长近四倍。现在关键在于Broadcom能以多快的速度获得足够的产能来满足这一需求。

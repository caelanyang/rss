Google may now be playing the world’s smallest trumpet, having won a major victory in what’s arguably the least important antitrust case out there.

> **译文：** 谷歌现在可能正在吹响世界上最小的小号——它在一场可以说是最无关紧要的反垄断诉讼中取得了重大胜利。

That would be the ad tech antitrust lawsuit, the second of the two antitrust cases brought by the Justice Department against Google in recent years, and the second the government won—in theory. But as with the first case—over search—the outcome of this one has gone Google’s way.

> **译文：** 那就是广告技术反垄断诉讼，这是司法部近年来对谷歌提起的两起反垄断案件中的第二起，也是政府理论上赢下的第二起。但和第一起关于搜索的案件一样，这起案件的结果也朝着有利于谷歌的方向发展。

Its punishment for being a monopoly in both cases is so light as to be meaningless.A year after the arguments over how to remedy Google’s ad tech monopoly—we’re talking about the Google ad tools used to sell ad space on independent websites—were heard in a Virginia court, Judge Leonie Brinkema issued a tantalizingly brief ruling on Wednesday.

> **译文：** 两起案件中，谷歌因垄断受到的惩罚都轻得毫无意义。在弗吉尼亚州法院就如何补救谷歌的广告技术垄断进行辩论一年后——我们说的是谷歌用于在独立网站上销售广告位的广告工具——莱奥妮·布林克马法官周三发布了一份简短得令人心痒的裁决。

She rejected the government’s proposal for “structural remedies”—that is, forcing Google to divest its ad tech businesses—and accepted most of the proposed “behavioral remedies.” In other words, Google can keep its toys but will have to play nicer with others. Big whoop. These toys aren’t worth much—and their value is degrading by the day.

> **译文：** 她驳回了政府提出的“结构性补救措施”——即强制谷歌剥离其广告技术业务——并接受了大部分提议的“行为性补救措施”。换句话说，谷歌可以保留自己的玩具，但必须更好地与他人相处。真是了不起。这些玩具其实不值多少钱——而且它们的价值正在一天天缩水。

The proportion of ad dollars flowing to independent websites was never great (Google search and YouTube as well as Meta Platforms and Amazon swallow up most ad dollars, after all), and it is shrinking steadily. Some of that is due to AI changes Google made to its search engine, which have reduced the traffic going to websites.

> **译文：** 流向独立网站的广告费占比从来就不高（毕竟谷歌搜索和YouTube以及Meta Platforms和亚马逊吞噬了大部分广告费），而且这一比例还在稳步下降。部分原因是谷歌对其搜索引擎进行的AI改造减少了流向网站的流量。

But some of it is also that ad money is shifting to video-streaming and mobile apps, two ad markets Google does not dominate. (We explained in this story that Google’s ad tech has fallen behind rivals in those markets.)

> **译文：** 但另一部分原因是广告资金正在转向视频流媒体和移动应用，而这两个广告市场并非谷歌主导。（我们在这篇报道中解释过，谷歌的广告技术在这些市场已经落后于竞争对手。）

By the numbers, Google’s network ad revenue—which reflects the ad tech business—accounted for just 9% of its ad revenue in the second quarter (and that’s before Google forks over a majority share to the websites).

> **译文：** 从数据来看，谷歌的网络广告收入——反映广告技术业务——在第二季度仅占其广告总收入的9%（而且这还是谷歌向网站分成大头之前的数据）。

In 2018, network ads accounted for 17% of total advertising. (Much of the evidence presented by the government stemmed from the 2010s, not the present day.)

> **译文：** 2018年，网络广告占总广告收入的17%。（政府提交的大部分证据来自2010年代，而非当下。）

We don’t know the reasons for Brinkema’s ruling, as the full text won’t be released for a couple of weeks, but during the trial last year, she asked whether forcing Google to divest its ad tech businesses could hurt independent websites.

> **译文：** 我们不知道布林克马裁决的具体理由，因为全文要过几周才会公布，但在去年庭审期间，她曾询问强制谷歌剥离广告技术业务是否会伤害独立网站。

It’s a good bet Brinkema took that into account, just as the judge who heard the search antitrust case took into account AI’s impact in deciding how Google should change its ways. Google’s defense was, in fact, that the open web ad market was in decline. It seems the judge listened.

> **译文：** 布林克马很可能考虑到了这一点，就像审理搜索反垄断案的法官在决定谷歌应如何改变时考虑了AI的影响一样。事实上，谷歌的辩护理由是开放网络广告市场正在衰退。看来法官听取了这一意见。

All this is a reminder of the flaws inherent to antitrust enforcement of the tech industry, given how quickly technology-driven markets evolve and how slowly antitrust cases take to wind through the courts. Microsoft’s Idea of TransparencyFinally!

> **译文：** 这一切都提醒人们，对科技行业进行反垄断执法存在固有缺陷——技术驱动的市场变化如此之快，而反垄断案件在法院的审理却如此缓慢。微软的透明度理念——终于来了！

Microsoft is overhauling how it reports its business segments and will start reporting revenue for its Azure cloud unit quarterly, the software giant said on Wednesday. That’s a big deal. Azure is core to Microsoft’s AI business, so we’ll finally get a better picture of how the company is benefiting from AI. It also means we can compare the big three cloud firms, as both Amazon and Google were already reporting revenue for their cloud units.

> **译文：** 微软周三表示，正在全面改革其业务部门的报告方式，并将开始按季度报告其Azure云部门的收入。这是件大事。Azure是微软AI业务的核心，因此我们终于能更清楚地了解该公司如何从AI中获益。这也意味着我们可以比较三大云服务商了，因为亚马逊和谷歌此前都已报告其云部门的收入。

In the June quarter, for instance, we now know that Amazon Web Services generated $42 billion, Azure generated $29.4 billion and Google Cloud, $24.7 billion. But hold the applause.

> **译文：** 例如，在六月季度，我们现在知道亚马逊网络服务创造了420亿美元收入，Azure创造了294亿美元，谷歌云则为247亿美元。但先别急着鼓掌。

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said the changes in segment reporting aimed to provide “increased transparency to investors.” Anytime a CEO says something about increasing transparency, you know things are about to become more opaque. In this case, the changes make it harder to figure out the profit margins on Microsoft’s individual segments. That’s because Microsoft is collapsing three segments into two.

> **译文：** 微软CEO萨蒂亚·纳德拉表示，部门报告方式的改变旨在“为投资者提供更高的透明度”。每当CEO提到提高透明度时，你就知道事情要变得更加不透明了。在这种情况下，这些变化使得更难看清微软各个部门的利润率。这是因为微软正在将三个部门合并为两个。

It is putting into one segment both Azure and its Microsoft 365 cloud software businesses, which it previously reported in separate segments.

> **译文：** 它将Azure和Microsoft 365云软件业务合并为一个部门，而此前这两个业务是分开报告的。

We used to see operating margins for those separate segments—the one with Azure had an operating margin of 41% in the fourth quarter, while the one that included Microsoft 365 had a margin of 58%. In the future, we’ll see revenue for individual units like Azure but not their operating income.

> **译文：** 过去我们可以看到这些独立部门的营业利润率——包含Azure的部门第四季度营业利润率为41%，而包含Microsoft 365的部门利润率为58%。未来，我们将看到Azure等单个业务的收入，但看不到它们的营业利润。

That suggests we’re likely to see an operating profit that reflects both cloud and software, making it harder to figure out how the profits of both are separately changing. That’s not ideal.

> **译文：** 这意味着我们很可能看到的营业利润将同时反映云和软件业务，从而更难判断两者各自的利润变化情况。这并不理想。

Microsoft’s pitch, though, is that both the Microsoft 365 and Azure units are sharing AI chips and cloud computing capacity, and that argues in favor of treating them as one segment for reporting purposes. In other words, investors won’t have to worry about how Microsoft is dividing up the chips. Fair enough, we guess.

> **译文：** 不过，微软的理由是，Microsoft 365和Azure两个部门共享AI芯片和云计算能力，因此将它们作为一个部门来报告是合理的。换句话说，投资者不必担心微软如何分配芯片。好吧，我们姑且接受。

Trump Finds New Way to Attack the News MediaPresident Donald Trump’s attacks on the news media are legendary, including defamation lawsuits and calls for TV networks to lose their license over coverage he doesn’t like. But on Wednesday, the Trump administration came up with a new tactic to undermine the news industry.

> **译文：** 特朗普找到攻击新闻媒体的新方式。总统唐纳德·特朗普对新闻媒体的攻击堪称传奇，包括提起诽谤诉讼，以及因不满报道而要求吊销电视台执照。但周三，特朗普政府又想出了破坏新闻业的新招数。

The Justice Department on Wednesday filed a brief with the court hearing The New York Times Co.’s copyright infringement lawsuit against OpenAI, arguing that training AI models on copyrighted material—the core of the Times’ case—doesn’t violate copyright laws.

> **译文：** 司法部周三向审理《纽约时报》公司对OpenAI提起的版权侵权诉讼的法院提交了一份意见书，辩称用受版权保护的材料训练AI模型——这是《纽约时报》诉讼的核心——并不违反版权法。

The administration argued that if the Times prevailed, its approach to copyright could hurt the AI sector, which wouldn’t be good for the country. “Rules of law that make it significantly more difficult to develop a robust AI industry in the United States therefore threaten national security and give a competitive advantage to foreign adversaries who are not so encumbered,” the government argued. We wouldn’t want “rules of law” to get in the way of anything!

> **译文：** 政府辩称，如果《纽约时报》胜诉，其版权主张可能损害AI行业，这对国家不利。政府称：“如果法律规则使在美国发展强大AI产业变得明显更加困难，就会威胁国家安全，并给不受此类约束的外国对手带来竞争优势。”我们可不想让“法律规则”妨碍任何事情！

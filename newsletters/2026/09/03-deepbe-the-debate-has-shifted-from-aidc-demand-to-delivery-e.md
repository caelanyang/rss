# Deep|BE: The Debate Has Shifted from AIDC Demand to Delivery Execution

Executive Summary

AIDC power scarcity remains the structural driver. We estimate North America faces a ~15GW AI power shortfall in 2027, as grid interconnection, turbine lead times, and downstream power infrastructure constrain how much announced capacity can actually be energized. This keeps time-to-power at a premium and supports Bloom’s behind-the-meter SOFC value proposition.

> **译文：** AIDC电力短缺仍是结构性驱动因素。我们估算，到2027年北美将面临约15GW的AI电力缺口，因为电网并网、涡轮机交付周期以及下游电力基础设施限制了已公布产能中实际可通电的比例。这使得“通电时间”价值凸显，并支撑了Bloom表后SOFC的价值主张。

The debate has shifted from demand to execution. Production slots appear substantially booked through 2028, but the market is increasingly focused on whether Bloom can convert headline capacity into shippable MW and site deliveries. We model ~2.7GW YE27 manufacturing capacity and ~2.2GW of 2027 deliveries vs. ~2.0GW consensus, with supplier ramp, testing, yield and service capacity becoming more relevant constraints.

> **译文：** 市场争论已从需求转向执行层面。生产排期到2028年基本已被订满，但市场越来越关注Bloom能否将名义产能转化为可发货的MW及现场交付。我们建模显示，2027年底制造产能约2.7GW，2027年交付量约2.2GW，而市场共识约为2.0GW；供应商爬坡、测试、良率和服务能力正成为更相关的约束条件。

Scandium looks manageable near term, but service capacity risk is overlooked. Our channel checks suggest Bloom has diversified scandium sourcing across Malaysia, Japan and Canada, while current pricing does not indicate acute scarcity. The more overlooked risk is whether Bloom’s service organization can scale quickly enough to support a rapidly expanding multi-GW installed base.

> **译文：** 钪的供应短期内看起来可控，但服务能力风险被忽视。我们的渠道调研显示，Bloom已在马来西亚、日本和加拿大实现钪来源多元化，当前价格也未显示严重短缺。更被忽视的风险在于，Bloom的服务组织能否快速扩张以支撑迅速增长的多GW级装机基础。

Bloom is more than a temporary gas-turbine shortage trade. We do not expect turbine supply to meaningfully normalize by 2030, extending Bloom’s time-to-power advantage. Longer term, modularity and native-DC compatibility with emerging 800V AIDC architectures could give SOFC a structural role even as conventional generation capacity expands.

> **译文：** Bloom不仅仅是一个暂时的燃气轮机短缺交易。我们预计涡轮机供应到2030年不会明显恢复正常，这将延长Bloom的“通电时间”优势。长期来看，模块化设计及与新兴800V AIDC架构的原生直流兼容性，可能使SOFC在传统发电产能扩张的同时仍占据结构性地位。

Backlog conversion is now the key rerating driver. Recent delays at Jupiter and Vineland look more like normal project-level timing risk than backlog impairment, but increasing customer concentration makes annual revenue more sensitive to permitting, construction and acceptance cadence. Following the July short reports, BE’s forward EV/EBITDA has reset from ~85x to ~42x; we see 2H26 delivery execution, 2027 guidance and progress on major AIDC projects as the key catalysts for Street estimates and the multiple to recover.

> **译文：** 积压订单转化现已成为关键的估值重估驱动因素。近期Jupiter和Vineland项目的延迟更像是正常的项目级时间风险，而非积压订单减值，但客户集中度上升使得年度收入对许可、建设和验收节奏更加敏感。在7月做空报告之后，BE的前瞻EV/EBITDA已从约85倍重置至约42倍；我们认为2026年下半年交付执行、2027年指引以及主要AIDC项目的进展是带动市场预期和估值倍数回升的关键催化剂。

Why now: Following a series of short reports in July, BE’s forward EV/EBITDA has reset from ~85x to ~42x. Despite a strong 26Q2 beat-and-raise, the stock has yet to regain momentum, suggesting the debate has shifted beyond the well-understood AIDC power demand. We see this as a good time to dig into the more important questions: how quickly Bloom can ramp capacity, deliver, and convert its AIDC backlog into revenue.

> **译文：** 为什么是现在：在7月一系列做空报告之后，BE的前瞻EV/EBITDA已从约85倍重置至约42倍。尽管2026年第二季度业绩超预期并上调指引，股价仍未恢复动能，表明市场争论已超越已被充分理解的AIDC电力需求。我们认为现在是深入探讨更重要问题的好时机：Bloom能以多快的速度提升产能、完成交付，并将其AIDC积压订单转化为收入。

Key Debates & Our View

#1 Can Bloom add capacity and, more importantly, deliver fast enough to meet demand?

> **译文：** 问题一：Bloom能否增加产能，更重要的是，能否足够快地交付以满足需求？

Our View: We think Bloom can support the near-term delivery ramp. Our channel checks suggest production slots are substantially booked through 2028, with Bloom internally targeting roughly 4GW of annualized manufacturing capacity by YE27 while we model ~2.7GW, reflecting a more realistic ramp in systems manufacturing than the company’s internal stretch target. For actual deliveries, we model ~2.2GW of 2027 deliveries versus ~2.0GW consensus. Scandium does not appear to be an immediate bottleneck, but service capacity could become a more meaningful constraint as the installed base scales.

> **译文：** 我们的观点：我们认为Bloom能够支撑近期交付爬坡。渠道调研显示，生产排期到2028年基本已被订满，Bloom内部目标是到2027年底实现约4GW的年化制造产能，而我们建模约为2.7GW，反映出我们对其系统制造爬坡速度比公司内部激进目标更为现实的判断。实际交付方面，我们建模2027年交付约2.2GW，而市场共识约为2.0GW。钪目前似乎不是直接瓶颈，但随着装机基础扩大，服务能力可能成为更有意义的约束。

Debate: Bloom’s demand strength is already well understood, and incremental project announcements alone are unlikely to drive another meaningful rerate. The debate has shifted to whether Bloom can physically supply what it has already sold. However, we think the market is still over-focused on headline capacity expansion and under-focused on actual delivery capability. Most capacity forecasts remain largely top-down, anchored to the size of the AIDC power shortfall and assumed BTM share, rather than a bottom-up assessment of Bloom-specific constraints such as effective manufacturing throughput, yield, supplier readiness, testing capacity, service infrastructure and site-level conversion.

> **译文：** 争论焦点：Bloom的需求强劲已被充分理解，仅靠新增项目公告不太可能推动另一次有意义的估值重估。争论已转向Bloom能否实际供应其已售出的产品。然而，我们认为市场仍过度关注名义产能扩张，而对实际交付能力关注不足。大多数产能预测仍主要是自上而下的，锚定于AIDC电力缺口的规模和假设的表后市场份额，而非基于对Bloom特有约束的自下而上评估，如有效制造吞吐量、良率、供应商准备度、测试能力、服务基础设施和现场转化能力。

A recent short report has highlighted potential supply-chain bottlenecks, including scandium availability and broader manufacturing constraints. The key question has therefore shifted from headline demand to whether new capacity, suppliers, and yields can ramp fast enough for booked demand to convert into shippable MW on schedule.

> **译文：** 近期一份做空报告指出了潜在的供应链瓶颈，包括钪的可用性和更广泛的制造约束。因此，关键问题已从名义需求转向新产能、供应商和良率能否足够快地爬坡，使已订需求按时转化为可发货的MW。

We believe the risk is manageable.

Management says its diversified supply chain can support up to 25GW/year of production and is not dependent on China, while current scandium pricing shows little evidence of tight supply or an imminent shortage.

> **译文：** 管理层表示，其多元化供应链可支撑每年高达25GW的生产，且不依赖中国，而当前钪的价格几乎没有显示出供应紧张或即将短缺的迹象。

Our channel checks suggest Bloom has diversified its scandium sourcing since the early 2010s, with supply relationships across Malaysia, Japan, and Canada that together could provide 15 tons of annual ex-China supply, or about half of its current needs.

> **译文：** 我们的渠道调研显示，Bloom自2010年代初以来已实现钪来源多元化，在马来西亚、日本和加拿大均有供应关系，合计可提供每年15吨的非中国供应量，约占其当前需求的一半。

The same checks suggest Bloom’s scandium intensity is closer to ~15 tons per GW, materially below some bearish estimates based on older cell designs and thicker electrolyte assumptions.

> **译文：** 同样的调研显示，Bloom的钪用量强度约为每GW 15吨，明显低于一些基于旧电池设计和更厚电解质假设的悲观估算。

This suggests scandium is unlikely to be a near-term bottleneck, but the margin of safety narrows as Bloom scales further into multi-GW annual production. We therefore continue to monitor qualified ex-China supply growth and actual scandium intensity per GW as the key variables.

> **译文：** 这表明钪不太可能成为近期瓶颈，但随着Bloom进一步扩大到多GW级年产量，安全边际正在收窄。因此，我们将持续关注合格的非中国供应增长和每GW实际钪用量强度作为关键变量。

A more underappreciated risk is service capacity. We believe that Bloom’s current service organization is not yet sized for a much larger multi-GW installed base, meaning the company will likely need to expand internal field-service capacity and develop more third-party service partners. As deployments scale, technician availability, spare parts, stack replacement, and field-response capability could become increasingly important constraints on uptime and customer satisfaction, even if manufacturing itself keeps pace.

> **译文：** 一个更被低估的风险是服务能力。我们认为Bloom当前的服务组织尚未为更大的多GW级装机基础做好准备，这意味着公司可能需要扩大内部现场服务能力并发展更多第三方服务合作伙伴。随着部署规模扩大，技术人员可用性、备件、电堆更换和现场响应能力可能成为影响运行时间和客户满意度的日益重要的约束，即使制造本身能跟上步伐。

> **译文：** 问题二：Bloom是否不仅仅是一个暂时的燃气轮机短缺交易？

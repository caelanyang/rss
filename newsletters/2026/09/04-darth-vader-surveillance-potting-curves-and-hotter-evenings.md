Hello, FlowingData readers. Welcome back to Friday. This week in data and visualization: Darth Vader needs to keep an eye on enemies, India counts its people, and temperature differences ramp up.
> **译文：** FlowingData的读者们，大家好。欢迎回到周五。本周的数据与可视化内容：达斯·维达需要盯紧敌人，印度清点人口，温差加剧。
Cooling down less in the evening — The summer of 2026 will most likely be the hottest summer on record since 1936. Part of that is hot days, but more significantly it’s cooling down less at night, which raises the overall average. For the Washington Post, Ben Noll reports.
> **译文：** 夜间降温减少——2026年夏季极有可能成为自1936年以来最热的夏天。部分原因是白天气温高，但更重要的是夜间降温幅度变小，从而拉高了整体平均值。Ben Noll为《华盛顿邮报》报道。
Related: NPR, in collaboration with researchers at Boston University, modeled mortality due to heat and estimate the true counts are five times higher than reported.
> **译文：** 相关：NPR与波士顿大学的研究人员合作，对高温导致的死亡率进行了建模，估计实际数字比报告的高出五倍。
India enlists 3 million people to count the full population — Due to Covid, India’s census count is overdue by five years. But they’re getting things going, which involves hiring 3 million people to count around one and a half billion. For Bloomberg, Satviki Sanjay describes the challenge.
> **译文：** 印度征召300万人进行全国人口普查——由于新冠疫情，印度的人口普查已逾期五年。但他们正在推进这项工作，其中包括雇用300万人来清点约15亿人口。Satviki Sanjay为彭博社描述了这一挑战。
The first order of business was to find enough people — more than 3 million — to knock on every door in the country. Teachers and low-level bureaucrats were ordered to participate and offered 25,000 rupees ($262) for a task that will likely take several weeks. “We knew we’d have census duty, but I had no idea what exactly that might entail,” says Priyanka Yadav, a 35-year-old primary school teacher from Gorakhpur, a district of about 4 million (she’ll be able to give you a more precise figure next year) 13 hours east of Delhi by train.
> **译文：** 首要任务是找到足够的人手——超过300万人——去敲遍全国的每一扇门。教师和基层公务员被要求参与，并提供25,000卢比（约262美元）的报酬，而这项任务可能需要数周时间。“我们知道会有普查任务，但我完全不知道具体要做什么，”Priyanka Yadav说，她是一位35岁的小学教师，来自戈勒克布尔——一个人口约400万的地区（明年她就能给你一个更精确的数字），乘火车距德里以东13小时。
In contrast, the U.S. Census Bureau hired about 500,000 workers for the decennial count.
> **译文：** 相比之下，美国人口普查局为十年一次的人口普查雇用了约50万名工作人员。
Observable notebooks get a rewrite — Observable, known for visualization-centric notebooks to share code, restarted fresh to adjust for this new chapter in making things with computers.
> **译文：** Observable笔记本全面改写——Observable以可视化为中心的笔记本共享代码而闻名，如今重新出发，以适应用计算机创造事物的新篇章。
We obviously cannot pretend that AI does not exist; large language models are astonishingly capable and are profoundly changing the world, and this change appears to be only accelerating. Likewise we cannot do nothing, “watching and waiting, observing from afar and merely hoping for the best” as Pope Leo writes. We therefore seek, in what little way we can with our humble notebook, to apply this technology in a way that encourages human understanding, collaboration, and creativity.
> **译文：** 我们显然不能假装AI不存在；大语言模型能力惊人，正在深刻改变世界，而且这种变化似乎还在加速。同样，我们也不能无所作为，“袖手旁观，远远观望，只寄希望于最好的结果”，正如Pope Leo所写。因此，我们试图以我们简陋的笔记本所能做到的微薄之力，以一种鼓励人类理解、协作和创造的方式来应用这项技术。
Years ago, the best way to learn how to visualize data with code was to study one-off snippets via Mike Bostock’s code blocks. Observable notebooks spun off this joy of sharing and learning code, and you can see bits in visualization across the web. Of course, the work is different now, so this update seems inevitable.
> **译文：** 多年前，学习用代码可视化数据的最佳方式是通过Mike Bostock的代码块研究零散的代码片段。Observable笔记本正是从这种分享和学习代码的乐趣中衍生出来的，你可以在整个互联网的可视化中看到它的影子。当然，现在的工作方式已经不同，所以这次更新似乎是不可避免的。
I’ve never been much into code notebooks, but I’ll have to kick the tires on this.
> **译文：** 我向来不太喜欢代码笔记本，但我得试试这个。
Logistics of fueling aircraft and feeding sailors with a limited supply chain — There are thousands of crew members aboard U.S. warships in the Middle East. They need a regular food supply. Aircraft on the ships need fuel to lift off. For the New York Times, Simon Ducroquet and John Ismay illustrate the challenges for the U.S. Navy when a base is destroyed and carriers need to navigate outside the range of Iranian missiles and drones.
> **译文：** 在有限供应链下为飞机加油和补给水兵的物流挑战——美国在中东的战舰上有数千名船员。他们需要定期的食物供应。舰载飞机需要燃料才能起飞。Simon Ducroquet和John Ismay为《纽约时报》展示了当基地被摧毁、航母需要在伊朗导弹和无人机的射程之外航行时，美国海军面临的挑战。
I appreciate the maps and illustrations to show how fuel and ships must flow for the fleet to remain functional.
> **译文：** 我很欣赏这些地图和插图，它们展示了燃料和船只必须如何流动才能让舰队保持运转。
Darth Vader makes the case for Flock cameras — Flock Safety has been getting some hate lately, because as it turns out, people actually care about privacy and are against mass surveillance. <a href=" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><span style="color: rgb(130, 17, 34);">But Darth Vader brings up some good points</span></a>. Hear him out.
> **译文：** 达斯·维达为Flock摄像头辩护——Flock Safety最近招致了不少批评，因为事实证明，人们确实在乎隐私，反对大规模监控。但达斯·维达提出了一些好观点。听他说完。
Form a curve, make a pot — Imagine a pottery wheel that turns clay on an axis. A potter forms the sides and the shape goes all around. Karim Douieb made an interactive that shows how different curves look as a family of pots.
> **译文：** 画一条曲线，做一只罐子——想象一台陶轮让黏土绕轴旋转。陶工塑造器壁，形状环绕一周。Karim Douieb制作了一个交互式作品，展示不同的曲线如何变成一系列陶罐的形态。
Potters don’t design bowls and vases, they design one curve. That single line, rotated around the axis, becomes the entire form.
> **译文：** 陶工设计的不是碗和花瓶，而是一条曲线。那一条线绕轴旋转，就变成了整个形体。
In Line, you draw that curve. One side, shaped with a handful of control points, and the app grows a family of proportions around it: each variant keeping the radii and angles that make your line recognizably yours. Taller, wider, slender, grand: different vessels, same gesture.
> **译文：** 在Line中，你画出那条曲线。一侧用几个控制点塑形，应用就会围绕它生成一系列比例变体：每个变体都保留着让你的线条可辨识的半径和角度。更高、更宽、更纤细、更宏伟：不同的器皿，同一个手势。
Estimating a much stronger than usual El Niño — For Climate.us, Emily Becker explains why it looks like El Niño is going to break records this year. Current estimates from NOAA adjusts for the ocean warming overall.
> **译文：** 预估一次比往常强得多的厄尔尼诺——Emily Becker为Climate.us解释为什么看起来今年厄尔尼诺将打破纪录。NOAA目前的估计对整体海洋变暖进行了校正。
It’s not obvious at a glance, but with the new model (top), you can see the stronger difference between blue on in the west and red on in the east. More variation leads to stronger El Niño.
> **译文：** 乍一看并不明显，但用新模型（上图），你可以看到西部蓝色和东部红色之间更强的差异。更大的变化导致更强的厄尔尼诺。

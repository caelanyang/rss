Hello, and welcome to BestBlogs.dev Issue #111.
Recent issues have asked what becomes scarce as model capability grows abundant. This week's 20 selections move the question forward. Once an agent enters the durable work of a product, team, or organization, the differentiator is whether today's corrections, judgments, and failures make tomorrow's work better.
> **译文：** 近几期周刊探讨了一个问题：当模型能力变得充裕时，什么会变得稀缺？本周的20篇精选将这个问题向前推进了一步。一旦智能体进入产品、团队或组织的持久工作中，真正的差异化因素在于：今天的修正、判断和失败，是否能让明天的工作变得更好。
GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 3.8 push capability, price, and safety into new territory. Claude Code, Anthropic, and Amazon are redesigning collaboration with agents. Meta, Forge Memory, Taobao, and Ctrip turn experience into knowledge files, reasoning recipes, regression tests, project context, and living documentation. Yet remembering is not learning. Experience compounds only when signals are trustworthy, changes are versioned, outcomes are verified, and people can reject a harmful update.
> **译文：** GPT-6 Astra 和 Gemini 3.8 将能力、价格和安全性推向了新领域。Claude Code、Anthropic 和 Amazon 正在重新设计人与智能体的协作方式。Meta、Forge Memory、淘宝和携程将经验转化为知识文件、推理配方、回归测试、项目上下文和活文档。然而，记住并不等于学习。只有当信号可信、变更可版本化、结果可验证、且人们能够拒绝有害更新时，经验才能产生复利。
That is this issue's theme: experience compounds. Models provide capability on demand. An organization owns its ability to turn success into a repeatable method, failure into a constraint against recurrence, and an expert correction into judgment everyone can use next time. The same compounding happens in agent systems, team processes, application moats, and individual thinking.
> **译文：** 这就是本期主题：经验复利。模型按需提供能力，而组织拥有的是将成功转化为可重复方法、将失败转化为防止再犯的约束、将专家修正转化为每个人下次都能使用的判断力的能力。同样的复利效应也发生在智能体系统、团队流程、应用护城河和个人思维之中。
Here are 10 highlights worth your attention this week:
> **译文：** 以下是本周值得关注的10个亮点：
🛡️ Capability and control must learn together. GPT-6 Astra is OpenAI's first broadly deployed model to reach the Critical cybersecurity threshold. In internal Codex simulations, it produced roughly half as many high-severity misalignment flags as GPT-5.6 Sol, yet its chain-of-thought monitorability declined. Google shipped its third Flash update in six weeks and introduced a cybersecurity-specialized model. Isolation, evaluation, monitoring, and access control cannot remain a one-time launch checklist.
> **译文：** 🛡️ 能力与控制必须同步学习。GPT-6 Astra 是 OpenAI 首个达到“关键”网络安全阈值并广泛部署的模型。在内部 Codex 模拟中，它产生的高严重性对齐偏差标志约为 GPT-5.6 Sol 的一半，但其思维链的可监控性有所下降。Google 在六周内发布了第三次 Flash 更新，并推出了一个网络安全专用模型。隔离、评估、监控和访问控制不能只是一次性的发布检查清单。
🎬 Generative media is entering real creative workflows. Google DeepMind researchers expect specialized models and unified multimodal systems to coexist, and argue that joint audio-video generation must learn the shared causes behind an event. More importantly, preference scores can reward outputs that are vivid and pleasing but less coherent. Reliable progress requires professional creative judgment, real task traces, early customer workflows, and production failures to flow continuously back into training and evaluation.
> **译文：** 🎬 生成式媒体正在进入真实的创意工作流程。Google DeepMind 的研究人员预计专用模型和统一多模态系统将共存，并认为联合音视频生成必须学习事件背后的共享因果。更重要的是，偏好分数可能奖励那些生动悦目但连贯性较差的输出。可靠的进步需要专业创意判断、真实任务轨迹、早期客户工作流以及生产环境中的失败持续回流到训练和评估中。
💻 AI-native teams redesign the development loop. The Claude Code team uses fan-out investigation to broaden search, then combines adversarial review with tests, screenshots, recordings, and metrics to build acceptance evidence. Across several Amazon cases, high-performing teams move specifications, steering context, strongly typed code, and local verification earlier. The shared lesson is that tools do not create organizational returns by themselves. Local speed becomes delivery capacity only when goals, context, validation, and review change together.
> **译文：** 💻 AI 原生团队正在重新设计开发闭环。Claude Code 团队使用扇出式调查来拓宽搜索范围，然后将对抗性审查与测试、截图、录屏和指标相结合，构建验收证据。在多个 Amazon 案例中，高绩效团队将规格说明、引导上下文、强类型代码和本地验证前置。共同的教训是：工具本身不会创造组织回报。只有当目标、上下文、验证和审查同步改变时，局部速度才能转化为交付能力。
🧩 Production agents often begin with a simple core. Anthropic recommends one agent loop, skills, and tools over existing business systems, plus the evaluation, memory, and safe execution production requires. A discussion of whether models will eat applications reaches a related conclusion: isolated features are vulnerable, while task decomposition, workflow, and outcome delivery require sustained product work. A moat is the system that turns capability into dependable results.
> **译文：** 🧩 生产级智能体往往始于一个简单的核心。Anthropic 建议在现有业务系统之上使用单一智能体循环、技能和工具，并配备生产所需的评估、记忆和安全执行机制。一场关于“模型是否会吞噬应用”的讨论得出了相关结论：孤立的功能是脆弱的，而任务分解、工作流和结果交付则需要持续的产品工作。护城河是将能力转化为可靠结果的系统。
🦞 Scale amplifies collaboration and risk at the same time. OpenClaw 2.0 brought together more than 16,000 pull requests from 933 contributors, including 569 first-time contributors, showing the new scale available to open-source projects. Ruan Yifeng supplies the counterweight: enormous change volume also magnifies weak review, supply-chain exposure, and convergence toward familiar technology stacks. As the cost of producing a contribution falls, direction, integration, isolation, and maintainer attention become more scarce.
> **译文：** 🦞 规模同时放大了协作与风险。OpenClaw 2.0 汇集了来自933位贡献者的超过16,000个拉取请求，其中包括569位首次贡献者，展示了开源项目可达到的新规模。阮一峰提供了另一面视角：巨大的变更量也放大了审查薄弱、供应链暴露以及对熟悉技术栈的趋同。当产生贡献的成本下降时，方向、集成、隔离和维护者注意力变得更加稀缺。
🧠 Organizational memory matters because it can change safely. Meta connects knowledge files, expert reasoning, evaluation, and an improvement loop so one correction becomes a minimal knowledge edit and a permanent regression case. Forge Memory gives engineering knowledge stable identity, evidence, and dual admission gates across six roles. Experience reaches the next decision only after diagnosis, verification, versioning, and controlled admission. Without those mechanisms, a memory system is merely an expanding archive of uncertain history.
> **译文：** 🧠 组织记忆之所以重要，是因为它可以安全地改变。Meta 将知识文件、专家推理、评估和改进循环连接起来，使一次修正成为一次最小的知识编辑和一个永久的回归用例。Forge Memory 赋予工程知识稳定的身份、证据和跨六个角色的双重准入机制。经验只有经过诊断、验证、版本化和受控准入，才能到达下一个决策。没有这些机制，记忆系统只是一个不断膨胀的、充满不确定历史的档案库。
📚 Knowledge should grow where work happens. Taobao places business knowledge, code, rules, and verification evidence inside a local project harness built from an agent, folders, and Git, making iteration itself a knowledge flywheel. Ctrip connects documentation generation to code changes across 74 repositories and seven business groups, reducing manual maintenance time by more than 80%. The most durable context is not another summary written after the fact. It is a production asset that changes with delivery and remains verifiable and traceable.
> **译文：** 📚 知识应在工作发生的地方生长。淘宝将业务知识、代码、规则和验证证据放入由智能体、文件夹和 Git 构建的本地项目框架中，使迭代本身成为知识飞轮。携程将文档生成与74个代码仓库和7个业务集团的代码变更相连接，将手动维护时间减少了80%以上。最持久的上下文不是事后撰写的另一份摘要，而是随交付而变化的、可验证且可追溯的生产资产。
🤝 Agents are moving from isolated tasks to durable coworkers. The Agent Teams proposal borrows discussion, consent, roles, negotiated OKRs, and retrospectives to add collaboration semantics to multi-agent systems. Tara Seshan describes the broader direction as persistent AI coworkers, with people moving from rowing to steering while retaining responsibility, taste, strategic framing, and interpersonal leadership. This future is still emerging, but it already exposes the next set of product problems: continuity of context, visible sources, transparent reasoning, and quality measures for ambiguous knowledge work.
> **译文：** 🤝 智能体正在从孤立任务走向持久同事。Agent Teams 提案借鉴了讨论、同意、角色、协商式 OKR 和回顾机制，为多智能体系统添加了协作语义。Tara Seshan 将更广泛的方向描述为“持久 AI 同事”，人类从“划桨”转向“掌舵”，同时保留责任、品味、战略框架和人际领导力。这一未来仍在形成中，但已经暴露出下一批产品问题：上下文的连续性、可见的来源、透明的推理，以及模糊知识工作的质量衡量。
🔄 Individuals need feedback flywheels too. Through repeated redesign of BaoCut, Baoyu reduced model calls from 33 to 12 and per-episode processing time from 31 minutes to 18, then extends the same goal-first method to personal development. Nick Saraev moves from attention and environmental design to probability, opportunity cost, and bottleneck analysis, offering a trainable system for clear thinking. AI can take on execution, but defining the problem, allocating attention, and accepting the result still improve only through deliberate practice against real feedback.
> **译文：** 🔄 个人也需要反馈飞轮。通过反复重构 BaoCut，Baoyu 将模型调用从33次减少到12次，单集处理时间从31分钟缩短到18分钟，并将同样的目标优先方法延伸到个人成长。Nick Saraev 从注意力和环境设计转向概率、机会成本和瓶颈分析，提供了一套可训练的清晰思维系统。AI 可以承担执行，但定义问题、分配注意力和接受结果，仍然只能通过对真实反馈的刻意练习来提升。
🧭 People still steer and remain accountable. Paul Graham measures exceptional founders by their ability to keep getting results under difficult conditions. Zeng Ming uses industrial history to examine task-based coordination, emergent strategy, and changing company boundaries. Lan Xiaohuan reminds us that organizations still isolate risk and bear responsibility because an AI system cannot be the accountable legal subject. AI can transform production without supplying goals, non-consensus judgment, or ownership of consequences. Experience compounds when people and organizations use what they learned to make a better next decision.
> **译文：** 🧭 人仍然掌舵并承担责任。Paul Graham 以在困难条件下持续取得成果的能力来衡量杰出创始人。曾鸣利用工业史来审视基于任务的协调、涌现战略和不断变化的公司边界。兰小欢提醒我们，组织仍然隔离风险并承担责任，因为 AI 系统不能成为负责任的法人主体。AI 可以改变生产，却无法提供目标、非共识判断或后果的所有权。当人们和组织用所学到的经验做出更好的下一个决策时，经验就产生了复利。
I hope these selections offer a useful lens. Stay curious, and see you next week!
> **译文：** 希望这些精选能为你提供有用的视角。保持好奇，下周见！

4. How the Claude Code team uses Claude Code

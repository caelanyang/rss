* *The Market Speaks.* Anthropic released Fable 5.1 this week, and the most interesting part of their announcement had nothing to do with the model or its capabilities, but rather its terms. Anthropic is walking back their controversial data retention policies  that sparked a huge backlash earlier this year. The reason? The company needs to make money, and OpenAI is competing hard (and OpenAI had a model release of their own, which I talked to OpenAI President Greg Brockman about in the Stratechery Interview  ). — Ben Thompson
> **译文：** * *市场发声。* Anthropic本周发布了Fable 5.1，其公告中最有趣的部分与模型或其能力无关，而在于条款。Anthropic正在撤回其引发今年早些时候巨大反弹的争议性数据保留政策。原因何在？公司需要赚钱，而OpenAI正在激烈竞争（OpenAI也发布了自己的模型，我在Stratechery访谈中与OpenAI总裁Greg Brockman就此进行了对话）。—— Ben Thompson
* *Apple Finds Religion.* Dedicated readers and listeners are no doubt familiar with Ben’s crusade to fix the Vision Pro, and specifically Apple’s approach to producing live events in immersive video . Well, Apple nailed it with their immersive broadcast of Friday Night Baseball. Come to Monday’s Dithering episode  to hear John’s experience with the Red Sox and Yankees, and stay for Ben’s smug satisfaction (and don’t worry, he registers a few additional complaints for good measure). — Andrew Sharp
> **译文：** * *苹果找到信仰。* 忠实读者和听众无疑熟悉Ben为修复Vision Pro所做的努力，特别是苹果在沉浸式视频中制作直播活动的方式。嗯，苹果通过其对Friday Night Baseball的沉浸式直播做到了。请收听周一的Dithering节目，了解John对红袜队和洋基队比赛的体验，并留下来听Ben得意洋洋的满足感（别担心，他还会额外提几条抱怨以保险起见）。—— Andrew Sharp
* *Society Loses Friction.* Meta announced last week that as part of a settlement with 29 states it will make sweeping changes to restrict the use of Instagram and Meta among teens. Ben had conflicted reactions to the news on Monday  , and on Thursday’s Sharp Tech  we went deeper on the libertarian vs. conservative tensions that will inform a variety of these regulation questions going forward. That conversation ultimately echoed a very early Stratechery riff  on the society-altering implications of tech that removes friction; 13 years later, our generation is still catching up. — AS
> **译文：** * *社会失去摩擦。* Meta上周宣布，作为与29个州和解协议的一部分，它将进行大刀阔斧的改革，限制青少年使用Instagram和Meta。Ben周一对此消息反应矛盾，而在周四的Sharp Tech节目中，我们更深入地探讨了自由意志主义与保守主义之间的张力，这些张力将影响未来各种监管问题。那段对话最终呼应了Stratechery早期关于消除摩擦的技术对社会变革影响的一段论述；13年后，我们这一代人仍在追赶。—— AS

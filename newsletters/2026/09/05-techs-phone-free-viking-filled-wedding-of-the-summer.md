After Code.org founder Hadi Partovi popped the question a year or so ago, he and his fiancée, Becca Oystila, weighed the options for their big day. Partovi envisioned something intimate.  “I was just like, ‘Let’s go to the courthouse,’” he said. Oystila, whose Los Angeles–based startup, Booka, makes a platform for hairstylists and makeup artists to manage their businesses, pictured something unique. They went with her plan.

> **译文：** 大约一年前，Code.org创始人Hadi Partovi求婚后，他和未婚妻Becca
> Oystila为他们的重要日子权衡了各种选择。Partovi设想的是私密温馨的仪式。“我当时就说，‘我们去法院领证吧，’”他说。而Oystila——她位于洛杉矶的初创公司Booka为发型师和化妆师提供业务管理平台——则想象的是独一无二的体验。他们最终采纳了她的方案。

The result was tech’s wedding of the summer—or at any rate, its least conventional. And since no self-respecting founder would ever just fully disconnect on their wedding weekend, the occasion also provided Oystila a chance to give Booka’s tech a stress test of sorts while she considers a planned nationwide expansion this fall.

> **译文：** 结果成就了科技界今夏最引人注目的婚礼——或者至少是最不循常规的一场。由于任何有自尊心的创始人都不会在婚礼周末彻底放下工作，这场庆典也为Oystila提供了一个机会，在她考虑今年秋天计划中的全国扩张之际，对Booka的技术进行某种压力测试。

For the celebration, Partovi and Oystila arranged a multiday slate of festivities in her native Sweden in July, just after the Midsommar solstice holiday. Guests included his twin brother, Ali, a venture capitalist; Sequoia Capital’s Alfred Lin; Rover co-founder Aaron Easterly; and Emily White, investor and former Snapchat executive. His Khosrowshahi cousins came, too. There was Fuzzy, chief technology officer at Notion; Kaveh, an Allen &amp; Co. managing director; and Dara, who is, of course, CEO of Uber. In all, the group numbered some 230 people.

> **译文：** 为了这场庆典，Partovi和Oystila于7月在瑞典——就在仲夏节假期之后——安排了一场为期数日的庆祝活动。宾客包括他的双胞胎兄弟、风险投资人Ali；红杉资本的Alfred
> Lin；Rover联合创始人Aaron Easterly；以及投资人、Snapchat前高管Emily
> White。他的Khosrowshahi表亲们也来了：Fuzzy是Notion的首席技术官；Kaveh是Allen &
> Co.的董事总经理；还有Dara——当然，他是Uber的CEO。宾客总计约230人。

One night included a black-tie ceremony and reception at the Grand Hotel in Stockholm. And one entire day was spent at Birka, an island that lies a short boat ride away from the city and contains the remains of a Viking village. Partovi and Oystila asked their guests to bring their own Viking Age costumes, study up on the era and adopt a period-appropriate persona for the day. (“You had to think about what character you were from back then—and you couldn’t talk about your day job,” Partovi said.) Refreshments included mead, roast lamb and fresh-caught perch.

> **译文：** 其中一晚在斯德哥尔摩的Grand
> Hotel举行了黑领结正装仪式和招待晚宴。还有整整一天是在Birka度过的——这座岛屿距离城市仅一小段船程，岛上保留着一座维京村落的遗迹。Partovi和Oystila请宾客们自带维京时代的服装，研究那个时代的历史，并在当天扮演一个符合时代背景的角色。（“你得想想自己当时是什么人物——而且不能聊你日常的工作，”Partovi说。）餐饮包括蜂蜜酒、烤羊肉和现捕的鲈鱼。

For entertainment, an expert local ax thrower led the group in an ax-throwing competition. A runereader offered fortune-telling sessions. Later, a hired band of actors in full Viking gear staged a pantomimed raid on the attendees. In the end, Partovi and Oystila wed atop a hill, with odes to Odin all around. As wedding favors, they handed out hand-carved drinking horns. (Some pictures from the revelry are below.)To further help their guests get into the spirit of things, Oystila made a full contingent of makeup artists available at the hotel—their hair-braiding and face-painting services were bookable through Booka, which has newly added a feature that allows its platform to be used for events. (To study how the app worked for the artists, Oystila stationed herself nearby with her laptop. “They sold out,” she said. “It was a lot of pressure.”)

> **译文：** 娱乐活动方面，一位当地的专业斧头投掷手带领大家进行了投斧比赛。一位如尼文占卜师提供了算命服务。后来，一队身着全套维京装束的演员对宾客们进行了一场哑剧式的“突袭”。最终，Partovi和Oystila在一座小山顶上完婚，四周环绕着献给奥丁的颂歌。作为婚礼回礼，他们赠送了手工雕刻的饮酒角。（以下是狂欢中的一些照片。）为了进一步帮助宾客融入氛围，Oystila在酒店安排了整整一支化妆师团队——他们的编发和面部彩绘服务可以通过Booka预订，该平台新增了一项功能，允许其系统用于活动场景。（为了研究这款应用对艺术家们的使用效果，Oystila带着笔记本电脑守在附近。“他们全部约满了，”她说，“压力很大。”）

Once at the island, everyone faced a strict no-phones rule. “You know, a lot of the wedding guests are normally very attached to their phones and work a lot,” said Oystila. “And a lot of those people were the ones actually telling us, ‘Oh, my god, it was so nice to just take a break from my phone and not even be able to go check it and see what was happening.”—Abram Brown (abe@theinformation.com)

> **译文：** 一旦登上岛屿，所有人都必须严格遵守禁止使用手机的规定。“你知道，很多婚礼宾客平时都离不开手机，工作也很忙，”Oystila说。“而恰恰是那些人告诉我们，‘天哪，能暂时放下手机、甚至无法查看上面发生了什么，真是太棒了。’”——Abram

Listening: “Dolly Parton’s America”

Last week, Dolly Parton’s unexpected death led to a singular moment in bitterly fractured America: an outpouring of unanimous admiration. On Twitter, Barack Obama praised her philanthropy and her campaign to popularize the Covid-19 vaccine. In Parton’s home state of Tennessee, Republican Gov. Bill Lee promised to bolster a proposal to rename Nashville’s airport in her honor. And even Jeff Bezos added to the pile of tributes, posting a picture of himself with Parton on Instagram—his first photo of something besides a rocket since his own mother’s death a year ago.

> **译文：** 上周，Dolly Parton的意外离世在这个严重分裂的美国引发了一个罕见的时刻：众口一词的赞誉。在Twitter上，Barack
> Obama称赞了她的慈善事业以及她推动Covid-19疫苗普及的运动。在Parton的家乡田纳西州，共和党州长Bill Lee承诺支持一项以她命名纳什维尔机场的提案。就连Jeff
> Bezos也加入了悼念行列，在Instagram上发布了自己与Parton的合照——这是他自一年前母亲去世以来发布的第一张非火箭照片。

The eulogies underscored the same truth about Parton’s wide reach that underpins “Dolly Parton’s America,” an excellent, nine-episode podcast about the singer-songwriter. The 2019 series, which doubles as a deeply reported history of country music and 20th-century Americana, sprang from the mind of Jad Abumrad, creator of Radiolab, and it won him a Peabody Award, one of journalism’s top honors. (I’ve previously recommended two other Abumrad pods: “Fela Kuti: Fear No Man” and “The Obit Project.”)

> **译文：** 这些悼词都印证了关于Parton广泛影响力的同一个事实——这正是“Dolly Parton’s
> America”这档关于这位创作歌手的优秀九集播客的基础。这档2019年的系列节目同时也是一部深度报道的乡村音乐史和20世纪美国文化史，它出自Radiolab创始人Jad
> Abumrad之手，并为他赢得了新闻界最高荣誉之一的皮博迪奖。（我之前还推荐过Abumrad的另外两档播客：“Fela Kuti: Fear No Man”和“The Obit Project”。）

For the series, Parton gave ample time to Abumrad, who came to realize that he, like many others, had misjudged her as an unrefined figure of rhinestone kitsch. Yes, Parton was buxom and glam-tastic, and she gladly said so herself. But as Abumrad explains, she was also a brilliant, funny lyricist; a shrewd businesswoman; and a gifted instrumentalist, who could play everything from the dulcimer to the pan flute. To paraphrase Parton herself, a dumb blond she very much was not.

> **译文：** 在制作这档系列节目时，Parton给了Abumrad充足的时间，而他逐渐意识到，自己和许多人一样，曾误将她视为一个粗俗的水钻媚俗形象。没错，Parton确实丰满迷人、光彩夺目，而且她也乐于承认这一点。但正如Abumrad所揭示的，她还是一位才华横溢、妙趣横生的词曲作者；一位精明的商人；以及一位天赋异禀的乐器演奏家，从扬琴到排箫无所不能。套用Parton自己的话来说，她绝非什么金发傻妞。

Abumrad begins “Dolly Parton’s America” with a lament similar to one I expressed: that deeply divided America has few universally adored stars. It’s an uncomfortable reminder of how little has changed between 2019 and 2026. Still, as Abumrad would surely agree, Parton is a hopeful reminder that the country’s calcified polarization isn’t unbreakable.—Abram Brown

> **译文：** Abumrad在“Dolly Parton’s
> America”开头表达了一种与我相似的感慨：深度分裂的美国已经很少有被普遍爱戴的明星了。这令人不安地提醒我们，从2019年到2026年，变化何其之少。不过，正如Abumrad一定会认同的那样，Parton是一个充满希望的提醒——这个国家僵化的两极分化并非坚不可摧。——Abram
> Brown

Reading: “Money to Burn” by William D. Cohan

Even in the Wall Street stratosphere, Leon Black ranks as a singular figure. Apollo, the investment firm he co-founded, controls $1.1 trillion in assets under management, and its profits have pushed Black’s personal fortune to an estimated $14.8 billion. That fabulous wealth has afforded him many treasures: a version of Edvard Munch’s “The Scream” hangs above his Upper East Side fireplace. (He paid $130 million for it.) And yet Black languishes in quasipublic exile, the result of his prominent links with Jeffrey Epstein. (For various services that Black insists never involved underage women, he paid Epstein $153 million.)

> **译文：** 即使在华尔街的顶尖圈层中，Leon
> Black也算得上是一个独特的人物。他联合创立的投资公司Apollo管理着1.1万亿美元的资产，其利润将Black的个人财富推高至约148亿美元。这笔惊人的财富为他带来了许多珍宝：一幅Edvard
> Munch的《呐喊》版本悬挂在他上东区壁炉上方。（他为此支付了1.3亿美元。）然而，Black却处于一种半公开的流放状态——这是他与Jeffrey
> Epstein之间密切关联的结果。（对于Black坚称绝不涉及未成年女性的各种服务，他向Epstein支付了1.53亿美元。）

Black, 75, typically shuns interviews, but he had lengthy conversations with William D. Cohan, the Vanity Fair scribe turned Puck News columnist, as Cohan worked on “Money to Burn,” which charts the multiple rises and falls of Black and his family. (Black’s own father was once a well-known CEO; he committed suicide in 1975 by jumping from his 44th-floor office.) The 668-page tome is definitive, lively and gossipy: Seldom has a chronicle involving annuities and AAA-rated debt made for finer entertainment.

> **译文：** 现年75岁的布莱克通常回避采访，但在《名利场》记者转投Puck
> News担任专栏作家的威廉·D·科汉撰写《金钱燃烧》一书期间，他与科汉进行了长时间的对话。该书讲述了布莱克及其家族多次起落的故事。（布莱克的父亲曾是一位知名CEO，1975年从44层办公室跳楼自杀。）这本668页的大部头内容翔实、生动活泼、八卦味十足：很少有涉及年金和AAA级债务的纪实作品能带来如此精彩的娱乐效果。

As always, Cohan is an amiable tour guide for a walk down Wall Street, offering an education on the various fads and trends that have shaped the place over the last several decades and continue to do so. Apollo has capitalized well on many of them, including the recent boom in private credit.

> **译文：** 一如既往，科汉是华尔街漫步中一位和蔼可亲的向导，为读者讲述了数十年来塑造华尔街并持续影响至今的各种风潮和趋势。Apollo充分利用了其中许多机会，包括近期私人信贷的繁荣。

Candidly, though, what I enjoyed most was Cohan’s indelible portrait of Black as a world-trotting mogul. In one instance, Black reminisced about how he and Donald Trump bribed a machine gun–toting guard to see Tina Turner perform at the Kremlin. “I pulled out 100 rubles, and we got in,” Black recalled. “A fabulous concert.”

> **译文：** 不过坦率地说，我最喜欢的是科汉对布莱克这位环球大亨的传神刻画。有一次，布莱克回忆了他和唐纳德·特朗普如何贿赂一名持机枪的警卫，以便在克里姆林宫观看蒂娜·特纳的演出。“我掏出100卢布，我们就进去了，”布莱克回忆道，“一场精彩的音乐会。”

Quite a few people may just look up Epstein’s name in the book’s index and flip directly to those pages, expecting salacious detail. They won’t be disappointed—and they will have much to pore over: By my count, Epstein’s name appears 590 times in “Money to Burn.” After they met in the ’90s, Black employed Epstein for “trust and taxation and estate planning work,” he told Cohan. Their relationship grew, and Black enjoyed how Epstein introduced him to other famous rich people outside Wall Street’s usual circle, like Bill Gates and Elon Musk.

> **译文：** 不少人可能只会翻到书后索引中爱泼斯坦的名字，直接跳到相关页面，期待看到香艳细节。他们不会失望——而且会有大量内容可供细读：据我统计，爱泼斯坦的名字在《金钱燃烧》中出现了590次。布莱克告诉科汉，两人在90年代相识后，他聘请爱泼斯坦处理“信托、税务和遗产规划方面的工作”。他们的关系不断加深，布莱克很喜欢爱泼斯坦将他引荐给华尔街常规圈子之外的其他知名富豪，如比尔·盖茨和埃隆·马斯克。

Black acknowledges that he decided to give Epstein the, uh, benefit of the doubt over his 2008 conviction for soliciting an underage sex worker. “I mean, he was with a 17-year-old prostitute, got prosecuted for it and got put away for a year,” Black said. “I took it seriously, but I didn’t take it that seriously.” (Later, the revelation that he had continued to embrace Epstein would play a part in forcing him to step down from running Apollo in 2021.) Separately, Black defends his decision to stay close to Epstein through a comparison to his longtime friendship with Michael Milken, the former junk bond king previously jailed for financial fraud. I can’t imagine Milken, who has spent decades rehabbing his image, will appreciate the analogy: Maybe that’s one friendship Black is willing to let go.—A.B.

> **译文：** 布莱克承认，对于爱泼斯坦2008年因招嫖未成年性工作者而被定罪一事，他决定给予他“疑点利益”。“我的意思是，他和一个17岁的妓女在一起，因此被起诉，被判了一年，”布莱克说，“我当时认真对待了这件事，但也没有那么认真。”（后来，他继续与爱泼斯坦保持密切关系的曝光，成为迫使他在2021年卸任Apollo管理职务的因素之一。）此外，布莱克还通过与长期好友迈克尔·米尔肯的友谊作比较，为自己与爱泼斯坦保持密切关系辩护。米尔肯是前垃圾债券之王，曾因金融欺诈入狱。我无法想象花了数十年重塑形象的米尔肯会欣赏这种类比：也许这是布莱克愿意放弃的一段友谊。——A.B.

Watching: “Coyote vs. Acme”

A corporate chieftain struts into Congress brimming with bombast and bravado, keen to dispel concerns that his company has knowingly sold harmful products to vulnerable, naive customers. I could be alluding to any number of Senate showdowns involving tech CEOs, but in this case, I’m describing a climactic scene in “Coyote vs. Acme,” a zany parody of legal dramas and corporate America starring a slew of Looney Tunes characters and comedian Will Forte as Kevin Avery, a class-action attorney.

> **译文：** 一位企业巨头大摇大摆走进国会，满口豪言壮语，急于打消人们对其公司明知产品有害却仍出售给弱势、天真客户的担忧。我可能是在暗指任何一场涉及科技CEO的参议院对决，但在这里，我描述的是《歪心狼 vs.
> ACME公司》中的高潮场景——一部由众多《乐一通》角色和喜剧演员威尔·福特主演的疯狂法律剧与企业美国讽刺片，福特饰演集体诉讼律师凯文·艾弗里。

As in “Who Framed Roger Rabbit?” people and toons live side by side in “Coyote vs. Acme,” and Forte’s Avery has taken on Wile E. Coyote for a client. Wile is suing Acme Corp. for the faulty contraptions—the rocket-powered roller skates, the giant-slingshot kit, and so forth—that failed him during his quest to capture the Roadrunner. Defending Acme is the company’s chief legal officer, Buddy Crane (John Cena). His boss is that CEO I mentioned: Foghorn Leghorn, who, I say, sure has come faa-ah from his farmstead days. Foghorn now ruthlessly rules the roost atop Acme, which has expanded from selling items for toons to developing ones for humans, too. Bugs, Daffy, Porky Pig and many other famous Looney Tunes appear in cameos and supporting roles. (They’re mostly all voiced by Eric Bauza, a sanctioned replacement to Mel Blanc, the man who originally developed the characters’ voices.)

> **译文：** 与《谁陷害了兔子罗杰》一样，在《歪心狼 vs.
> ACME公司》中，人类和卡通角色共存。福特饰演的艾弗里接下歪心狼作为客户，后者正在起诉ACME公司，因为那些有缺陷的装置——火箭动力溜冰鞋、巨型弹弓套装等等——在他追捕哔哔鸟的过程中屡屡失灵。为ACME辩护的是公司首席法务官巴迪·克兰（约翰·塞纳饰）。他的老板就是我提到的那位CEO：福格霍恩·莱格霍恩——我得说，他确实早已远离了当年农场的日子。如今福格霍恩冷酷地统治着ACME的顶端，公司已从销售卡通角色用品扩展到也为人类开发产品。兔八哥、达菲鸭、猪小弟等众多著名《乐一通》角色以客串和配角身份出现。（他们大多由埃里克·鲍扎配音，他是最初创造这些角色声音的梅尔·布兰克的官方继任者。）

Sight gags abound. I particularly enjoyed how a demonstration for a can of Predator Spray came at the expense of Pepé Le Pew, the aggressively amorous skunk who, as you might recall, got canceled a while back as a hand-drawn exemplar of sexual predation.

By Martin Peers

Sep 7, 2026

Thanks for reading The Briefing, our nightly column where we break down the day’s news.

> **译文：** 感谢阅读 The Briefing，我们的晚间专栏，为您解读当日新闻。

If you like what you see, I encourage you to subscribe to our reporting here. Greetings!

> **译文：** 如果您喜欢所读内容，我鼓励您在此订阅我们的报道。向您问好！

We haven’t had much of a summer lull, let’s face it, what with the relentless pace of AI-related deal news, not to mention earnings.

> **译文：** 坦白说，今年夏天我们几乎没有喘息之机——AI相关交易新闻层出不穷，更不用说财报季的密集轰炸了。

Nevertheless, the end of summer vacation changes the pace a bit this week, when we’ve got both Apple’s annual iPhone event and earnings announcements from Oracle and Adobe.

> **译文：** 不过，暑假结束让本周的节奏有所变化——本周既有苹果一年一度的iPhone发布会，也有甲骨文和Adobe的财报公布。

And we’re also on the lookout for Anthropic’s IPO filing, which seems likely to come sometime in the next few weeks.

> **译文：** 同时我们也在关注Anthropic的IPO申请文件，预计可能在未来几周内提交。

Leaving aside Anthropic—whose timing is highly uncertain—Oracle’s earnings announcement should be the most interesting scheduled news event of the week.

> **译文：** 暂且不论Anthropic——其时间表高度不确定——甲骨文的财报应是本周最值得关注的既定新闻事件。

Yes, Apple fans will get all worked up about the foldable iPhone expected to be unveiled on Wednesday, when new CEO John Ternus takes the stage. (Bloomberg had a nice rundown on Friday). But that’s mostly marketing.

> **译文：** 没错，苹果粉丝们会为预计周三发布的可折叠iPhone兴奋不已，届时新任CEO John Ternus将登台亮相。（彭博社周五有一篇不错的综述。）但那主要是营销层面的看点。

Oracle’s numbers—due out on Thursday—will give us a much-needed update on how Larry Ellison’s costly adventures in AI data centers are going.

> **译文：** 甲骨文的财报——周四公布——将为我们提供急需的最新信息，了解Larry Ellison在AI数据中心上的高昂投入进展如何。

Investor sentiment around Oracle is not exactly upbeat right now, reflecting anxieties about the company’s big spending to build out new data centers and how that’s affecting its debt levels and cash flows.

> **译文：** 目前投资者对甲骨文的情绪并不乐观，反映出市场对其大规模建设新数据中心的开支以及这对债务水平和现金流影响的担忧。

Indeed, in July S&amp;P downgraded Oracle’s credit rating, citing in part its reliance on OpenAI as a customer.

> **译文：** 事实上，7月份标普下调了甲骨文的信用评级，部分原因正是其将OpenAI作为客户的依赖。

It also cited Oracle’s expected capital expenditures forecast for this fiscal year, which is expected to lift its cash burn sharply.

> **译文：** 标普还提到了甲骨文本财年的预期资本支出预测，预计这将使其现金消耗大幅上升。

Analysts estimate Oracle will burn $48 billion this fiscal year, double the amount for the year ending in May, according to S&amp;P Global Market Intelligence.

> **译文：** 据标普全球市场情报数据，分析师估计甲骨文本财年将烧掉480亿美元，是截至5月财年的两倍。

And while Oracle stock has bounced off its lows of late July, it is down 19% for the year so far.

> **译文：** 尽管甲骨文股价已从7月底的低点反弹，但今年以来仍下跌了19%。

Still, fiscal 2027, which began in June, is also when Oracle’s revenue growth is expected to accelerate sharply, as revenue from new AI compute rental deals begins to flow.

> **译文：** 不过，始于6月的2027财年也被视为甲骨文营收增长预计将大幅加速的时期，因为新的AI算力租赁交易的收入将开始流入。

Oracle has projected top-line growth of about 28% for the first quarter—compared with 21% in the May quarter—accelerating to 34% for the full year.

> **译文：** 甲骨文预计第一季度营收增长约28%——相比之下5月当季为21%——全年加速至34%。

Investors will likely focus on whether the growth rates are getting any upward revision, and whether the costs of the data center expansion are also rising.

> **译文：** 投资者可能会关注增长率是否会上调，以及数据中心扩张的成本是否也在上升。

Adobe in the SpotlightWe’ve also got Adobe reporting earnings on Thursday.

> **译文：** Adobe成为焦点——本周四Adobe也将公布财报。

This will be a noteworthy call, given that the enterprise software firm last week named the successor to Shantanu Narayen, who has been the man in charge since 2007.

> **译文：** 这将是一次值得关注的电话会议，因为这家企业软件公司上周刚刚确定了Shantanu Narayen的继任者——Narayen自2007年以来一直执掌该公司。

Starting in December, Adobe will be run by Anil Chakravarthy, who has been leading Adobe’s digital experiences business—which includes data analytics and marketing services— for the past few years, although he got a title change in January when Adobe revamped its org chart.

> **译文：** 从12月起，Adobe将由Anil Chakravarthy掌舵。过去几年他一直领导Adobe的数字体验业务——涵盖数据分析和营销服务——尽管今年1月Adobe重组组织架构时他的头衔有所调整。

While Narayen is still in charge, we may hear from Chakravarthy on Thursday’s call.

> **译文：** 虽然Narayen仍在负责，但周四的电话会议上我们可能会听到Chakravarthy的发言。

What’s intriguing about the choice of Chakravarthy is that digital experience is not only the smaller of Adobe’s two main business segments but also grew more slowly than digital media last year (Adobe combined the two segments in the revamp).

> **译文：** 选择查克拉瓦蒂之所以引人关注，是因为数字体验不仅是Adobe两大主营业务中规模较小的一个，而且去年其增长速度也慢于数字媒体业务（Adobe在重组中将这两个业务合并）。

Moreover, Goldman Sachs analyst Gabriela Borges suggested in an interview on The Information’s TITV on Friday that Adobe had more of an opportunity in its consumer business—essentially digital media services such as Photoshop—than in areas like marketing, where competition is intense.

> **译文：** 此外，高盛分析师加布里埃拉·博尔赫斯上周五在The Information的TITV节目中接受采访时表示，Adobe在消费者业务——主要是Photoshop等数字媒体服务——方面的机会比在营销等领域更大，后者竞争激烈。

And yet the person who ran digital media, David Wadhwani, is leaving as a result of Chakravarthy’s ascension. Huh.

> **译文：** 然而，曾负责数字媒体业务的大卫·瓦德瓦尼却因查克拉瓦蒂的晋升而离职。嗯。

Not that either side of the business has been a rocket ship: Adobe has been growing at a very steady 10% to 11% annual pace since 2021.

> **译文：** 倒不是说这两块业务中哪一块表现亮眼：自2021年以来，Adobe一直保持着非常稳定的10%至11%的年增长率。

Analysts are expecting the company to report 11.8% growth for the August quarter—nothing spectacularly different.

> **译文：** 分析师预计该公司8月当季将报告11.8%的增长——并无特别出彩之处。

The stock hasn’t been a strong performer, falling 24% so far this year. Yes, Adobe has not yet escaped the Saaspocalypse.

> **译文：** 该股表现并不强劲，今年以来已下跌24%。没错，Adobe尚未摆脱“SaaS末日”的阴影。

We’re curious what Chakravarthy will have to say if he speaks on Thursday. In Other News • Humain, Saudi Arabia’s state-owned AI company, announced on Thursday an Arabic large language model developed based on Chinese AI firm MiniMax’s model.

The loan comes as ByteDance is ramping up its capex to expand its AI infrastructure.

> **译文：** 这笔贷款是在字节跳动加大资本支出以扩展其AI基础设施之际获得的。

• TikTok has backed out of a meeting with the U.S. House Select Committee on China that’s meant to address national security concerns.

The company cited “a desire to avoid broader scrutiny of its child safety practices,” according to a statement from committee chair John Moolenaar on Wednesday.

> **译文：** 据委员会主席约翰·穆勒纳尔周三的一份声明，该公司给出的理由是“希望避免其儿童安全做法受到更广泛的审查”。

> **译文：** • 彭博社周五报道，阿布扎比AI公司G42——阿联酋AI战略的核心企业——已讨论将多数控制权移交给美国公司，以便明年继续购买最先进的芯片，包括英伟达的H100芯片。

Options included selling a majority stake or creating a new entity in the U.S., the report said. G42 did not respond for a request to comment at this time.

> **译文：** • 彭博社援引知情人士消息报道，DeepSeek计划在中国北方内蒙古的一个数据中心安装至少16万颗华为AI芯片。

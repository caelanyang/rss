For the past two years, software investors have worried that AI would eat into seats, pricing, and eventually the software model itself.
> **译文：** 过去两年，软件投资者一直担心AI会侵蚀席位、定价，最终动摇软件模式本身。
This week, Palo Alto Networks and Snowflake showed the other side of that trade.
> **译文：** 本周，Palo Alto Networks和Snowflake展示了这一交易的另一面。
Palo Alto is seeing AI create more identities, traffic, and attack surfaces to secure. Snowflake is seeing AI workloads drive more consumption across the data platform.
> **译文：** Palo Alto看到AI创造了更多需要保护的身份、流量和攻击面。Snowflake则看到AI工作负载推动数据平台上更多消费。
In both cases, AI is driving more usage across the software stack.
> **译文：** 在这两个案例中，AI都在推动软件栈上更多的使用量。

1. ☁️ Palo Alto: AI Security Comes Together
Palo Alto closed Q4 FY26 (ending in July) with revenue up 34% Y/Y to $3.4 billion ($60 million beat), while non-GAAP EPS reached $1.02 ($0.04 beat).
> **译文：** Palo Alto FY26第四季度（截至7月）收官，营收同比增长34%至34亿美元（超出预期6000万美元），非GAAP每股收益达1.02美元（超出预期0.04美元）。
Acquisitions still make the headline growth rate messy, but the underlying demand indicators were strong. Palo Alto added a record $970 million of net new Next-Gen Security ARR, while landing roughly 220 net new platformizations, up 44% and twice the Q3 pace. If you recall, platformization is the fancy word for multi-product adoption.
> **译文：** 收购仍使整体增长率显得杂乱，但潜在需求指标强劲。Palo Alto新增创纪录的9.7亿美元净新增下一代安全ARR，同时新增约220个净新平台化客户，环比增长44%，是第三季度速度的两倍。如果你还记得，平台化是多产品采用的花哨说法。
Palo Alto finished FY26 with a 38% adjusted free cash flow margin, essentially preserving its high-30s profitability while absorbing two major acquisitions (CyberArk and Chronosphere).
> **译文：** Palo Alto FY26全年调整后自由现金流利润率为38%，在吸收两项重大收购（CyberArk和Chronosphere）的同时基本保持了近40%的盈利能力。
The GAAP numbers weren’t as pretty. Palo Alto swung to a $282 million net loss, but this was mostly accounting noise driven by one-off acquisition adjustments. The quarter notably included a $524 million mark-to-market charge related to CyberArk convertible notes, along with $281 million of acquired intangible amortization and $487 million of stock-based compensation.
> **译文：** GAAP数据则不那么好看。Palo Alto转为2.82亿美元的净亏损，但这主要是由一次性收购调整带来的会计噪音。该季度特别包含了与CyberArk可转换债券相关的5.24亿美元按市值计价费用，以及2.81亿美元的收购无形资产摊销和4.87亿美元的股权激励费用。
FY27 revenue guidance of $14.1–$14.2 billion implies 23%–24% growth and came in above consensus, while management expects NGS ARR to grow 22%–23% to reach ~$11.1 billion and maintained its 38% adjusted FCF margin target.
> **译文：** FY27营收指引为141亿至142亿美元，意味着23%至24%的增长，高于市场共识；管理层预计NGS ARR将增长22%至23%，达到约111亿美元，并维持38%的调整后FCF利润率目标。
Management also reaffirmed its 40%+ adjusted free cash flow margin target for FY28 and $20 billion NGS ARR target for FY30.
> **译文：** 管理层还重申了FY28超过40%的调整后自由现金流利润率目标，以及FY30达到200亿美元NGS ARR的目标。
The central question for investors is whether this M&A spree creates genuine platform synergies. Early cross-sell data suggests it is.
> **译文：** 投资者面临的核心问题是，这轮并购狂潮是否创造了真正的平台协同效应。早期的交叉销售数据表明确实如此。
Platformization Accelerates
Next-Gen Security ARR reached $9.1 billion, up 63% Y/Y, while remaining performance obligations climbed 34% to a record $21.2 billion. Those growth rates still benefit heavily from CyberArk and Chronosphere, so they shouldn’t be read as organic growth.
> **译文：** 下一代安全ARR达到91亿美元，同比增长63%，剩余履约义务增长34%至创纪录的212亿美元。这些增长率仍大量受益于CyberArk和Chronosphere，因此不应被视为有机增长。
But Palo Alto provided another useful lens for FY26:
> **译文：** 但Palo Alto为FY26提供了另一个有用的视角：
Network & AI Security revenue: +17% Y/Y.
Cortex revenue: +25%.
Idira (CyberArk rebranding) revenue: +21% on a pro forma basis.
> **译文：** Idira（CyberArk更名后）收入：备考基础上增长21%。
In other words, each major platform is growing at a healthy double-digit rate.
> **译文：** 换句话说，每个主要平台都在以健康的两位数速度增长。
More importantly, customers are consolidating more spending with Palo Alto. Net retention among platformized customers remained above 120%. More than 65% of NGS ARR now comes from platformized customers.
> **译文：** 更重要的是，客户正在将更多支出集中到Palo Alto。平台化客户的净留存率保持在120%以上。目前超过65%的NGS ARR来自平台化客户。
Palo Alto’s customers spending more than $5 million in NGS ARR rose 45% Y/Y to 223, while customers above $10 million increased 50% to 78. One Q4 telecom deal alone was worth $126 million and combined firewalls, SASE, Idira, and Cortex XSIAM.
> **译文：** Palo Alto中NGS ARR超过500万美元的客户同比增长45%至223家，超过1000万美元的客户增长50%至78家。仅第四季度一笔电信交易就价值1.26亿美元，整合了防火墙、SASE、Idira和Cortex XSIAM。
AI security stack comes together
AI agents generate machine-to-machine traffic, access credentials, query internal data, and increasingly take actions autonomously. Palo Alto says agentic traffic across SASE has increased more than 9x in nine months. That makes AI both a new attack surface and a catalyst for upgrading legacy security infrastructure.
> **译文：** AI智能体产生机器对机器的流量、访问凭证、查询内部数据，并日益自主采取行动。Palo Alto表示，SASE上的智能体流量在九个月内增长了9倍以上。这使得AI既成为新的攻击面，也成为升级传统安全基础设施的催化剂。
Palo Alto is building around three main layers:
Prisma AIRS: Secure the AI. ARR reached roughly $120 million only one year after launch, making it Palo Alto’s fastest-scaling product. Customers jumped from more than 300 in Q3 to more than 800 in Q4. AIRS now spans runtime security, agent identities, red teaming, observability, and agent gateways.
> **译文：** Prisma AIRS：保护AI安全。发布仅一年后，ARR就达到约1.2亿美元，使其成为Palo Alto增长最快的产品。客户从第三季度的300多家跃升至第四季度的800多家。AIRS现已覆盖运行时安全、智能体身份、红队测试、可观测性和智能体网关。
Cortex: Detect and respond. XSIAM ARR surpassed $700 million, up roughly 70% Y/Y, with close to 1,000 customers. Chronosphere’s observability ARR also crossed $500 million, more than 2.5x its level just two quarters ago. Half of Chronosphere’s Q4 net-new logos included an XSIAM cross-sell, showing Palo Alto is already plugging the acquisition into the broader platform.
> **译文：** Cortex：检测与响应。XSIAM ARR超过7亿美元，同比增长约70%，客户接近1,000家。Chronosphere的可观测性ARR也突破5亿美元，是仅两个季度前的2.5倍以上。Chronosphere第四季度新增客户中有一半包含XSIAM交叉销售，表明Palo Alto已经在将收购整合到更广泛的平台中。
Idira: Control identity and access. The $25 billion CyberArk acquisition gives Palo Alto control over the credentials and permissions used by humans, machines, and AI agents. Early cross-selling is encouraging: CyberArk ACV grew 27% Y/Y, Palo Alto generated more than 200 CyberArk new-logo wins from its existing installed base, and shared leads between the two sales organizations have increased by roughly 50% since May.
> **译文：** Idira：控制身份与访问。以250亿美元收购CyberArk，使Palo Alto能够控制人类、机器和AI智能体使用的凭证和权限。早期交叉销售成效令人鼓舞：CyberArk ACV同比增长27%，Palo Alto从其现有客户群中为CyberArk带来了200多个新客户赢单，自5月以来两个销售组织之间的共享线索增加了约50%。
That creates a fairly simple architecture: AIRS secures AI applications and agents, Cortex detects and responds when something goes wrong, and Idira controls what they can access.
> **译文：** 这形成了一个相当简单的架构：AIRS保护AI应用和智能体，Cortex在出现问题时进行检测和响应，Idira控制它们可以访问的内容。
But wait! There’s one more layer: autonomous action. Alongside earnings, Palo Alto announced the acquisition of Console, an AI-native platform that lets users build agentic workflows using natural language. The goal is to move Cortex beyond surfacing security issues toward agents that can investigate and remediate them automatically.
> **译文：** 但等等！还有一层：自主行动。在发布财报的同时，Palo Alto宣布收购Console——一个AI原生平台，允许用户使用自然语言构建智能体工作流。目标是让Cortex超越表面安全问题，转向能够自动调查和修复问题的智能体。
In CEO Nikesh Arora’s words, this is the shift toward “software-as-an-agent.”
> **译文：** 用CEO Nikesh Arora的话说，这是向“软件即智能体”的转变。
Takeaway: Palo Alto is starting to show that its acquisition spree is creating something bigger than the individual parts. Platformization is accelerating, AIRS is scaling rapidly, and CyberArk and Chronosphere are already cross-selling into the broader platform. AI is becoming both a demand catalyst and a real revenue stream. The main challenge ahead is justifying a much richer valuation. The stock has roughly doubled since we last added to our PANW position in App Economy Portfolio in March.
> **译文：** 要点：Palo Alto开始证明其收购狂潮正在创造出比各个部分总和更大的价值。平台化正在加速，AIRS快速扩展，CyberArk和Chronosphere已经在向更广泛的平台交叉销售。AI正在成为需求催化剂和真正的收入来源。未来的主要挑战是证明更高估值的合理性。自我们3月份在App Economy Portfolio中最后一次增持PANW头寸以来，该股已大约翻倍。
2. ❄️ Snowflake: AI Fuels Consumption
Snowflake’s growth acceleration is no longer looking like a one-quarter blip.
> **译文：** Snowflake的增长加速不再像是单一季度的短暂现象。
Product revenue surged 37% Y/Y to $1.49 billion, up from +34% Y/Y last quarter and +30% two quarters ago. Total revenue rose +35% Y/Y to $1.55 billion ($70 million beat), while non-GAAP EPS was $0.62 ($0.17 beat).
> **译文：** 产品收入同比增长37%至14.9亿美元，高于上季度的同比增长34%和两个季度前的30%。总收入同比增长35%至15.5亿美元（超出预期7,000万美元），非GAAP每股收益为0.62美元（超出预期0.17美元）。
Management also raised FY27 product revenue guidance by $230 million to $6.07 billion, lifting expected Y/Y growth to 36%.
> **译文：** 管理层还将2027财年产品收入指引上调2.3亿美元至60.7亿美元，将预期同比增长率提升至36%。
So if we zoom out, the FY27 product revenue growth outlook has moved from 27% to 31% to 36% in the past six months. And there might be more acceleration ahead. Q3 guidance calls for 37%–38% product revenue growth, slightly faster than Q2.
> **译文：** 因此，如果我们拉远来看，2027财年产品收入增长预期在过去六个月中从27%升至31%，再升至36%。未来可能还有更多加速。第三季度指引预计产品收入增长37%–38%，略快于第二季度。
CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy revealed on the call that AI products directly explain roughly half of Snowflake’s recent growth acceleration. But AI’s true impact may be larger because it is also accelerating migrations and increasing consumption across the core data platform.
> **译文：** CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy在电话会议上透露，AI产品直接解释了Snowflake近期增长加速的大约一半。但AI的真正影响可能更大，因为它还在加速核心数据平台上的迁移并增加消费。
The quarter in numbers
Net revenue retention: 126% (+1pp Y/Y).
$1M+ customers: 828 (+27% Y/Y).
Remaining performance obligations: $9.0 billion (+30% Y/Y).
> **译文：** 剩余履约义务：90亿美元（同比增长30%）。
Non-GAAP operating margin: 15% (+4pp Y/Y).
GAAP operating margin: -17% (+13pp Y/Y).
RPO remains the softer metric, dipping sequentially from $9.2 billion to $9.0 billion despite 30% Y/Y growth. Some of that reflects Q2 renewal seasonality and faster burn-down as customers consume ahead of contractual schedules. In a consumption model, actual platform usage and product revenue remain cleaner indicators of business health.
> **译文：** RPO仍然是较软的指标，尽管同比增长30%，但环比从92亿美元降至90亿美元。部分原因反映了第二季度续约的季节性，以及客户在合同时间表之前消费导致更快的消耗。在消费模式下，实际平台使用量和产品收入仍然是业务健康程度更清晰的指标。
The gap between GAAP and adjusted margins remains enormous because Snowflake still relies heavily on stock-based compensation , which accounted for 27% of revenue. Still, that ratio is down 12 percentage points Y/Y. The absolute level remains high, but the direction is clearly improving.
> **译文：** GAAP与调整后利润率之间的差距仍然巨大，因为Snowflake仍然严重依赖股票薪酬，占收入的27%。尽管如此，该比例同比下降了12个百分点。绝对水平仍然较高，但方向明显在改善。
AI fuels the core business
Cortex Code (CoCo) surpassed 9,100 accounts, adding more than 2,000 during the quarter, while CoWork expanded to 5,800 accounts.
> **译文：** Cortex Code（CoCo）超过9,100个账户，本季度新增2,000多个，而CoWork扩展到5,800个账户。
These products enable direct AI consumption, but the bigger opportunity lies in the second-order effect.
> **译文：** 这些产品实现了直接的AI消费，但更大的机会在于二阶效应。
Customers need more governed data available to their AI applications. More users can interact with that data through conversational interfaces. Agents can increasingly retrieve information, analyze it, and take actions. Each additional workload increases Snowflake consumption.
> **译文：** 客户需要更多受治理的数据可供其AI应用使用。更多用户可以通过对话界面与这些数据交互。智能体可以越来越多地检索信息、分析信息并采取行动。每一个额外的工作负载都会增加Snowflake的消费。
AI is also helping Snowflake bring workloads onto the platform faster. Coding models can automate parts of legacy migrations that historically required large teams and months or years of manual rewriting.
> **译文：** AI还在帮助Snowflake更快地将工作负载引入平台。编码模型可以自动化传统迁移的部分工作，而这些迁移历来需要大型团队和数月或数年的手动重写。
So AI creates new workloads, but also makes it easier to move onto Snowflake in the first place. That helps explain why the core business is accelerating alongside the AI portfolio rather than being cannibalized by it.
> **译文：** 因此，人工智能创造了新的工作负载，但也让客户更容易首先迁移到Snowflake。这有助于解释为什么核心业务与AI产品组合同步加速增长，而非被其蚕食。
The customer data supports the same story. Snowflake added 692 net new customers, up 32% Y/Y, indicating strong momentum.
> **译文：** 客户数据也印证了这一说法。Snowflake净新增692家客户，同比增长32%，显示出强劲的增长势头。
A total of 65 customers now generate more than $10 million in trailing annual product revenue, up from 45 a year ago. In a consumption model, that expansion matters. Snowflake needs customers to keep discovering new ways to use their data. AI is giving them just that.
> **译文：** 目前共有65家客户的过去十二个月产品收入超过1000万美元，而一年前这一数字为45家。在按量计费的模式下，这种扩张至关重要。Snowflake需要客户不断发现使用数据的新方式，而AI恰好提供了这样的契机。
The gross margin tradeoff
Snowflake lowered its FY27 non-GAAP product gross-margin outlook from 75% to 74%, partly because AI products carry additional model and inference costs. But operating-margin guidance moved in the opposite direction, rising from 13.5% to 14.5%. This confirms continued operating leverage alongside accelerating top-line growth.
> **译文：** Snowflake将其2027财年非GAAP产品毛利率预期从75%下调至74%，部分原因是AI产品带来了额外的模型和推理成本。但营业利润率指引却反向上升，从13.5%提高至14.5%。这证实了在营收加速增长的同时，运营杠杆仍在持续显现。
In other words, Snowflake is choosing to absorb some near-term AI costs while the broader platform scales.
> **译文：** 换言之，Snowflake选择在更广泛的平台规模化过程中吸收部分短期AI成本。
Takeaway: Snowflake’s AI thesis is moving from defense to offense. This quarter showed that AI can make that governed data layer more valuable. Product growth has accelerated from 30% to 37% in two quarters, and operating margins are expanding at the same time. So far, Snowflake looks increasingly like one of the software companies where AI expands consumption rather than cannibalizing it.
> **译文：** 要点总结：Snowflake的AI叙事正从防守转向进攻。本季度表明，AI能够使受管控的数据层更具价值。产品收入增速在两个季度内从30%加速至37%，同时营业利润率也在扩大。迄今为止，Snowflake越来越像是AI扩大消费而非蚕食消费的软件公司之一。

The Briefing

By Martin Peers

Aug 10, 2026

Thanks for reading The Briefing, our nightly column where we break down the day’s news. If you like what you see, I encourage you to subscribe to our reporting here. Greetings! If at first people don’t pay attention to what you say, say it again. That appears to be Meta Platforms CEO Mark Zuckerberg’s philosophy.
> **译文：**感谢您阅读我们的每日新闻专栏“简报”，我们会在文中为您解读当日新闻。如果您喜欢我们的内容，欢迎在此订阅我们的报道。问候！如果人们一开始不听你说话，那就再说一遍。这似乎是 Meta Platforms 首席执行官马克·扎克伯格的处世哲学。

About two weeks after publishing a roughly 1,000-word opinion piece in The Wall Street Journal headlined “The AI Future Is for Everyone,” Zuckerberg published a 6,500 word version of the same piece on Meta.com on Monday. (Today’s essay carried a nearly-identifical headline, while the first half-dozen paragraphs were almost word for word what ran in the WSJ opinion piece.) Hint to Zuckerberg: Shorter is better.
> **译文：**在《华尔街日报》发表了一篇题为“人工智能的未来属于每个人”的约1000字的评论文章大约两周后，扎克伯格于周一在Meta.com上发表了同一篇文章的6500字版本。（今天的文章标题几乎完全相同，而前六段的内容几乎与《华尔街日报》的评论文章一字不差。）给扎克伯格的建议：言简意赅才是王道。

Zuckerberg’s opus was part of a growing number of lengthy essays, published under the bylines of AI leaders, that discuss AI and society. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is a frequent contributor to the genre, with his most recent publication “Policy on the AI Exponential.” OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has his own blog, supplemented by weighty posts from his company like this one. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella posts on X. For the most part, these essays are long and dry.
> **译文：**扎克伯格的这篇巨著，只是众多以人工智能领袖署名发表的、探讨人工智能与社会关系的长篇论文中的一部分。Anthropologie 首席执行官达里奥·阿莫迪 (Dario Amodei) 经常为这类文章撰稿，他最近的作品是《人工智能指数增长政策》。OpenAI 首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼 (Sam Altman) 拥有自己的博客，并会发表一些来自该公司的重要文章，例如这篇。微软首席执行官萨蒂亚·纳德拉 (Satya Nadella) 则在 X 平台上发表文章。总的来说，这些文章篇幅冗长，内容枯燥。

In other words, they’re perfect for an AI chatbot to summarize. It makes you wonder if they’re meant to prove to consumers the value of AI! Zuckerberg, though, seems to be mostly looking to counter the doomsaying about AI by emphasizing its democratizing potential.
> **译文：**换句话说，它们非常适合人工智能聊天机器人进行总结。这不禁让人思考，它们是否意在向消费者证明人工智能的价值！不过，扎克伯格似乎更想通过强调人工智能的民主化潜力来反驳那些关于人工智能的悲观论调。

He repeated past claims that in the future, “everyone will have an exceptionally capable personal agent that understands you, your goals and everything you care about.” What Zuckerberg hasn’t done is ask whether anyone in their right mind wants that, but we digress. Meta is marketing itself and its own (future) product, which makes sense. Anthropic takes a different tack.
> **译文：**他重申了过去的说法，即未来“每个人都将拥有一个能力超群的私人助理，他了解你、你的目标以及你所关心的一切。”扎克伯格没有问的是，是否有人真的想要这样的私人助理，不过我们暂且不谈这个。Meta公司正在推销自己及其（未来的）产品，这合情合理。而Anthropic公司则采取了不同的策略。

As we reported last week in this in-depth profile of Amodei, he rejected the idea of going on a charm offensive to improve the public perception of AI. Instead, Anthropic recently ran this ad that painted a scary picture of AI. Amodei’s habit of dwelling on AI’s dangers hasn’t been popular with tech CEOs.
> **译文：**正如我们上周在对阿莫迪的深度报道中所述，他拒绝了通过魅力攻势来改善公众对人工智能的看法。相反，Anthropic公司最近投放了一则广告，将人工智能描绘成一幅可怕的景象。阿莫迪总是强调人工智能的危险性，这种做法并不受科技公司CEO们的欢迎。

Zuckerberg, for instance, questioned in today’s missive “why anyone who believes that AI will eliminate most jobs…would rush to build that future.” But it has to be said that, judging from opinion polls like this one, Amodei is having more luck than any other tech CEO in persuading average people of his views on AI. AI’s Financing SupershowFor quite a while, the real AI story has been not so much the technology’s innovations but the financing that aims to deliver all the AI businesses want.
> **译文：**例如，扎克伯格在今天的信中质疑道：“为什么任何相信人工智能会取代大部分工作的人……会急于构建这样的未来？” 但必须承认，从像这样的民意调查来看，阿莫迪在说服普通民众接受他对人工智能的看法方面，比其他任何科技公司首席执行官都更成功。人工智能融资盛宴 很长一段时间以来，人工智能的真正故事与其说是技术创新，不如说是旨在满足人工智能企业所有需求的融资。

That financing effort appears to be pulling in all sectors of the banking industry. Take Monday. Australian asset manager Macquarie announced a venture with Singapore investment firm GIC to establish a data center infrastructure leasing platform for Anthropic.
> **译文：**这项融资计划似乎吸引了银行业的各个领域参与。以周一为例，澳大利亚资产管理公司麦格理宣布与新加坡投资公司GIC合作，为Anthropic公司建立一个数据中心基础设施租赁平台。

Meanwhile, Nvidia announced a “strategic partnership” with a group of Wall Street firms, including Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, KKR and Goldman Sachs, to raise $500 billion for “the build-out of AI infrastructure over time.” (We wrote last week about Apollo’s desire to do more of these deals.) Most of these Wall Street firms already have other AI ventures going with companies such as Meta Platforms and Broadcom. Is there any limit to the deals they can do?
> **译文：**与此同时，英伟达宣布与包括阿波罗、黑石、贝莱德、KKR和高盛在内的一批华尔街公司建立“战略合作伙伴关系”，计划筹集5000亿美元，用于“逐步构建人工智能基础设施”。（我们上周曾报道过阿波罗希望达成更多此类交易。）这些华尔街公司中的大多数已经与Meta Platforms和博通等公司开展了其他人工智能项目。他们能达成的交易数量是否有限制？

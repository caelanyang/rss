Remember two weeks ago when Apple passed Nvidia in market capitalization?

> **译文：** 还记得两周前苹果市值超过英伟达吗？

That’s now ancient history.

At today’s close, Nvidia’s market capitalization was $1 trillion higher than Apple’s.

> **译文：** 截至今日收盘，英伟达的市值比苹果高出1万亿美元。

In the past two weeks, Nvidia stock has risen 18% and Apple’s has fallen 10% or so.

> **译文：** 过去两周，英伟达股价上涨了 18%，而苹果股价下跌了约 10%。

That seems to be mostly due to broad market dynamics, such as the state of the Iran war, but the fact that insatiable demand for Nvidia’s AI chips has sent chip rental prices through the roof may also have something to do with it.

> **译文：** 这似乎主要是由于伊朗战争等大范围市场动态造成的，但英伟达人工智能芯片的巨大需求导致芯片租赁价格飙升，这可能也与此有关。

We got a reminder about the frothiness of demand for AI capacity right now from executives at two small Nvidia-backed cloud firms, CoreWeave and Nebius, speaking on earnings calls in the past two days.

> **译文：** 过去两天，两家由英伟达支持的小型云公司 CoreWeave 和 Nebius 的高管在财报电话会议上发言，再次提醒我们目前对人工智能容量的需求非常旺盛。

Nebius CEO Arkady Volozh revealed today that Nebius had held its first auction of computing capacity during the second quarter, with prices for Nvidia’s Blackwell generation of chips “15% above the highest price we ever charged before.” Volozh also said Nebius was selling more capacity closer to the time customers needed it to take advantage of high prices.

> **译文：** Nebius首席执行官Arkady Volozh今天透露，Nebius在第二季度举行了首次计算能力拍卖，英伟达Blackwell系列芯片的价格“比我们以往的最高售价高出15%”。Volozh还表示，Nebius正在客户临近需求时出售更多计算能力，以充分利用高价优势。

We heard a similar story from CoreWeave, whose CEO, Michael Intrator, told analysts on Tuesday evening that “pricing and margins for our Blackwell and [later-generation] Vera Rubin” chips were “setting new highs, while pricing for prior-generation [chips] is at or above where it was years ago.” CoreWeave raised prices 25% in July “in response to the current demand environment,” said CoreWeave finance chief Nitin Agrawal.

> **译文：** 我们从CoreWeave公司也听到了类似的说法。该公司首席执行官Michael Intrator周二晚间告诉分析师，“我们Blackwell和（下一代）Vera Rubin芯片的定价和利润率均创下新高，而上一代芯片的定价则与几年前持平或更高。” CoreWeave首席财务官Nitin Agrawal表示，CoreWeave在7月份将价格提高了25%，“以应对当前的市场需求”。

CoreWeave is also looking to do short-term deals to take advantage of higher prices.

> **译文：** CoreWeave 也希望达成短期交易，以利用更高的价格。

CoreWeave executives called out this pricing dynamic as bullish for the company’s business model. “We are more confident than ever in the long-term ROI [return on investment] of our product,” Agrawal said.

> **译文：** CoreWeave的高管们认为这种定价策略对公司的商业模式来说是利好消息。“我们比以往任何时候都更加相信我们产品的长期投资回报率，”Agrawal说道。

Wall Street agreed.

CoreWeave rose 19% on Wednesday, while Nebius stock soared 34%.

> **译文：** 周三，CoreWeave股价上涨19%，而Nebius股价飙升34%。

Both companies are bleeding money in their data center expansion, but investors don’t care right now.

> **译文：** 两家公司都在数据中心扩建上投入巨资，但投资者目前并不在意。

As MoffettNathanson analyst Nick Del Deo said in a report on Tuesday, “pricing has captured the market’s attention of late.” The pricing phenomenon may also account for some of SpaceX’s recent recovery—its stock rose 9.7% on Wednesday to $146.15, 35% above where it was last week.

> **译文：** 正如MoffettNathanson分析师Nick Del Deo周二在一份报告中所说，“定价近来引起了市场的广泛关注。”定价现象或许也能解释SpaceX近期股价的部分回升——其股价周三上涨9.7%至146.15美元，较上周上涨35%。

SpaceX led the way on short-term high-priced deals when it rented capacity to Anthropic and Google in the past few months.

> **译文：** 在过去的几个月里，SpaceX 将运力出租给 Anthropic 和 Google，引领了短期高价交易的潮流。

BNP Paribas noted on Wednesday that the pricing Nebius got on short-term deals lately were “roughly equivalent to the economics of SpaceX’s recently signed deals.”Despite Wall Street’s applause, it’s easy to overinterpret the significance of high prices for compute capacity.

> **译文：** 法国巴黎银行周三指出，Nebius近期短期交易的定价“与SpaceX近期签署的交易的经济效益大致相当”。尽管华尔街对此表示赞赏，但人们很容易过度解读计算能力高价的意义。

All that’s happening is that it’s hard to find the capacity you need right now.

> **译文：** 现在的问题是，你很难找到你所需的产能。

Executives at both companies made it clear that prices are higher for short-term deals—as Volozh described it, “for customers with an immediate time-bounded need.” Companies signing deals lasting several years aren’t paying the same premium.

> **译文：** 两家公司的管理人员都明确表示，短期交易的价格更高——正如沃洛日所描述的，“这是为那些有紧急时效性需求的客户准备的”。签订多年期合同的公司则无需支付同样的溢价。

That makes sense: No one would pay a premium for capacity they need in 2029, say, because by then there will likely be more data centers than there are now (even with the political opposition in many states).

> **译文：** 这很有道理：没有人会为 2029 年需要的容量支付溢价，因为到那时数据中心的数量可能会比现在更多（即使在许多州存在政治反对）。

The cloud industry is, after all, spending hundreds of billions to expand capacity.

> **译文：** 毕竟，云计算行业正在投入数千亿美元来扩展容量。

Investors might want to take a slightly longer-term view of CoreWeave and Nebius.

> **译文：** 投资者或许应该对 CoreWeave 和 Nebius 采取稍长一些的看法。

Google Raises Prices

Speaking of high prices, the AI-driven lift in memory chip costs continues to ripple across the smartphone sector.

> **译文：** 说到高价，人工智能驱动的内存芯片成本上涨正在智能手机领域持续产生连锁反应。

Google on Wednesday unveiled its latest generation of Pixel smartphones, with prices $100 above last year’s generation.

> **译文：** 谷歌周三发布了最新一代 Pixel 智能手机，价格比去年的一代高出 100 美元。

The top-tier Pixel 11 Pro, for instance, will cost $1,099, $100 more than the 10 Pro released a year ago.

> **译文：** 例如，顶级 Pixel 11 Pro 的售价为 1099 美元，比一年前发布的 10 Pro 贵 100 美元。

Similarly, the base model, the Pixel 11, will cost $899, up $100 from the Pixel 10.

> **译文：** 同样，入门级机型 Pixel 11 的售价为 899 美元，比 Pixel 10 高出 100 美元。

The foldable Pixel will cost $1,899, up $100 over last year’s foldable phone.

> **译文：** 可折叠 Pixel 手机售价为 1899 美元，比去年的可折叠手机贵 100 美元。

The Pixel is, let‘s face it, a niche device (as fun as it might be to use, as I can attest).

> **译文：** 说实话，Pixel 是一款小众设备（尽管用起来可能很有趣，我可以作证）。

This price rise won’t win the brand more fans, although it's not like smartphone buyers have much of a choice.

> **译文：** 这次涨价并不会为该品牌赢得更多粉丝，尽管智能手机买家似乎也没有太多选择。

Google’s event comes a few weeks before Apple is expected to unveil its latest iPhone lineup.

> **译文：** 谷歌的发布会比苹果预计发布最新款 iPhone 系列早几周举行。

Apple has already raised prices on MacBooks and iPads, and the new iPhones are also expected to cost more.

> **译文：** 苹果公司已经提高了MacBook和iPad的价格，预计新款iPhone的价格也会上涨。

Buying a smartphone is simply getting more expensive.

> **译文：** 购买智能手机的成本越来越高了。

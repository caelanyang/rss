Hello! The anti-data center movement continues to heat up in the United States. Small municipalities are increasingly instituting bans on the power-hungry facilities, and now New York and Texas have become the first states to implement state-wide moratoriums. Both states cited concerns about rising electricity use and grid reliability.
> **译文：**您好！美国的反数据中心运动持续升温。越来越多的小型市政当局开始禁止建设这些耗电大户，而纽约州和德克萨斯州也成为首批实施全州范围暂停建设数据中心的州。这两个州都表示，他们担心电力消耗不断增加以及电网可靠性会受到影响。

Curiously, while it’s true that New York is dealing with grid capacity issues, so far, data centers aren’t the cause. We spoke with the state’s grid operator, who said that aging grid infrastructure and old power plants that had to retire are the real problems. This official also noted that the increase in electricity demand in New York has been driven mostly by manufacturing facilities and electrification of buildings and vehicles. It’s not nearly as inundated with data centers as Texas and Virginia are.
> **译文：**奇怪的是，虽然纽约确实面临电网容量问题，但到目前为止，数据中心并非罪魁祸首。我们采访了该州的电网运营商，他表示，老化的电网基础设施和不得不退役的老旧发电厂才是真正的问题所在。这位官员还指出，纽约电力需求的增长主要来自制造业设施以及建筑物和车辆的电气化。纽约的数据中心数量远不及德克萨斯州和弗吉尼亚州那么多。

What’s the best measure of AI’s great proliferation?

> **译文：** 衡量人工智能大规模普及的最佳指标是什么？

Most obviously, we can sum up the enormous amounts of money generated by OpenAI and Anthropic—a task my colleagues at The Information approach with great vigor. We could also look at how the technology has become a top-of-mind issue in Washington. Or we might consider how AI has become a frequent villain of choice in Hollywood—particularly in horror films. With the technology getting so much screen time, good old Michael Myers must be close to firing his agent.

> **译文：** 最显而易见的是，我们可以总结一下OpenAI和Anthropic创造的巨额财富——我的同事们在《信息报》（The Information）上对此进行了深入研究。我们还可以探讨一下这项技术是如何成为华盛顿关注的焦点。或者，我们也可以思考一下人工智能是如何成为好莱坞电影（尤其是恐怖电影）中常见的反派角色的。鉴于这项技术在银幕上如此频繁地出现，恐怕老牌恐怖片杀手迈克尔·迈尔斯也快要炒掉他的经纪人了。

Another indication may be the fact that AI is at the center of a very fine new off-Broadway play, “Disruption,” headlined by film star John David Washington (“Tenet” and “BlacKkKlansman”): Live theater is marvelous, but it takes something powerfully momentous to push it away from its comfort zone of fairy tales and rhapsodic animals. “Disruption” follows “Data,” another off-Broadway production about an algorithm gone amok, from earlier this year and “Maybe Happy Ending,” a Tony-winning musical about a pair of robots who fall in love, from 2024.

> **译文：** 另一个迹象或许是，人工智能成为了一部非常精彩的外百老汇新剧《颠覆》（Disruption）的核心主题，该剧由影星约翰·大卫·华盛顿（曾出演《信条》和《黑色党徒》）领衔主演：现场戏剧固然精彩，但只有真正震撼人心的作品才能让它跳出童话故事和天马行空的动物形象的舒适圈。《颠覆》是继今年早些时候上演的另一部外百老汇作品《数据》（Data）和2024年即将上演的托尼奖获奖音乐剧《也许幸福结局》（Maybe Happy Ending）之后推出的。《数据》讲述的是一个失控的算法的故事。

No one gets a fully joyful conclusion in “Disruption.” The titular breakthrough comes at the hands of Washington’s tech gazillionaire character, Nick. (And yes, John David really does both sound and look a lot like his father, Denzel.) With a blockbuster IPO behind him, he has hastened onto his next venture—an AI startup—and already has Eric Schmidt on the hook for a $50 million investment. But he wants more and knows what he needs most is tangible proof of his predictive algorithm’s effectiveness in guiding people’s lives—ostensibly toward truest contentment. So Nick allows the AI to begin studying six of his oldest friends unbeknownst to them, of course. Heartbreak and tragedy ensue.

> **译文：** 在《颠覆》这部电影中，没有人能获得圆满的结局。片名中的突破出自华盛顿饰演的科技亿万富翁尼克之手。（没错，约翰·大卫的声音和外形都酷似他的父亲丹泽尔·华盛顿。）凭借一次轰动一时的IPO，他迅速投入到下一个项目——一家人工智能初创公司——并已成功说服埃里克·施密特投资5000万美元。但他并不满足于此，他深知自己最需要的是切实证明其预测算法在引导人们生活（表面上是通往真正的幸福）方面的有效性。于是，尼克在六位挚友不知情的情况下，让人工智能开始研究他们。随之而来的是心碎和悲剧。

“Disruption,” which runs through the beginning of October, originated in a London production in 2023, receiving a raft of warm reviews. Playwright Andrew Stein deserves credit for a fair amount of prescience: He penned an initial draft back in 2018.

> **译文：** 《颠覆》（Disruption）将持续上演至十月初，该剧最初于2023年在伦敦首演，并获得了众多好评。剧作家安德鲁·斯坦的先见之明值得称道：早在2018年，他就创作了该剧的初稿。

“ChatGPT came out for public use between when our production in London was announced and when rehearsal began,” he recalled. “So we hit this moment where this went from being a sci-fi thing to being a zeitgeist thing.”

> **译文：** “ChatGPT是在我们宣布伦敦演出计划到开始排练这段时间向公众开放的，”他回忆道。“所以我们正好赶上了一个转折点，这个项目从科幻题材变成了时代精神的象征。”

In preparing “Disruption” for its London debut, Stein and director Hersh Ellis pored over books written by Ray Kurzweil and Yuval Noah Harari. “I would describe Andrew and myself as ‘AI curious,’” said Ellis. “We’re both people who believe in the power and the potential of what AI can do. And we’re also people who are not naive to what could happen if it goes unchecked.”

> **译文：** 为了筹备《颠覆》在伦敦的首映，斯坦和导演赫什·埃利斯仔细研读了雷·库兹韦尔和尤瓦尔·诺亚·赫拉利的著作。“我会用‘人工智能好奇者’来形容我和安德鲁，”埃利斯说。“我们都相信人工智能的力量和潜力。但我们也清楚，如果人工智能不受控制，将会发生什么。”

Exporting the play to New York, they kept the script mostly unchanged. A larger budget has allowed a significant upgrade to the production values, with seating that looks down at the stage: The fishbowl-like setup provides an intimate observation of the characters, a parallel to the omniscient AI’s own minute inspection of them.

> **译文：** 将该剧移植到纽约后，他们基本保留了剧本原貌。更充足的预算使得制作水平得以大幅提升，观众席呈俯视式设计，如同置身于鱼缸之中，让观众得以近距离观察剧中人物，这与无所不知的人工智能对他们细致入微的观察遥相呼应。

These days Stein relies on AI as a constant resource for research—he and Ellis are at work on a new play about the building of the Panama Canal—but finds it steadily creeping into more of his everyday life. “Sometimes it will say to me something like, ‘Based on what I know about you and your patterns,’ and that’s when I know that, yeah, I’m in deep,” he said.—Abram Brown (abe@theinformation.com)

> **译文：** 如今，斯坦因将人工智能作为研究的重要资源——他和埃利斯正在合作创作一部关于巴拿马运河建设的新剧——但他发现人工智能正逐渐渗透到他日常生活的方方面面。“有时候它会对我说，‘根据我对你和你的行为模式的了解’，这时我就知道，嗯，我陷得太深了。”他说道。——阿布拉姆·布朗 (abe@theinformation.com)

Listening: “The Betrayal of Shohei Ohtani”

In 2024, history repeated itself: A betting scandal erupted in baseball involving arguably the sport’s most prominent name, Shohei Ohtani, the Los Angeles Dodgers’ pitcher-slugger extraordinaire. It resurfaced memories of Pete Rose, the all-time hits leader banned from baseball for gambling on the sport, and the 1919 Chicago Black Sox, who’d been in cahoots with a gambling ring to throw the World Series. What had happened with Ohtani turned out to be a complex affair: Money had indeed flowed from his bank account to a bookie, but that had taken place without his knowledge. His interpreter and aide-de-camp, Ippei Mizuhara, had siphoned off millions of dollars from Ohtani and had been the one placing the bets.

> **译文：** 2024年，历史重演：棒球界爆发了一起赌球丑闻，牵涉到这项运动中最具影响力的人物之一——洛杉矶道奇队的超级投手兼强棒大谷翔平。这让人回想起皮特·罗斯，这位棒球史上安打王因参与赌球而被禁赛；以及1919年的芝加哥黑袜队，他们与赌博集团勾结，操纵世界大赛。大谷翔平的事件最终被证实是一桩复杂的案件：他的银行账户确实有资金流入了赌博公司，但这并非他本人所为。他的翻译兼助手水原一平从大谷翔平那里挪用了数百万美元，并亲自下注。

ESPN owned much of the initial coverage of the Ohtani saga, and reporter Tisha Thompson returns to it in “The Betrayal of Shohei Ohtani,” the latest podcast from the network’s 30 for 30 documentary division.

> **译文：** ESPN 曾对大谷翔平事件进行过大量早期报道，记者 Tisha Thompson 在 ESPN 旗下纪录片部门 30 for 30 的最新播客节目“大谷翔平的背叛”中再次探讨了这一事件。

A slick retelling of the drama, it broadens the tale with journalistic verve: Thompson explains how she and her colleagues followed the money going to the gambling operation and pursued every twist and turn: At one point, Mizuhara told Thompson he had fessed up to Ohtani—within hours, it became clear that Ohtani was still entirely in the dark.

> **译文：** 这部影片以流畅的叙述方式重述了这起事件，并以新闻报道的活力拓展了故事：汤普森解释了她和同事们如何追踪流向赌博活动的资金，并追踪每一个曲折的转折：有一次，水原告诉汤普森他已经向大谷坦白了——几个小时后，很明显大谷仍然完全不知情。

Investigators spoke to Thompson, too. (Cumbersomely, they needed to rely on Google Translate to parse communication between Mizuhara and Ohtani—imagine what Gemini could’ve done for them.) The series also provides an intimate look at the psychology behind addictive gambling, and it left me reflecting on how much more trouble someone like Mizuhara can get themselves into these days, a time when Kalshi’s revenue can double by billions of dollars in the span of a few months.—Abram Brown

> **译文：** 调查人员也与汤普森进行了交谈。（令人费解的是，他们不得不依靠谷歌翻译来解读水原和大谷之间的通信——想想如果当时有Gemini软件，他们该有多方便。）该系列节目还深入剖析了赌博成瘾背后的心理，让我不禁思考，像水原这样的人如今会给自己惹上多大的麻烦，毕竟卡尔希的收入在短短几个月内就能翻数十亿美元。——艾布拉姆·布朗

Reading: “Little Blue Dot” by Katherine Dunn

Earlier this month, I drove from Munich, Germany, several hours into the Austrian alps. There I spent several days hiking, then returned to Munich and trained to Berlin, where I rented a bike and zipped merrily through a city that sprawls across 344 square miles—making it seven times larger than San Francisco and 15 times bigger than Manhattan. I suffered nary a single wrong turn, and I never picked up a physical map.

> **译文：** 本月初，我从德国慕尼黑出发，驱车数小时进入奥地利阿尔卑斯山。在那里，我徒步旅行了几天，然后返回慕尼黑，乘火车前往柏林。抵达柏林后，我租了一辆自行车，兴高采烈地穿梭在这座占地344平方英里（约1050平方公里）的城市中——它的面积是旧金山的七倍，曼哈顿的十五倍。我一路骑行，没有迷路，而且全程都没用过纸质地图。

As I went, a thought echoed in my mind that was promptly reinforced by “Little Blue Dot,” an engaging history of the development and world-altering effects of the GPS system: Traversing strasse and alp would’ve been much more arduous without the little dot on my phone guiding me via Google Maps and AllTrails, a hiking app. As author Katherine Dunn notes: “The physical markers that guided us pre-GPS, even our sensory skills of understanding time and place, have become rusty at best.”

> **译文：** 一路走来，一个念头在我脑海中不断回响，而《小蓝点》这本书则进一步印证了这一点。这本书生动地讲述了GPS系统的发展历程及其对世界产生的深远影响：如果没有手机上那个小蓝点通过谷歌地图和徒步旅行应用AllTrails为我指引方向，穿越山路和高山将会艰难得多。正如作者凯瑟琳·邓恩所说：“在GPS出现之前，那些指引我们方向的实体标记，甚至我们感知时间和地点的感官能力，如今都已生疏不堪。”

“Little Blue Dot” argues that much of the world’s recent past maps pretty well to the rise of that satellite network—particularly in the collision of geopolitical forces and rivalries. GPS was born out of Cold War anxiety and an American desire to develop the most sophisticated weapons it could: A bomb is only as effective as your knowledge of where to drop it.

> **译文：** 《小蓝点》一书认为，世界近代史与卫星网络的兴起有着惊人的相似之处——尤其是在地缘政治力量和竞争的碰撞中。全球定位系统（GPS）诞生于冷战的焦虑以及美国渴望研发最先进武器的愿望：炸弹的效用取决于你对投放位置的精准把握。

During the ’90s, the technology began to find use on the home front, too, and, of course, it underpins a great number of the companies that arose during the mobile-era boom: An Uber does not reach you without it, and neither does that box of dog biscuits. As GPS has reshaped our lives, it has ushered in a time marked by a distinct discordance. The network has, for instance, made it easier to track climate change.

> **译文：** 上世纪90年代，这项技术也开始应用于家庭领域，当然，它也支撑着移动互联网时代蓬勃发展时期涌现的众多公司：没有它，优步就无法送到你家门口，就连那盒狗饼干也一样。随着GPS重塑我们的生活，它也开启了一个充满明显不协调的时代。例如，GPS网络使得追踪气候变化变得更加容易。

It has also made it more possible than ever to travel the planet, exacerbating climate change. “GPS made it easier than ever to kill people, but also to rescue them,” writes Dunn, a former Wall Street Journal reporter and Fortune magazine editor. “To better understand our world and yet continue to destroy it.”—A.B.

> **译文：** 它也使得环游世界变得前所未有的容易，加剧了气候变化。“GPS让杀人变得前所未有的容易，但也让营救变得前所未有的容易，”邓恩写道，他曾是《华尔街日报》的记者和《财富》杂志的编辑。“它让我们更好地了解我们的世界，却又继续破坏它。”——A.B.

Watching: “Furious”

At a time when Hollywood still seems overly devoted to rummaging through old VHS tapes for content to repurpose onto our screens, I worry the suits there will get the wrong idea from Hulu’s “Furious,” loosely based on a 1987 film, “Black Widow,” about a female federal investigator pursuing a female killer: that they really should keep right on trying to reboot anything and everything. I’d encourage them to do so only if they can shatter the known limits of biology and somehow clone “Furious” writer-creator Elizabeth Meriwether, giving all the projects to her duplicates. In less capable hands, “Furious” would be an unfun, trope-laden mess.

> **译文：** 如今好莱坞似乎仍然热衷于翻找旧录像带，寻找素材重新搬上荧幕，我担心好莱坞的高管们会从Hulu的《速度与激情》（Furious）中得到误导。这部剧改编自1987年的电影《黑寡妇》（Black Widow），讲述了一位女联邦探员追捕一名女杀手的故事。我担心他们会因此误以为应该继续重启一切。除非他们能够突破生物学的极限，克隆出《速度与激情》的编剧兼主创伊丽莎白·梅里韦瑟（Elizabeth Meriwether），并将所有项目交给她的复制体，否则我才会鼓励他们这么做。如果落入能力稍逊的人手中，《速度与激情》将会变成一部毫无乐趣、充斥着老套情节的烂片。

“Furious” has some familiar cop show beats. There is a young, damaged FBI rookie, Alice, who seeks out the mentorship of a worn-down older agent, Nora, in a quest to catch a quick-witted killer, Cathy, whose vengeful streak elicits some sympathy.

> **译文：** 《愤怒》有一些熟悉的警匪剧套路。年轻而内心受创的FBI新人艾丽丝，为了抓捕机智过人的杀手凯西，寻求一位疲惫不堪的老探员诺拉的指导。凯西的复仇心切也让艾丽丝产生了一些同情。

Fortunately, Meriwether has a trio of very fine actresses giving life to those very standard character types: Emmy Rossum (“Shameless”), Quincy Tyler Bernstine (a stage actress with a Tony Award nomination for “Doubt: A Parable”) and Lola Petticrew (“Say Nothing”), respectively. The cat-and-mouse game between Alice and Cathy is propulsive and captivating, with Cathy offing a slew of Jeffrey Epstein types whom she previously met as a captive in a child sex-trafficking ring.

> **译文：** 幸运的是，梅里韦瑟找到了三位非常优秀的演员来演绎这些略显老套的角色：艾米·罗森（《无耻之徒》）、昆西·泰勒·伯恩斯坦（曾凭借《怀疑：一个寓言》获得托尼奖提名的舞台剧演员）和洛拉·佩蒂克鲁（《别说出来》）。爱丽丝和凯茜之间的猫鼠游戏紧张刺激，引人入胜，凯茜一路除掉了许多像杰弗里·爱泼斯坦那样的人，这些人她之前曾作为儿童性交易团伙的俘虏与他们相遇。

Alice herself is wrestling with her own past of domestic abuse. Nora stinks of regret and tequila.

> **译文：** 爱丽丝本人也在与自己遭受家庭暴力的过往经历作斗争。诺拉身上散发着悔恨和龙舌兰酒的气味。

It might be a tediously grim affair, but it is not, and the show is well paced, with moments of real humor, a testament to Meriwether’s comedic background: Before “Furious,” she did “New Girl,” the Zooey Deschanel sex-capade. (You may have also seen her 2022 effort: “The Dropout,” Hulu’s Elizabeth Holmes biopic series.) As far as the funnies go, some kudos should be bestowed directly on Tyler Bernstine: She gives tremendous weathered, withering side-eye.—A.B.

> **译文：** 这部剧或许会让人觉得沉闷乏味，但事实并非如此，它的节奏把握得很好，不乏真正的幽默时刻，这足以证明梅里韦瑟的喜剧功底：在出演《速度与激情》之前，她曾参演过佐伊·丹斯切尔主演的性喜剧《杰茜驾到》（New Girl）。（你可能也看过她2022年的作品：Hulu出品的伊丽莎白·霍姆斯传记剧集《辍学生》（The Dropout）。）说到笑点，泰勒·伯恩斯坦的表演绝对值得称赞：她那饱经风霜、犀利无比的眼神令人印象深刻。——A.B.

Macro

U.S. economic activity remains resilient

“The market and U.S. economy continue to display a remarkable degree of resilience in the face of the war in the Middle East, significant pressure on energy markets, stubborn inflation and increasing public market volatility related to questions around AI. When we look at our proprietary data, largely KPIs rolled up each month across our nearly 300 portfolio companies, we see a U.S. economy that continues to expand at an annual rate of 2% to 2.5% in real terms with 6% annual growth in corporate revenues.” - The Carlyle Group ($CG ) CEO Harvey Schwartz
> **译文：**“面对中东战乱、能源市场巨大压力、持续高企的通胀以及人工智能相关问题引发的公开市场波动加剧，市场和美国经济依然展现出惊人的韧性。我们分析了我们专有的数据，这些数据主要基于我们近300家投资组合公司每月汇总的关键绩效指标（KPI），数据显示，美国经济实际年增长率持续保持在2%至2.5%之间，企业收入年增长率达到6%。”——凯雷集团（$CG）首席执行官哈维·施瓦茨

Consumer spending is still stable

“In the month of July, we just got the final sort of payments report. The consumers of Bank of America put 5% more in the economy, and what you’ve been seeing in June and July is that the if you do a third, a third, third, high income, medium income, low income, the lower income people’s wages grow a little faster. And they’re spending a little bit more, and so you’re starting to see more convergence in the spending patterns.” - Bank of America ($BAC ) CEO Brian Moynihan
> **译文：**“7月份，我们刚刚拿到了最终的支付报告。美国银行的客户为经济注入了5%的资金。而6月和7月的情况表明，如果按收入水平（高收入、中等收入、低收入）划分，低收入人群的工资增长速度略快，他们的支出也略高，因此，消费模式开始趋于一致。”——美国银行（$BAC）首席执行官布莱恩·莫伊尼汉

“...we saw stable underlying transaction, volume and account trends across the business, driving recurring revenue growth of 2% in the quarter with recurring revenue representing 84% of our total adjusted revenue.” - Fiserv ($FISV ) CFO Paul Todd
> **译文：**“……我们看到公司整体交易量、交易额和账户数量等基本面趋势保持稳定，推动本季度经常性收入增长2%，经常性收入占调整后总收入的84%。”——Fiserv（股票代码：FISV）首席财务官保罗·托德

“While consumers remain under pressure and month-to-month consumption patterns are choppy, demand across our categories has remained relatively stable” - Kimberly-Clark ($KMB ) COO Russell Torres
> **译文：**“尽管消费者仍面临压力，且月度消费模式波动不定，但我们各品类的需求保持相对稳定。”——金佰利公司（$KMB）首席运营官罗素·托雷斯

The labor market is stable but subdued

“Looking to the second half of 2026, the labor market remains stable even as overall hires and quits rate remain near their lowest level since 2015” - ZipRecruiter ($ZIP ) President Dave Travers
> **译文：**ZipRecruiter（股票代码：$ZIP）总裁戴夫·特拉弗斯表示：“展望2026年下半年，尽管整体招聘率和离职率仍接近2015年以来的最低水平，但劳动力市场依然保持稳定。”

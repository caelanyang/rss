# Review | PLTR 2Q26: Growth and Forward Visibility Both Move Higher as the AI Sovereignty Narrative Gains Traction

PLTR reported 2Q26 revenue of $1.94bn, up 93% YoY and 19% QoQ. YoY growth accelerated for the 12th consecutive quarter, and results came in ahead of expectations.

> **译文：** PLTR公布2026年第二季度营收为19.4亿美元，同比增长93%，环比增长19%。这是该公司连续第12个季度实现同比增长，业绩超出预期。

U.S. revenue grew 115%, while U.S. Commercial revenue grew 149%, accelerating further from Q1. This was the largest upside surprise in the quarter. Ahead of earnings, several sell-side channel checks had suggested that U.S. Commercial was underperforming, raising concerns about decelerating growth and the sustainability of the company’s premium valuation. The actual results provided a strong rebuttal to that negative pre-earnings narrative.

> **译文：** 美国业务营收增长115%，其中美国商业业务营收增长149%，较第一季度增速进一步加快。这是本季度最大的业绩惊喜。财报发布前，多家卖方渠道的调查显示，美国商业业务表现不佳，引发了人们对增长放缓以及公司高估值可持续性的担忧。而实际业绩有力地驳斥了这些负面的财报前预测。

Guidance: Management raised FY26 revenue guidance from $7.662bn to $8.158bn, an increase of $496mn. Q2 revenue beat the high end of prior-quarter guidance by $134mn, implying that approximately $362mn of the full-year guidance increase reflects a further upward revision to H2 expectations rather than a simple roll-forward of the Q2 beat.

> **译文：** 业绩指引：管理层将2026财年营收指引从76.62亿美元上调至81.58亿美元，增加4.96亿美元。第二季度营收超出上一季度指引上限1.34亿美元，这意味着全年业绩指引上调约3.62亿美元，反映的是下半年预期的进一步上调，而非简单地将第二季度业绩超预期延续至下半年。

Management’s overall commentary was more constructive than in Q1. Although Q1 results were strong, management emphasized capacity constraints, a national-security-first approach to resource allocation, and continuing-resolution risk for the government business. These comments left investors concerned about whether growth could be sustained and managed. Q2 results indicate that, at least in the near term, these constraints did not prevent simultaneous acceleration in both commercial and government, materially improving forward visibility.

> **译文：** 管理层的整体评论比第一季度更具建设性。尽管第一季度业绩强劲，但管理层强调了产能限制、优先考虑国家安全的资源分配方式以及政府业务持续存在的处置风险。这些评论令投资者担忧增长能否持续并得到有效管理。第二季度业绩表明，至少在短期内，这些限制并未阻止商业和政府业务的同步加速增长，显著提升了未来的发展前景。

Several of the conclusions in our Preview were validated by the quarter, while the actual growth mix was better than we had expected. The Preview estimated Q2 total revenue of approximately $1.900bn versus the reported $1.935bn, and government revenue of approximately $1.030bn versus the reported $990mn. The government shortfall was more than offset by commercial, particularly U.S. Commercial, which grew 149% YoY. As in the prior quarter, we suspect the company may have some flexibility in managing the timing of revenue recognition across government and commercial projects. The 149% result was well above our base case, which assumed Q1’s reported 133% growth rate would hold. Accordingly, the quarter’s beat was not simply driven by government backlog conversion; commercial momentum was stronger than expected.

> **译文：** 本季度业绩验证了我们此前预测中的几项结论，而实际增长结构也优于预期。预测显示，第二季度总营收约为19000亿美元，而实际公布的营收为19.35亿美元；政府业务营收约为10.3亿美元，而实际公布的营收为9.9亿美元。政府业务的下滑被商业业务的增长所弥补，尤其是美国商业业务，其同比增长高达149%。与上一季度一样，我们认为公司在政府和商业项目的收入确认时间方面可能具有一定的灵活性。149%的增长率远高于我们此前的基准预测，即假设第一季度133%的增长率能够保持。因此，本季度业绩超预期并非仅仅得益于政府积压订单的转化；商业业务的增长势头也强于预期。

Narrative: The setup has improved somewhat. PLTR now trades at a meaningful discount to DDOG, SNOW, and CRWD on an EV/EBIT basis, while the company’s AI sovereignty narrative is gaining traction. The overall narrative backdrop is therefore becoming more constructive.

> **译文：** 叙述：现状有所改善。PLTR目前的EV/EBIT估值较DDOG、SNOW和CRWD有显著折价，同时该公司关于人工智能主权的论述也逐渐获得认可。因此，整体而言，市场环境正变得更加积极。

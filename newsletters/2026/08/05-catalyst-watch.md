1. The Yield Curve May Be Changing

Warsh wants to let capital markets react to economic data rather than focus on the Fed’s policy. He calls this having the markets “play the ball, not the referee.” Here are his thoughts:

> **译文：** 沃什希望资本市场对经济数据做出反应，而不是关注美联储的政策。他称之为让市场“自己做决定，而不是让裁判做决定”。以下是他的观点：

“Market participants are learning to play the ball, not the referee—and market prices will continue to respond in the direction and magnitude they see fit. This is, in my view, a change for the better—and we are just getting started...What I’ve really been trying to do...is getting an unfiltered message from markets. Getting a direct message. Letting buyers and sellers meet at prices for Treasuries, for the foreign exchange value of the dollar, and then trying to judge for ourselves, what does that mean about our remit? How are we doing on inflation? How are we doing on employment? We’re trying not to interfere with that market signal.” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/d3bc74a9-91b0-4940-91d1-62da0b5dcc7d?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “市场参与者正在学习如何顺势而为，而不是被动等待裁判——市场价格将继续按照他们认为合适的方向和幅度做出反应。在我看来，这是一个好转的趋势——而我们才刚刚起步……我一直努力做的……是获取来自市场的真实信息。获取直接的信息。让买卖双方在国债价格和美元汇率上达成交易，然后我们再去判断，这对我们的职责意味着什么？我们在通胀方面做得如何？我们在就业方面做得如何？我们努力不去干预市场信号。” - 美联储[https://substack.com/redirect/d3bc74a9-91b0-4940-91d1-62da0b5dcc7d?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro]主席凯文·沃什

At face value, Warsh wants to be able to interpret market signals in order to help carry out policy more effectively. Still, there is a fine line between interpreting signals and letting markets determine the course of interest rates.

> **译文：** 表面上看，沃什希望能够解读市场信号，从而更有效地执行政策。然而，解读信号和任由市场决定利率走势之间，界限模糊不清。

Our interpretation of Warsh’s approach is that he will be happy to allow the long end of the yield curve to move more freely so that he can use that signal to determine short-term rate policy.

> **译文：** 我们对沃什做法的理解是，他乐于让收益率曲线的长期部分更自由地移动，以便他可以利用该信号来决定短期利率政策。

If accurate, this would be an inversion of the prevailing monetary policy mechanism of the last several decades in which Fed control of short-term rates helped to pin interest rates across the yield curve.

> **译文：** 如果属实，这将颠覆过去几十年盛行的货币政策机制，在过去，美联储通过控制短期利率来帮助稳定收益率曲线上的利率。

If the Fed abdicates control of this mechanism, it means that the power of the Fed itself would recede. The month-to-month outlook for the Fed Funds rate would become much less relevant than the evolving shape of the yield curve. This would likely have a structural impact on capital markets and the broader economy.

> **译文：** 如果美联储放弃对这一机制的控制，意味着美联储自身的权力将会减弱。联邦基金利率的月度前景将远不如收益率曲线的演变重要。这可能会对资本市场和整体经济产生结构性影响。

Macro

The US economy is a standout

“The United States was a standout, not just because of the World Cup, but actually because of the underlying fundamentals of the economy. Your callers earlier were talking about strong investment in infrastructure and AI and data centers, that’s hundreds of billions of dollars, but it’s really broader than that. It’s manufacturing, it’s energy, it’s pharma. It’s all the reshoring that’s coming into the country. So we think it’s pretty broad-based. You still have pretty high employment levels, wage growth, and GDP growing at 2%. Consumers are still spending, especially on experiences, and that’s the industry we’re really in. So we feel good about the United States continuing to grow.” - InterContinental Hotels Group  ($IHG ) CEO Elie Maalouf

> **译文：** “美国表现突出，不仅仅是因为世界杯，更是因为它的经济基本面。之前的听众提到，美国在基础设施、人工智能和数据中心领域投入巨资，总额达数千亿美元，但实际上，美国经济的贡献远不止于此。它还涵盖制造业、能源、制药等多个领域，以及大量产业回流到美国本土。因此，我们认为美国经济的贡献基础非常广泛。美国的就业率依然很高，工资增长强劲，GDP增速也保持在2%左右。消费者仍在消费，尤其是在体验式消费方面，而这正是我们所处的行业。所以我们对美国经济的持续增长充满信心。”——洲际酒店集团（$IHG）首席执行官埃利·马卢夫

Consumer spending is resilient

“Certainly, the last couple of years, payments, while being strong and reflecting the resiliency of the U.S. consumer spending and some really attractive new sources of revenue, has been a little shy of the historical growth algorithm.” - Jack Henry & Associates  ($JKHY ) CFO Mimi Carsley

> **译文：** “当然，过去几年，尽管支付业务表现强劲，反映了美国消费者支出的韧性以及一些极具吸引力的新收入来源，但其增速略低于历史平均水平。”——杰克·亨利及合伙人公司（Jack Henry & Associates，股票代码：JKHY）首席财务官米米·卡斯利

Bifurcation is real

“...that bifurcating consumer is real. So we still see very strong design sales. We still see large tickets. We see folks willing to invest in whole room solutions and upgrades and leathers and power and are still spending, and we’re seeing that channel grow. At the same time, we’ve been transparent around that; we’ve worked to sharpen some opening price points, again, not deep discount relative to some in our industry, but to enable people that it’s aspirational to get into the La-Z-Boy quality to give them that opportunity and some pared-down offerings. So we continue to work on both ends of that spectrum of that bifurcated consumer, and we’ll continue to stay close to that.” - La-Z-Boy  ($LZB ) CEO Melinda Whittington

> **译文：** “……消费者群体分化是真实存在的。因此，我们仍然看到非常强劲的设计类产品销售，高价产品依然畅销。我们看到消费者愿意投资整套房间解决方案、升级改造、皮革材质和电源配置，并且仍在消费，我们也看到这一渠道正在增长。与此同时，我们一直保持透明；我们努力优化一些入门级产品的价格，当然，与业内某些品牌相比，折扣幅度并不大，而是为了让那些渴望拥有 La-Z-Boy 品质的消费者有机会实现这一目标，并提供一些精简版产品。因此，我们将继续努力满足消费者群体分化带来的两端需求，并保持密切关注。”——La-Z-Boy（股票代码：LZB）首席执行官 Melinda Whittington

“...the K-shaped economy persists, though we did see some sequential improvement during the quarter. We drove comp growth across all income cohorts, which is encouraging, and our value proposition continues to resonate broadly. That said, the vast majority of our growth continues to be driven by our higher income members, which is consistent with what we’ve seen for some time now.” - BJ’s Wholesale Club  ($BJ ) CEO Bob Eddy

> **译文：** “尽管本季度环比有所改善，但K型经济格局依然存在。我们实现了所有收入群体的同店销售增长，这令人鼓舞，而且我们的价值主张也继续获得广泛认可。即便如此，我们绝大部分的增长仍然来自高收入会员，这与我们一段时间以来的观察结果一致。”——BJ’s Wholesale Club（$BJ）首席执行官鲍勃·埃迪

Walmart is seeing relatively low inflation

“Bob, generally, we’ve seen a pretty low inflationary environment throughout the year. We’re between 1% and 2% in total. And the rollbacks, we think, can help over this quarter and the last few months. So generally not any big concerns right now on inflation. Fuel costs are probably the one thing that, of course, we’re watching because of the magnitude of it. And hopefully, those can come down over time.” - Walmart  ($WMT ) CEO John Furner

> **译文：** “鲍勃，总的来说，我们今年的通胀环境一直很低。总通胀率在1%到2%之间。我们认为，这些限制措施在本季度和过去几个月会有所帮助。所以目前通胀方面总体上没有什么大问题。当然，燃油成本可能是我们唯一关注的因素，因为它的涨幅巨大。希望燃油成本能够随着时间的推移而下降。”——沃尔玛（$WMT）首席执行官约翰·弗纳

Treasury is intervening in Treasury markets

“...we’re trying to signal that we think that this is a thinly traded area of the market, that we’re in August, and there’s been a lot of corporate issuance that’s influenced the market. And we believe that there are many underlying factors in turn that the market is not looking at, and we are going to make a market in these. We routinely do buybacks, and we’re going to increase the size of the buyback. And, you know, Sara, I would note that it could be more than the $4 billion per issue.” - U.S. Treasury  Secretary Scott Bessent

> **译文：** “……我们想表明，我们认为这是市场交易量较低的领域。现在是八月份，大量的企业债券发行影响了市场。我们相信，市场并未关注到许多潜在因素，而我们将着手解决这些问题。我们通常会进行股票回购，而且我们将扩大回购规模。萨拉，我想指出的是，每次发行的规模可能会超过40亿美元。”——美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特

# Deep|LLM: RSI Is the Most Important Variable, and Compute Is the Deepest Moat

July 2026 produced the sharpest drawdown of this cycle for the AI trade. Semiconductors posted their worst month since 2002, and more than $1 trillion of AI-linked market value came off. The concern behind the move is straightforward: coding looks like a special case that is hard to replicate, enterprise adoption is running behind expectations, and the “next coding” that would justify hundreds of billions of dollars of capex has not yet appeared.

> **译文：**2026年7月，人工智能交易市场经历了本轮周期中最剧烈的下跌。半导体行业经历了自2002年以来最糟糕的一个月，超过1万亿美元的人工智能相关市场市值蒸发。这一跌幅背后的担忧很简单：编码似乎是一个难以复制的特殊案例，企业采用速度低于预期，而能够支撑数千亿美元资本支出的“下一代编码”尚未出现。

We think the debate is framed around the wrong question. The market is evaluating AI through an application lens, looking for the next killer use case and checking whether current revenue covers capital expenditure. The industry’s own objective, however, is AGI, and the mechanism most labs point to on that path is recursive self-improvement (RSI). A more useful way to think about it is as a sequence of capability milestones, each with its own commercial payoff. Coding capability has already been monetized, in the form of this year’s first-half revenue inflection. The next milestone is continual learning, which lets models accumulate knowledge in deployment the way an employee does; combined with deployment mechanisms such as forward-deployed engineering, it will open up most of the enterprise opportunity. Full RSI, in which models participate in and accelerate their own development, sits furthest out and will determine the competitive structure that follows. Progress is visible at each stage, and in several places it is accelerating.

> **译文：**我们认为这场辩论围绕着一个错误的问题展开。市场目前正从应用角度评估人工智能，寻找下一个杀手级应用案例，并检验当前收入是否足以覆盖资本支出。然而，行业自身的最终目标是通用人工智能（AGI），而大多数实验室认为实现这一目标的关键机制是递归式自我改进（RSI）。更有效的理解方式是将其视为一系列能力里程碑，每个里程碑都对应着相应的商业回报。编码能力已经实现货币化，体现在今年上半年收入的增长中。下一个里程碑是持续学习，它使模型能够在部署过程中像员工一样积累知识；结合前沿部署工程等部署机制，这将开启企业的大部分机遇。完全的RSI，即模型参与并加速自身发展，是目前最遥远的阶段，并将决定未来的竞争格局。每个阶段都取得了显著进展，并且在某些方面正在加速发展。

First, the “context problem” the market worries about is a capability question rather than a use-case question, and it is what continual learning is designed to address. Once models can accumulate tacit organizational knowledge the way employees do, the search for a single “next coding-like” use case dissolves: enterprise use cases open up progressively. The TAM reference then shifts from a software budget measured in hundreds of billions to a labor market measured in tens of trillions.

> **译文：**首先，市场所担忧的“情境问题”本质上是一个能力问题，而非用例问题，而持续学习正是为了解决这个问题。一旦模型能够像员工一样积累隐性组织知识，对单一“类似编码的下一个用例”的追寻便不复存在：企业用例将逐步涌现。届时，TAM（技术市场规模）的衡量标准也将从数千亿美元的软件预算转向数十万亿美元的劳动力市场。

Second, early signals of RSI are already observable. Model iteration has moved from annual to monthly. GPT-5.5 took roughly one month from the end of pre-training to release. OpenAI cut prices by up to 80% three weeks after GPT-5.6 shipped, against a history in which price cuts followed months of inference optimization. Lab code is now written almost entirely by models, and AI’s measured contribution to internal R&D velocity rose from 5% to 15–20% over six months.

> **译文：**其次，RSI 的早期信号已经显现。模型迭代周期已从每年缩短至每月。GPT-5.5 从预训练结束到正式发布大约只用了一个月时间。OpenAI 在 GPT-5.6 发布三周后就将价格下调了高达 80%，这与以往的价格下调通常需要数月推理优化之后的情况截然不同。实验室代码现在几乎完全由模型编写，人工智能对内部研发速度的贡献在六个月内从 5% 提升至 15-20%。

Third, if RSI crosses its threshold, catch-up strategies built on distilling frontier model outputs are likely to become progressively harder, and compute becomes the least compressible constraint and the deepest competitive moat. When the frontier iterates monthly, a follower may still hold a fixed time lag, but it cannot readily replicate a frontier lab’s full learning, data, and experimentation stack. Native self-improvement capability requires proprietary experimental infrastructure and the compute to run it. This is also the view inside China’s leading labs: China has not hit a scaling wall, but does not yet have enough compute to reach one.

> **译文：**第三，如果RSI突破阈值，基于提炼前沿模型输出的追赶策略可能会变得越来越困难，计算能力将成为最难克服的制约因素和最深的竞争壁垒。当前沿技术每月迭代更新时，追随者或许仍能保持一定的时滞，但却无法轻易复制前沿实验室完整的学习、数据和实验体系。原生自改进能力需要专有的实验基础设施和运行所需的计算能力。这也是中国领先实验室内部的观点：中国尚未遇到规模瓶颈，但计算能力还不足以达到瓶颈。

Capex, on this reading, should not be dismissed as a bubble. The latest earnings season shows real current returns from AI-driven cloud growth, and beyond that, capex is what ensures a player has sufficient compute when the RSI threshold arrives. Strategic necessity is not the same thing as shareholder return, and we treat that distinction explicitly in a scenario framework. The variable that matters most is less “what is the next application” than when RSI arrives and who gets there first.

> **译文：**从这个角度来看，资本支出不应被视为泡沫。最新的财报季显示，人工智能驱动的云计算增长带来了实际的当前回报，而且，资本支出还能确保企业在达到相对成功指数（RSI）阈值时拥有足够的计算能力。战略需求与股东回报并非同一概念，我们在情景分析框架中明确区分了这一点。最重要的变量与其说是“下一个应用是什么”，不如说是RSI何时到来以及谁能率先实现这一目标。

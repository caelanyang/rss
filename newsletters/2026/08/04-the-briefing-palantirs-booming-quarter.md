Palantir is truly the Nvidia of AI software.

On Monday, the company delivered another rip-roaring quarterly result, showing revenue up a blistering 93%, well ahead of what it had projected.

> **译文：** 周一，该公司公布了又一份亮眼的季度业绩报告，收入飙升 93%，远超预期。

The company has accelerated revenue growth every quarter for three years, from a 13% rate of expansion in the second quarter of 2023.

> **译文：** 该公司连续三年每个季度加速实现营收增长，2023 年第二季度增速达到 13%。

In that time, Palantir’s top line has increased from $533 million a quarter to $1.9 billion.

> **译文：** 在此期间，Palantir 的季度营收从 5.33 亿美元增长到 19 亿美元。

Palantir stock rose 11% in after-hours trading.

Its growth over the past three years isn’t quite as dazzling as that of Nvidia—whose stranglehold on the AI chip market lifted revenue to $216 billion in its most recent fiscal year from $27 billion three years earlier—but it’s pretty stunning nevertheless.

> **译文：** 虽然它过去三年的增长不如英伟达那样耀眼——英伟达凭借其在人工智能芯片市场的绝对优势，使其最近一个财年的收入从三年前的 270 亿美元增长到 2160 亿美元——但它的增长仍然相当惊人。

What makes this particularly notable is that Palantir doesn’t develop its own large language models.

> **译文：** 尤其值得注意的是，Palantir 并没有开发自己的大型语言模型。

It has figured out how to make a massive amount of money—Palantir’s operations generated $2.1 billion in cash in the first half of 2026, while capital expenditures were only $22 million—from software that works on top of other companies’ AI.

> **译文：** Palantir 已经找到了赚取巨额利润的方法——2026 年上半年，Palantir 的运营产生了 21 亿美元的现金，而资本支出仅为 2200 万美元——这得益于其基于其他公司人工智能的软件。

That’s something every other software company is aiming to do.

> **译文：** 这是其他所有软件公司都努力想要实现的目标。

Palantir is far ahead of the rest.

As my colleague Laura Bratton described, Palantir offers “end-to-end software, helping customers link and organize data they store in systems such as Snowflake, Salesforce and SAP.” It develops AI agents and other apps to utilize that data, and its consultants help clients make AI work for them.

> **译文：** 正如我的同事劳拉·布拉顿所描述的那样，Palantir 提供“端到端软件，帮助客户链接和组织存储在 Snowflake、Salesforce 和 SAP 等系统中的数据”。它开发人工智能代理和其他应用程序来利用这些数据，其顾问帮助客户让人工智能为他们服务。

It likely helps that co-founder and CEO Alexander Karp is an iconoclastic showman whose proselytizing about the dangers posed by AI firms such as Anthropic and OpenAI doubles as marketing for Palantir’s services.

> **译文：** 联合创始人兼首席执行官 Alexander Karp 是一位特立独行的表演家，他大力宣扬 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 等人工智能公司带来的危险，这或许对 Palantir 的成功有所帮助。而他这种宣传方式同时也为 Palantir 的服务做了营销。

Repeating a message he has lately sounded loudly, including on CNBC a month ago, he asserted in a letter accompanying earnings that “every organization in the world is awakening to the risks of handing the creators of the language models the keys to their institutions,” referring to the organizations’ data, which he says AI models aim to capture.

> **译文：** 他最近多次高调宣扬这一观点，包括一个月前在 CNBC 上发表的言论。他在随附收益报告的一封信中断言，“世界上每个组织都开始意识到将机构的控制权交给语言模型的创建者所带来的风险”，他指的是这些组织的数据，而人工智能模型的目标是获取这些数据。

His message seems hyperbolic, but no matter—it’s helping Palantir thrive.

> **译文：** 他的说法似乎有些夸张，但这无关紧要——它正在帮助 Palantir 蓬勃发展。

Snap Has a Good DayIs Snap bouncing back?

Maybe.

The social media service popular with teens reported 19% higher revenues for the second quarter, sending the long-battered stock up 10% in after-hours trading.

> **译文：** 深受青少年欢迎的社交媒体服务公司公布第二季度营收增长 19%，推动长期遭受重创的股价在盘后交易中上涨 10%。

But let’s not get too excited.

Most of the growth came from Snap’s subscriptions and other revenue, which rocketed 85% to $316 million, or about 20% of the total.

> **译文：** 大部分增长来自 Snap 的订阅和其他收入，这些收入飙升 85% 至 3.16 亿美元，约占总收入的 20%。

Advertising showed signs of life—it rose 9%, which is better than the 2.7% reported in the first quarter.

> **译文：** 广告业出现复苏迹象——增长了 9%，好于第一季度报告的 2.7%。

But it’s well below what other digital media firms are reporting.

> **译文：** 但远低于其他数字媒体公司报告的水平。

The bigger worry is that Snap’s engine, its user base, is still not growing where it matters most—the U.S. and Europe.

> **译文：** 更令人担忧的是，Snap 的核心用户群在最重要的地区——美国和欧洲——仍然没有增长。

The company’s North American daily active users stayed flat compared with the first quarter at 92 million (and that user count is 7% below where it was 12 months earlier).

> **译文：** 该公司北美地区的日活跃用户数与第一季度持平，为 9200 万（该用户数比 12 个月前下降了 7%）。

The European DAU count picked up to 98 million, compared with 97 million in the first quarter, but that’s still down 2% from a year ago.

> **译文：** 欧洲日活跃用户数增至 9800 万，高于第一季度的 9700 万，但仍比去年同期下降了 2%。

Snap’s user growth is coming mostly from other parts of the world, which are much smaller sources of revenue.

> **译文：** Snap 的用户增长主要来自世界其他地区，而这些地区的收入来源要小得多。

Until Snap can revive user growth in the U.S. in particular, its future will be clouded.

> **译文：** 除非 Snap 能够重振其在美国的用户增长，否则它的未来将充满不确定性。

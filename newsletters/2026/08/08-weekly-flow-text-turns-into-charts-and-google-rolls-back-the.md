** Recap in data visualization for the week

> **译文：****本周数据可视化回顾

Here's what we saw: a timeline of the future, a traveling lawnmower, and the most dangerous vehicles.

> **译文：**我们看到了：未来时间线、移动式割草机和最危险的车辆。

Datatype font turns text into charts — Enter text and get some charts using the Datatype font   . There’s a bar chart, sparkline, and pie chart. This could be useful if you want to stick a small chart inline with a body of text or have a table needs a visual supplement.

> **译文：**Datatype 字体可以将文本转换为图表——输入文本即可使用 Datatype 字体生成图表。它提供条形图、迷你图和饼图。如果您想在正文中插入小型图表，或者需要为表格添加视觉补充，这将非常有用。

Google rolls back its fake satellite image machine — Last week, Google released a feature for Google Earth that let anyone use their Nano Banana image generation on satellite images. What could go wrong? Google pressed pause on the feature a day later   .

> **译文：**谷歌撤回其伪造卫星图像生成器——上周，谷歌为谷歌地球发布了一项新功能，允许任何人使用其Nano Banana图像生成器对卫星图像进行处理。这能出什么问题呢？一天后，谷歌就暂停了这项功能。

Timeline of the future — As anyone who has watched a time travel movie or is Natasha Bedingfield can tell you, the future is still unwritten, but prediction market Manifold tried anyway. A timeline layout shows predictions    a couple centuries into the future.

> **译文：**未来时间线——看过穿越时空电影或者自己就是娜塔莎·贝丁菲尔德的人都知道，未来尚未可知，但预测市场 Manifold 还是尝试了一下。时间线图展示了几个世纪后的未来预测。

Lawn mowing game as traveling salesman problem in disguise — The Pudding made a game that challenged players to figure out the best way to mow a lawn. There is grass, there are obstacles, and you move the mower to cut all the grass. Then they analyzed player patterns in the context of the traveling salesman problem   .

> **译文：**一款看似简单的割草游戏实则暗藏旅行商问题——The Pudding 开发了一款游戏，挑战玩家找出最佳的割草方法。游戏中有草、有障碍物，玩家需要操控割草机将所有草割掉。之后，他们运用旅行商问题分析了玩家的游戏模式。

Vehicles with the highest and lowest death rates — The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety has been estimating death rates by vehicle since 1989. They published their most recent estimates    for the period from 2021 through 2024.

> **译文：**死亡率最高和最低的车辆——自 1989 年以来，公路安全保险协会一直在估算各种车辆的死亡率。他们发布了 2021 年至 2024 年的最新估算数据。

The model with the highest driver death rate was the Kia Rio, with 170 deaths per million registration years. The Ram 3500 crew cab long bed four-wheel-drive had the highest other-driver death rate, at 204 deaths per million registration years.

> **译文：**驾驶员死亡率最高的车型是起亚Rio，每百万注册年死亡人数为170人。而Ram 3500四驱长轴距双排座皮卡的其他驾驶员死亡率最高，每百万注册年死亡人数为204人。

Eighteen out of the 20 models with the lowest driver death rates are SUVs, including four small, nine midsize, four large and one very large model. Six models had death rates of 0.

> **译文：**在驾驶员死亡率最低的20款车型中，有18款是SUV，其中包括4款小型SUV、9款中型SUV、4款大型SUV和1款超大型SUV。其中6款车型的死亡率为0。

The Chevrolet Camaro, Chevrolet Malibu and the two-wheel-drive versions of the Dodge Charger and Dodge Charger HEMI feature among both the models with the highest driver death rates and those with the highest other-driver death rates.

> **译文：**雪佛兰 Camaro、雪佛兰 Malibu 以及道奇 Charger 和道奇 Charger HEMI 的两驱版本，既位列驾驶员死亡率最高的车型之列，也位列其他驾驶员死亡率最高的车型之列。

There are many confounding factors, such as driving behavior of less experienced drivers or of people who drive a Camaro or Charger. However, I’d probably still avoid a mini car like the Kia Rio.

> **译文：**有很多混杂因素，例如经验不足的驾驶员或驾驶雪佛兰科迈罗或道奇Charger等车型的人的驾驶行为。不过，我可能还是会避免选择像起亚Rio这样的小型车。

Also, watch out for those large pickup trucks, which have the highest death rate for the people on the other side of the crash. Anecdotally, this makes a lot of sense to me. The large pickups on the highway drive me crazy.

> **译文：**另外，还要小心那些大型皮卡，它们造成的事故中，另一侧乘客的死亡率最高。就我个人经验而言，这很有道理。高速公路上的大型皮卡简直让我抓狂。

Mapping rooftops after an earthquake — Akash Wadhwani visualized the mapping efforts of volunteers after an earthquake    in Turkey in 2023. Emergency workers use publicly available maps, but the map was sparse when the disaster occurred. So thousands of volunteers quickly started drawing to fill in the empty space.

> **译文：**地震后绘制屋顶地图——阿卡什·瓦德瓦尼（Akash Wadhwani）将2023年土耳其地震后志愿者的地图绘制工作可视化。救援人员使用公开地图，但灾难发生时地图信息稀少。因此，成千上万的志愿者迅速开始绘制地图，填补空白。

> **译文：** 🌎 DLocal：销量增长带动了消费率 1. 🦎 伯克希尔：现金开始流动

Berkshire Hathaway Q2 revenue rose 10% Y/Y to $101.8 billion, while operating profit before tax reached $14.4 billion.

> **译文：** 伯克希尔哈撒韦公司第二季度营收同比增长 10% 至 1018 亿美元，税前营业利润达到 144 亿美元。

Manufacturing was the standout, with revenue up 13% and profit rising 24%.

> **译文：** 制造业表现突出，收入增长 13%，利润增长 24%。

Insurance was softer, with underwriting profit down 13% and GEICO underwriting earnings falling 45%.

> **译文：** 保险业疲软，承保利润下降 13%，GEICO 承保收益下降 45%。

The bigger story is the capital allocation under new CEO Greg Abel. Berkshire bought $23 billion of stocks while selling just $3 billion, its first quarter as a net buyer of stocks in more than three years. That included roughly $10 billion of Alphabet [ https://substack.com/redirect/e2cb1f52-6d69-47fb-9e63-7e51e212e9a4?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]. Berkshire also repurchased $4.5 billion of its own stock in Q2, followed by an estimated $3.4 billion more in July.

> **译文：** 更引人注目的是新任首席执行官格雷格·阿贝尔 (Greg Abel) 领导下的资本配置。伯克希尔哈撒韦公司买入了价值 230 亿美元的股票，而仅卖出了 30 亿美元，这是该公司三年多来首次在一个季度内成为股票净买家。这其中包括约 100 亿美元的 Alphabet 股票[https://substack.com/redirect/e2cb1f52-6d69-47fb-9e63-7e51e212e9a4?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro]。伯克希尔哈撒韦公司还在第二季度回购了价值 45 亿美元的自身股票，并在 7 月份又回购了约 34 亿美元。

As a result, the cash pile fell to $365.5 billion from roughly $397 billion in Q1. That is still enormous, but the direction has changed. Berkshire is also deploying capital through acquisitions, including the $6.8 billion purchase of homebuilder Taylor Morrison.

> **译文：** 因此，伯克希尔哈撒韦公司的现金储备从第一季度的约3970亿美元降至3655亿美元。虽然数额仍然庞大，但方向已经改变。伯克希尔哈撒韦公司也在通过收购来部署资本，其中包括以68亿美元收购住宅建筑商泰勒·莫里森公司。

Bottom Line: The Abel era is starting to look different. Berkshire remains extraordinarily liquid, but buybacks, equity purchases, and acquisitions are finally putting meaningful chunks of its cash pile to work. The question is no longer when Berkshire will deploy capital. It is whether Abel can earn Buffett-like returns on it.

> **译文：** 结论：阿贝尔时代正在发生改变。伯克希尔哈撒韦的流动性依然非常充裕，但股票回购、股权收购和并购终于开始有效利用其大量现金储备。现在的问题不再是伯克希尔哈撒韦何时会部署资金，而是阿贝尔能否从中获得堪比巴菲特的回报。

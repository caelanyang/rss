According to Reuters, the U.S. government is drafting restrictions on new models of data-center optical transceivers from China, potentially to be implemented by the FCC through its equipment authorization and Covered List frameworks. However, no formal rule text has been released. It remains unclear whether a “Chinese optical transceiver” would be defined by place of manufacture, customs country of origin, place of incorporation, ownership and control, or the location and composition of its R&D and supply chain. The proposal could also be revised or shelved.

> **译文：** 据路透社报道，美国政府正在起草针对中国新型数据中心光收发器的限制措施，这些措施可能由联邦通信委员会（FCC）通过其设备授权和受管制清单框架实施。然而，目前尚未公布正式的规则文本。“中国光收发器”的定义仍不明确，例如，其依据是制造地、海关原产国、注册地、所有权和控制权，还是其研发和供应链的所在地及构成。该提案也可能被修改或搁置。

The FCC is the U.S. Federal Communications Commission. Traditionally, it has been responsible for the technical authorization of communications and radio-frequency electronic equipment. In recent years, however, the FCC has increasingly used the equipment authorization framework to advance national security and supply chain objectives. The issue should therefore not be reduced to the proposition that “test reports issued by laboratories in China will no longer be accepted.” Laboratory policies primarily affect where testing and certification take place, whereas the Covered List and related measures could directly affect whether new models may enter the U.S. market.

> **译文：** 美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）传统上负责通信和射频电子设备的技术认证。然而，近年来，FCC越来越多地利用设备认证框架来推进国家安全和供应链目标。因此，这个问题不应简化为“中国实验室出具的测试报告将不再被接受”。实验室政策主要影响测试和认证的地点，而受管制清单及相关措施则可能直接影响新型号设备能否进入美国市场。

FCC 26-50, which has already been released, primarily closes a loophole through which components from entities on the Covered List could enter the United States embedded in third-party end products. It does not amount to a comprehensive ban on all Chinese optical transceivers. The specific implementation criteria for measures targeting Chinese optical transceivers will remain uncertain until formal documentation is released.

> **译文：** 已发布的FCC 26-50号条例主要堵住了一个漏洞，该漏洞允许来自受管制清单实体的组件嵌入第三方终端产品中进入美国。它并非全面禁止所有中国光收发器。针对中国光收发器的具体措施实施标准，在正式文件发布之前仍不明朗。

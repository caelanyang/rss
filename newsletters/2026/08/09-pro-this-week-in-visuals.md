1. 💊 Eli Lilly: Volume Crushes Price

Lilly’s Q2 revenue jumped 48% Y/Y to $23.0 billion ($2.3 billion beat), while adjusted EPS was $8.38 ($1.80 beat). Revenue growth came from a 60% increase in volume, more than offsetting a 13% decline in realized prices.

> **译文：**礼来公司第二季度营收同比增长48%至230亿美元（超出预期23亿美元），调整后每股收益为8.38美元（超出预期1.80美元）。营收增长主要得益于销量增长60%，足以抵消实际售价13%的下滑。

Mounjaro surged 91% to $9.9 billion and Zepbound grew 46% to $4.9 billion, bringing combined GLP-1 revenue to nearly $15 billion. International Mounjaro sales more than doubled to $5.2 billion as Lilly expands access globally, despite significant price reductions including China reimbursement.

> **译文：**Mounjaro销售额飙升91%至99亿美元，Zepbound增长46%至49亿美元，两款GLP-1类药物合计收入接近150亿美元。尽管价格大幅下调（包括中国医保报销），但由于礼来公司在全球范围内扩大了Mounjaro的市场准入，其国际销售额仍翻了一番多，达到52亿美元。

Foundayo, Lilly’s newly launched oral GLP-1 obesity pill, generated $98 million in its first quarter on the market. The next-generation pipeline also advanced materially: retatrutide (triple-hormone obesity injection targeting GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon) delivered positive results in three additional Phase 3 obesity trials. Lilly plans to file with the FDA in Q1 2027.

> **译文：**礼来公司新推出的口服GLP-1减肥药Foundayo上市首季度营收达9800万美元。其下一代研发管线也取得了显著进展：retatrutide（一种靶向GLP-1、GIP和胰高血糖素的三重激素减肥注射剂）在三项新的3期肥胖症临床试验中均取得了积极结果。礼来计划于2027年第一季度向FDA提交上市申请。

Lilly raised FY26 revenue guidance to $85–$87 billion, a $2.5 billion midpoint increase. Underlying EPS guidance was also raised by $2.78 at the midpoint, but $3.03 of acquisition-related R&D charges offset that improvement, leaving reported guidance at $35.50–$36.50.

> **译文：**礼来公司将2026财年营收预期上调至850亿至870亿美元，中值上调25亿美元。每股收益预期也上调2.78美元，但3.03美元的收购相关研发费用抵消了这一增长，最终每股收益预期为35.50至36.50美元。

Bottom Line: The GLP-1 story remains a volume machine. Lilly is deliberately giving up price to expand access, and demand is more than compensating. Foundayo adds a new format today, while retatrutide increasingly looks like the next major leg of the obesity franchise.

> **译文：**结论：GLP-1 的故事依然是销量的保证。礼来公司有意降低价格以扩大其可及性，而市场需求也远远弥补了这一损失。Foundayo 今天推出了一种新的剂型，而 retatrutide 越来越像是肥胖症治疗领域下一个重要的支柱。

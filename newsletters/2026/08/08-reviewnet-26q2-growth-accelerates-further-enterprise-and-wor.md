Key Takeaway

26Q1 was characterized by “strong revenue, but relatively conservative and less transparent forward KPIs and guidance, leaving the market concerned about a potential growth slowdown.” 26Q2 reversed that setup: “revenue growth accelerated further, while RPO, DBNRR, large customers, and guidance all improved simultaneously.”

> **译文：**2026年第一季度的特点是“营收强劲，但前瞻性关键绩效指标和指引相对保守且透明度较低，令市场担忧潜在的增长放缓”。2026年第二季度则扭转了这一局面：“营收增长进一步加速，同时RPO、DBNRR、大客户和指引均有所改善”。

26Q1 revenue growth itself did not decelerate, but RPO, DBNRR, gross margin, and full-year guidance were relatively conservative, leading investors to question the quality of growth. 26Q2 saw a broad-based recovery: revenue growth accelerated from 33.5% to 36%; DBNRR recovered from 118% to 120%; large-customer growth improved from 25% to 27%; total RPO grew ~7% q/q; 26Q3 guidance still implies ~31% y/y growth; and the magnitude of the FY26 revenue guidance raise was meaningfully greater than simply flowing through the 26Q2 beat.

> **译文：**2026财年第一季度营收增长本身并未放缓，但RPO、DBNRR、毛利率和全年业绩指引相对保守，导致投资者对增长质量产生质疑。2026财年第二季度出现全面复苏：营收增长从33.5%加速至36%；DBNRR从118%回升至120%；大客户增长率从25%提升至27%；总RPO环比增长约7%；2026财年第三季度业绩指引仍预示着约31%的同比增长；且2026财年营收指引的上调幅度远超2026财年第二季度业绩超预期。

The results also validated the core thesis in the 26Q2 preview: growth drivers are shifting from traditional CDN/WAF toward broader enterprise platform expansion, with Workers, Zero Trust/SASE, the partner channel, and large-customer consolidation all becoming increasingly important. At the same time, Workers monetization is progressing faster than anticipated in the preview; the preview’s concern around H2 IT-budget/financing constraints has not yet shown up in 26Q2 results or 26Q3 guidance.

> **译文：**结果也验证了2026年第二季度预览报告中的核心论点：增长驱动力正从传统的CDN/WAF转向更广泛的企业平台扩展，其中Workers、零信任/SASE、合作伙伴渠道以及大客户整合的重要性日益凸显。与此同时，Workers的商业化进程比预览报告中预期的更快；预览报告中对下半年IT预算/融资限制的担忧尚未在2026年第二季度业绩或2026年第三季度业绩指引中显现。

Greetings!

Elon Musk fans have never had so many opportunities to hear from the man.

> **译文：** 埃隆·马斯克的粉丝们从未有过如此多的机会听到他本人的声音。

On Tuesday, his newly public SpaceX will deliver its first earnings report, and we all expect Musk will be on the earnings call, in what would be his second call this earnings season following Tesla’s a couple of weeks earlier. (For a more expansive rundown of what will be a crowded earnings week, see below).

> **译文：** 
> 周二，他新近上市的SpaceX将发布首份财报，我们都预计马斯克会出席财报电话会议，这将是他继几周前特斯拉财报电话会议之后，本财报季的第二次电话会议。（关于本周密集的财报周的更详细概述，请参见下文）。

Two days after SpaceX’s earnings report comes out, on Thursday, SpaceX’s first lockup restrictions on early shareholder selling expire.

> **译文：** SpaceX 财报发布两天后，也就是周四，SpaceX 对提前出售股票的首批限制措施到期。

That will bring hundreds of millions of extra shares onto the market, dramatically expanding the float.

> **译文：** 这将使数亿股额外股票进入市场，大幅扩大流通股数量。

Lockup expirations typically put downward pressure on a stock in the weeks before they occur, and SpaceX has been no different: Its stock has fallen steadily in recent weeks.

> **译文：** 禁售期结束前几周，股票通常会面临下行压力，SpaceX 也不例外：其股价在最近几周持续下跌。

SpaceX closed Friday at $108.37, 49% lower than its post-IPO peak and 20% below its IPO price.

> **译文：** SpaceX 周五收盘价为 108.37 美元，比 IPO 后的最高价低 49%，比 IPO 价格低 20%。

Fair’s fair, of course.

SpaceX was ridiculously overvalued at the IPO price of $135, and with a market capitalization now of $1.4 trillion, it’s still vastly overvalued.

> **译文：** SpaceX 在 IPO 时股价为 135 美元，估值过高；如今市值已达 1.4 万亿美元，仍然被严重高估。

Then again, value investing isn’t a concept Musk acolytes would understand.

> **译文：** 不过话说回来，价值投资这个概念，马斯克的追随者们恐怕是理解不了的。

All that said, we’d bet SpaceX stock bounces a bit this week, if only because of what’s likely to be an upbeat financial report.

> **译文：** 综上所述，我们打赌 SpaceX 的股票本周会小幅反弹，原因很可能是该公司会发布一份乐观的财务报告。

Wall Street analysts are projecting revenue for the June quarter of $6.819 billion.

> **译文：** 华尔街分析师预测，该公司6月份季度的收入将达到68.19亿美元。

We don’t know what growth that represents—SpaceX’s IPO filing didn’t contain 2025 quarterly numbers—but given that the company’s revenue for all of 2025 was $18.6 billion, the estimate likely represents a decent increase on last year.

> **译文：** 我们不知道这代表着怎样的增长——SpaceX 的 IPO 文件中没有包含 2025 年的季度数据——但考虑到该公司 2025 年全年的收入为 186
> 亿美元，这一估计可能比去年有了相当大的增长。

In the first quarter, moreover, SpaceX had reported $4.69 billion, an increase of 15% on the year-earlier period.

> **译文：** 此外，SpaceX 第一季度营收为 46.9 亿美元，比上年同期增长 15%。

Alongside revenue growth, however, we’ll see how badly SpaceX is bleeding cash.

> **译文：** 然而，在关注营收增长的同时，我们也将看到 SpaceX 的现金流究竟有多糟糕。

Analysts are estimating the company will report a cash burn of $10.9 billion, thanks to $14 billion in capital expenditures, according to S&P Global Market Intelligence. (In the first quarter, SpaceX reported capex of $10 billion.) While SpaceX is spending a fraction of the $40 billion to $50 billion each of the big cloud firms is currently laying out on capex every quarter, its revenue is a fraction of what the cloud giants bring in, which means it doesn’t have the same deep pockets.

> **译文：** 据标普全球市场情报公司（S&P Global Market
> Intelligence）的分析师预测，SpaceX公司将报告109亿美元的现金消耗，这主要归因于140亿美元的资本支出。（SpaceX第一季度报告的资本支出为100亿美元。）尽管SpaceX的资本支出仅为目前大型云公司每季度400亿至500亿美元资本支出的一小部分，但其收入也远不及这些云巨头，这意味着SpaceX的资金实力远不及它们。

Analysts project SpaceX’s capex will double next year, to $25 billion in the June quarter of 2027.

> **译文：** 分析师预测，SpaceX 的资本支出明年将翻一番，到 2027 年 6 月季度将达到 250 亿美元。

One reason that’s important to track is that there’s a very good chance SpaceX and Tesla will merge.

> **译文：** 值得关注的一个原因是，SpaceX 和特斯拉很有可能合并。

And Tesla, as it revealed when it reported earnings, is now burning cash itself, thanks to capex increases.

> **译文：** 正如特斯拉在公布财报时所透露的那样，由于资本支出增加，特斯拉目前自身也在烧钱。

What a combination that would be.

> **译文：** 由于广告业务停滞不前，Snap 股票最近一直低迷不振。

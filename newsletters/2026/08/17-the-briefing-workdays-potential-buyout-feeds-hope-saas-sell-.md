The Briefing

By Laura Mandaro

Aug 16, 2026

Thanks for reading The Briefing, our nightly column where we break down the day’s news. If you like what you see, I encourage you to subscribe to our reporting here. Greetings! In software land, it’s getting harder and harder to call out a singular vibe shift when the vibes keep shifting every 24 hours. On Thursday, when news broke that Silver Lake was in talks to acquire Workday, the human resource software company’s stock shot up 19%.


> **译文：** 感谢您阅读我们的每日新闻专栏“简报”，我们会在此为您解读当日新闻。如果您喜欢我们的内容，欢迎在此订阅。问候！在软件行业，由于市场氛围每24小时都在变化，想要准确把握某个单一的趋势转变变得越来越难。周四，当银湖资本（Silver Lake）洽谈收购Workday的消息传出后，这家人力资源软件公司的股价飙升了19%。

Such a deal would be a big vote of confidence in a prominent software firm and, combined with a 55% appreciation in Workday’s shares since late June, a possible sign that the AI-driven SaaSpocalypse is ebbing. But on Friday, the buzz seemed to have worn off. Workday’s shares sank almost 4%. The stocks of other software companies, which similarly spiked on Thursday following the Silverlake-Workday reports, also widely dropped. Prepare for more mood swings in the coming weeks.


> **译文：** 这样一笔交易无疑是对一家知名软件公司投下的信任票，再加上Workday股价自6月底以来上涨了55%，这或许预示着人工智能驱动的SaaS末日论正在消退。但到了周五，这股热潮似乎已经消退。Workday股价下跌了近4%。其他软件公司的股票在周四Silverlake收购Workday的消息公布后也同样大幅下跌。预计未来几周市场情绪还会出现更多波动。

Some of the recent bounce in SaaS stocks may stem from technical trading: money coming out of stocks with high momentum and into the “relative losers of late,” which have been software stocks, said Jackson Ader, managing director of software equity research at KeyBanc Capital Markets, last week on TITV. Making matters worse, recent earnings reports haven’t painted a clear picture of the industry at all.


> **译文：** KeyBanc Capital Markets软件股票研究董事总经理杰克逊·阿德尔上周在TITV节目中表示，近期SaaS股票的部分反弹可能源于技术性交易：资金从高动能股票流出，转而流入“近期相对表现不佳”的股票，而这些股票恰恰是软件股。更糟糕的是，近期发布的财报完全没有清晰地反映出该行业的现状。

While Atlassian’s strong cloud growth sent the stock soaring, and Palantir has continued to report scorching revenue gains, IBM shares crashed after the firm reported a drop in purchases of its mainframe computers as customers spent more on AI. The next big data point comes in two weeks when Salesforce reports results. Analysts polled by Refinitiv expect revenue to rise more than 10% to $11 billion. But as with past quarters, most of the attention will be on growth from its AI products.


> **译文：** 尽管Atlassian强劲的云计算业务增长推动其股价飙升，Palantir也持续公布了令人瞩目的营收增长，但IBM的股价却因该公司公布的大型机销量下滑而暴跌，原因是客户在人工智能领域的支出增加。下一个重要的数据点将在两周后公布，届时Salesforce将发布业绩报告。Refinitiv调查的分析师预计，Salesforce的营收将增长超过10%，达到110亿美元。但与过去几个季度一样，市场关注的焦点将主要集中在其人工智能产品的增长上。

Software stock analysts have been bracing for more pain.


> **译文：** 软件股分析师们一直在为更大的痛苦做准备。

Karl Keirstead, head of AI and software equity research at UBS, says the executives of many blue chip companies he speaks with are “absolutely articulating a view that, given the performance improvements in these AI models and the ability to custom-build alternatives, they would like their spending with software company X, Y, Z to be down 30% over the next three years.”“I‘m in the camp that it’s going to be a rocky ride for the next 12 months,” he said in mid July.


> **译文：** 瑞银人工智能和软件股票研究主管卡尔·基尔斯蒂德表示，他接触过的许多蓝筹公司高管都“明确表示，鉴于这些人工智能模型的性能提升以及定制替代方案的能力，他们希望未来三年在软件公司 X、Y、Z 上的支出减少 30%”。他在 7 月中旬表示：“我认为未来 12 个月将会是一段充满挑战的时期。”

A takeover offer for Workday—whose shares are still down 7% this year—would show there’s a path for out-of-favor software companies.


> **译文：** Workday（其股价今年仍下跌了7%）的收购要约表明，不受欢迎的软件公司也有出路。

Generally, a company like WorkDay would be catnip for PE firms: While its stock has suffered, it throws off plenty of cash. (WorkDay generated $2.8 billion in free cash flow for its year ended in January.) But as we reported in January, these institutions have held off on acquisitions because they’re wrestling with private portfolios of software companies that are as under threat of AI as their publicly held peers.


> **译文：** 通常来说，像WorkDay这样的公司对私募股权公司来说极具吸引力：尽管其股价下跌，但它却能产生大量现金流。（截至1月份的财年，WorkDay的自由现金流为28亿美元。）但正如我们在1月份报道的那样，这些机构之所以迟迟没有进行收购，是因为它们正忙于管理其持有的软件公司私人投资组合，而这些公司与上市同行一样，都面临着人工智能的威胁。

A WorkDay acquisition would also set a valuation multiple for other potential software buyouts. Notably, the Reuters report on Silverlake’s acquisition interest did not include an actual purchase price, leaving that multiple unknown. Still, shares are 13% higher than before the report emerged—indicating investors are betting this SaaS story has a happy ending. —Akash Pasricha contributed to this report.


> **译文：** WorkDay的收购也将为其他潜在的软件收购案设定估值倍数。值得注意的是，路透社关于Silverlake有意收购WorkDay的报道并未提及实际收购价格，因此估值倍数仍不得而知。尽管如此，WorkDay的股价仍比报道发布前上涨了13%，这表明投资者押注这家SaaS公司最终会迎来圆满结局。——Akash Pasricha对本文亦有贡献。

Here’s what else is going onMore clarity is emerging about how Nvidia is using its financial heft to help customers like OpenAI—while it tries to avoid spooking Wall Street with commitments that could tank its stock and credit rating. On Friday, we reported that Nvidia was close to an agreement to provide around $100 billion in credit support for OpenAI to lease a data center in Ohio that would be among the biggest of its kind.


> **译文：** 以下是其他进展：英伟达如何利用其雄厚的财力帮助像OpenAI这样的客户，以及它如何努力避免做出可能导致股价暴跌和信用评级受损的承诺，从而引发华尔街恐慌，目前情况正变得越来越清晰。上周五，我们报道了英伟达即将与OpenAI达成协议，为其提供约1000亿美元的信贷支持，用于租赁位于俄亥俄州的一座数据中心，该数据中心建成后将成为同类数据中心中规模最大的之一。

That’s a lot, but it’s lower than an earlier report that it could backstop $250 billion—and just a fraction of what the project will cost. The $100 billion would cover the first phase of the project; Nvidia would be incentivized to help guarantee the second phase, but it's technically not on the hook for the rest and could wait a couple of years to decide. Nvidia wants to limit its credit exposure to the Ohio project to 25% of the total financing, we reported.


> **译文：** 这笔金额虽然不少，但低于此前报道的2500亿美元担保额——而且仅占该项目总成本的一小部分。这1000亿美元将用于项目的第一阶段；英伟达有动力为第二阶段提供担保，但从技术上讲，它无需承担剩余部分的责任，可以等待几年后再做决定。我们此前报道称，英伟达希望将其在俄亥俄州项目的信贷敞口限制在总融资额的25%。

That’s the same ceiling it announced on Monday, when it unveiled a partnership with six of Wall Street’s biggest firms to raise $500 billion to help customers buy Nvidia chips. Then on Saturday, we reported that Nvidia is discussing investing $3 billion into the IPO of SB Energy, the SoftBank unit that is developing the Ohio site. SoftBank, of course, is a major OpenAI investor: Including past and committed investments, it is sinking more than $64 billion into the startup.


> **译文：** 这与该公司周一宣布的融资上限相同，当时该公司宣布与华尔街六大公司合作，筹集5000亿美元，以帮助客户购买英伟达芯片。随后在周六，我们报道了英伟达正在考虑向SB Energy（软银旗下负责俄亥俄州项目开发的子公司）的IPO投资30亿美元。当然，软银也是OpenAI的主要投资者：包括过去和已承诺的投资在内，软银已向这家初创公司投入超过640亿美元。

And Nvidia is also investing in Lancium, the power developer behind OpenAI’s separate Stargate Texas campus.


> **译文：** 英伟达也在投资 Lancium，该公司是 OpenAI 位于德克萨斯州的独立 Stargate 园区背后的电力开发商。

All told, this suggests Nvidia isn’t exactly backing off using its financial might to support chip customers. (As of April, Nvidia had more than $80 billion in cash and marketable equity and debt securities on its balance sheet as of April and generated a whopping $49 billion in cash just in the April quarter alone.) But it’s trying to make sure it doesn’t look like it’s taking all of the risk. In Other News


> **译文：** 总而言之，这表明英伟达并没有完全放弃利用其雄厚的财力来支持芯片客户。（截至4月份，英伟达的资产负债表上拥有超过800亿美元的现金、有价股和债券，仅在4月份当季就产生了高达490亿美元的现金。）但它正努力确保自己不会给人留下承担所有风险的印象。其他新闻

Anthropic’s revenue rose around 14 times in the second quarter from the same period a year ago, the company has told investors. More here.


> **译文：** Anthropic公司告诉投资者，其第二季度营收较去年同期增长约14倍。更多信息请点击此处。

SpaceX closed its acquisition of coding assistant Cursor on Friday, one of the biggest takeovers of a venture-backed startup. The company has told employees that it will likely phase out the Cursor name on some new products.

🕵️ Palantir: Sovereign AI

Palantir Q2 revenue jumped 93% Y/Y to $1.94 billion ($130 million beat), marking the 12th consecutive quarter of acceleration. Adjusted EPS of $0.41 beat by $0.06. The Rule of 40 [ https://substack.com/redirect/4504af28-b813-4671-ad69-52ee809b990f?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] score climbed again to 155, with adjusted free cash flow reaching $1.22 billion at a 63% margin.

> **译文：** Palantir第二季度营收同比增长93%至19.4亿美元（超出预期1.3亿美元），连续第12个季度实现增长。调整后每股收益为0.41美元，超出预期0.06美元。“40法则”评分再次攀升至155分，调整后自由现金流达到12.2亿美元，利润率为63%。

US revenue keeps pulling away.

💼 US Commercial: $764 million (+149% Y/Y, +28% Q/Q).

> **译文：** 💼 美国商业：7.64 亿美元（同比增长 149%，环比增长 28%）。

🪖 US Government: $809 million (+90% Y/Y, +18% Q/Q).

> **译文：** 🪖 美国政府：8.09亿美元（同比增长90%，环比增长18%）。

Total US revenue reached $1.57 billion, up 115% Y/Y and now representing 81% of Palantir’s business. International revenue grew a much slower 33% to $363 million, with CEO Alex Karp again dismissive of Europe: “The growth sucks.”

> **译文：** 美国总营收达到15.7亿美元，同比增长115%，占Palantir业务的81%。国际营收增长速度则慢得多，仅增长33%至3.63亿美元，首席执行官Alex Karp再次对欧洲市场不屑一顾：“增长糟透了。”

The pipeline behind the print looks even more bullish. Palantir closed 220 deals worth at least $1 million, including 73 above $10 million.

> **译文：** 这份报告背后的项目储备看起来更加强劲。Palantir完成了220笔单笔交易，每笔交易金额至少为100万美元，其中73笔超过1000万美元。

TCV (Total Contract Value): The total value of contracts signed during the quarter reached a record $2.13 billion in US Commercial, up 153% Y/Y.

> **译文：** TCV（合同总价值）：本季度签署的合同总价值在美国商业领域达到创纪录的 21.3 亿美元，同比增长 153%。

RDV (Remaining Deal Value): Contracted revenue not yet recognized climbed 124% Y/Y (and a staggering 27% Q/Q) to $6.24 billion in US Commercial, giving Palantir an increasingly large backlog behind future growth.

> **译文：** RDV（剩余交易价值）：美国商业领域尚未确认的合同收入同比增长 124%（环比增长 27%），达到 62.4 亿美元，为 Palantir 未来的增长提供了越来越大的积压订单。

Palantir’s new framing is “sovereign AI.” Management argues customers increasingly want AI without handing proprietary data, workflows, or competitive intelligence to frontier model providers. AIP (Palantir’s AI Platform) sits between companies and the models, letting customers swap LLMs while keeping their data and operational logic under their own control. Karp put it more bluntly: customers should not become “vassal states of the language labs.”

> **译文：** Palantir 的新概念是“自主人工智能”。管理层认为，客户越来越希望获得人工智能，但又不希望将专有数据、工作流程或竞争情报拱手让给前沿模型提供商。AIP（Palantir 的人工智能平台）位于企业和模型之间，允许客户在保持自身数据和运营逻辑控制权的同时，更换语言实验室模型 (LLM)。Karp 的说法更加直白：客户不应该成为“语言实验室的附庸”。

We discussed last quarter that tokens are the new coal [ https://substack.com/redirect/ac6fb586-101c-4ed6-9a2c-12c53926292c?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]. Models and tokens are becoming cheaper commodities. Palantir wants to own the governed operational layer where companies turn them into actual work.

> **译文：** 我们上个季度讨论过，代币是新的煤炭[https://substack.com/redirect/ac6fb586-101c-4ed6-9a2c-12c53926292c?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro]。模型和代币正变得越来越便宜。Palantir 希望掌控受监管的运营层，让企业能够将模型转化为实际工作。

Palantir raised FY26 revenue guidance by roughly $500 million to $8.15–$8.16 billion, implying 82% growth, versus 71% expected just three months ago. US Commercial is now expected to grow at least 134% to more than $3.42 billion. Adjusted free cash flow guidance increased to ~$4.6 billion (from ~$4.3 billion previously).

> **译文：** Palantir 将 2026 财年营收预期上调约 5 亿美元至 81.5 亿至 81.6 亿美元，这意味着 82% 的增长率，而三个月前的预期为 71%。美国商业业务的预期增长率目前至少为 134%，超过 34.2 亿美元。调整后的自由现金流预期也从之前的约 43 亿美元上调至约 46 亿美元。

Bottom Line: The fundamental story somehow keeps getting stronger. Revenue growth accelerated, US Commercial is now 39% of the top line, and free cash flow margins have crossed 60%. The valuation is still extreme at nearly 80x FY26 EBITDA, but Palantir is doing something equally extreme: accelerating above 90% revenue growth at nearly $8 billion of annual revenue while simultaneously expanding margins.

> **译文：** 结论：基本面持续走强。营收增长加速，美国商业业务目前占总营收的39%，自由现金流利润率超过60%。尽管估值仍然偏高，接近2026财年EBITDA的80倍，但Palantir正在做一件同样令人瞩目的事情：在年营收接近80亿美元的情况下，营收增速超过90%，同时利润率也在不断提升。

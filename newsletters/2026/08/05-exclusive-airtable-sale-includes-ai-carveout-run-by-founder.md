What an ending.

Airtable, valued at $11 billion in 2021 after a trio of funding rounds in under two years, is being acquired by Italian conglomerate Bending Spoons for just $1.285 billion in cash, the companies said on Tuesday.

> **译文：** Airtable 在不到两年的时间里完成了三轮融资，2021 年的估值达到了 110 亿美元。两家公司周二表示，意大利企业集团 Bending Spoons 将以 12.85 亿美元的现金收购 Airtable。

The price tag for the once-hot productivity app seems like a blow to shareholders, particularly those like hedge fund XN, Thrive Capital and Greenoaks Capital that led its last rounds.

> **译文：** 这款曾经风靡一时的生产力应用程序的定价似乎对股东们来说是一个打击，尤其是像对冲基金 XN、Thrive Capital 和 Greenoaks Capital 这样领投其最后几轮融资的股东们。

And the price, amounting to less than three times annualized revenue, should lower expectations for future sales of enterprise software startups (Airtable was No. 2 on our recent list of such targets.)

> **译文：** 而此次收购价格不到年化收入的三倍，应该会降低人们对企业软件初创公司未来销售的预期（Airtable 在我们最近的此类目标名单中排名第二）。

But there’s more to this deal than meets the eye.

Alongside the deal price, Airtable also has around $900 million in cash on its balance sheet that can be distributed to its preferred stockholders.

> **译文：** 除了交易价格外，Airtable 的资产负债表上还有约 9 亿美元的现金，可以分配给其优先股股东。

What’s left goes to its common shareholders, according to people with knowledge of the deal.

> **译文：** 据知情人士透露，剩余部分将分配给其普通股股东。

That’s in part due to Airtable CEO Howie Liu’s energetic fundraising, which brought in $1.3 billion over the company’s 14-year history.

> **译文：** 这部分要归功于 Airtable 首席执行官 Howie Liu 的积极筹款，该公司成立 14 年来已筹集了 13 亿美元。

As a result, many shareholders should at least get back the money they invested, the people said.

> **译文：** 因此，知情人士表示，许多股东至少应该能拿回他们的投资款。

Earlier investors, such as CRV and Caffeinated Capital, should get a sizable return.

> **译文：** 早期投资者，例如 CRV 和 Caffeinated Capital，应该会获得可观的回报。

There’s more.

Bending Spoons is not buying all of Airtable.

Six months ago, Airtable launched HyperAgent, its platform for building and deploying AI agents.

> **译文：** 六个月前，Airtable 推出了 HyperAgent，这是一个用于构建和部署 AI 代理的平台。

Just a few days before selling to Bending Spoons, Airtable spun off HyperAgent to a standalone company called HyperAgent Inc., according to an SEC filing and people with knowledge of the deal.

> **译文：** 据美国证券交易委员会的文件和知情人士透露，就在将公司出售给 Bending Spoons 的前几天，Airtable 将 HyperAgent 分拆成了一家名为 HyperAgent Inc. 的独立公司。

Airtable investors will get a stake in HyperAgent, according to the people, though the exact structure, including cost, couldn’t be learned.

> **译文：** 据知情人士透露，Airtable 的投资者将获得 HyperAgent 的股份，但具体的结构（包括成本）尚不清楚。

Liu has also told investors that the new HyperAgent startup will be able to raise its own funding and he plans to run it full-time after the Bending Spoons deal closes, said a person with knowledge of the communications.

> **译文：** 一位知情人士透露，刘还告诉投资者，新的 HyperAgent 初创公司将能够自行筹集资金，他计划在 Bending Spoons 交易完成后全职运营该公司。

Of course, just getting the investment back isn’t a great return for later investors.

> **译文：** 当然，对于后来的投资者来说，仅仅收回投资并不是一个好的回报。

Buying the S&amp;P 500 in September 2020 would have doubled investors’ money.

> **译文：** 如果在 2020 年 9 月买入标普 500 指数，投资者的资金将翻倍。

And common stock holders fared worse.

Investors who bought common stock in a small secondary transaction in 2021, valued between $5.5 billion and $11 billion, lost money, according to a person with direct knowledge of the situation. “It’s venture; it happens,” the person said.

> **译文：** 据一位知情人士透露，2021年参与一笔价值55亿至110亿美元小型二级市场交易的普通股投资者蒙受了损失。“这就是风险投资，这种情况时有发生。”该人士表示。

And while employees are getting some money to account for their equity, the sale price is likely a disappointment.

> **译文：** 虽然员工们会得到一些钱来补偿他们的股权，但出售价格可能会令人失望。

Bain Capital Ventures partner Aaref Hilaly estimates there is about $850 million for common shareholders in the deal.

> **译文：** 贝恩资本风险投资公司合伙人阿雷夫·希拉利估计，这笔交易中普通股股东将获得约 8.5 亿美元。

If the founders own about 20%, there should be about $127 million left for about 900 employees.“Common shareholders should get something from this, just nowhere near what they were hoping for,” he said on  The Information’s TITV streaming show Thursday.

> **译文：** 如果创始人持有约 20% 的股份，那么应该还剩下约 1.27 亿美元，供约 900 名员工使用。“普通股股东应该能从中得到一些好处，只是远不及他们所期望的，”他在周四 The Information 的 TITV 流媒体节目中说道。

He noted the company had been valued at $4 billion recently on secondary-market exchanges.

> **译文：** 他指出，该公司最近在二级市场交易所的估值已达 40 亿美元。

Spokespeople for Airtable and Bending Spoons didn’t respond to requests for comment.

> **译文：** Airtable 和 Bending Spoons 的发言人均未回应置评请求。

Airtable’s reorganization and sale could prove to be a turning point for privately held enterprise software startups.

> **译文：** Airtable 的重组和出售可能成为私营企业软件初创公司的一个转折点。

Almost since the last tech bubble collapsed in 2022, venture capitalists have been wrestling with what to do with portfolios full of software startups whose valuations looked far too high given the sell-off in public peers.

> **译文：** 自 2022 年上次科技泡沫破裂以来，风险投资家们一直在努力思考如何处理那些估值过高的软件创业公司的投资组合，因为上市同行的抛售潮让他们的估值显得过高。

Mounting competition from AI apps further worsened the outlook for many, slowing revenue growth and pushing founders to hastily remake their startups as AI companies.

> **译文：** 来自人工智能应用的日益激烈的竞争进一步恶化了许多公司的前景，导致收入增长放缓，并迫使创始人匆忙将他们的初创公司改造为人工智能公司。

Airtable’s sale shows one path to the finish line: A deeply discounted price and a fresh start as an AI company.

> **译文：** Airtable 的出售表明了通往终点的一条道路：以极低的折扣价重新开始，成为一家人工智能公司。

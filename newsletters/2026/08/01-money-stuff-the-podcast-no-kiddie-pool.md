Podcast

This week’s episode of the Money Stuff podcast is out now. My guest-co-host 
this week is Bloomberg’s Silvia Killingsworth. We discuss the Situational 
Awareness situation, record dates, CVRs, and sourcing weird volatility risk 
from retail customers to sell to hedge funds. The Money Stuff podcast will be 
off for August, though we will be re-running an old interview or two.


> **译文：** 本周的“理财之道”播客节目现已上线。本周的特邀嘉宾是彭博社的西尔维娅·基林斯沃思。我们讨论了情境感知机制、股权登记日、可转换债券以及如何从散户客户那里获取异常波动风险并出售给对冲基金。“理财之道”播客节目将于八月暂停更新，但我们会重播一两期之前的访谈节目。

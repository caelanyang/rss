Greetings fellow Spectrum readers. I’m continually impressed by the resiliency of communities hit by natural disasters. They often rebuild with highly innovative energy solutions to help them weather the next storm and the ravages of climate change.

> **译文：** 各位Spectrum的读者朋友们，大家好。我一直对遭受自然灾害的社区展现出的韧性印象深刻。他们通常会利用极具创新性的能源解决方案进行重建，以帮助他们抵御下一次风暴和气候变化的侵袭。

One community that stands out is the island of Vieques in Puerto Rico. Tired of enduring the long power outages that follow hurricanes, the community is installing its own hydrogen fuel cell production system and mobile microgrid. During blackouts, a combination of solar, hydrogen fuel cells and batteries will be moved around the island to power critical infrastructure.

> **译文：** 波多黎各的别克斯岛社区堪称典范。该岛社区不堪忍受飓风过后长时间的停电，正在建设自己的氢燃料电池生产系统和移动微电网。停电期间，太阳能、氢燃料电池和蓄电池的组合将被部署到岛上各处，为关键基础设施供电。

And then there’s the balcony solar movement, where residents are setting up their own backup or supplementary power with solar panels that plug into outlets. The movement is proceeding faster than standards can keep up.

> **译文：** 此外，还有阳台太阳能发电运动，居民们自行安装太阳能电池板，通过插座供电，作为备用或补充电源。这一运动的发展速度已经超过了现有标准的更新速度。

Fear of AI Bubble Could Pop The Bubble

By Kevin Xu • 5 Aug 2026

View in browser

View in browser

I have been paying attention to the nationwide AI data center moratoriums for more than a hot minute. I called out this risk in my 2025 annual letter . Our team (as in me and Claude) started tracking local moratoriums and built a public dashboard. When I shared the dashboard in this newsletter in April,  it was tracking 55 active moratoriums. Today, the dashboard is showing 183 with two statewide moratoriums or pauses – one in New York, one in the otherwise data center friendly state of Texas .

> **译文：** 我关注全国范围内的AI数据中心建设禁令已经有一段时间了。我在2025年的年度信函中就指出了这一风险。我们的团队（包括我和克劳德）开始追踪各地的禁令，并建立了一个公开的仪表盘。今年4月，我在本期简报中分享了这个仪表盘，当时它追踪到55项有效的禁令。而今天，仪表盘显示禁令数量已增至183项，其中包括两个州级禁令或暂停令——一个在纽约州，另一个在通常对数据中心建设较为友好的德克萨斯州。

Numbers and data visualizations are cool and all, but the human side of this story tends to get lost. That’s why I read with great interest Jasmine Sun's fantastic, tour-de-force of a reporting piece chronicling her travel through Wisconsin and Michigan to talk to various parties involved and invested in either side of this data center controversy.

> **译文：** 数字和数据可视化固然很酷，但故事中的人性一面往往被忽略。正因如此，我饶有兴趣地阅读了贾斯敏·孙（Jasmine Sun）那篇精彩绝伦、极具震撼力的报道。报道详细记录了她走访威斯康星州和密歇根州，采访数据中心争议各方人士的经历。

No Data Centers In My Backyard money, power, and populism in the AI buildout

> **译文：** 我家后院不要数据中心：人工智能建设中的金钱、权力和民粹主义

@jasmine’s substack

•Jasmine Sun

Her entire post is worth reading and re-reading. I learned a ton from it. One particular angle that I have not thought about, but was made clear as day from her conversations on the ground, is how aware the anti data center activists are of the precarious business models of all the major AI players. And if AI were a bubble, they don’t want to be stuck with a defunct, empty, or half-finished data center when the bubble pops. What these activists may or may not have known or intended is that slowing the data center buildout, and thus denying revenue for AI hyperscalers and frontier labs, could accelerate the bubble popping.

> **译文：** 她整篇文章都值得反复阅读。我从中受益匪浅。其中一个我之前从未想到的角度，在她与一线人员的交流中得到了清晰的阐述：反数据中心活动人士非常清楚所有主要人工智能企业岌岌可危的商业模式。如果人工智能是一个泡沫，那么当泡沫破裂时，他们肯定不希望自己被一个废弃、空置或半成品的数据中心所困扰。这些活动人士或许知道，或许不知道，或者有意为之，那就是减缓数据中心的建设速度，从而切断人工智能超大规模数据中心和前沿实验室的收入来源，可能会加速泡沫的破裂。

This passage  with activist Sarah Babbs of Saline, Michigan, drove this sentiment home:

> **译文：** 这段与密歇根州萨林市活动家莎拉·巴布斯的对话，更加深刻地表达了这种观点：

> “Sarah—like many others—is worried that AI is a bubble. ‘They’re not making money, their business model’s failing, their product isn’t even that good,’ she contended. In that world, could the AI companies still cover the cost of the grid expansion? Would they build the berms to hide the dirt piles? Would the buildings end up as stranded assets, abandoned like the dead factories, left as waste for her community to clean up? ‘It feels like supporting what happened right before the 2008 crash.’”

> **译文：** “萨拉和许多其他人一样，担心人工智能是个泡沫。‘他们赚不到钱，他们的商业模式失败了，他们的产品甚至都没那么好，’她这样说道。在这样的世界里，人工智能公司还能承担电网扩建的费用吗？他们会建造堤坝来掩盖土堆吗？这些建筑最终会不会像废弃的工厂一样，沦为搁浅资产，被遗弃，留给她的社区清理？‘这感觉就像在支持2008年金融危机前夕发生的事情。’”

This real fear to avoid holding the bag may become a self-fulfilling prophecy.

> **译文：** 这种害怕承担后果的恐惧可能会成为自我实现的预言。

From Cash to Debt

Although almost all the moratoriums are enacted on the local level, their collective effects could have national and even global repercussions at this delicate moment. The market is feeling increasingly less lenient about the massive data center capex, because more and more of it is being funded with debt, not cash.

> **译文：** 尽管几乎所有暂停令都是在地方层面实施的，但在当前这个微妙的时刻，它们的综合影响可能会波及全国乃至全球。市场对数据中心巨额资本支出越来越不宽容，因为越来越多的此类支出是通过债务而非现金融资的。

During the earlier phase of the buildout, call it 2023 to 2025, investors and the capital market at large generally accepted the already eye-popping investment numbers at the time. That’s because the money at least came from the cash generated by the big tech companies’ existing businesses to fund future growth with AI, however speculative.

> **译文：** 在早期建设阶段，也就是2023年至2025年期间，投资者和整个资本市场普遍接受了当时已经令人瞠目结舌的投资数字。这是因为这些资金至少来源于大型科技公司现有业务产生的现金流，用于支持未来人工智能领域的增长，尽管这种增长带有投机性。

That dynamic is starting to change.

Among four big tech companies – Meta, Google, Amazon, Oracle – their combined corporate bond issuances (aka debt) has increased from barely nothing three years ago, to more than $100 billion last year, to almost $200 billion year to date, mostly to fund more data centers. Meanwhile, the free cash flow that used to fund these investments are dwindling. And this is not including the long list of GPU neoclouds, all of which have done a fair amount of borrowing too.

> **译文：** 四大科技巨头——Meta、谷歌、亚马逊和甲骨文——的合计公司债券发行量（即债务）从三年前的几乎为零，增长到去年的超过1000亿美元，今年迄今为止更是接近2000亿美元，主要用于建设更多数据中心。与此同时，曾经用于这些投资的自由现金流却在不断减少。这还不包括数量庞大的GPU云平台，它们也都进行了相当规模的借贷。

This is happening not only because there is more building to be done to meet AI demand, but also that some of the hyperscalers are running out of free cash flow to fund it all. Google’s earnings announcement last week showed its quarterly free cash flow go negative  for the first time ever (!), since becoming a public company in 2004. Meta’s earnings report showed a more than 90% drop in free cash flow quarter to quarter, though still (barely) positive. Amazon and Microsoft’s reports threaded the needle perfectly in showing massive growth in its respective cloud divisions driven by AI, while still remaining healthily free cash flow positive and keeping pace with new data center investment.

> **译文：** 这种情况的出现不仅是因为需要建设更多数据中心来满足人工智能的需求，还因为一些超大规模数据中心运营商的自由现金流不足以支撑所有这些建设。谷歌上周发布的财报显示，自2004年上市以来，其季度自由现金流首次出现负值（！）。Meta的财报显示，其自由现金流环比下降超过90%，尽管仍然（勉强）为正。亚马逊和微软的财报则恰到好处地展现了各自云业务在人工智能驱动下的巨大增长，同时保持了健康的自由现金流，并与新的数据中心投资保持同步。

So it is not all debt and all bad news. Google Cloud is growing at an astonishing 80+% rate, so the borrowing and capex is (more likely than not) money well spent. Meta’s story is more precarious, but too early to tell.

> **译文：** 所以并非全是债务和坏消息。谷歌云正以惊人的80%以上的速度增长，因此借贷和资本支出（很可能）是物有所值的。Meta的情况则更为不稳定，但现在下结论还为时过早。

What is not too early to tell is that there will be more debt-fueled AI data centers in the future than in the past. The only way for the bubble to deflate gently, not pop violently, is if the AI-related revenue with a decent profit margin pours in faster than the debts are issued out.

> **译文：** 现在断言未来以债务驱动的人工智能数据中心数量将比过去更多还为时过早。要想让泡沫平稳破裂，而不是剧烈崩盘，唯一的办法就是确保利润可观的人工智能相关收入能够以超过债务发行速度的速度涌入。

This “thread the needle” moment is not supposed to happen two years ago, nor can it wait to happen two years into the future. It needs to happen now.

> **译文：** 这个“穿针引线”的时刻不应该发生在两年前，也不能等到两年后。它必须现在就发生。

Threading the Needle with Debt

It is hard enough to thread this needle and bridge this delicate transition from cash to debt to (high quality) revenue-funded AI buildout. A wave of data center moratoriums could lengthen the transition from debt to revenue, so more companies may have to borrow more or borrow for longer, even though the demand and revenue potential is evident. Amazon CEO, Andy Jassy, said on the company’s earnings call that:

> **译文：** 要巧妙地平衡从现金流到债务再到（高质量）营收驱动的人工智能建设这一微妙转变，已经非常困难。数据中心建设暂停令的浪潮可能会延长从债务到营收的过渡期，因此，即使需求和营收潜力显而易见，更多公司可能不得不增加借贷或延长借贷期限。亚马逊首席执行官安迪·杰西在公司财报电话会议上表示：

> “Once a data center opens with servers plugged in, we start generating significant revenue right away and then get to monetize these data centers for 30-plus years without having to spend that start-up capital again.”

> **译文：** “一旦数据中心投入使用并接入服务器，我们就能立即开始产生可观的收入，然后就可以在 30 多年内持续从这些数据中心获利，而无需再次投入启动资金。”

These chips, servers, and networking equipment are much more flexible, with a better-oiled supply chain, and operate on a shorter time frame. With AI demand still strong, these equipment can generate revenue from the second they are turned on. But they need a building – a large, concrete, powered-up, kind of noisy, and kind of ugly warehouse of a building – to be turned on! And the local activists, like Sarah of Saline, who don’t want to be saddled with the aftermath of a debt-ridden bubble may end up causing it, by fighting against this building being built in the first place.

> **译文：** 这些芯片、服务器和网络设备更加灵活，供应链运转更顺畅，运行周期也更短。人工智能需求依然强劲，这些设备一开通就能立即产生收益。但它们需要一座厂房——一座大型的、混凝土结构的、通电的、噪音不小、外观也略显丑陋的仓库式建筑——才能投入使用！而像萨林镇的莎拉这样的当地活动人士，不愿承担债务泡沫的后果，她们反对建造这座厂房，最终可能会反而加剧泡沫的产生。

There is nothing wrong with borrowing debt to invest, as long as the investment pays back before the debt comes due. And there is every indication from the hyperscalers’ earnings reports that investments in AI are paying back. But if the payback period of capital expenditure gets lengthened by local politics, while the cost of borrowing is going up (30-year treasury yield has been more than 5% for more than a month), the debt burden may end up popping the bubble just as the needle was about to be threaded.

> **译文：** 借贷投资本身并没有错，只要投资能在债务到期前收回成本即可。超大规模数据中心运营商的财报也充分表明，人工智能领域的投资正在获得回报。但是，如果地方政治因素导致资本支出回收期延长，而借贷成本却不断攀升（30年期国债收益率已连续一个月超过5%），那么沉重的债务负担最终可能会在泡沫即将破裂之际将其扼杀。

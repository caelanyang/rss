Over the last year, we’ve seen more generalist venture capital firms make forays into defense tech, a niche once relegated to specialized investors comfortable with revenue based on uneven government contracts and who weren’t restricted from investing in weapons makers by their limited partners.

> **译文：**过去一年，我们看到更多综合性风险投资公司涉足国防科技领域，这一领域曾经仅限于那些乐于接受基于不稳定的政府合同的收入，且不受有限合伙人限制而投资武器制造商的专业投资者。

Furientis, a startup founded last year that builds missiles for intercepting attacks, is close to raising $25 million in a funding round that would value it more than $125 million including the money invested, two people familiar with the investment tell us.

> **译文：**据两位知情人士透露，去年成立的初创公司 Furientis 正在筹备一轮 2500 万美元的融资，该公司致力于制造用于拦截攻击的导弹，此次融资将使其估值超过 1.25 亿美元（包括已投资的资金）。

And we’re hearing Benchmark, known for backing Uber and Snap in their early days, has been in talks to lead, the people said.

> **译文：**据知情人士透露，曾投资 Uber 和 Snap 等公司的 Benchmark 正在洽谈领投事宜。

Furientis would stand out as a Benchmark investment.

> **译文：**Furientis 将成为一项值得关注的投资。

So far this year, the venture firm has led investments in AI agent startup Gumloop and sales platform Monaco, the type of asset-light software investments that have been the firm’s bread and butter over decades.

> **译文：**今年到目前为止，这家风险投资公司已经领投了人工智能代理初创公司 Gumloop 和销售平台 Monaco，这类轻资产软件投资一直是该公司几十年来赖以生存的根本。

That said, it also led a $170 million round for the space data center company Starcloud earlier this year.

> **译文：**尽管如此，该公司今年早些时候还领投了太空数据中心公司 Starcloud 的 1.7 亿美元融资。

And it bought $250 million in shares in late 2025 and early 2026 in Cerebras, the AI chips designer that went public earlier this year.

> **译文：**该公司在 2025 年末和 2026 年初购买了价值 2.5 亿美元的 Cerebras 股票，Cerebras 是一家人工智能芯片设计公司，于今年早些时候上市。

Benchmark and Furientis declined to comment.

> **译文：**Benchmark和Furientis均拒绝置评。

Details of the round could change.

If it goes ahead with the investment, Benchmark would be joining an increasingly crowded field of VC firms backing defense tech.

> **译文：**如果这项投资最终达成，Benchmark 将加入到日益拥挤的、投资国防科技的风险投资公司行列中。

The commercial success of SpaceX and Anduril, and a defense-tech friendly Trump administration, have attracted more investors to these startups.

> **译文：**SpaceX 和 Anduril 的商业成功，以及特朗普政府对国防科技的友好态度，吸引了更多投资者关注这些初创公司。

Ribbit Capital, the Micky Malka-founded firm that once hewed to a fintech focus, recently co-led a $300 million round for another missile startup, Mach Industries.

> **译文：**由米奇·马尔卡 (Micky Malka) 创立的 Ribbit Capital 公司，曾专注于金融科技领域，最近与他人共同领投了另一家导弹初创公司 Mach Industries 的 3 亿美元融资。

Kleiner Perkins, known for investments in Amazon and Google, earlier this year backed autonomous ship builder Saronic Technologies, leading its Series D at a $9.25 billion post-money valuation.

> **译文：**以投资亚马逊和谷歌而闻名的凯鹏华盈（Kleiner Perkins）今年早些时候投资了无人船舶制造商萨罗尼克科技（Saronic Technologies），领投了其 D 轮融资，融资后估值达 92.5 亿美元。

And OpenAI investor Thrive Capital has backed multiple rounds in Palmer Luckey’s Anduril Industries, recently co-leading its Series H funding round at a $61 billion post-money valuation.

> **译文：**OpenAI 的投资者 Thrive Capital 已多次投资 Palmer Luckey 的 Anduril Industries，最近还共同领投了其 H 轮融资，投后估值达 610 亿美元。

Some traditional venture capital firms have in the past shied away from defense tech not only because of the high costs of building and running hardware businesses, but also because selling to one buyer (the government) doesn’t scale in the way traditional VC businesses have.

> **译文：**过去，一些传统的风险投资公司一直对国防科技避而远之，不仅是因为建立和运营硬件业务的成本很高，还因为向单一买家（政府）销售产品无法像传统风险投资公司那样实现规模化。

But the war with Iran has depleted American missile supplies, making novel solutions all the more appealing to the U.S. government.

> **译文：**但与伊朗的战争耗尽了美国的导弹储备，使得新的解决方案对美国政府更具吸引力。

The Pentagon recently allocated $53 billion to missile and rocket production and is also attempting to simplify the procurement process, which might accommodate startups selling into the government.

> **译文：**五角大楼最近拨款 530 亿美元用于导弹和火箭生产，并且正在努力简化采购流程，这可能会有利于初创公司向政府销售产品。

The El Segundo, Calif., Furientis aims to solve the supply crunch by making missile interceptors en masse and for a lot less than legacy defence contractors.

> **译文：**位于加州埃尔塞贡多的 Furientis 公司旨在通过大规模生产导弹拦截器来解决供应短缺问题，而且成本远低于传统国防承包商。

Co-founder and CEO Brody Franzen, who previously worked at Virgin Galactic and the missile startup Castelion, told Fortune earlier this year that Furientis is using commercial off-the-shelf components and automotive-style assembly processes to keep costs to $250,000 per missile, compared to the $1 million to $5 million cost of its legacy competitors.

> **译文：**联合创始人兼首席执行官布罗迪·弗兰岑（Brody Franzen）曾就职于维珍银河公司和导弹初创公司Castelion，他今年早些时候告诉《财富》杂志，Furientis公司正在使用商用现成组件和汽车式装配工艺，将每枚导弹的成本控制在25万美元以内，而其传统竞争对手的成本则在100万美元到500万美元之间。

Silent Ventures, a solo GP firm led by defense tech investor Jackson Moses, led Furientis’s $5 million pre-seed round in May.

> **译文：**由国防科技投资者杰克逊·摩西 (Jackson Moses) 领导的独资普通合伙人公司 Silent Ventures 在 5 月份领投了 Furientis 的 500 万美元种子前融资。

Bessemer Ventures, Multiball Capital and SV Angel also invested.

> **译文：**Bessemer Ventures、Multiball Capital 和 SV Angel 也进行了投资。

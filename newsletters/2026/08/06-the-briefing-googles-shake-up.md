Greetings!A flood of June-quarter earnings updates—from the likes of Uber, DoorDash, Shopify and Disney—was interrupted on Thursday by Google’s announcement that its AI guru, Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis, had been elevated to chair of the AI unit and chief scientist of Google’s parent company, Alphabet.

> **译文：** 大家好！周四，Uber、DoorDash、Shopify 和迪士尼等公司纷纷发布了 6 月份季度的盈利报告，但随后谷歌宣布，其人工智能专家、谷歌 DeepMind 首席执行官 Demis Hassabis 已被提升为人工智能部门主席兼谷歌母公司 Alphabet 的首席科学家，这一消息打断了大部分财报发布。

That news coincided with the revelation that several others from DeepMind, including Chief Scientist Jeff Dean, were leaving to start their own firm.

> **译文：** 与此同时，DeepMind 的几位高管，包括首席科学家 Jeff Dean，也宣布离职创办自己的公司。

Some interpreted the shake-ups as bad news for Google, whose stock fell 4%.

> **译文：** 一些人认为这些变动对谷歌来说是坏消息，谷歌股价下跌了 4%。

In reality, today’s news doesn’t mean much.

Hassabis wasn’t the one running DeepMind on a day-to-day basis.

> **译文：** Hassabis 并不是 DeepMind 的日常运营负责人。

That was really Koray Kavukcuoglu, DeepMind's Chief Technology Officer, who is now formally taking the reins of Google DeepMind. (For more on Kavukcuoglu, see this profile we published a year ago.) Hassabis, in contrast, is the public face of Google DeepMind as well as a visionary scientist, roles he will continue to perform (he’s a Nobel Prize winner, don’t forget).

> **译文：** 那其实是DeepMind首席技术官Koray Kavukcuoglu，他现在正式接管了谷歌DeepMind。（关于Kavukcuoglu的更多信息，请参阅我们一年前发布的这篇人物简介。）相比之下，Hassabis是谷歌DeepMind的公众形象代表，同时也是一位富有远见的科学家，他将继续扮演这些角色（别忘了，他可是诺贝尔奖得主）。

As for Dean, my colleague Erin Woo tells me the chatter internally is that Dean had become less central to Google’s Gemini efforts, even if he remains revered by employees.

> **译文：** 至于迪恩，我的同事艾琳·吴告诉我，内部传言说，迪恩在谷歌的 Gemini 项目中已经不再那么重要了，尽管他仍然受到员工的尊敬。

The real question about today’s shake-up is whether it changes the list of possible successors to Sundar Pichai, CEO of both Google and Alphabet.

> **译文：** 今天这次人事变动的真正问题是，它是否会改变谷歌和Alphabet首席执行官桑达尔·皮查伊的继任者名单。

Hassabis has been on that list—at least in the minds of smart outsiders—as has Google Cloud chief Thomas Kurian.

> **译文：** 哈萨比斯一直名列其中——至少在一些聪明的局外人看来是这样——谷歌云首席执行官托马斯·库里安也位列其中。

You might say that if Hassabis prefers the role of scientist to that of divisional CEO, he’d hardly want to be Alphabet’s CEO.

> **译文：** 你可能会说，如果哈萨比斯更喜欢科学家的角色而不是部门首席执行官的角色，那么他肯定不想成为 Alphabet 的首席执行官。

He will continue to run Isomorphic Labs, Alphabet’s drug discovery unit, but that’s consistent with the idea that he wants to be a scientist.

> **译文：** 他将继续运营 Alphabet 旗下的药物研发部门 Isomorphic Labs，但这与他想成为一名科学家的想法是一致的。

In other words, today’s news is good for Kurian’s chances.

> **译文：** 换句话说，今天的消息对库里安的胜算有利。

That said, the question of Pichai’s successor isn’t one that has to be decided any time soon.

> **译文：** 也就是说，皮查伊的继任者问题并不需要很快决定。

Pichai is riding high right now.

While he has lately faced investor pressure on Google’s aggressive capital expenditure plans, and questions about its latest AI models have circulated, those are short-term quibbles.

> **译文：** 虽然他最近面临着投资者对谷歌激进的资本支出计划的压力，而且对其最新的人工智能模型也存在疑问，但这些都是短期问题。

Stepping back, Pichai has answered his critics by demonstrating that Google can be a leader in AI technology.

> **译文：** 退一步讲，皮查伊用实际行动回应了批评者，证明谷歌可以在人工智能技术领域成为领导者。

He’s also rebutted worries that AI would undermine its search cash cow, and meantime cloud is soaring.

> **译文：** 他还反驳了人工智能会削弱其搜索业务这一摇钱树的担忧，与此同时，云计算业务正在蓬勃发展。

Pichai is in his early 50s, which means he could be in the job for years to come.

> **译文：** 皮查伊今年五十出头，这意味着他可能还会继续担任这个职位很多年。

But company boards always need options.

Some might argue that Hassabis could still be in the running given that the chief of a company as big as Google doesn’t necessarily make daily operational decisions.

> **译文：** 有人可能会认为，鉴于像谷歌这样的大公司的首席执行官不一定参与日常运营决策，哈萨比斯仍然有可能成为最终人选。

The CEO’s job is to plot the company’s course for the future—to ensure it has a future.

> **译文：** 首席执行官的职责是为公司的未来发展规划路线，确保公司拥有未来。

But the CEO still has to get their hands dirty in operational decisions.

> **译文：** 但首席执行官仍然需要亲力亲为地参与运营决策。

Other parts of the job are not exactly fun—remember Pichai’s appearance before Congress a few years ago?

> **译文：** 这份工作的其他部分并不那么有趣——还记得几年前皮查伊在国会作证的情景吗？

For someone who’s more interested in “actively shaping the future” of AI, as Pichai said of Hassabis today, the CEO job is likely not in his future.

> **译文：** 皮查伊今天谈到哈萨比斯时说，哈萨比斯更热衷于“积极塑造人工智能的未来”，那么CEO的职位可能不在他的未来计划之内。

Maybe he wants to win a second Nobel Prize instead.

> **译文：** 或许他更想赢得第二个诺贝尔奖。

Figma’s SlumpUh-oh.

Shares of design firm Figma dropped 15% in after-hours trading on Wednesday, after the company projected a sharp slowdown in revenue growth and a lower profit margin for the third quarter and announced that a couple of longtime executives were leaving.

> **译文：** 设计公司 Figma 的股价在周三盘后交易中下跌了 15%，此前该公司预计第三季度收入增长将大幅放缓，利润率也将下降，并宣布几位长期高管即将离职。

After reporting 48% growth in the second quarter, 2 percentage points faster than in the first quarter, Figma projected growth of 36% for the third quarter.

> **译文：** Figma 在第二季度实现了 48% 的增长，比第一季度增长了 2 个百分点，并预测第三季度将增长 36%。

That’s still strong, but a 12 percentage point slowdown is not to be ignored—particularly at a time when AI-fueled competitors are appearing everywhere (including AI powerhouses like Anthropic).

> **译文：** 虽然增速依然强劲，但 12 个百分点的放缓不容忽视——尤其是在人工智能驱动的竞争对手层出不穷（包括像 Anthropic 这样的人工智能巨头）的当下。

Moreover, Figma projected an operating profit margin of 9% for the full year, compared to 13% for the first six months of the year.

> **译文：** 此外，Figma 预计全年营业利润率为 9%，而上半年营业利润率为 13%。

Figma’s explanation focused on the projected profit margin: The company is investing.

> **译文：** Figma 的解释侧重于预计利润率：该公司正在进行投资。

It has created new products it is still testing and isn’t requiring customers to spend money on.

> **译文：** 该公司已经研发出一些新产品，目前仍在测试阶段，并不要求客户花钱购买。

So Figma will have to see how those products mature before it makes them “generally available.” Investors weren’t persuaded.

> **译文：** 因此，Figma需要观察这些产品的发展成熟情况，才能决定是否将其“全面推出”。投资者们对此并不买账。

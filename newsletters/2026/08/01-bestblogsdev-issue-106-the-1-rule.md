Hey there! Welcome to BestBlogs.dev Issue #106.

Jeff Dean offers a useful way to read this week's AI news: the 1% rule. Model capability can get surprisingly close to useful, surprisingly fast. What determines whether a product actually works is the remaining layer of specifications, context, tools, memory, evaluation, orchestration, and operational judgment. That last one percent is not a small finishing pass. It contains most of the difficulty between an impressive demonstration and a system people can depend on.


> **译文：** 杰夫·迪恩提供了一种解读本周人工智能新闻的有效方法：1%法则。模型能力可以以惊人的速度接近实用水平。真正决定产品能否有效运作的，是剩余的规范、上下文、工具、内存、评估、编排和操作判断等环节。这最后的1%并非无关紧要的收尾工作，它包含了从令人印象深刻的演示到人们可以信赖的系统之间的大部分难点。

The 20 selections in this issue follow that thread from several directions. GPT-5.6, Kimi K3, and Gemini Robotics push capability and efficiency forward. Claude Code, MCP, skills, and WorkBuddy bring the focus back to harnesses, verification, and organizational knowledge. A set of long interviews then asks what happens to infrastructure, products, work, and human agency when intelligence becomes more abundant.


> **译文：** 本期精选的20篇文章从多个角度探讨了这一主题。GPT-5.6、Kimi K3和Gemini Robotics推动了能力和效率的提升。Claude Code、MCP、技能和WorkBuddy则将焦点重新聚焦于工具、验证和组织知识。一系列深度访谈探讨了当智能变得更加丰富时，基础设施、产品、工作和人类自主性将会发生怎样的变化。

Here are 10 highlights worth your attention this week:


> **译文：** 以下是本周值得您关注的10个亮点：

🧩 In a Y Combinator conversation, Jeff Dean argues that the model is only one part of an AI system. Retrieval, memory, tools, context, evaluation, and orchestration determine whether an agent can complete a real task rather than produce a plausible response. His account of the TPU's origin also shows why simple first-principles estimates can reveal bottlenecks before leaderboard chasing does. The final 1% is not polish. It is where the product becomes real.


> **译文：** 🧩 在与 Y Combinator 的一次对话中，杰夫·迪恩 (Jeff Dean) 指出，模型只是人工智能系统的一部分。检索、记忆、工具、上下文、评估和协调等因素决定了智能体能否完成真正的任务，而不仅仅是产生一个看似合理的响应。他对 TPU 起源的阐述也解释了为什么简单的基于第一性原理的估算能够在追逐排行榜之前就揭示出瓶颈。最后的 1% 并非润色，而是产品真正发挥作用的关键所在。

⚡ OpenAI reports that inference-kernel work for GPT-5.6 Sol reduced serving costs by as much as 20%, while improved speculative decoding increased token-generation efficiency by more than 15%. ByteByteGo then decomposes an agent request into orchestration, API, and inference layers, showing where persistent WebSockets, stable prompt prefixes, deferred tool discovery, and Code Mode remove repeated work. The metric that matters is shifting from price per token to cost per successful task.


> **译文：** ⚡ OpenAI 报告称，GPT-5.6 Sol 的推理内核工作量减少了高达 20% 的服务成本，而改进的推测性解码则将 token 生成效率提高了 15% 以上。ByteByteGo 随后将代理请求分解为编排层、API 层和推理层，展示了持久 WebSocket、稳定的提示前缀、延迟工具发现和代码模式如何减少重复工作。关键指标正从每个 token 的价格转变为每次成功任务的成本。

🧠 Kimi K3 has 2.78 trillion total parameters and activates roughly 104.2 billion per token. Boyang reads the technical report through LatentMoE, KDA linear attention, context parallelism, and load-aware scheduling to show how the model scales without allowing communication costs to explode. The best thing about this analysis is that it makes a 3T-class model less magical: scale becomes a set of engineering choices that can be understood, reused, tested, and challenged.


> **译文：** 🧠 Kimi K3 总共有 2.78 万亿个参数，每个代币大约激活 1042 亿个参数。Boyang 通过 LatentMoE、KDA 线性注意力机制、上下文并行和负载感知调度等技术文档，展示了该模型如何在不增加通信成本的情况下实现扩展。这项分析最精彩之处在于，它让 3T 级模型不再那么神秘：扩展性变成了一系列可以理解、复用、测试和验证的工程选择。

🤖 Gemini Robotics 2 brings vision, language, and action into whole-body control, dexterous manipulation, and multi-robot collaboration. Anthropic's Drone-Bench supplies an important boundary condition: models are getting better at autonomous drone control, yet reliable 3D reconstruction from visual input remains a major bottleneck. Calling hardware is not the same as understanding the physical world, and the distance between a controlled demonstration and a dependable system is still consequential.


> **译文：** 🤖 Gemini Robotics 2 将视觉、语言和动作融入全身控制、灵巧操作和多机器人协作。Anthropic 的 Drone-Bench 提供了一个重要的边界条件：模型在自主无人机控制方面不断进步，但从视觉输入中可靠地重建 3D 模型仍然是一个主要瓶颈。调用硬件并不等同于理解物理世界，受控演示与可靠系统之间的差距仍然很大。

🔍 Karthika Raghavan's field guide to LLM-as-a-Judge moves from foundational papers and production use to systematic bias, domain collapse, prompt injection, and deliberate attacks. Asking another model to assign a score does not automatically create a trustworthy evaluation. Position, verbosity, confidence, familiar phrasing, and adversarial instructions can all move the result. Verification loops need verification of their own, a lesson worth applying before trusting any polished agent leaderboard.


> **译文：** 🔍 Karthika Raghavan 的《LLM 作为评判者的实战指南》从基础论文和生产应用入手，深入探讨了系统性偏差、领域崩溃、提示注入和蓄意攻击等问题。让另一个模型给出分数并不能自动产生可信的评估结果。位置、冗长程度、置信度、熟悉的措辞以及对抗性指令都会影响结果。验证循环本身也需要验证，在信任任何精心打造的智能体排行榜之前，都应该牢记这一点。

💻 Claude Code creator Boris Cherny explains that the team reruns prompt ablations as models change, adding context, tools, or instructions only when difficult real tasks reveal repeated failures. An independent Agent engineering study makes the broader distinction that answering is not the same as taking responsibility. A field note on the “orchestrator tax” adds a practical warning: once raw transcripts and duplicated orientation flow back into the main context, they continue degrading the orchestrator's working memory long after their immediate token cost is paid.


> **译文：** 💻 Claude Code 的创建者 Boris Cherny 解释说，随着模型的变化，团队会重新运行提示消融测试，只有在实际任务中遇到反复失败时才会添加上下文、工具或指令。一项独立的代理工程研究提出了更广泛的区分：回答问题并不等同于承担责任。一篇关于“协调器税”的实地考察报告补充了一个实际的警告：一旦原始记录和重复的方向信息回流到主上下文中，即使它们最初的代币成本已经支付完毕，它们仍会持续消耗协调器的工作内存很长时间。

🛠️ FactSet principal AI engineer Yogendra Miraje treats skills as product features: prompts establish who an agent is, tools establish what it can connect to, and skills establish how a task is completed. MCP 2026-07-28 advances the connectivity layer with a stateless core, versioned extensions, and production-oriented authorization. Agent capabilities can multiply quickly, but the harness, selective discovery, evaluation, permissions, and governance need to mature before that growth remains manageable.


> **译文：** 🛠️ FactSet首席人工智能工程师Yogendra Miraje将技能视为产品特性：提示信息确定代理的身份，工具确定其连接范围，而技能则确定任务的完成方式。MCP 2026-07-28通过无状态核心、版本化扩展和面向生产的授权机制，进一步完善了连接层。代理的功能可以迅速增长，但在此之前，相关的资源管理、选择性发现、评估、权限控制和治理机制需要更加成熟，才能确保这种增长可控。

📚 Much of the last one percent for enterprise agents lives in organizational knowledge. An Alibaba Technology practice divides that knowledge into business intent, architecture, individual systems, and infrastructure. WorkBuddy strategy product manager Anne explains productization through model, context, harness, and loop. A separate personal engineering reflection warns about cognitive debt and intent debt: faster code production does not mean a team still understands the theory of its own system or can safely participate in future decisions.


> **译文：** 📚 企业代理的最后1%效率很大程度上取决于组织知识。阿里巴巴技术实践将这些知识划分为业务意图、架构、独立系统和基础设施。WorkBuddy战略产品经理Anne通过模型、上下文、工具和循环来解释产品化。另一篇个人工程反思文章则警示人们注意认知债务和意图债务：更快的代码生成速度并不意味着团队仍然理解自身系统的理论，或者能够安全地参与未来的决策。

🌐 Sam Altman describes intelligence as infrastructure with implications for chips, energy, data centers, and human agency. In The Economist's interview, Elon Musk imagines physical AI as a route to material abundance while acknowledging tensions around safety, concentrated power, and human control. Tencent Research Institute fellow Li Hongsheng offers a useful counterweight through industrial-evolution frameworks: models, applications, organizations, and infrastructure move on different time scales, so a sweeping narrative cannot replace a grounded judgment about the current stage.


> **译文：** 🌐 山姆·奥特曼将智能描述为一种基础设施，其影响涵盖芯片、能源、数据中心和人类行为。在《经济学人》的采访中，埃隆·马斯克设想物理人工智能是通往物质富足的途径，同时也承认围绕安全、权力集中和人类控制等方面的紧张局势。腾讯研究院研究员李洪生通过产业演化框架提供了一个有益的平衡视角：模型、应用、组织和基础设施的发展遵循不同的时间尺度，因此，笼统的叙述不能取代对当前阶段的客观判断。

🎯 Anthropic product leader Diane Penn says evals are the new PRDs because they translate ambiguous needs into signals that research, engineering, and product can test together. Zhang Xiaojun's three-hour interview with vLLM core maintainer Kaichao You places model architecture, inference engines, harnesses, electricity, open-source governance, and company building inside the same co-design problem. Whether the work is product or infrastructure, the durable advantage comes from taking responsibility for outcomes over time.


> **译文：** 🎯 Anthropic 产品负责人 Diane Penn 表示，评估是新的产品需求文档 (PRD)，因为它们能将模糊的需求转化为研究、工程和产品团队可以共同测试的信号。张晓军与 vLLM 核心维护者尤凯超进行了长达三小时的访谈，访谈中，模型架构、推理引擎、框架、电力、开源治理和公司建设等诸多因素都被纳入到同一个协同设计问题中。无论工作内容是产品还是基础设施，持久的优势都来自于对长期结果负责。

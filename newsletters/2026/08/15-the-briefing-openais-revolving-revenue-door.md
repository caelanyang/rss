It may be the dog days of summer, but the biggest AI labs are breaking up the monotony with a few executive changes.

> **译文：** 虽然正值盛夏酷暑，但最大的几家人工智能实验室正在通过一些人事变动来打破这种单调乏味的局面。

The latest is OpenAI Chief Revenue Officer Denise Dresser.

> **译文：** 最新一位是 OpenAI 首席营收官 Denise Dresser。

The ChatGPT maker said Thursday she would leave the firm after just eight months on the job.

> **译文：** ChatGPT 的创始人周四表示，她将在上任仅八个月后离开公司。

In her place, OpenAI has hired Dali Rajic, formerly president and chief operating officer of Wiz, the Israel-founded cybersecurity company Google bought earlier this year for $32 billion.

> **译文：** OpenAI 聘请了 Dali Rajic 来接替她，Dali Rajic 曾担任 Wiz 的总裁兼首席运营官，Wiz 是一家以色列网络安全公司，今年早些时候被谷歌以 320 亿美元收购。

The hiring underscores just how much cybersecurity has emerged as a key selling point for AI companies.

> **译文：** 此次招聘凸显了网络安全已成为人工智能公司重要的卖点。

At Wiz, which sold cloud security software, Rajic is credited with personally closing eight-figure deals with big businesses.

> **译文：** 在销售云安全软件的 Wiz 公司，Rajic 因亲自与大企业达成数额达八位数的交易而备受赞誉。

He oversaw annualized revenue growth from around $200 million when he joined the company in 2024 to nearly $2 billion today, according to a person with knowledge of the figures.

> **译文：** 据知情人士透露，在他2024年加入公司时，公司年收入约为2亿美元，而如今已接近20亿美元。

Before joining Wiz, Rajic served as an executive at cybersecurity firm Zscaler and several other software firms, standing out among the startups’ T-shirt–wearing tech staff with his button-down shirts.“It’s a very interesting move on [OpenAI’s] behalf.

> **译文：** 在加入 Wiz 之前，Rajic 曾在网络安全公司 Zscaler 和其他几家软件公司担任高管，他身穿纽扣衬衫，在那些穿着 T 恤的初创公司技术人员中显得格外引人注目。“这对 [OpenAI] 来说是一个非常有趣的举措。

It would be an easier decision for them to grab somebody from Oracle or Salesforce or ServiceNow, but taking someone from cybersecurity…that’s a recognition that cybersecurity isn’t a niche, it’s breaking into the mainstream,” said Cyberstarts founder Gili Raanan, who wrote Wiz’s seed check and previously served on its board.

> **译文：** Cyberstarts 的创始人 Gili Raanan 表示：“对他们来说，从 Oracle、Salesforce 或 ServiceNow 挖人会更容易，但从网络安全领域挖人……这表明网络安全不再是小众领域，它正在进入主流。” Gili Raanan 曾为 Wiz 开出种子轮融资支票，并曾在 Wiz 的董事会任职。

OpenAI is racing with Anthropic to release products that address the threat of havoc AI introduces.

> **译文：** OpenAI 正与 Anthropico 竞相推出产品，以应对人工智能带来的破坏性威胁。

Anthropic’s Mythos model dominated the news cycle for several weeks this spring after Anthropic said Mythos was so powerful it could pose a devastating threat to the world’s biggest financial institutions and government agencies.

> **译文：** 今年春天，Anthropic 公司的 Mythos 模型在新闻界占据了数周的头条，此前该公司表示 Mythos 模型威力强大，可能对全球最大的金融机构和政府机构构成毁灭性威胁。

OpenAI has said its own models could pose similar threats and recently disclosed how several models broke containment during testing and hacked other companies.

> **译文：** OpenAI 曾表示，其自身的模型也可能构成类似的威胁，并且最近披露了几个模型在测试期间如何突破隔离并入侵其他公司。

It recently launched cybersecurity agents that use its AI models to scan companies’ code for vulnerabilities and suggest ways to patch them.

> **译文：** 该公司最近推出了网络安全代理，利用其人工智能模型扫描公司代码中的漏洞，并提出修复方法。

Rajic’s purview will extend well beyond cybersecurity, as did his predecessor’s.

> **译文：** 拉吉克的职责范围将远远超出网络安全领域，就像他的前任一样。

As we detailed in this May profile of Dresser, the former Slack and Salesforce executive was hired to make OpenAI a more serious contender for the IT budgets Anthropic had been mopping up. “When I joined OpenAI, it became immediately clear that we needed someone of her caliber to lead our go-to-market efforts,” former OpenAI top executive Fidji Simo, who hired her, told us in May.

> **译文：** 正如我们在五月份对德雷塞尔的专访中所详述的那样，这位前Slack和Salesforce高管受聘于OpenAI，旨在使OpenAI在与Anthropic争夺IT预算方面更具竞争力，而Anthropic此前一直在蚕食这些预算。“我加入OpenAI后，立刻意识到我们需要像她这样的人才来领导我们的市场拓展工作，”聘用她的前OpenAI高管菲吉·西莫在五月份告诉我们。

Dresser set out to do that via a punishing schedule of client meetings around the world, as well as behind-the-scenes efforts to reorganize OpenAI’s fast-growing salesforce.

> **译文：** 为了实现这一目标，德雷塞尔制定了严格的全球客户会议计划，并在幕后努力重组 OpenAI 快速增长的销售团队。

OpenAI’s sales team grew threefold under her leadership.

> **译文：** 在她的领导下，OpenAI的销售团队规模扩大了三倍。

She hired at least six senior leaders, including some from Salesforce and Google.

> **译文：** 她至少聘请了六位高级领导，其中包括来自 Salesforce 和 Google 的一些高管。

Many more from those and other big tech companies joined them.

> **译文：** 来自这些公司和其他大型科技公司的许多员工也加入了他们。

On Thursday, OpenAI employees who worked with Dresser posted on LinkedIn that they would deeply miss her.

> **译文：** 周四，曾与 Dresser 共事的 OpenAI 员工在 LinkedIn 上发帖表示，他们将深深怀念她。

One person who had worked with her questioned whether she was given the resources and time to succeed in her position.

> **译文：** 一位曾与她共事的人质疑，她是否拥有足够的资源和时间来胜任她的职位。

OpenAI maintains that the enterprise business is doing swimmingly: It’s on track to hit a target of deriving 50% of its revenue from enterprise customers, up from 40% at the beginning of the year.

> **译文：** OpenAI 坚称其企业业务发展势头良好：有望实现 50% 的收入来自企业客户的目标，高于年初的 40%。

And its annualized revenue from business customers rose 32% in July from June, though it hasn’t disclosed the level.

> **译文：** 该公司7月份来自企业客户的年度收入比6月份增长了32%，但该公司尚未披露具体数额。

Still, it won’t be lost on OpenAI’s customers—̉and its rivals—that the highest-profile people spearheading these efforts have left the company recently.

> **译文：** 不过，OpenAI 的客户——以及它的竞争对手——都不会忽略这样一个事实：领导这些努力的最高级别人物最近已经离开了公司。

Brad Lightcap, who started the year trying to woo clients with Dresser at the World Economic Forum and more recently had been running an effort to integrate AI with private equity firms’ clients, announced his resignation this week.

> **译文：** 布拉德·莱特卡普 (Brad Lightcap) 今年年初曾试图在世界经济论坛上代表 Dresser 公司争取客户，最近又致力于将人工智能与私募股权公司的客户相结合，他于本周宣布辞职。

And last month Simo, who had been on medical leave, stepped down from her position as CEO of AGI deployment to act as a part-time adviser. — Stephanie Palazzolo contributed to this report.

> **译文：** 上个月，此前因病休假的西莫辞去了AGI部署公司首席执行官的职务，转而担任兼职顾问。——斯蒂芬妮·帕拉佐洛对本报道亦有贡献。

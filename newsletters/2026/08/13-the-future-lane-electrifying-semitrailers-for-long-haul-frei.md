Companies are racing to put battery power directly into the trailer itself. It’s a clever workaround for one of trucking’s toughest electrification problems. To understand it, I did some old-fashioned reporting on the companies building this technology. I looked at how Nivalis, TIP Group, BPW, Trailer Dynamics, ZF, and Range Energy are each approaching the challenge.

> **译文：** 各公司正竞相将电池直接集成到拖车中。这是一种巧妙的变通方案，可以解决卡车运输业电气化面临的最棘手问题之一。为了深入了解这一方案，我采取了一些传统的调研方式，考察了研发这项技术的公司。我研究了Nivalis、TIP Group、BPW、Trailer Dynamics、ZF和Range Energy等公司各自如何应对这一挑战。

Their electric drive axles use regenerative braking to recapture energy, especially on downhill grades, while adding propulsion the tractor doesn’t have to supply. Nivalis’s kit also plugs into any commercial outlet for opportunistic charging. Its battery draws additional power from solar panels mounted on the trailer’s roof. What I found is that these trailers could ease the biggest barrier to electrifying trucking: sparse charging infrastructure on long-haul routes.

> **译文：** 它们的电动驱动桥利用再生制动回收能量，尤其是在下坡路段，同时还能提供牵引车无需提供的额外动力。Nivalis 的这套设备还可以插入任何商用电源插座进行即时充电。其电池还能从安装在拖车车顶的太阳能电池板获取额外电力。我发现，这些拖车有望缓解卡车电气化的最大障碍：长途运输路线上充电基础设施的匮乏。

They could also help fleets meet increasingly stringent carbon emissions laws. In an ongoing Nivalis pilot, partners say the powered axle could save 7,000 liters of diesel per truck annually. It’s a small shift in engineering with outsized implications for how freight moves. Full details are in the article, linked below.

> **译文：** 它们还能帮助车队满足日益严格的碳排放法规。在Nivalis正在进行的一项试点项目中，合作伙伴表示，动力轴每年可为每辆卡车节省7000升柴油。这虽是一项小小的工程改进，却对货运方式产生了深远的影响。详情请见下方链接的文章。

Inside the Race to Electrify Semitrailers for Long-Haul Freight

> **译文：** 深入了解长途货运半挂车电气化竞赛

European pilot joins the quest to electrify trailers.

> **译文：** 欧洲试点项目加入拖车电气化进程。

Electrified trailers are emerging as a serious freight-decarbonization bet, with Nivalis, and competitors in Europe and North America are racing to retrofit trailers rather than tractor units. Nivalis's new Sommer fleet pilot , developed with BPW and TIP Group, is adding fresh data to a crowded field still short on published payback figures. Fleet operators want proof that projected savings hold up in real-world commercial operation.

> **译文：** 电动拖车正逐渐成为货运脱碳的重要选择，Nivalis 及其在欧洲和北美的竞争对手正竞相对拖车而非牵引车进行改造。Nivalis 与 BPW 和 TIP Group 合作开发的 Sommer 车队试点项目，为这个竞争激烈、投资回报数据仍然匮乏的领域提供了新的数据。车队运营商需要证据来证明预期的成本节约能够在实际商业运营中得到验证。

Two New Wheelchairs Reveal What “Smart” Really Means Today

> **译文：** 两款新型轮椅揭示了当今“智能”的真正含义

App-controlled chairs are not the same as self-driving ones.

> **译文：** 应用程序控制的座椅与自动驾驶座椅并不相同。

Two commercial “smart” wheelchairs debuting in the U.S. this year — Robooter’s app-connected, FDA-cleared model and the Strutt EV1 — offer very different visions of what “smart” mobility means. Though both look like breakthroughs, engineers, clinicians, and disability advocates say the real questions are: Whose definition of “smart” is being used, and what tradeoffs does it demand of users?

> **译文：** 今年在美国上市的两款商用“智能”轮椅——Robooter公司通过应用程序连接并获得FDA批准的型号以及Strutt EV1——对“智能”出行方式提出了截然不同的诠释。尽管这两款轮椅看起来都像是突破性的创新，但工程师、临床医生和残疾人权益倡导者表示，真正的问题在于：究竟是谁在定义“智能”，以及这种定义对用户提出了哪些权衡取舍？

Training Driving AI at 50,000× Real Time

GM’s approach to scalable autonomy .

GM tackles autonomous driving’s “edge cases” through several tools: vision-language-action models, a proprietary simulator called Boxworld that runs 50,000 times faster than real time, and adversarial stress-testing to expose failures before they hit the road. It then transfers those simulated lessons to real-world driving models through “on-policy distillation,” a reinforcement learning model that compresses hours of simulated experience into safety instincts a production-ready model can inherit efficiently.

> **译文：** 通用汽车利用多种工具应对自动驾驶的“极端情况”：视觉-语言-动作模型、名为 Boxworld 的专有模拟器（运行速度比实时快 5 万倍）以及对抗性压力测试（用于在车辆上路前发现故障）。然后，通用汽车通过“策略内蒸馏”将这些模拟经验迁移到实际驾驶模型中。“策略内蒸馏”是一种强化学习模型，可以将数小时的模拟经验压缩成安全本能，供量产模型高效继承。

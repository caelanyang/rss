We can’t call it news, as it was announced a few months ago, but it’s worth taking note of now that it’s actually happening.

> **译文：** 感谢阅读《简报》，这是我们每晚的专栏，为您解读当日新闻。如果您喜欢我们的内容，建议在此订阅我们的报道。大家好！蒂姆·库克将于明天结束其长达15年的苹果CEO任期，由约翰·特努斯接任，这可能是本周科技界最重要的事件。由于几个月前已公布，我们不能称之为新闻，但如今它真正发生，值得关注。

After all, while CEOs of small to midsize tech companies come and go fairly often—PayPal has had three in the past five years, for instance—we don’t get a CEO changeover at the half-dozen biggest tech companies very often.The last CEO transition at one of those big companies was Andy Jassy, who took the reins at Amazon in 2021 (yes, five years ago!).

> **译文：** 毕竟，中小型科技公司的CEO更替相当频繁——例如，PayPal在过去五年里换了三任CEO——但六大科技巨头的CEO更替并不常见。上一次大型科技公司的CEO交接是安迪·贾西，他于2021年接掌亚马逊（是的，已经是五年前了！）。

You have to go back to 2015 for the one before that, when Sundar Pichai took over at Google, and a year before that, when Satya Nadella became Microsoft CEO. It may be a few years before we get another one. With Cook’s exit, the oldest big tech CEO is Jensen Huang, who is reportedly 63, while his counterparts at other companies aren’t yet 60. It’s hard to imagine Huang stepping down anytime soon: He seems to have the energy of a 25-year-old. As for Ternus, what should we expect?

> **译文：** 再往前要追溯到2015年，桑达尔·皮查伊接任谷歌CEO，而在此之前一年，萨蒂亚·纳德拉成为微软CEO。下一次可能还要等上几年。随着库克的离任，大型科技公司CEO中最年长的是黄仁勋，据报道他63岁，而其他公司的CEO都还未满60岁。很难想象黄仁勋会很快卸任：他似乎拥有25岁年轻人的精力。至于特努斯，我们该期待什么？

This isn’t a case, as frequently happens with CEO changeovers, of anything needing to be “fixed” at Apple. The iPhone maker has done very well under Cook—revenue has expanded to an expected $477.7 billion for the year to September from $108 billion in fiscal 2011 when he took over. In the near term, the expected AI overhaul of Siri and the release of the new foldable iPhone should give sales something of a tailwind.

> **译文：** 这并非像常见的CEO更替那样，苹果需要“修复”什么问题。在库克的领导下，这家iPhone制造商表现非常出色——营收从他接任的2011财年的1080亿美元增长到截至9月的一年预计4777亿美元。短期内，Siri的AI全面升级和新款可折叠iPhone的发布应该会为销售带来一定助力。

In the medium term, though, Apple still faces the lack of a major new product, the same issue that has clouded its future for a while. After all, the iPhone market is mature. Meanwhile, Apple faces potential competition from OpenAI, which plans to release new devices tailored for the AI world sometime in the next year. Ternus has time to make his mark, but not that much time. Cook, by the way, is not departing Apple completely. He becomes executive chair, while John Ternus succeeds him as CEO.

> **译文：** 然而，从中期来看，苹果仍面临缺乏重大新产品的问题，这一直以来都为其未来蒙上阴影。毕竟，iPhone市场已经成熟。与此同时，苹果还面临来自OpenAI的潜在竞争，后者计划在明年某个时候发布专为AI世界打造的新设备。特努斯有时间留下自己的印记，但时间并不充裕。顺便说一句，库克并未完全离开苹果。他将出任执行董事长，而约翰·特努斯接任CEO。

Apple described Cook’s new role as “engaging with policymakers around the world,” which is an important job given the geopolitical tensions between the U.S. and China. Will his presence constrain Ternus from pursuing his own agenda? We’ll have to see.

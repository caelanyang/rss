Hello! I thought that I had heard of every possible way one could make hydrogen for clean energy, but recently I heard a new one: It’s called stimulated geologic hydrogen. Basically you break open rocks underground that have a lot of iron, then you expose them to water and a catalyst. This creates a chemical reaction that produces clean hydrogen that can be collected.

> **译文：** 您好！我原以为自己已经了解了所有可能的制氢方法，但最近又听说了一种新方法：刺激地质制氢。简单来说，就是敲开地下富含铁的岩石，然后将其暴露于水和催化剂中。这样会产生化学反应，生成可以收集的清洁氢气。

It’s a little like fracking, which has its downsides, but we found a startup that’s breaking rocks in a different way. They send high-voltage electric pulses through the rock to make fine, controlled fractures. “We break rocks with electricity” is the company slogan. For our latest energy feature, we visited one of these rock-busting sites and investigated the merits of stimulated geologic hydrogen.

> **译文：** 这有点像水力压裂法，虽然它也有一些缺点，但我们找到了一家用不同方式破碎岩石的初创公司。他们向岩石中输送高压电脉冲，从而产生精细可控的裂缝。“我们用电破碎岩石”是这家公司的口号。在我们最新的能源专题报道中，我们探访了其中一个岩石破碎作业现场，并调查了刺激地质氢的优势。

In other news, a quiet shift in technology is happening in the carbon capture world. Machines that pull carbon dioxide out of the atmosphere have never been very efficient, and one of the main issues is the use of steam for the heating step in the process. So several companies are switching to electrical approaches, such as beaming microwaves and using resistive heating. I visited the lab of one of these companies, Sustaera, in the Research Triangle area of North Carolina. Here's what they're up to.

> **译文：** 另据报道，碳捕获领域正在悄然发生技术变革。从大气中捕获二氧化碳的机器效率一直不高，其中一个主要问题是加热过程中需要使用蒸汽。因此，一些公司正在转向电加热方式，例如微波加热和电阻加热。我参观了其中一家公司——位于北卡罗来纳州三角研究园的Sustaera的实验室。以下是他们的最新进展。

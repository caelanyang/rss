Forget the SaaSpocalypse! It's a SaaS renaissance (SaaSaissance, anyone?). Shares of Salesforce rocketed 23% on Thursday, leading a recovery for many of the most beaten-down software stocks, including ServiceNow, Figma and Asana, after Salesforce reported upbeat July-quarter earnings on Wednesday night.

> **译文：** 感谢您阅读我们的每日新闻专栏“简报”，我们会在此为您解读当日新闻。如果您喜欢我们的内容，欢迎在此订阅。问候！别再提什么“SaaS末日”了！现在是SaaS复兴时期（有人想听“SaaS复兴”吗？）。Salesforce股价周四飙升23%，带动了包括ServiceNow、Figma和Asana在内的许多此前跌幅最大的软件股的反弹。此前，Salesforce于周三晚间公布了令人振奋的7月季度财报。

The rally signaled a striking change in sentiment for the sector, which has been slammed by worries about the impact of AI on traditional software. Thursday’s rally extended to cybersecurity-related stocks that hadn’t been swept up in the SaaSpocalypse, including CrowdStrike (up 20.5%) and Okta (up 29%), both of which also reported earnings on Wednesday. But the Salesforce rally was what got attention.

> **译文：** 这波上涨行情标志着该行业情绪发生了显著转变，此前该行业一直受到人工智能对传统软件冲击的担忧重创。周四的上涨行情也波及到一些尚未受到SaaS市场崩盘影响的网络安全相关股票，包括CrowdStrike（上涨20.5%）和Okta（上涨29%），这两家公司都在周三发布了财报。但Salesforce的上涨行情才是真正引人注目的焦点。

Salesforce, one of the biggest and highest-profile software names, at one point this year was down 43% from where it finished 2025. Those ugly days may be over: It finished the day down just 4% for the year. Of course, it‘s likely that investor sentiment will change again. After all, Salesforce’s quarterly numbers weren’t that good.

> **译文：** Salesforce是业内规模最大、最受瞩目的软件公司之一，今年股价一度较2025年底的预期下跌了43%。不过，这段艰难时期或许已经过去：截至今日，Salesforce股价今年以来仅下跌了4%。当然，投资者情绪很可能再次发生变化。毕竟，Salesforce的季度业绩并不理想。

Revenue grew 11%, 2 points slower than for the first quarter, and if you exclude the contribution from Salesforce’s acquisition of Informatica last November, growth was only 6.4%. But Salesforce is projecting a slight pickup in the second half in organic growth, which comes from signing new business or persuading existing customers to spend more, rather than from acquisitions. And while it’s only slight, it contributed to Salesforce raising its full-year revenue projection.

> **译文：** 营收增长11%，比第一季度放缓2个百分点；如果剔除去年11月Salesforce收购Informatica的贡献，增长率仅为6.4%。但Salesforce预计下半年内生增长将略有回升，这主要来自新业务的拓展或现有客户增加消费，而非收购。尽管增幅不大，但这促使Salesforce上调了全年营收预期。

It’s all about expectations, of course. Investors appear to be realizing they hugely overreacted to the threat posed by AI by selling down software stocks as much as they did. Software firms are still growing, even if the rate of expansion is coming down.

> **译文：** 当然，一切都取决于预期。投资者似乎已经意识到，他们之前抛售软件股的力度过大，对人工智能带来的威胁反应过度。尽管扩张速度有所放缓，但软件公司仍在增长。

As KeyBanc analyst Jackson Ader said on The Information’s TITV on Thursday, “We’re all coming around to the idea that things are going to be more durable…and we are not going away…and that’s why you see…Salesforce come out with fine but not stellar results but [you] see really outsized reaction.” Enough said. Tech’s Deal SpreeWe’re certainly no longer in Lina Khan’s America.

> **译文：** 正如KeyBanc分析师杰克逊·阿德尔周四在The Information的TITV节目中所说：“我们都逐渐接受了这样一个观点：事物会更加持久……我们不会消失……这就是为什么你会看到……Salesforce的业绩不错，但并非惊艳，却引发了巨大的市场反应。” 无需赘言。科技行业的并购狂潮 我们当然不再生活在莉娜·汗时代的美国了。

Our scoop on Wednesday night that Nvidia had agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion continues a slew of acquisitions in the tech sector in the past 18 months, including Stripe’s reported $7 billion purchase of OpenRouter, SpaceX’s purchase of Cursor, Salesforce’s acquisition of Informatica and Google’s purchase of Wiz.

> **译文：** 我们周三晚间独家报道，英伟达已同意以 129 亿美元收购 Hugging Face，这延续了过去 18 个月科技行业的一系列收购案，其中包括 Stripe 据报道以 70 亿美元收购 OpenRouter、SpaceX 收购 Cursor、Salesforce 收购 Informatica 以及谷歌收购 Wiz。

Given that Khan, chair of the Federal Trade Commission in the Biden administration, went to court (unsuccessfully) to stop Meta Platforms from buying Within, a tiny startup making a virtual reality app, simply on the principle that Meta shouldn’t be allowed to buy something, you can guess that most of the deals done under the Trump administration would not have gone through (at least without a big fight).

> **译文：** 鉴于拜登政府时期的联邦贸易委员会主席汗曾诉诸法庭（但最终败诉），试图阻止 Meta Platforms 收购一家名为 Within 的小型虚拟现实应用程序初创公司，理由仅仅是 Meta 不应该被允许收购，你可以猜想，特朗普政府时期达成的绝大多数交易都不会成功（至少不会不经过一场激烈的斗争）。

Tech companies are surely eyeing other deals, conscious that they can only be certain of another two years of a hands-off antitrust regime. (For more on this subject, see Jessica E. Lessin’s column from earlier this week.)

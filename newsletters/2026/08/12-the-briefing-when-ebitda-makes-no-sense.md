Greetings!

The business of running a fast-growing but young cloud computing firm in the age of AI is not necessarily a profitable one.

> **译文：** 在人工智能时代，经营一家快速发展但尚处于起步阶段的云计算公司并不一定能盈利。

Take CoreWeave, perhaps the best known of a crop of neoclouds trying to take on the well-established cloud firms.

> **译文：** 以 CoreWeave 为例，它可能是众多试图挑战老牌云公司的新兴云服务商中最知名的一家。

On Tuesday it reported second-quarter earnings showing that revenue rose 112% to $2.575 billion while cash burn rose by about the same percentage to $5.7 billion.

> **译文：** 该公司周二公布的第二季度财报显示，营收增长 112% 至 25.75 亿美元，而现金消耗也增长了约相同的百分比，达到 57 亿美元。

Oops.

Investors didn’t seem to care: CoreWeave stock jumped 13% in after-hours trading.

> **译文：** 投资者似乎并不在意：CoreWeave 股票在盘后交易中上涨了 13%。

In CoreWeave’s case, Wall Street may be more focused on other profitability metrics, such as the widely used earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, which the company adjusted to exclude stock compensation.

> **译文：** 就 CoreWeave 而言，华尔街可能更关注其他盈利能力指标，例如广泛使用的息税折旧摊销前利润，该公司已对其进行调整，排除了股票补偿。

On that measure, CoreWeave is looking healthy!

Its “adjusted Ebitda” doubled to $1.5 billion.

But while Ebitda can be a useful metric for some companies, it’s a nonsensical metric for analyzing CoreWeave.

> **译文：** 虽然 EBIDA 对某些公司来说可能是一个有用的指标，但对于分析 CoreWeave 来说，它是一个毫无意义的指标。

The company is both borrowing huge amounts to finance its business and having to record ever-increasing amounts of depreciation expense from the deployment of more and more chips, servers and data centers.

> **译文：** 该公司一方面借入巨额资金来为其业务融资，另一方面又不得不因部署越来越多的芯片、服务器和数据中心而计提不断增加的折旧费用。

As a result, its depreciation and amortization expenses more than doubled to $1.39 billion, while interest costs on its debt rose 140% to $640 million.

> **译文：** 因此，其折旧和摊销费用增加了一倍多，达到 13.9 亿美元，而其债务利息成本上升了 140%，达到 6.4 亿美元。

Excluding those expenses from an assessment of CoreWeave’s performance makes no sense.

> **译文：** 在评估 CoreWeave 的业绩时，将这些费用排除在外是毫无意义的。

It’s like measuring profits by excluding all of a company’s expenses.

> **译文：** 这就像在衡量利润时排除公司的所有支出一样。

Sure, free cash flow—the measure that shows a cash burn of $5.7 billion—isn’t a perfect way to evaluate a company at CoreWeave’s state of expansion either.

> **译文：** 当然，自由现金流（该指标显示现金消耗为 57 亿美元）也不是评估像 CoreWeave 这样处于扩张阶段的公司的完美方法。

The cash burn mostly reflects the enormous capital expenditures CoreWeave is investing in building out its network of data centers.

> **译文：** 现金消耗主要反映了 CoreWeave 在建设其数据中心网络方面投入的巨额资本支出。

That spending should decline at some point, once CoreWeave has built all the data centers it needs, although it will never disappear entirely given the costs of maintenance and regular chip upgrades.

> **译文：** 一旦 CoreWeave 建成了它需要的所有数据中心，这笔支出应该会在某个时候减少，但考虑到维护成本和定期芯片升级，这笔支出永远不会完全消失。

On a call on Tuesday, CoreWeave executives presented a very bullish outlook for the company.

> **译文：** 在周二的电话会议上，CoreWeave 的高管们对公司前景表达了非常乐观的看法。

But until we get an idea of how much cash the business will throw off after taking into account interest costs, as well as how much capex will be necessary on an ongoing basis, it’s tough to say how profitable a business CoreWeave will be.

> **译文：** 但是，在了解扣除利息成本后该业务将产生多少现金，以及持续需要多少资本支出之前，很难说 CoreWeave 的业务盈利能力如何。

Kalshi’s Good BetSpeaking of profits and losses, The Information on Tuesday scooped the news that prediction market Kalshi’s annualized revenue rate doubled to more than $4 billion in July from $2 billion two months earlier.

> **译文：** Kalshi 的好赌注：说到盈亏，《信息报》周二独家报道，预测市场 Kalshi 的年化收入从两个月前的 20 亿美元翻了一番，7 月份超过 40 亿美元。

In other words, monthly revenue doubled to $333 million.

> **译文：** 换句话说，月收入翻了一番，达到 3.33 亿美元。

But our story also reported that Kalshi’s operating expenses totaled $300 million in June, thanks to spending on marketing.

> **译文：** 但我们的报道也指出，由于在营销方面的支出，Kalshi 6 月份的运营费用总计达 3 亿美元。

And Kalshi is looking to raise money again in what would be its third fundraising in eight months.

> **译文：** Kalshi 正在寻求再次筹集资金，这将是该公司八个月内的第三次筹款活动。

It raised $1 billion in May and a similar amount last December.

> **译文：** 该公司在5月份筹集了10亿美元，去年12月也筹集了类似数额的资金。

While Kalshi’s valuation rockets every time, the fact that it has to keep raising money suggests it’s either not yet profitable or barely making money.

> **译文：** 尽管 Kalshi 的估值每次都在飙升，但它不得不不断筹集资金这一事实表明，它要么还没有盈利，要么几乎没赚到钱。

Kalshi’s soaring annualized revenue rate and valuation are both positive signs.

> **译文：** Kalshi 的年化收入增长率和估值均大幅增长，这都是积极的信号。

But the company’s outlook would be even more positive if it could stop raising money!

> **译文：** 但如果该公司能够停止筹集资金，其前景将会更加乐观！

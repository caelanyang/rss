NVIDIA keeps outrunning the law of large numbers

> **译文：** 英伟达持续超越大数定律

The company just crossed $96 billion in quarterly revenue, with growth accelerating above 100%. Management now expects revenue to grow another 70% next year, adding more than $200 billion in annual revenue. At this scale, that should sound almost implausible. Yet even that outlook is supply-constrained.

> **译文：** 该公司季度营收刚刚突破960亿美元，增速加快至100%以上。管理层预计明年营收将再增长70%，年收入增加超过2000亿美元。在如此规模下，这听起来几乎令人难以置信。然而，即便是这一展望也受制于供应端约束。

But the story is also getting more complicated. NVIDIA is helping finance the infrastructure its customers need, while some of those same customers are developing competing chips of their own.

> **译文：** 但故事也变得更加复杂。英伟达正在帮助为其客户所需的基础设施提供融资，而其中一些客户同时也在开发自己的竞争性芯片。

So the debate now comes down to two questions:

> **译文：** 因此，当前的争论归结为两个问题：

How long can the AI infrastructure boom keep compounding?

> **译文：** AI基础设施的繁荣还能复合增长多久？

How much of it can NVIDIA continue to capture?

So, what to make of all this?

🚀 Growth accelerated again: Revenue growth jumped from 85% in Q1 to 106% in Q2, while Data Center accelerated from 92% to 117%. NVIDIA added nearly $15 billion of revenue sequentially, and Q3 guidance calls for another $12 billion.

> **译文：** 🚀 增长再次加速：营收增速从第一季度的85%跃升至第二季度的106%，数据中心增速从92%加速至117%。英伟达环比新增近150亿美元营收，第三季度指引预计再增120亿美元。

☁️ Demand is broadening: Hyperscale revenue reached $48.7 billion, while AI Clouds, Industrial, and Enterprise grew even faster to $40.3 billion. NVIDIA is increasingly benefiting from neoclouds, enterprises, and sovereign AI alongside Big Tech.

> **译文：** ☁️ 需求正在扩大：超大规模客户收入达到487亿美元，而AI云、工业和企业领域增长更快，达到403亿美元。英伟达正越来越多地从新云服务商、企业和主权AI以及大型科技公司中获益。

🔄 Rubin is arriving without a digestion pause: Blackwell Ultra is still ramping, yet Rubin shipments have already started. So far, each new architecture is layering onto the previous one rather than creating an air pocket.

> **译文：** 🔄 Rubin架构到来且无消化空档：Blackwell Ultra仍在爬坡期，而Rubin已开始出货。到目前为止，每一代新架构都在前一代基础上叠加，而非形成需求真空。

📉 Margins are finally bending: Gross margin is expected to fall from 75% in Q2 to 74% in Q3 and roughly 71%-72% in Q4, largely because of higher memory costs. The pressure appears supply-driven rather than a sign of weaker demand or pricing.

> **译文：** 📉 利润率终于开始承压：毛利率预计将从第二季度的75%降至第三季度的74%，第四季度约为71%-72%，主要原因是内存成本上升。这一压力似乎来自供应端，而非需求或定价走弱的信号。

🧱 NVIDIA is locking up supply: Supply and capacity commitments jumped from $119 billion to $279 billion in three months, largely to secure memory. That's a huge vote of confidence in future demand—and a much larger commitment if the cycle eventually slows.

> **译文：** 🧱 英伟达正在锁定供应：供应和产能承诺在三个月内从1190亿美元跃升至2790亿美元，主要用于确保内存供应。这是对未来需求的巨大信心投票——如果周期最终放缓，这也是一份大得多的承诺。

🔮 The outlook remains extraordinary: NVIDIA expects roughly 70% revenue growth in FY28, far above prior Wall Street expectations. If that holds, the AI infrastructure cycle is still expanding rapidly despite NVIDIA already operating at enormous scale.

> **译文：** 🔮 前景依然非凡：英伟达预计2028财年营收增长约70%，远高于华尔街此前的预期。如果这一预期成立，意味着AI基础设施周期仍在快速扩张，尽管英伟达已经以巨大的规模运营。

Big picture: Growth accelerated, demand broadened, and Rubin is arriving before Blackwell has slowed. The main new wrinkle is that sustaining this pace is becoming more expensive, with memory costs pressuring margins and supply commitments rising rapidly.

> **译文：** 大局观：增长加速，需求扩大，而Rubin在Blackwell尚未放缓之前就已到来。新的主要变数是，维持这一速度的成本越来越高，内存成本挤压利润率，供应承诺迅速攀升。

2. Business highlights

⚡ Rubin raises the value of a gigawatt

NVIDIA is capturing more revenue from every AI factory generation.

> **译文：** 英伟达正从每一代AI工厂中获取更多收入。

Management estimates its revenue opportunity per gigawatt keeps increasing:

> **译文：** 管理层估计，其每吉瓦的收入机会持续增加：

~$18 billion per gigawatt with Hopper.

~$25 billion per gigawatt with Blackwell.

> **译文：** Blackwell每吉瓦约250亿美元。

And now ~$40 billion per gigawatt with Vera Rubin.

> **译文：** 而Vera Rubin现在每吉瓦约400亿美元。

Rubin combines GPUs, CPUs, networking, and software to deliver 30x higher throughput per megawatt and 35x lower token costs than Grace Blackwell Ultra.

> **译文：** Rubin结合了GPU、CPU、网络和软件，每兆瓦吞吐量比Grace Blackwell Ultra高出30倍，代币成本降低35倍。

Production shipments have already started, with purchase orders from every major hyperscaler, AI cloud, and system OEM.

> **译文：** 生产出货已经开始，来自所有主要超大规模云厂商、AI云和系统OEM的采购订单均已到位。

🏦 NVIDIA becomes an AI financier

NVIDIA has invested nearly $50 billion in frontier AI labs and partnered with major financial institutions to help raise more than $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure.

> **译文：** 英伟达已在前沿AI实验室投资近500亿美元，并与主要金融机构合作，帮助为AI基础设施筹集超过5000亿美元的第三方资本。

It is also using credit support and take-or-pay commitments to help AI labs and neoclouds finance capacity. Critics call this circular financing. NVIDIA argues it is simply removing a capital bottleneck for customers whose demand is growing faster than their balance sheets.

> **译文：** 英伟达还在利用信贷支持和照付不议承诺，帮助AI实验室和新型云厂商融资产能。批评者称此为循环融资。英伟达辩称，这只是在为客户消除资本瓶颈，因为他们的需求增长速度超过了自身资产负债表的承受能力。

Either way, NVIDIA is moving beyond selling infrastructure to helping make that infrastructure possible. There may also be a strategic benefit: financing infrastructure today can help lock in NVIDIA deployments before competing silicon reaches scale.

> **译文：** 无论如何，英伟达正在超越销售基础设施本身，转向帮助使这些基础设施成为可能。这可能还有战略好处：今天为基础设施融资，有助于在竞争性芯片达到规模之前锁定英伟达的部署。

🤗 NVIDIA agrees to buy Hugging Face

According to The Information, NVIDIA has agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, nearly triple its 2023 valuation.

> **译文：** 据The Information报道，英伟达已同意以129亿美元收购Hugging Face，几乎是其2023年估值的三倍。

Hugging Face is one of the main hubs for developers to discover, share, and deploy open AI models, often dubbed the “GitHub of AI.” With only about $150 million in annual revenue, NVIDIA is clearly buying strategic positioning rather than near-term profits.

> **译文：** Hugging Face是开发者发现、分享和部署开源AI模型的主要枢纽之一，常被称为“AI领域的GitHub”。由于其年收入仅约1.5亿美元，英伟达显然是在购买战略定位，而非短期利润。

The logic ties directly to Jensen’s argument on the call: NVIDIA benefits whenever AI models proliferate. Open models are particularly attractive because startups and enterprises generally don’t build custom chips and overwhelmingly rely on existing compute infrastructure.

> **译文：** 这一逻辑直接呼应了Jensen在电话会上的论点：只要AI模型 proliferate，英伟达就能受益。开源模型尤其有吸引力，因为初创企业和企业通常不会自研定制芯片，而是绝大多数依赖现有计算基础设施。

Owning Hugging Face would move NVIDIA one layer closer to developers and tighten the link between open-model adoption and its broader computing platform. The risk is that NVIDIA ownership could weaken the neutrality that helped make Hugging Face so valuable in the first place.

> **译文：** 收购Hugging Face将使英伟达更接近开发者一层，并加强开源模型采用与其更广泛计算平台之间的联系。风险在于，英伟达的所有权可能削弱中立性——而正是这种中立性最初让Hugging Face变得如此有价值。

CEO Jensen Huang:

On custom chips:

“Whereas many of these XPUs are inference-specific chips for one cloud or one service, NVIDIA is a platform, an entire AI factory platform that spans the entire AI life cycle that you can use in any cloud. It’s in every cloud. You can run anywhere. [...] I have 100% confidence that our technology will continue to be extraordinary for them and that the economics of using our technology, whether it’s from data processing to training, to post-training, to agentic processing, our technology’s going to be extraordinary for them.”

> **译文：** “许多这些XPU是为单一云或单一服务设计的推理专用芯片，而英伟达是一个平台，一个完整的AI工厂平台，覆盖整个AI生命周期，你可以在任何云中使用它。它存在于每一朵云中。你可以在任何地方运行。[...] 我百分之百相信，我们的技术对他们来说将继续是非凡的，而且使用我们技术的经济效益——无论是数据处理、训练、后训练还是智能体处理——我们的技术对他们来说都将是卓越的。”

This is Jensen’s answer to OpenAI’s Jalapeño and the broader custom-silicon threat. His argument isn’t that customers won’t build their own chips. It’s that those chips tend to optimize specific workloads, while NVIDIA’s advantage is a fungible platform spanning training, inference, networking, CPUs, and multiple clouds. The question is whether that breadth remains valuable enough to justify NVIDIA’s premium economics.

> **译文：** 这是Jensen对OpenAI的Jalapeño以及更广泛的定制芯片威胁的回应。他的论点不是客户不会自研芯片，而是那些芯片往往针对特定工作负载进行优化，而英伟达的优势在于一个可跨训练、推理、网络、CPU和多云环境使用的通用平台。问题在于，这种广度是否仍然足够有价值，以支撑英伟达的溢价经济模式。

On what happens when AI becomes agentic:

“When the world goes to agentic, fully agentic systems, you are going to have agents running all the time, working with other agents running all the time. [...] I think the most important thing that matters for the industry is that, one, AI is now doing productive and useful work. Two, AI is generating profitable tokens. Three, if we had more compute, we could generate more profitable tokens, which results in more profit for all of the services. This is the exact phase where we are at, which is the reason why everybody is leaning in.”

> **译文：** “当世界走向智能体化、完全智能体化的系统时，你将拥有始终运行的智能体，与其他始终运行的智能体协作。[...] 我认为对行业来说最重要的事情是：第一，AI现在正在做有生产力的、有用的工作。第二，AI正在生成可盈利的代币。第三，如果我们有更多算力，我们就能生成更多可盈利的代币，从而为所有服务带来更多利润。这正是我们目前所处的阶段，也是为什么所有人都在全力投入的原因。”

Today’s AI is still largely triggered by humans. Jensen believes the next phase involves millions of agents operating continuously and interacting with other agents. If that happens, inference demand stops being directly tied to human usage, and the amount of compute required could increase dramatically.

> **译文：** 如今的AI在很大程度上仍由人类触发。詹森认为，下一阶段将涉及数百万个智能体持续运行并与其他智能体交互。如果这种情况发生，推理需求将不再直接与人类使用挂钩，所需计算量可能会急剧增加。

4. What to watch next

NVDA is up roughly 20% YTD, still outperforming the S&P 500.

> **译文：** 英伟达股价年初至今上涨约20%，仍跑赢标普500指数。

Even after the post-earnings jump, the stock trades at only about 20x forward earnings, well below the multiple it commanded earlier in the AI boom and below the rest of US Big Tech.

> **译文：** 即使在财报发布后股价跳涨，该股交易价格也仅为远期市盈率约20倍，远低于AI热潮早期所享有的估值倍数，也低于美国其他大型科技股。

The latest 13F filings for Q2 2026 showed that hedge funds are not accumulating NVDA as much as they used to. The stock remains one of the most widely held names, although many funds are still underexposed relative to its 8% weight in the S&P 500.

> **译文：** 最新的2026年第二季度13F文件显示，对冲基金对英伟达的增持力度不如以往。该股仍是最广泛持有的股票之一，但相对于其在标普500指数中8%的权重，许多基金仍处于低配状态。

At that valuation, the debate is less about whether NVIDIA looks cheap today and more about how durable these extraordinary earnings can be.

> **译文：** 在这样的估值水平下，争论的焦点不再是英伟达今天是否看起来便宜，而是这些非凡的盈利能持续多久。

In semiconductors, a low P/E can sometimes signal peak earnings rather than a bargain. The multiple may be compressed precisely because the market is questioning whether today’s profitability is sustainable. So far, that skepticism has been repeatedly proven wrong.

> **译文：** 在半导体行业，低市盈率有时可能意味着盈利见顶，而非估值偏低。市盈率被压缩，恰恰可能是因为市场在质疑当前盈利能力是否可持续。到目前为止，这种怀疑已被反复证明是错误的。

Here’s what I’m watching:

Custom silicon: OpenAI just published the first results for Jalapeño, its custom inference chip. The company says it delivered 1.5x-1.9x more throughput per watt and materially lower latency than the NVIDIA systems tested across several models. OpenAI still plans to use NVIDIA broadly, but Jalapeño shows that NVIDIA’s largest customers have a growing incentive to move specialized inference workloads onto their own silicon.

> **译文：** 定制芯片：OpenAI刚刚发布了其定制推理芯片Jalapeño的首批测试结果。该公司表示，与所测试的英伟达系统相比，该芯片在多个模型上的每瓦吞吐量提升了1.5倍至1.9倍，延迟也显著降低。OpenAI仍计划广泛使用英伟达产品，但Jalapeño表明，英伟达最大的客户有越来越大的动力将专业推理工作负载迁移到自研芯片上。

Circular financing: NVIDIA says AI labs receiving some form of balance-sheet support could account for roughly one-quarter of its business next year. Financing customers does not make the underlying demand fake, but it increases NVIDIA’s exposure if AI labs eventually struggle to monetize the infrastructure they are building.

> **译文：** 循环融资：英伟达表示，获得某种形式资产负债表支持的AI实验室可能占其明年业务的约四分之一。为客户提供融资并不会使潜在需求变得虚假，但如果AI实验室最终难以将其建设的基础设施变现，这将增加英伟达的风险敞口。

Margins and supply: Memory scarcity is expected to push gross margin down before price increases help it recover next year. The question is whether NVIDIA can continue securing sufficient supply to meet extraordinary demand without sacrificing too much of its economics.

> **译文：** 利润率与供应：内存短缺预计将推动毛利率下降，之后价格上调将在明年帮助其回升。问题在于，英伟达能否在不牺牲过多经济效益的情况下，持续获得足够的供应以满足非凡的需求。

📉 The bear case is that AI infrastructure spending eventually outruns the profits it can generate, while custom silicon takes a growing share of inference.

> **译文：** 📉 看空观点认为，AI基础设施支出最终将超过其所能产生的利润，而定制芯片将在推理领域占据越来越大的份额。

📈 The bull case is that agentic AI keeps expanding compute demand faster than efficiency gains and competition can reduce it.

> **译文：** 📈 看多观点认为，智能体AI将持续推动计算需求增长，其速度将超过效率提升和竞争所带来的需求缩减。

So far, NVIDIA is still winning that race.

> **译文：** 到目前为止，英伟达仍在赢得这场竞赛。

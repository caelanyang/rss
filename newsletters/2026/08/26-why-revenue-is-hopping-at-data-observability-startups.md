Welcome back!

It’s Alix.A cluster of startups taking on database giants Snowflake, Databricks and Datadog are experiencing a revenue windfall thanks to rising adoption of AI agents.
> **译文：** 这是 Alix。由于人工智能代理的日益普及，一群挑战数据库巨头 Snowflake、Databricks 和 Datadog 的初创公司正在经历收入暴增。

That could lead to a wave of startup dealmaking, from new investments to M&amp;A.
> **译文：** 这可能会导致创业公司交易浪潮，从新的投资到并购。

Earlier Tuesday, I reported that ClickHouse’s annual recurring revenue jumped 40% from May to $350 million, and that OpenAI’s use of the startup, which provides free open-source software and paid cloud services to help companies analyze large volumes of data, has jumped this year.
> **译文：** 周二早些时候，我报道了 ClickHouse 的年度经常性收入从 5 月份增长了 40%，达到 3.5 亿美元，并且 OpenAI 对这家初创公司的使用量今年大幅增长。这家初创公司提供免费的开源软件和付费云服务，以帮助公司分析大量数据。

OpenAI’s use is notable, since the ChatGPT maker has also been a customer of publicly traded Datadog, the giant in observability—or monitoring the actions humans and AI agents take on computers.
> **译文：** OpenAI 的使用值得关注，因为 ChatGPT 的制造商也是上市公司 Datadog 的客户，Datadog 是可观测性领域的巨头，即监控人类和 AI 代理在计算机上采取的行动。

ClickHouse last raised funding at a $15 billion valuation in January and fielded many approaches this year from strategic buyers, according to a person with direct knowledge of the discussions. “We don’t dismiss interest because we have a responsibility to our shareholders but we are not selling the company, quite the contrary,” Chief Executive Aaron Katz told me.
> **译文：** 据一位知情人士透露，ClickHouse今年1月完成融资，估值达150亿美元，并在今年收到了多家战略买家的收购意向。首席执行官Aaron Katz告诉我：“我们不会拒绝任何收购意向，因为我们对股东负有责任，但我们不会出售公司，恰恰相反。”

ClickHouse isn’t alone in benefiting from the large data needs spurred by AI agents.
> **译文：** ClickHouse 并非唯一一家受益于人工智能代理带来的大数据需求的公司。

Cribl, which sells technology known as telemetry that helps companies move and store data for monitoring, says its annual recurring revenue is near $400 million, up 33% from February. “Our enterprise customers are experiencing a massive data growth driven in part by AI and the advent of agents and we’re helping them handle it,” said Cribl cofounder and CEO Clint Sharp.
> **译文：** Cribl公司销售一种名为遥测的技术，帮助企业移动和存储数据以进行监控。该公司表示，其年度经常性收入接近4亿美元，较2月份增长了33%。Cribl联合创始人兼首席执行官克林特·夏普表示：“我们的企业客户正面临着数据量的爆炸式增长，这在一定程度上是由人工智能和智能代理的出现推动的，而我们正在帮助他们应对这一挑战。”

The eight-year-old company says it helps customers including Zoom, ServiceNow and Hilton by routing them to cheaper storage and the most efficient monitoring tool. “The problem we solve is that your data is growing at a 30% compound growth rate, and your budget isn't,” Sharp said.
> **译文：** 这家成立八年的公司表示，它通过引导客户使用更便宜的存储设备和最高效的监控工具，帮助包括 Zoom、ServiceNow 和 Hilton 在内的客户。“我们解决的问题是，您的数据以 30% 的复合增长率增长，而您的预算却没有相应增长，”夏普说道。

Cribl, which last raised venture funding in August 2024 at a $3.5 billion valuation, is aiming for an initial public offering in the next couple of years, said Sharp.
> **译文：** Sharp表示，Cribl最近一次获得风险投资是在2024年8月，当时的估值为35亿美元，该公司计划在未来几年内进行首次公开募股。

And Grafana, a 12-year-old startup that sells tools that let developers visualize in real time how their applications, cloud servers and AI agents are behaving, says it’s recently seen a pickup in revenue.
> **译文：** Grafana 是一家成立 12 年的初创公司，销售的工具可以让开发者实时可视化他们的应用程序、云服务器和 AI 代理的运行情况。该公司表示，最近收入有所增长。

That’s in part thanks to the company’s AI assistant, which helps companies observe activity on their own apps, said Scott Fingerhut, Grafana’s chief marketing officer.
> **译文：** Grafana 首席营销官 Scott Fingerhut 表示，这部分要归功于该公司的 AI 助手，该助手可以帮助公司观察其自身应用程序上的活动。

Fingerhut said half of Grafana’s customers this year used the assistant to get the company’s software to monitor their systems or configure tools.
> **译文：** Fingerhut 表示，今年 Grafana 一半的客户使用该助手来获取该公司的软件，以便监控他们的系统或配置工具。

The company, which was valued at $9.6 billion in March, passed $400 million in annual recurring revenue last September.
> **译文：** 该公司在 3 月份的估值为 96 亿美元，去年 9 月的年度经常性收入突破了 4 亿美元。

It frequently comes up as a possible acquisition target for a larger company interested in data monitoring, say software bankers.
> **译文：** 软件银行家表示，对于对数据监控感兴趣的大公司来说，它经常被视为潜在的收购目标。

Elastic, Snowflake PurchasesAlready, larger companies have been buying startups that specialize in monitoring data.
> **译文：** Elastic、Snowflake 收购案：大型公司已经开始收购专门从事数据监控的初创公司。

Elastic, a publicly traded company that provides an open-source search engine for large data sets, on Monday bought Deductive AI, which helps engineers monitor data to resolve issues, for $85 million.
> **译文：** Elastic是一家公开上市的公司，为大型数据集提供开源搜索引擎。周一，该公司以8500万美元收购了Deductive AI，后者帮助工程师监控数据以解决问题。

Two weeks ago, Dynatrace paid $915 million for Arize AI, whose AI tracks how models and agents behave.
> **译文：** 两周前，Dynatrace 斥资 9.15 亿美元收购了 Arize AI，该公司的人工智能可以追踪模型和代理的行为。

Snowflake closed its $1 billion purchase of Observe in June, betting that customers already storing their data in its platform will want the tools to see how their applications are performing.
> **译文：** Snowflake 于 6 月份完成了对 Observe 的 10 亿美元收购，该公司押注已经将数据存储在其平台上的客户会希望获得这些工具来查看其应用程序的性能。

The acquisition, first reported by The Information, followed cybersecurity company Palo Alto Networks’ purchase of observability startup Chronosphere for $3.35 billion.
> **译文：** 据 The Information 报道，此次收购发生在网络安全公司 Palo Alto Networks 以 33.5 亿美元收购可观测性初创公司 Chronosphere 之后。

Other acquisitions are likely, investors and bankers say.
> **译文：** 投资者和银行家表示，未来可能还会出现其他收购案。

Cybersecurity firms, big tech companies such as Google, Amazon and Microsoft, and networking companies like Cisco could all make a play for startups that monitor data. “As AI agents take on more autonomous work, companies need visibility into what they’re doing and safeguards to catch failures or security risks before they cause harm,” said Mike Jung, partner at Founders Circle Capital, which has backed Databricks, Vercel, ClickHouse and Cribl.
> **译文：** 网络安全公司、谷歌、亚马逊和微软等大型科技公司以及思科等网络公司都可能竞相收购数据监控初创公司。“随着人工智能代理承担更多自主工作，企业需要了解其运行情况，并需要采取安全措施，在故障或安全风险造成损害之前将其发现并解决，” Founders Circle Capital 的合伙人 Mike Jung 表示。Founders Circle Capital 曾投资过 Databricks、Vercel、ClickHouse 和 Cribl 等公司。

Question: How is the 24-year-old SpaceX like a young startup?

> **译文：** 问：成立 24 年的 SpaceX 与一家年轻的创业公司有何相似之处？

It’s burning twice as much cash as it brings in as revenue!

> **译文：** 它的烧钱额是收入的两倍！

That’s one takeaway from SpaceX’s first earnings report as a public company, in which the company revealed it burned $16 billion in the second quarter, on $7.8 billion in revenue.

> **译文：** 这是SpaceX作为一家上市公司发布的首份收益报告得出的一个结论，该公司在报告中透露，其第二季度亏损160亿美元，而收入为78亿美元。

That’s thanks to a whopping $18.4 billion in capital expenditures, mostly due to SpaceX’s AI data center expansion.

> **译文：** 这主要归功于高达 184 亿美元的资本支出，其中大部分用于 SpaceX 的人工智能数据中心扩建。

And like a young startup, SpaceX has big ambitions.

> **译文：** 就像一家年轻的创业公司一样，SpaceX 怀揣着远大的抱负。

On the company’s earnings call with analysts, CEO Elon Musk claimed it was now expecting to hit $1 trillion in revenue by 2030, a year earlier than it had projected before its IPO, and there was a chance it could hit that threshold by 2029.

> **译文：** 在公司与分析师的财报电话会议上，首席执行官埃隆·马斯克声称，公司预计到 2030 年收入将达到 1 万亿美元，比 IPO 前预测的时间提前了一年，并且有可能在 2029 年就达到这一目标。

That’s a big claim, considering that first-half revenue was only $12.5 billion.

> **译文：** 考虑到上半年营收仅为 125 亿美元，这确实是一个很高的成就。

True, finance chief Bret Johnsen said SpaceX’s annualized revenue rate would hit $100 billion by the end of this year.

> **译文：** 没错，首席财务官布雷特·约翰逊表示，SpaceX 的年化收入到今年年底将达到 1000 亿美元。

But ARR is one month’s revenue multiplied by 12.

It’s not a real metric (and Musk emphasized that the $1 trillion projection was revenue, not ARR).

> **译文：** 这并非一个真正的衡量标准（马斯克强调，1 万亿美元的预测指的是收入，而不是 ARR）。

Musk also claimed that SpaceX’s Starlink could deliver most of the internet connectivity in the world in “less than 10 years,” while his No. 2, Gwynne Shotwell, claimed that SpaceX’s Starlink mobile service—to launch at the end of 2027—would “acquire quite a few” of the customers of the major cellphone providers in the U.S.

> **译文：** 马斯克还声称，SpaceX 的星链可以在“不到 10 年”的时间内为世界上大部分地区提供互联网连接，而他的二号人物格温·肖特韦尔则声称，SpaceX 的星链移动服务（将于 2027 年底推出）将“获得美国主要手机运营商的相当一部分”客户。

Whoa!

So not only does SpaceX have enormous ambitions to dominate broadband internet, it aims to compete directly with major cellular providers.

> **译文：** 因此，SpaceX 不仅雄心勃勃地想要主导宽带互联网，而且还计划与主要的移动运营商直接竞争。

And that’s all in addition to its huge AI ambitions and its desire to launch data centers in space and travel to far-off planets.

> **译文：** 除此之外，该公司还有着宏大的人工智能雄心，以及在太空建立数据中心和前往遥远星球的愿望。

Investors seem underwhelmed (or perhaps overwhelmed?) by all these big claims: SpaceX stock fell 6.5% in after-hours trading.

> **译文：** 投资者似乎对所有这些夸大其词的说法感到失望（或者说，他们可能被这些说法冲昏了头脑？）：SpaceX 股票在盘后交易中下跌了 6.5%。

The investor reaction makes sense.

Investors have come to hate big tech companies’ massive outlays on capex for AI, even though those companies are funding that capex from massive profits on other businesses.

> **译文：** 投资者开始憎恨大型科技公司在人工智能领域的巨额资本支出，尽管这些公司是用其他业务的巨额利润来支付这些资本支出的。

And SpaceX is spending more on capex, relative to what its businesses generate in cash, than any big tech firm.

> **译文：** 与任何大型科技公司相比，SpaceX 在资本支出方面的投入相对于其业务产生的现金流而言要高得多。

SpaceX’s only real profit center is its Starlink internet business, but that makes nowhere near enough to fund all the company’s ambitions (Starlink made just $1.65 billion in second-quarter operating income, for instance).

> **译文：** SpaceX 唯一真正的利润中心是其 Starlink 互联网业务，但这远远不足以资助公司的所有雄心壮志（例如，Starlink 第二季度的营业收入仅为 16.5 亿美元）。

SpaceX will undoubtedly get a lift from the various deals it has done to rent out its computing capacity and from its pending acquisition of AI firm Cursor.

> **译文：** SpaceX无疑会从其出租计算能力的各种交易以及即将收购人工智能公司Cursor的交易中受益。

But becoming a major competitor in mobile and building more AI capacity won’t come cheap.

> **译文：** 但要想成为移动领域的主要竞争者并建立更强大的人工智能能力，成本并不低。

The returns are questionable, both in AI and telecom.

> **译文：** 人工智能和电信领域的投资回报都值得怀疑。

Remember that the big telecom providers have to spend a fortune on spectrum and equipment, and they’re all fighting over a market with little growth.

> **译文：** 别忘了，大型电信运营商必须在频谱和设备上花费巨资，而且它们都在争夺一个增长缓慢的市场。

Musk has sky-high ambitions.

Watching him try to fulfill them promises to be a lot of fun.

> **译文：** 看着他努力实现这些目标，肯定会很有趣。

Bandwidth ConstraintsAI has created massive demand for electricity and chips, driving up the prices of both, which has in turn lifted the costs of smartphones and computers.

> **译文：** 带宽限制：人工智能对电力和芯片的需求巨大，推高了两者的价格，进而推高了智能手机和电脑的成本。

Elon Musk on Tuesday pointed out that the demand for bandwidth to transmit data will also soar as a result of AI. “With the advent of AI and humanoid robotics and vehicle robotics and just a massive number of robots, the appetite of bandwidth will be much greater than it has been in the past,” he told analysts.

> **译文：** 埃隆·马斯克周二指出，人工智能的发展也将导致数据传输带宽需求飙升。他告诉分析师：“随着人工智能、人形机器人、车载机器人以及大量机器人的出现，对带宽的需求将比以往任何时候都大得多。”

In other words, all those robots and self-driving cars and AI-enabled devices will be communicating constantly, straining the capacity of broadband providers, which are now mostly cable companies and cell firms.

> **译文：** 换句话说，所有这些机器人、自动驾驶汽车和人工智能设备将不断进行通信，给宽带提供商（目前主要是有线电视公司和移动通信公司）的容量带来压力。

Musk says Starlink will save the day, providing the bandwidth to meet all those demands.

> **译文：** 马斯克表示，星链将力挽狂澜，提供足够的带宽来满足所有这些需求。

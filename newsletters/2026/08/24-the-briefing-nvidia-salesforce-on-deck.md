> **译文：** 感谢您阅读我们的每日新闻专栏“简报”，我们会在此为您解读当日新闻。如果您喜欢我们的内容，欢迎在此订阅我们的报道。问候！目前科技界最勤奋的人之一当属英伟达的企业公关团队，他们几乎从未淡出过新闻视野。

Aside from updates on its latest AI chips, Nvidia seems to be investing in almost every part of the AI sector, from data center developers to AI model makers to data-labeling providers. And this week, we get Nvidia’s second-quarter earnings, which will help explain how the company can afford to do everything it’s doing. In truth, its trajectory continues to be extraordinary.

> **译文：** 除了最新的AI芯片之外，英伟达似乎在AI领域的各个方面都进行了投资，从数据中心开发商到AI模型构建者，再到数据标注提供商。本周，我们将迎来英伟达的第二季度财报，这将有助于解释该公司为何能够负担得起如此庞大的业务。事实上，它的发展轨迹依然令人瞩目。

After revenue growth slowed to 65% in its last fiscal year, from 114% the year before, Nvidia’s growth is now accelerating—analysts expect revenue this year to grow 83%, according to S&P Global Market Intelligence. And Nvidia is expected to report this week even faster growth, of 97%, for the July quarter.That would compare with 85% growth in the first quarter. What’s going on?

> **译文：** 英伟达上个财年的营收增速从前一年的114%放缓至65%，但如今增速正在加快——据标普全球市场情报公司（S&P Global Market
> Intelligence）的分析师预测，该公司今年的营收将增长83%。而且，英伟达预计本周还将公布7月份季度的业绩，增速将高达97%。相比之下，第一季度的增速为85%。这究竟是怎么回事？

Most obviously, the AI boom has underwritten an explosion in data center development, much of which is leading to intense demand for Nvidia’s AI chips. That’s coming from big tech companies such as Alphabet and Amazon building out more data centers, neoclouds like CoreWeave and Nebius, and data center developments funded by private equity. Meanwhile, more businesses are using AI models for various purposes.

> **译文：** 最显而易见的是，人工智能的蓬勃发展推动了数据中心建设的爆炸式增长，而这又直接导致了对英伟达人工智能芯片的巨大需求。这种需求主要来自Alphabet和亚马逊等大型科技公司建设更多数据中心、CoreWeave和Nebius等新型云平台，以及私募股权投资的数据中心开发项目。与此同时，越来越多的企业正在将人工智能模型应用于各种用途。

That’s helped boost Nvidia’s share of chips for the inference market —inference is running existing models, as opposed to training them—as we reported in June. And while Nvidia’s costs are going up—memory chip prices are rising, for instance—Nvidia is also raising its prices as we reported on Saturday. The net effect is that Nvidia has become an enormous cash machine.

> **译文：** 正如我们六月份报道的那样，这帮助英伟达提升了其在推理芯片市场的份额——推理是指运行现有模型，而不是训练模型。虽然英伟达的成本正在上升——例如，内存芯片的价格正在上涨——但正如我们周六报道的那样，英伟达也在提高其产品价格。最终结果是，英伟达已经成为一台巨大的印钞机。

Its free cash flow—what its business generates in cash after capital expenditures are taken into account—rose 85.7% to $48.6 billion in the first quarter. Analysts project Nvidia will report about the same level of free cash flow for its second fiscal quarter, although they also expect free cash flow will rise to $55 billion in the third quarter and $61 billion in the fourth quarter. That would amount to $213 billion for the year.

> **译文：** 英伟达第一季度的自由现金流（即扣除资本支出后公司产生的现金）增长了85.7%，达到486亿美元。分析师预计，英伟达第二财季的自由现金流将与第一季度持平，同时预计第三季度将增至550亿美元，第四季度将增至610亿美元。全年自由现金流预计将达到2130亿美元。

Apple, long a giant in cash production, is expected to generate just $144 billion in its 2026 fiscal year, S&P data shows.It’s little wonder, then, that Nvidia can afford to both buy back shares and invest billions in a wide range of companies. It’s no coincidence that this is occurring as companies like Alphabet, Meta and Amazon all become cash-burning giants, thanks to massive capex.

> **译文：** 标普数据显示，苹果公司长期以来都是现金流巨头，但预计其2026财年的现金流仅为1440亿美元。因此，英伟达既能负担得起股票回购，又能向众多公司投资数十亿美元也就不足为奇了。与此同时，Alphabet、Meta和亚马逊等公司由于巨额资本支出而成为烧钱巨头，这绝非巧合。

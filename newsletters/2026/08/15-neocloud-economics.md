AI has created a massive capacity gap.

There is more demand for compute than the biggest cloud companies can supply.

> **译文：** 计算需求超过了最大的云公司所能提供的量。

That gap has fueled the rise of neoclouds, specialized providers built around AI infrastructure. They focus on securing raw power, specialized data-center capacity, and accelerator clusters rather than replicating the broad software ecosystems of AWS [ ], Azure [ ], or Google Cloud [ ].

> **译文：** 这种差距推动了新型云的兴起，这些新型云是围绕人工智能基础设施构建的专业服务提供商。它们专注于获取原始算力、专用数据中心容量和加速器集群，而不是复制 AWS [ ]、Azure [ ] 或 Google Cloud [ ] 等广泛的软件生态系统。

Three distinct neocloud models reported this week, offering a clear view into how different strategies tackle this compute bottleneck:

> **译文：** 本周公布了三种不同的新云模型，清晰地展示了不同的策略如何应对这一计算瓶颈：

CoreWeave: Pure-play NVIDIA GPU fleet rental scaling on long-term enterprise commitments.

> **译文：** CoreWeave：基于长期企业承诺的纯 NVIDIA GPU 集群租赁扩展方案。

Nebius: An AI-focused cloud platform built from scratch on international data-center infrastructure.

> **译文：** Nebius：一个基于国际数据中心基础设施从零开始构建的、专注于人工智能的云平台。

Cerebras: A proprietary chipmaker pivoting into cloud-hosted fast inference.

> **译文：** Cerebras：一家专有芯片制造商，正在转型进入云端快速推理领域。

The economics across all three are counterintuitive. Capacity must be funded and built months before it can generate revenue, driving negative free cash flow. Debt, leases, depreciation, and customer concentration matter almost as much as growth.

> **译文：** 这三者的经济效益都与直觉相悖。产能建设必须提前数月完成，才能产生收入，从而导致自由现金流为负。债务、租赁、折旧和客户集中度几乎与增长同等重要。

Let’s review what we learned.

🧱 CoreWeave is Sold Out

CoreWeave is the purest version of the neocloud model. It buys NVIDIA GPUs, installs them in data centers, and rents the compute to customers such as OpenAI, Microsoft, and Meta.

> **译文：** CoreWeave 是新云模式最纯粹的版本。它购买 NVIDIA GPU，将其安装在数据中心，并将计算资源出租给 OpenAI、微软和 Meta 等客户。

Most of the business is already spoken for. Committed contracts generated 98% of Q2 revenue, while on-demand usage contributed just 2%.

> **译文：** 大部分业务已经确定。已签订的合同贡献了第二季度收入的98%，而按需使用仅贡献了2%。

Revenue jumped 112% Y/Y to $2.6 billion, but gross margin contracted by 8 points to 66% as data-center rent, power, and other ramp costs grew even faster.

> **译文：** 营收同比增长 112% 至 26 亿美元，但由于数据中心租金、电力和其他启动成本增长更快，毛利率下降 8 个百分点至 66%。

CoreWeave posted a $49 million operating loss and a $626 million net loss, heavily weighed down by $640 million in interest expense tied directly to its GPU-collateralized debt facilities.

> **译文：** CoreWeave公布了4900万美元的运营亏损和6.26亿美元的净亏损，其中6.4亿美元的利息支出与其GPU抵押债务融资直接相关，严重拖累了亏损。

And the income statement captures only part of the spending. Q2 CapEx reached $9.4 billion, more than three times quarterly revenue.

> **译文：** 而损益表仅反映了部分支出。第二季度资本支出达到94亿美元，是季度收入的三倍多。

What to make of all this?

📚 Demand keeps outrunning capacity: Backlog reached $104 billion, up 246% Y/Y, before another $25 billion of customer commitments signed in early Q3. Near-term capacity is effectively sold out.

> **译文：** 📚 需求持续超过产能：积压订单达到 1040 亿美元，同比增长 246%，而第三季度初又签署了价值 250 亿美元的客户订单承诺。近期产能实际上已经售罄。

💰 Margins are expected to turn: Gross margin contracted as CoreWeave raced to bring new capacity online, but adjusted operating margin improved sequentially from 1% to 5%. New Q2 contracts are expected to carry contribution margins 5 to 10 points above recent deals, even before July’s roughly 25% price increase.

> **译文：** 💰 利润率预计将会好转：由于CoreWeave加紧投产新增产能，毛利率有所下降，但调整后的营业利润率环比从1%提升至5%。预计第二季度新合同的贡献利润率将比近期交易高出5至10个百分点，即便是在7月份约25%的价格上涨之前也是如此。

🧠 The mix is getting better: Storage, CPU, networking, and software now exceed $400 million of ARR. Managed inference jumped from $1 million to more than $100 million of booked ARR in a single quarter.

> **译文：** 🧠 业务组合正在不断改善：存储、CPU、网络和软件的年度经常性收入 (ARR) 现已超过 4 亿美元。托管推理业务的年度经常性收入在一个季度内从 100 万美元跃升至超过 1 亿美元。

🏗️ Growth remains extremely expensive: CoreWeave raised its 2026 CapEx outlook to $35 billion to $39 billion as it races toward more than 1.85 GW of active power by year-end.

> **译文：** 🏗️ 增长仍然极其昂贵：CoreWeave 将其 2026 年资本支出预期上调至 350 亿美元至 390 亿美元，该公司正努力在年底前实现超过 1.85 吉瓦的活跃发电量。

Bottom line: CoreWeave’s $104 billion backlog sounds almost absurd next to a targeted 2026 exit revenue run rate of $19 billion. But conversion is constrained by physical capacity. The investment case comes down to how quickly it can turn power and GPUs into revenue without letting financing costs overwhelm the margin gains.

> **译文：** 总而言之，CoreWeave 1040亿美元的订单积压听起来几乎不可思议，而其2026年的目标营收年化率仅为190亿美元。但产能转化率受限于物理产能。这项投资的关键在于，它能否在不让融资成本抵消利润增长的情况下，尽快将电力和GPU转化为收入。

☁️ Nebius Finds Pricing Power

Nebius did not start as a typical AI infrastructure startup.

> **译文：** Nebius 最初并不是一家典型的 AI 基础设施初创公司。

🇷🇺 Yandex roots: Nebius emerged from the 2024 breakup of Russian tech giant Yandex. Its Nasdaq-listed Dutch holding company sold the Russian operations for $5.4 billion and kept a smaller group of international businesses that became Nebius.

> **译文：** 🇷🇺 Yandex 的起源：Nebius 源于俄罗斯科技巨头 Yandex 于 2024 年的分拆。其在纳斯达克上市的荷兰控股公司以 54 亿美元的价格出售了俄罗斯业务，并保留了一小部分国际业务，这些业务最终发展成为 Nebius。

♻️ Public-company reset: The remaining company kept its Nasdaq listing, rebranded as Nebius Group, and put Yandex co-founder Arkady Volozh back in charge.

> **译文：** ♻️ 上市公司重组：剩余的公司保留了其纳斯达克上市地位，更名为 Nebius Group，并让 Yandex 联合创始人 Arkady Volozh 重新掌管公司。

☁️ AI infrastructure pivot: Rather than rebuild the old internet conglomerate, Nebius used its engineering talent, cloud expertise, and capital base to build a purpose-built AI cloud.

> **译文：** ☁️ AI 基础设施转型：Nebius 没有重建旧的互联网集团，而是利用其工程人才、云专业知识和资本基础，构建了一个专门的 AI 云。

AI Cloud generated $575 million, or 98% of Q2 revenue, by renting GPU capacity through its own cloud platform.

> **译文：** AI Cloud 通过其自有云平台出租 GPU 容量，创造了 5.75 亿美元的收入，占第二季度收入的 98%。

Revenue surged 454% Y/Y to $582 million, while gross margin expanded 6 points to 77%. The business also generated $236 million of adjusted EBITDA at a 41% margin.

> **译文：** 营收同比增长 454% 至 5.82 亿美元，毛利率增长 6 个百分点至 77%。该业务还创造了 2.36 亿美元的调整后 EBITDA，毛利率为 41%。

Nebius posted a $176 million operating loss, but depreciation and amortization alone reached $260 million as billions of dollars of new infrastructure started hitting the income statement.

> **译文：** Nebius 公布了 1.76 亿美元的运营亏损，但仅折旧和摊销一项就达到了 2.6 亿美元，因为数十亿美元的新基础设施开始计入损益表。

What to make of all this?

💰 Pricing is moving higher: Four new AI cloud deals averaged more than $1 billion of contract value and $20 million to $25 million per megawatt. Shorter-term capacity is now fetching as much as $40 million to $50 million per MW.

> **译文：** 💰 价格持续上涨：四项新的人工智能云交易平均合同价值超过10亿美元，每兆瓦价格在2000万至2500万美元之间。短期容量的价格现在高达每兆瓦4000万至5000万美元。

⏱️ Payback is getting faster: Management estimates the Q2 contracts will repay their associated CapEx and operating costs in about 22 months, down from the previous two-to-three-year range.

> **译文：** ⏱️ 投资回报速度加快：管理层预计第二季度合同将在大约 22 个月内收回相关的资本支出和运营成本，低于之前的两到三年。

🏗️ The buildout is enormous: Q2 CapEx reached $5.7 billion, almost 10 times quarterly revenue. Nebius still expects $20 billion to $25 billion for the year.

> **译文：** 🏗️ 该项目规模庞大：第二季度资本支出达到 57 亿美元，几乎是季度营收的 10 倍。Nebius 仍然预计全年营收将在 200 亿至 250 亿美元之间。

🤝 Customers are helping finance it: Nebius expects more than $9 billion of customer prepayments in 2026, covering roughly 50% to 60% of the associated CapEx.

> **译文：** 🤝 客户正在帮助融资：Nebius 预计 2026 年客户预付款将超过 90 亿美元，约占相关资本支出的 50% 至 60%。

Bottom line: Nebius is spending at extraordinary scale, but rising prices, faster paybacks, and customer prepayments are making each new megawatt more attractive. That capital efficiency may ultimately matter more than its 454% revenue growth.

> **译文：** 总而言之：Nebius 的投资规模惊人，但不断上涨的价格、更快的投资回报以及客户预付款使得每一兆瓦新增装机容量都更具吸引力。这种资本效率最终可能比其 454% 的收入增长更为重要。

🧠 Cerebras Moves to the Cloud

Cerebras is the odd one out among neoclouds. Instead of buying NVIDIA GPUs, it designs its own wafer-scale processor and monetizes it either by selling systems or renting the compute through Cerebras Cloud.

> **译文：** 在众多新型云计算公司中，Cerebras 显得格格不入。它不购买 NVIDIA 的 GPU，而是设计自己的晶圆级处理器，并通过出售系统或通过 Cerebras Cloud 出租计算资源来实现盈利。

The revenue mix is shifting quickly. Q2 revenue rose 74% Y/Y to $180 million, with Cloud & Other Services surging 281% to $126 million while hardware fell 23% to $54 million.

> **译文：** 营收结构正在快速变化。第二季度营收同比增长74%至1.8亿美元，其中云服务及其他服务营收飙升281%至1.26亿美元，而硬件营收则下降23%至5400万美元。

The company had a brutal $477 million operating loss, but the headline needs context. Cerebras went public in May, triggering substantial stock-based compensation. Its core operating loss was just $34 million, compared with $477 million under GAAP.

> **译文：** 该公司亏损高达4.77亿美元，但这一消息需要结合具体情况来看。Cerebras于5月上市，触发了大规模的股票期权激励计划。其核心运营亏损仅为3400万美元，而按照美国通用会计准则（GAAP）计算的亏损则高达4.77亿美元。

Core results exclude stock comp, customer-warrant charges, and certain pass-through items. This stock-based compensation overhang should normalize over upcoming quarters as initial post-IPO equity grants settle.

> **译文：** 核心业绩不包括股权激励、客户认股权证费用以及某些转嫁项目。随着IPO后首批股权授予的结算，这种基于股票的薪酬负担将在未来几个季度内趋于正常化。

Reported gross margin was just 14%, but on a core basis it was 41%, up about 9 points Y/Y. That was down from 46.5% in Q1, partly because Cerebras is temporarily paying to rent back systems it previously sold so it can meet cloud demand.

> **译文：** 报告显示毛利率仅为14%，但按核心业务计算则为41%，同比增长约9个百分点。这低于第一季度的46.5%，部分原因是Cerebras暂时付费回租之前售出的系统，以满足云端需求。

What to make of all this?

☁️ Cloud has become the growth engine. Core cloud revenue nearly quadrupled to $128 million and surpassed hardware for the first time.

> **译文：** ☁️ 云计算已成为增长引擎。核心云收入几乎翻了两番，达到 1.28 亿美元，并首次超过了硬件收入。

📈 The outlook improved. Cerebras raised FY26 core revenue guidance to $880 million to $890 million, alongside higher gross-margin and operating-margin expectations.

> **译文：** 📈 前景有所改善。Cerebras 将 2026 财年核心营收预期上调至 8.8 亿美元至 8.9 亿美元，同时提高了毛利率和营业利润率预期。

🏗️ Capacity is still the bottleneck. More than 600 MW of data-center capacity is now live or contracted through 2027. Core gross margin is expected to bottom in Q3 before new capacity comes online and reduces the need for expensive rented capacity.

> **译文：** 🏗️ 容量仍然是瓶颈。目前已有超过 600 兆瓦的数据中心容量投入使用或已签订合同至 2027 年。预计在第三季度，核心毛利率将触底，届时新的容量将投入使用，从而减少对昂贵租赁容量的需求。

📚 Demand is far ahead of revenue. Remaining performance obligations reached $25.4 billion. OpenAI remains a major customer, but converting that backlog requires substantially more infrastructure.

> **译文：** 📚 需求远超收入。剩余履约义务已达 254 亿美元。OpenAI 仍然是主要客户，但要兑现这些积压订单，需要大幅增加基础设施。

Bottom line: Cerebras is evolving from a chip seller into a fast-inference cloud. Stock comp and other accounting adjustments obscure that progress, but the real test is converting its enormous backlog into revenue while rebuilding margins as new capacity comes online.

> **译文：** 总而言之，Cerebras 正在从一家芯片销售商转型为一家快速推理云服务提供商。股票价格调整和其他会计调整掩盖了这一进展，但真正的考验在于如何将庞大的积压订单转化为收入，并在新产能上线后重建利润率。

What to Watch

Across all three neoclouds, the same tension keeps showing up. Demand is outpacing available compute, but serving it requires enormous amounts of capital before revenue arrives.

> **译文：** 在所有三个新型云平台中，同样的矛盾反复出现。计算需求超过了可用计算能力，但满足这些需求需要巨额资金投入，才能在产生收益之前完成。

That same compute shortage is pushing tech giants to build their own capacity. Meta is scaling custom silicon and gigawatts of GPUs, while SpaceX has already started selling access to its Colossus cluster. The longer-term question is whether neoclouds remain essential infrastructure partners or temporary relief valves once mega-cap AI capacity fully comes online.

> **译文：** 同样的计算资源短缺也促使科技巨头们构建自己的计算能力。Meta公司正在扩展定制芯片和数千瓦级GPU，而SpaceX已经开始出售其Colossus集群的使用权。更长远的问题是，一旦超大规模人工智能计算能力全面上线，新型云计算究竟是继续作为重要的基础设施合作伙伴，还是仅仅作为临时的缓解措施。

The next phase comes down to capacity, margins, and funding. Neoclouds need to turn contracted demand into energized infrastructure, improve returns as utilization rises, and fund the next wave of expansion without letting debt or dilution overwhelm the economics.

> **译文：** 下一阶段的关键在于容量、利润率和资金。新型云需要将已签订合同的需求转化为活跃的基础设施，随着利用率的提高而提升回报，并在不让债务或股权稀释影响经济效益的前提下，为下一波扩张提供资金。

The demand is locked in. Capital efficiency will separate the winners.

> **译文：** 需求已锁定。资本效率将决定最终的赢家。

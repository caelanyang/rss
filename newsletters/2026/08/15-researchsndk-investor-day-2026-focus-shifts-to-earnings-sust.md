The Long-Term Sustainable Model SanDisk presented at its Investor Day reinforces the view we set out in our takeaway from the Future of Memory and Storage Conference 2026: the recent deceleration in ASP gains is a healthy development for the memory market, not a signal of impending industry oversupply. With LTAs/NBMs taking a rising share of supply and the product mix shifting further toward data centers, we expect memory pricing to hover near elevated levels rather than collapse after the peak. Memory vendors’ earnings power should therefore prove more sustainable through a prolonged period of tight supply.

> **译文：** SanDisk在投资者日上提出的长期可持续发展模式，进一步印证了我们在2026年未来存储大会上的观点：近期平均售价增速放缓对内存市场而言是一个健康的趋势，而非即将出现行业供过于求的信号。随着LTA/NBM在供应中所占份额的不断提升，以及产品组合进一步向数据中心领域倾斜，我们预计内存价格将在接近高位后保持稳定，而非在达到峰值后暴跌。因此，在供应长期紧张的情况下，内存厂商的盈利能力应该能够得到更充分的保障。

We currently model a mild pricing pullback in CY2028 and a mid-to-high-teens bit shipment CAGR for FY2028–FY2030, in line with the company’s long-term bit supply growth target. Under this base case, we project CY2027/28/29 EPS of $292/$334/$388, implying forward P/E multiples of 5.2x/4.6x/3.9x at the current share price.

> **译文：** 我们目前预测2028年价格将出现小幅回调，2028财年至2030财年期间，钻头出货量复合年增长率将保持在15%至20%之间，这与公司长期钻头供应增长目标相符。在此基准情景下，我们预计2027/28/29财年每股收益分别为292美元/334美元/388美元，按当前股价计算，相应的预期市盈率分别为5.2倍/4.6倍/3.9倍。

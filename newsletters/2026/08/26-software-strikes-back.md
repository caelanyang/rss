Software finally had a good night

Investors have spent much of the year questioning whether AI will accelerate software growth or slowly eat into it.

> **译文：** 今年大部分时间里，投资者都在质疑人工智能究竟会加速软件行业的增长，还是会慢慢蚕食软件行业。

On Wednesday, two of the industry’s biggest names gave them something else to think about.

> **译文：** 周三，业内两位重量级人物让他们有了新的思考方向。

CrowdStrike delivered record net new ARR and raised its outlook again. Salesforce posted its strongest new business growth in four years and pointed to organic revenue reacceleration ahead. Both stocks surged more than 10% after hours.

> **译文：**
> CrowdStrike公布了创纪录的净新增年度经常性收入（ARR），并再次上调了业绩预期。Salesforce公布了四年来最强劲的新业务增长，并指出未来有机收入将再次加速增长。两家公司的股票在盘后交易中均上涨超过10%。

But the more interesting part is where the growth is showing up and what the market may have missed by lumping all software into the same basket.

> **译文：** 但更有趣的是，增长体现在哪些方面，以及市场将所有软件都归为一类可能错失了哪些机会。

🦅 CrowdStrike: What Doesn’t Kill It

For years, the CrowdStrike investment thesis has been that every new threat makes Threat Graph, the AI brain behind Falcon, smarter. The past two years suggest something similar about the company itself.

> **译文：** 多年来，CrowdStrike 的投资理念一直是：每出现一种新的威胁，Falcon 背后的 AI 核心——威胁图谱（Threat
> Graph）就会变得更加智能。过去两年的发展也印证了这一点。

In July 2024, a faulty update caused one of the largest IT outages in history and put customer trust under extraordinary pressure. Two years later, CrowdStrike just delivered what CEO George Kurtz called the best quarter in company history.

> **译文：**
> 2024年7月，一次错误的系统更新引发了历史上规模最大的IT故障之一，并给客户信任带来了巨大压力。两年后，CrowdStrike刚刚实现了首席执行官乔治·库尔茨所称的公司历史上最佳季度业绩。

Revenue grew 26% Y/Y to $1.47 billion ($30 million beat), marking the fifth consecutive quarter of acceleration, while non-GAAP EPS reached $0.31 ($0.02 beat).

> **译文：** 营收同比增长 26% 至 14.7 亿美元（超出预期 3000 万美元），连续第五个季度实现加速增长；非 GAAP 每股收益达到
> 0.31 美元（超出预期 0.02 美元）。

Non-GAAP operating income jumped 46% to a record $372 million, a 25% margin, while free cash flow reached a Q2 record $377 million, up 33% Y/Y.

> **译文：**
> 非GAAP营业收入跃升46%，达到创纪录的3.72亿美元，利润率为25%；自由现金流达到第二季度创纪录的3.77亿美元，同比增长33%。

The GAAP picture remains less flattering, with the company still showing an operating loss margin of 2%. The main reason was stock-based compensation , which accounted for roughly 26% of revenue.

> **译文：** 按美国通用会计准则（GAAP）计算，情况依然不容乐观，公司营业亏损率仍高达2%。主要原因是股票期权激励支出，约占收入的26%。

The recurring-revenue engine remains the main KPI:

> **译文：** 经常性收入引擎仍然是主要的关键绩效指标：

Ending ARR grew 25% Y/Y to $5.84 billion, continuing a remarkably predictable trajectory.

> **译文：** 期末年度经常性收入同比增长 25% 至 58.4 亿美元，延续了此前可预测的增长轨迹。

Net new ARR reached an all-time record $333 million, up 51% Y/Y, accelerating sharply from +32% last quarter. New-logo net new ARR also hit a record, while both gross and net retention improved, showing that the acceleration extends beyond existing customers buying more modules.

> **译文：**
> 新增净ARR达到创纪录的3.33亿美元，同比增长51%，较上季度的32%增幅显著提升。新客户新增净ARR也创下新高，同时毛留存率和净留存率均有所提高，表明增长势头不仅限于现有客户购买更多模块。

The Flex Flywheel

Ending ARR from Flex customers surged 101% Y/Y to $2.29 billion, already approaching 40% of CrowdStrike’s total ARR. More than 935 accounts adopted Flex during the quarter.

> **译文：** 来自 Flex 客户的期末年度经常性收入 (ARR) 同比增长 101%，达到 22.9 亿美元，已接近 CrowdStrike
> 总 ARR 的 40%。本季度共有超过 935 个账户采用了 Flex 服务。

Customers moving to Flex increased ending ARR by roughly 40% on average, while platform adoption continued to deepen.

> **译文：** 迁移到 Flex 的客户平均使期末 ARR 增长了约 40%，同时平台采用率也持续加深。

The appeal of Flex is simple: customers commit a security budget upfront and can deploy it across Falcon modules over time, making it easier for CrowdStrike to consolidate more security workloads onto a single platform.

> **译文：** Flex 的吸引力很简单：客户预先投入安全预算，并可以随着时间的推移将其部署到 Falcon 模块中，这使得
> CrowdStrike 更容易将更多安全工作负载整合到单个平台上。

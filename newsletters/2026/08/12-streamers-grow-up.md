📺 Streaming’s coming of age

For years, media companies treated streaming like a land grab. That era is ending.

> **译文：** 多年来，媒体公司一直把流媒体视为争夺地盘的工具。这种时代正在结束。

Streaming is starting to look like a mature business. Disney reached a 13% streaming margin, Paramount hit 15%, and Warner Bros. came in at nearly 17%. Subscriber totals matter less than churn, pricing, engagement, and how much profit each viewer can generate.

> **译文：** 流媒体业务正逐渐走向成熟。迪士尼的流媒体利润率达到了13%，派拉蒙达到了15%，华纳兄弟也接近17%。用户总数的重要性远不及用户流失率、定价、用户参与度以及每位用户能带来的利润。

Meanwhile, the businesses streaming is replacing keep shrinking. Warner’s Networks revenue fell 17%. Paramount’s TV Media declined 9%. Cord-cutting and weaker advertising continue to eat away at linear TV.

> **译文：** 与此同时，流媒体正在取代的传统电视业务持续萎缩。华纳电视网的收入下降了17%，派拉蒙电视媒体的收入下降了9%。用户取消有线电视订阅以及广告收入疲软继续蚕食着传统电视的市场份额。

Paramount’s Warner Bros. deal has cleared most international regulators, but a US antitrust fight has pushed the timeline into 2027. The longer it drags, the more expensive the deal becomes.

> **译文：** 派拉蒙收购华纳兄弟的交易已经获得了大多数国际监管机构的批准，但美国的反垄断诉讼将交易完成时间推迟到了2027年。拖得越久，交易成本就越高。

Can streaming profits grow fast enough to outrun the decline of the old bundle? And how expensive could the merger delay become?

> **译文：** 流媒体服务的利润增长速度能否足以弥补原有捆绑服务业务的下滑？合并延迟又会造成多大的损失？

🏰 Disney: Parks Answer the Doubters

Disney’s fiscal year ends in September, so the June quarter was Q3 FY26.

> **译文：** 迪士尼的财政年度于 9 月结束，因此 6 月份的季度是 2026 财年第三季度。

📸 Big picture: Revenue rose +7% Y/Y to $25.2 billion ($0.2 billion miss), while adjusted EPS jumped +28% to $2.06 ($0.21 beat). Total segment operating income rose +21% to $5.6 billion, ahead of expectations. Disney maintained its FY26 outlook for ~12% adjusted EPS growth and raised its buyback target again to at least $9 billion.

> **译文：** 📸 总体而言：营收同比增长7%至252亿美元（低于预期2亿美元），调整后每股收益增长28%至2.06美元（超出预期0.21美元）。部门总营业收入增长21%至56亿美元，超出预期。迪士尼维持其2026财年调整后每股收益增长约12%的预期，并再次上调股票回购目标至至少90亿美元。

📈 Streaming margin expands again: Disney+/Hulu revenue grew +11% to $5.5 billion, while SVOD operating margin reached 13%, extending last quarter’s profitability inflection. Disney remains on track for double-digit streaming margins in FY26, though management says international monetization still has room to improve.

> **译文：** 📈 流媒体利润率再次扩大：Disney+/Hulu 营收增长 11% 至 55 亿美元，SVOD 运营利润率达到 13%，延续了上季度的盈利增长势头。迪士尼仍有望在 2026 财年实现两位数的流媒体利润率，但管理层表示，国际业务的盈利能力仍有提升空间。

🍿 Entertainment gets its hit: Entertainment operating income surged +64% Y/Y, helped by streaming profitability and Toy Story 5, which crossed $1 billion at the global box office. The film also lifted merchandise sales and Disney+ engagement, showing how a successful franchise can reverberate across the company.

> **译文：** 🍿 娱乐业务迎来爆发：娱乐业务营业收入同比增长 64%，这主要得益于流媒体盈利能力和《玩具总动员5》全球票房突破 10 亿美元大关。该片还带动了周边产品销售和 Disney+ 用户参与度，展现了成功的系列电影如何对整个公司产生深远影响。

🏰 Experiences answer the skeptics: Experiences revenue rose +10% to a record $10.0 billion, while operating income jumped +20% to $3.0 billion. Domestic park attendance grew +3%, and per-guest spending rose +4%, with Walt Disney World having a particularly strong quarter. International visitation remains soft, but forward bookings are healthy.

> **译文：** 🏰 体验业务的强劲表现让质疑者哑口无言：体验业务收入增长10%，创下100亿美元的纪录；营业利润增长20%，达到30亿美元。国内乐园游客人数增长3%，人均消费增长4%，其中华特迪士尼世界表现尤为突出。国际游客数量依然疲软，但预订情况良好。

🏈 Sports remains the weak spot: Sports revenue reached roughly $4.5 billion, while operating income fell -17% to $858 million, hurt by shorter NBA playoff series and rights timing. ESPN remains the clearest drag on Disney’s otherwise improving profit mix.

> **译文：** 🏈 体育业务依然是迪士尼的薄弱环节：体育业务收入约为45亿美元，而营业利润下降17%至8.58亿美元，主要受NBA季后赛赛程缩短和版权时间安排的影响。ESPN仍然是迪士尼整体盈利能力提升的最大拖累因素。

🤖 Disney+ gets a roadmap: CEO Josh D’Amaro said Disney will begin expanding Disney+ beyond video in spring 2027, adding games, merchandise, and other experiences designed to lower churn and increase lifetime fan value. Disney is also considering free ad-supported offerings as it turns Disney+ into the company’s broader digital hub.

> **译文：** 🤖 Disney+发布发展路线图：首席执行官乔什·达马罗表示，迪士尼将于2027年春季开始拓展Disney+的内容，不再局限于视频，而是增加游戏、周边商品和其他体验，旨在降低用户流失率并提升粉丝终身价值。迪士尼也在考虑推出免费的广告支持内容，力求将Disney+打造成为公司更广泛的数字平台。

Bottom Line: Streaming profitability is becoming repeatable, while Experiences just delivered the quarter investors feared it couldn’t. That gives D’Amaro more room to execute his “One Disney” strategy, with Disney+ increasingly positioned as the front door to content, commerce, and experiences.

> **译文：** 结论：流媒体业务的盈利能力正趋于稳定，而体验业务也刚刚实现了投资者此前担忧的业绩。这为达马罗实施其“一个迪士尼”战略提供了更多空间，迪士尼+正日益成为内容、商务和体验的门户。

APP Budget Trends

We recently interviewed two gaming-company experts and four agency experts on recent AppLovin budgets.

> **译文：** 我们最近采访了两位游戏公司专家和四位代理机构专家，了解 AppLovin 的最新预算情况。

Agency A

AppLovin Budget Growth

YoY growth from 1Q25 to 1Q26: 56% / 30.4% / 97% / 68.6% / 132.8%.

> **译文：** 2025 年第一季度至 2026 年第一季度同比增长率：56% / 30.4% / 97% / 68.6% / 132.8%。

In April, the expert expected 2Q26 YoY growth of 65.5%. Actual growth materially exceeded expectations at 74.2%.

> **译文：** 今年4月，专家预测2026年第二季度同比增长65.5%。实际增长率大幅超出预期，达到74.2%。

The expert expects 3Q26 YoY growth of 77.4%.

At the end of 2025, the forecast for 2026 YoY growth was 44%. Actual performance has been far stronger.

> **译文：** 2025年底，对2026年同比增长率的预测为44%。实际表现远超预期。

Why 2Q26 exceeded expectations

2Q last year was already a relatively high base for AppLovin, as AXON gained traction. E-commerce also began ramping from 3Q last year. Growth has remained healthy despite the tougher comparison.

> **译文：** 去年第二季度AppLovin的基数已经相对较高，这得益于AXON的快速发展。此外，电商业务也从去年第三季度开始加速增长。尽管去年同期基数较高，但AppLovin的增长依然保持稳健。

With 3Q growth expected at roughly 77% to 79%, the full-year AppLovin growth outlook should move higher. Estimates are still being revised, but could rise by roughly 5% to 7%.

> **译文：** 预计第三季度增长率约为77%至79%，AppLovin全年增长预期应该会更高。目前预测仍在修正中，但可能会上调约5%至7%。

Gaming-client growth eased modestly this quarter, while e-commerce clients performed well.

> **译文：** 本季度游戏客户增长略有放缓，而电子商务客户表现良好。

E-commerce advertising

E-commerce represented 34% of spend in Q2, is expected to reach 38% in Q3 and 42% to 45% in Q4. The 2026 mix is expected at 38% to 40%.

> **译文：** 第二季度电子商务支出占总支出的34%，预计第三季度将达到38%，第四季度将达到42%至45%。预计到2026年，这一比例将达到38%至40%。

Client additions have been strong. Google clients, DTC e-commerce advertisers and advertisers from other platforms are migrating to AppLovin.

> **译文：** 客户增长势头强劲。谷歌客户、DTC电商广告商以及其他平台的广告商都在迁移到AppLovin。

The number of new e-commerce advertisers may rise roughly 20% to 40% YoY and 20% to 25% QoQ.

> **译文：** 新增电子商务广告商数量可能同比增长约 20% 至 40%，环比增长约 20% 至 25%。

Gaming advertising

Gaming-advertiser count declined slightly, with some budget moving to Meta and a smaller amount to Google. Meta’s Advantage+ offering and gaming AAC activity have pulled some IAA spend away.

> **译文：** 游戏广告商数量略有下降，部分预算转移到了 Meta，少量转移到了 Google。Meta 的 Advantage+ 服务和游戏 AAC 活动吸引了部分 IAA 支出。

Meta took a limited amount of AppLovin gaming budget in Q2, likely from only one or two clients and not their full budgets. For example, a client with 50% of spend on Meta may shift less than 10% to 20% there. The absolute impact on AppLovin budget was not meaningful.

> **译文：** Meta在第二季度占用了AppLovin有限的游戏预算，可能仅来自一两个客户，而非全部预算。例如，一个客户在Meta上的支出占其总支出的50%，可能只会将其中不到10%到20%的预算转移到Meta。因此，对AppLovin预算的绝对影响微乎其微。

Impact of e-commerce budgets on gaming budgets

User-acquisition costs for gaming clients remain elevated. CPM increased 4.7% YoY in 1Q, 5.2% YoY in 4Q last year, and 8.6% YoY in 2Q26.

> **译文：** 游戏客户端的用户获取成本仍然居高不下。第一季度 CPM 同比增长 4.7%，去年第四季度同比增长 5.2%，2026 年第二季度同比增长 8.6%。

The influx of e-commerce advertisers is putting upward pressure on overall traffic costs.

> **译文：** 电子商务广告商的涌入给整体流量成本带来了上行压力。

Higher conversion rates encourage advertisers to bid more aggressively. At the operating level, CPM was still higher even when ROI targets were unchanged and the objective was to protect ROI rather than scale. We believe both factors drove the 8.6% increase.

> **译文：** 更高的转化率促使广告主更积极地出价。在运营层面，即使投资回报率目标不变，且目标是保护投资回报率而非扩大规模，CPM 仍然较高。我们认为这两个因素共同导致了 8.6% 的增长。

AXON product updates

AXON continues to strengthen its infrastructure, including second-level bid updates, richer Discovery data feedback, longer user-runtime data with stronger signals out to roughly D28, and improved final reporting in the testing environment.

> **译文：** AXON 继续加强其基础设施，包括二级竞价更新、更丰富的发现数据反馈、更长的用户运行时间数据（信号强度增强至大约 D28），以及在测试环境中改进的最终报告。

On these enhancements, ad performance this quarter has surpassed Unity. Q2 CPM rose 8.6%, CTR rose 7.2% and CVR rose 10.8%, all ahead of Unity. D1 retention improved 5.2%, D28 retention improved 10.3%, and ROAS improved 23.4%. Most metrics outperformed Unity.

> **译文：** 得益于这些改进，本季度广告效果已超越 Unity。第二季度 CPM 增长 8.6%，点击率 (CTR) 增长 7.2%，转化率 (CVR) 增长 10.8%，均高于 Unity。首日留存率提升 5.2%，第 28 天留存率提升 10.3%，广告支出回报率 (ROAS) 提升 23.4%。大多数指标均优于 Unity。

Unity had been growing faster than AppLovin over the past several quarters, but AppLovin pulled ahead this quarter. While some gaming budget moved to Meta, AppLovin also captured meaningful budget from Unity. Its D28 results reflect both quality improvements and infrastructure, including data feedback and signal enhancement.

> **译文：** 过去几个季度，Unity 的增长速度一直超过 AppLovin，但本季度 AppLovin 反超了 Unity。虽然部分游戏预算转移到了 Meta，但 AppLovin 也从 Unity 那里获得了相当可观的预算。其 D28 业绩反映了质量提升和基础设施的改进，包括数据反馈和信号增强。

CTV

CTV remains a small mix but is growing fastest. CTV ad budget grew roughly 70% YoY and represented less than 8% of spend. Web spend is smaller but growing at a similar pace.

> **译文：** CTV广告占比虽小，但增长速度最快。CTV广告预算同比增长约70%，占总支出的不到8%。网络广告支出规模较小，但增长速度与CTV广告相近。

Many gaming clients can automatically generate ad creative from their existing asset libraries for web campaigns. SparkLabs and other AI capabilities are improving automated creative integration and supporting web demand.

> **译文：** 许多游戏客户端可以利用其现有素材库自动生成用于网络广告活动的广告素材。SparkLabs 和其他人工智能技术正在改进自动化素材整合，并满足网络广告需求。

Inventory is ample. More accurately, client demand has yet to fully ramp.

> **译文：** 库存充足。更准确地说，客户需求尚未完全恢复。

App Store Link-out

This currently saves gaming companies roughly 3% to 6% in costs, with most savings reinvested in technology vendors.

> **译文：** 目前，这可以为游戏公司节省大约 3% 到 6% 的成本，而节省下来的大部分成本都会重新投资于技术供应商。

Could drive 1% to 2% growth in advertising.

Agency B

AppLovin Ad Budget Growth

2Q25 QoQ growth was 7%, 3Q25 was 10%, and 4Q25 was 9% to 9.5%.

> **译文：** 2025 年第二季度环比增长 7%，2025 年第三季度环比增长 10%，2025 年第四季度环比增长 9% 至 9.5%。

1Q26 QoQ growth was 1%; 2Q26 was 8%, in line with expectations.

> **译文：** 2026 年第一季度环比增长 1%；2026 年第二季度环比增长 8%，符合预期。

Impact of e-commerce budgets on gaming budgets

AppLovin optimizes operations by vertical, so e-commerce and gaming do not directly affect one another. Competition is mainly within a vertical. For example, beauty advertisers compete for similar inventory, while gaming and beauty target different user cohorts and traffic pools.

> **译文：** AppLovin 按垂直领域优化运营，因此电商和游戏不会直接相互影响。竞争主要发生在同一垂直领域内部。例如，美妆广告商竞争类似的广告位，而游戏和美妆的目标用户群体和流量来源则有所不同。

CTV

CTV still feels more like a narrative than a material contributor. Advertisers remain more focused on AXON.

> **译文：** CTV 仍然更像是叙事媒介而非内容贡献者。广告商仍然更关注 AXON。

Agency C

AppLovin Ad Budget Growth and Advertiser Count

Advertiser count was 24 in September and 45 in December. Monthly spend was $2.38m in 3Q25 and $3.8m in 4Q25.

> **译文：** 9 月份广告客户数量为 24 家，12 月份为 45 家。2025 年第三季度月支出为 238 万美元，2025 年第四季度月支出为 380 万美元。

Advertiser count reached 52 in 1Q26, in line with expectations. Monthly spend was $4.52m, above the $4.28m forecast.

> **译文：** 2026年第一季度广告客户数量达到52家，符合预期。月度支出为452万美元，高于此前预测的428万美元。

In April, the 2Q26 forecast was 59 advertisers and $5.47m in monthly spend. Actual advertiser count was slightly lower at 58, but monthly spend was higher at $5.59m.

> **译文：** 4月份，对2026年第二季度的预测是广告商数量为59家，月支出为547万美元。实际广告商数量略低，为58家，但月支出更高，为559万美元。

In April, the expert expected 65 advertisers and $6.51m of monthly spend in 3Q26. The current forecast is 63 advertisers and $6.73m.

> **译文：** 今年4月，专家预测2026年第三季度将有65家广告商，月支出为651万美元。目前的预测是63家广告商，月支出为673万美元。

4Q26 is expected to have 66 advertisers and $8.59m of spend. Non-e-commerce spend is expected at $7.7m, making 4Q26 the first quarter in which e-commerce advertisers exceed non-e-commerce advertisers.

> **译文：** 预计2026年第四季度将有66家广告商，广告支出达859万美元。非电商广告支出预计为770万美元，这将使2026年第四季度成为电商广告商数量首次超过非电商广告商的季度。

“Given our position, we believe e-commerce will ultimately account for 80% of AppLovin budget.”

> **译文：** “鉴于我们的市场地位，我们相信电子商务最终将占 AppLovin 预算的 80%。”

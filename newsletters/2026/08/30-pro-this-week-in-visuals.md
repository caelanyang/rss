> **译文：** 🏥 HealthEquity：利润率持续攀升 1. 📶 Marvell：定制芯片突破

Marvell Q2 FY27 revenue (ending August 1st) rose 37% Y/Y to a record $2.74 billion ($30 million beat), with non-GAAP EPS of $0.94 ($0.01 beat).

> **译文：** Marvell 2027财年第二季度（截至8月1日）营收同比增长37%，创纪录达到27.4亿美元（超出预期3000万美元），非GAAP每股收益为0.94美元（超出预期0.01美元）。

Data Center revenue jumped 46% to $2.17 billion, representing 79% of total sales, as demand for AI infrastructure remained exceptionally strong.

> **译文：** 数据中心营收跃升46%至21.7亿美元，占总营收的79%，AI基础设施需求依然异常强劲。

Custom silicon should bring the next leg of growth. Management expects a significant acceleration beginning in H2 FY27, followed by custom silicon revenue more than doubling in FY28 as hyperscalers increasingly design their own AI chips. Marvell also expanded its partnership with Google across AI accelerators, storage, networking, and near-memory compute, backed by a six-year warrant agreement.

> **译文：** 定制芯片将带来下一轮增长。管理层预计2027财年下半年起将显著加速，随后在2028财年定制芯片营收将增长一倍以上，因为超大规模云厂商越来越多地自行设计AI芯片。Marvell还扩大了与Google在AI加速器、存储、网络和近内存计算领域的合作，并签署了为期六年的认股权证协议。

Connectivity remains a major driver. Demand for optical interconnects is accelerating as AI clusters require more bandwidth, while Marvell called its CXL memory-expansion business a “home run” with deployments across multiple hyperscalers.

> **译文：** 连接业务仍是主要驱动力。随着AI集群需要更高带宽，光互连需求正在加速增长，同时Marvell称其CXL内存扩展业务是“本垒打”，已在多家超大规模云厂商中部署。

Marvell now expects FY27 revenue of roughly $12 billion (up from $11.5 billion previously) and raised its FY28 target to $18 billion (up from $16.5 billion). Q3 revenue guidance of $3.15 billion was also well ahead of consensus and implies another ~15% sequential jump.

> **译文：** Marvell目前预计2027财年营收约为120亿美元（高于此前预期的115亿美元），并将2028财年目标上调至180亿美元（高于此前预期的165亿美元）。第三季度营收指引为31.5亿美元，也大幅超出市场共识，意味着环比再增长约15%。

Bottom Line: Marvell’s AI story is broadening from connectivity into custom compute. With custom silicon set to accelerate sharply, management is raising expectations faster than the current quarter alone would suggest. But expectations are already enormous: the stock trades over 50x forward earnings, and much of the Google opportunity through FY28 was already embedded in guidance. That helps explain why shares fell despite Marvell raising both FY27 and FY28 outlooks.

> **译文：** 底线：Marvell的AI故事正从连接扩展到定制计算。随着定制芯片即将大幅加速，管理层正在以比当前季度本身所暗示的更快的速度上调预期。但市场预期已经非常之高：该股交易价格超过远期收益的50倍，且到2028财年的Google机会大部分已包含在指引中。这有助于解释为何尽管Marvell上调了2027和2028财年展望，股价仍然下跌。

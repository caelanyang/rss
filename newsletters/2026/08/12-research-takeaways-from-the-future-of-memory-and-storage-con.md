We attended the Future of Memory and Storage conference (FMS) last week. This note sets out our top-level takeaways.

> **译文：** 我们上周参加了未来内存与存储大会（FMS）。本文总结了我们的主要收获。

A clear theme at the conference was the need to reduce dependence on DRAM and HBM, and to look to solutions that address every part of the memory and storage hierarchy (now often referred to by Nvidia’s G1 to G4 labels).

> **译文：** 本次大会的一个明确主题是需要减少对 DRAM 和 HBM 的依赖，并寻求能够解决内存和存储层次结构各个部分（现在通常用英伟达的 G1 到 G4 标签来指代）的解决方案。

This was echoed by Micron’s comment at the KeyBanc forum yesterday that the number one constraint faced by their customers is DRAM.

> **译文：** 昨天，美光在 KeyBanc 论坛上也发表了类似的评论，称其客户面临的最大制约因素是 DRAM。

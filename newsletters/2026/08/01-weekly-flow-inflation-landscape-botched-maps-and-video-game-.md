Timeline of Supreme Court justices, who appointed them and when — The U.S. Supreme Court has had 116 justices over all American history. USAFacts made a timeline that shows the tenure of each justice, alongside who appointed them  .


> **译文：** 美国最高法院大法官任命时间线——美国最高法院自成立以来共有116位大法官。USAFacts制作了一条时间线，展示了每位大法官的任期以及任命人。

------------------------------------------------------------

Crypto empire of the president — The U.S. president sits atop a crypto network through his family and LLCs, adding billions in wealth, which all feels kind of scammy. Molly White, for Citation Needed, pieced together the network  using data from financial filings.


> **译文：** 总统的加密货币帝国——美国总统通过其家族和有限责任公司掌控着一个加密货币网络，积累了数十亿美元的财富，这一切都让人感觉疑点重重。莫莉·怀特（Molly White）为“需要引用”（Citation Needed）网站撰稿，利用财务文件中的数据拼凑出了这个网络。

------------------------------------------------------------

Sons of the president invest in defense businesses — The Washington Post reports on the large investments in defense tech by the Trump sons  :


> **译文：** 总统之子投资国防企业——《华盛顿邮报》报道了特朗普之子对国防科技领域的巨额投资：

Neither Eric Trump nor Trump Jr., vice presidents at the Trump Organization who do not hold official roles in the federal government, had notable experience in the defense tech industry before they began investing heavily in companies seeking Pentagon and other federal contracts.


> **译文：** 特朗普集团副总裁埃里克·特朗普和特朗普二世（两人均未在联邦政府担任正式职务）在开始大量投资寻求五角大楼和其他联邦合同的公司之前，在国防科技行业都没有显著的经验。

A network diagram shows the links to the various investments, which are definitely in no way linked to their father and the spike over the past couple years is purely coincidence.


> **译文：** 网络图显示了与各项投资的联系，这些投资绝对与他们的父亲没有任何关系，而过去几年的激增纯属巧合。

------------------------------------------------------------

Timeline of the war in Iran, seen through daily attacks — Adina Renner, reporting for the New York Times, provides a timeline of the war in Iran through the lens of daily attacks  .


> **译文：** 从每日袭击事件看伊朗战争的时间线——《纽约时报》记者阿迪娜·雷纳通过每日袭击事件的视角，提供了伊朗战争的时间线。

------------------------------------------------------------

Map of James Bond filming locations and notable events — I like the framing of this interactive map “from a Bond fan to Bond Fans worldwide  .” See filming locations, places in the books, and residency of the tailors. I’m actually not a James Bond fan, but this project makes me want to look more.


> **译文：** 詹姆斯·邦德电影拍摄地点及重要事件地图——我喜欢这张互动地图的构想：“来自一位邦德影迷，献给全世界的邦德影迷”。地图上标注了拍摄地点、小说中的场景以及裁缝的住所。我其实不是詹姆斯·邦德的粉丝，但这个项目让我很想深入了解一下。

------------------------------------------------------------

Hand-drawn, retro video game maps — This one ticks all the right boxes for me. Hand-Drawn Game Guides   is a project by Philip Summers that is exactly what it sounds like. Summers illustrates guides and maps for retro video games, such as Legend of Zelda, Mega Man, and Contra.


> **译文：** 手绘复古游戏地图——这正合我意。“手绘游戏指南”是菲利普·萨默斯（Philip Summers）的一个项目，顾名思义，它就是手绘游戏指南和地图。萨默斯为《塞尔达传说》、《洛克人》和《魂斗罗》等复古游戏绘制地图和攻略。

------------------------------------------------------------

Google makes it easy to generate fake satellite imagery — Google’s image generation model Nano Banana is now a few clicks away in Google Earth  , so you can use satellite views as your canvas and imagine a community, city, and world how you want it. It’s like SimCity but more real.


> **译文：** 谷歌让生成虚拟卫星图像变得轻而易举——谷歌的图像生成模型 Nano Banana 现在只需在谷歌地球中点击几下即可使用，因此您可以将卫星视图作为画布，随心所欲地想象社区、城市乃至世界。它就像模拟城市，但更加真实。

That sounds fun, until people with ill intentions come along. Henk van Ess describes the dangers and demonstrates how easy it is to fake images  .


> **译文：** 听起来很有趣，直到心怀不轨的人出现。亨克·范·埃斯描述了其中的危险，并演示了伪造图像是多么容易。

------------------------------------------------------------

Botched map of Africa in U.S. government presentation — In a presentation by the State Department, a generated map provides all the highlighted countries mislabeled  .


> **译文：** 美国政府演示文稿中的非洲地图有误——在国务院的一份演示文稿中，生成的地图上所有突出显示的国家都标注错误。

------------------------------------------------------------

For the Pudding, Jasmine Nackash compared the dictionary of essential words   taught to English language learners in 1953 to the current dictionary updated in 2023. The word shift is a reflection of our surroundings.


> **译文：** 在布丁环节，贾斯敏·纳卡什将 1953 年英语学习者学习的基本词汇词典与 2023 年更新的现行词典进行了比较。“词义转变”反映了我们周围的环境。

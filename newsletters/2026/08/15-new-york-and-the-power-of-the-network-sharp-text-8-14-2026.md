New York and the Power of the Network

Friday, August 14, 2026

Eugene Gologursky/Getty Images

At the end of last week’s Sharp Tech , we had a great time retracing the ruthless steps that Ken Griffin and his Citadel hedge fund took to acquire Leopold Aschenbrenner’s Situational Awareness book at a steep discount in the middle of Aschenbrenner’s multi-day wedding in Carmel, California . Then the day after we recorded that episode, with Griffin still on the mind, I came across news that Citadel will not be responding to Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s hostile messaging and aggressive tax policies by unwinding the company’s plans for massive new offices in New York City. Instead, the fund reaffirmed its participation in what will be a multi-billion dollar project for a redeveloped skyscraper that will house Citadel’s New York offices on a 15-year lease agreement.

> **译文：** 在上周的《Sharp Tech》节目结尾，我们饶有兴致地回顾了肯·格里芬和他的城堡投资集团（Citadel）是如何在利奥波德·阿申布伦纳于加州卡梅尔举行多日婚礼期间，以极低的价格收购其著作《情境意识》（Situational Awareness）的。就在我们录制完那期节目的第二天，格里芬的事件仍然萦绕在我心头，我却看到消息称，城堡投资集团不会因为市长佐兰·马姆达尼的敌对言论和激进的税收政策而取消在纽约市新建大型办公楼的计划。相反，该基金重申了其参与一项价值数十亿美元的摩天大楼重建项目，该项目将以15年的租赁协议为城堡投资集团在纽约的办公场所提供办公空间。

Digesting that story a few days ago sent me down two parallel rabbit holes that I found pretty interesting, so let’s start with Citadel. Here was last week’s news, from Fortune :

> **译文：** 几天前读到那则新闻后，我又由此展开了两个颇为有趣的平行研究，所以我们先从 Citadel 开始吧。以下是上周《财富》杂志的相关报道：

One filmed a widely regarded publicity “stunt” outside the other’s $238 million penthouse. The latter responded by telling the former he was doxxing him, told him to read a history book, and waved a major $4.5 billion project over the former’s head. Months passed, a new tax was introduced, and fans of both sides charged the other. And after all this back and forth, nothing changed.

> **译文：** 其中一人在另一人价值2.38亿美元的顶层公寓外拍摄了一段广受关注的“宣传噱头”视频。后者随即回应，指责对方泄露了自己的个人信息，并让他去读历史书，还拿出一个价值45亿美元的大型项目来嘲讽对方。几个月过去了，一项新税种出台，双方的支持者都开始指责对方。然而，经过一番拉锯战，最终结果却没有任何改变。

Ken Griffin’s public feud with New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has not stopped Citadel from doubling down in Manhattan. … [A]ccording to a Tuesday morning earnings call from Vornado CEO Steven Roth, Citadel will carry forward with the 350 Park Avenue redevelopment after all.

> **译文：** 肯·格里芬与纽约市市长佐兰·马姆达尼的公开争执并未阻止城堡投资集团在曼哈顿加倍投资。……根据沃纳多集团首席执行官史蒂文·罗斯周二上午的财报电话会议，城堡投资集团最终仍将推进公园大道350号的重建项目。

Citadel is expected to remain involved in the project as a “60% partner,” Roth said on the call. “Citadel as our 1-million-square-foot anchor tenant.” Roth’s comments solidify Citadel’s presence in New York—with the company verifying the comments made on the call to Fortune —despite the ongoing public spat between its CEO and the city’s mayor.

> **译文：** 罗斯在电话会议上表示，Citadel预计将继续以“60%的合作伙伴”身份参与该项目。“Citadel将作为我们100万平方英尺的主力租户。”罗斯的这番话巩固了Citadel在纽约的地位——该公司已向《财富》杂志证实了电话会议上的言论——尽管其首席执行官与纽约市长之间仍存在公开争执。

As that story notes, the spat at issue dates to an April video from Mamdani in which the mayor of New York introduced his pied-à-terre tax in front of Griffin’s $238 million penthouse property on the edge of Central Park in Midtown, Manhattan, singling out Griffin by name. Citadel responded at the time with COO Gerald Beeson writing to employees , “We are about to commence the redevelopment of 350 Park Avenue, creating 6,000 highly paid construction jobs and supporting the creation of more than 15,000 permanent jobs in mid-town New York. The project—if we move forward—will entail more than $6 billion dollars of spending.”

> **译文：** 正如报道所述，这场争端源于马姆达尼四月份发布的一段视频。视频中，纽约市长在格里芬位于曼哈顿中城中央公园边缘价值2.38亿美元的顶层公寓前宣布了他的“临时住所税”，并点名批评了格里芬。当时，城堡投资集团首席运营官杰拉尔德·比森致信员工回应称：“我们即将启动公园大道350号的重建项目，这将创造6000个高薪建筑工作岗位，并支持在纽约市中心创造超过15000个永久性就业岗位。如果项目得以推进，总投资将超过60亿美元。”

Well, Citadel is officially moving forward. Griffin still resides primarily in Miami (hence his pied-à-terre liability), but his fund will remain rooted in Manhattan even as it expands its footprint in Florida. The news is definitely satisfying for fans of Mamdani, and not terribly surprising to anyone who works in finance. Griffin himself said in May that “We will probably go through with the building when it’s all said and done.”

> **译文：** 城堡投资集团（Citadel）正式宣布将推进其在佛罗里达州的业务。格里芬（Griffin）目前仍主要居住在迈阿密（因此他拥有一处房产），但他的基金总部仍将设在曼哈顿，即便其业务正在佛罗里达州扩张。对于马姆达尼（Mamdani）的粉丝来说，这无疑是个好消息，而对于任何从事金融行业的人来说，这并不令人意外。格里芬本人在五月份曾表示：“等一切尘埃落定，我们很可能会继续推进这栋大楼的建设。”

What I find interesting is why it’s harder to downsize a fund’s presence in New York than it is for, say, Griffin to relocate his corporate headquarters from Chicago to Miami (where he recently bought an entire apartment building ). Talking to friends in finance, there are a few different factors in play here. The first and most obvious is that talented employees are an asset unto themselves for firms like Citadel, and the fund risks bleeding talented people if it were to issue a top-down edict ordering those employees to uproot their lives and move to Miami.

> **译文：** 我觉得有趣的是，为什么缩减基金在纽约的业务规模比格里芬将公司总部从芝加哥迁至迈阿密（他最近在迈阿密买下了一整栋公寓楼）要难得多。和金融界的朋友聊过之后，我发现这里面有几个不同的因素在起作用。首先也是最显而易见的一点是，对于像 Citadel 这样的公司来说，优秀员工本身就是宝贵的资产，如果基金自上而下地命令员工背井离乡搬到迈阿密，就有可能造成人才流失。

As for the employees themselves, leaving New York for Florida means forfeiting optionality should they want to change jobs and join one of the thousands of funds who don’t relocate and continue doing business in New York. Moreover, while many aging executives have kids in college and can realize the tax benefits of relocation without incurring too much friction, that’s not true of middle management in their 30s and 40s.

> **译文：** 对员工而言，离开纽约前往佛罗里达意味着放弃了跳槽的选择权，他们可能无法加入数千家不搬迁、继续在纽约开展业务的基金公司。此外，虽然许多年长的管理人员子女正在上大学，可以比较顺利地享受搬迁带来的税收优惠，但对于三四十岁的中层管理人员来说，情况并非如此。

Many finance execs have gone to great lengths to navigate an insanely competitive application process and secure placement in private schools for their kids—schools that exist to service the wealthy children of the finance industry. Abandoning all that work to relocate their families to South Florida is unappealing not only because it’s inconvenient for everyone involved, but also because similar infrastructure doesn’t quite exist in Miami.

> **译文：** 许多金融高管费尽心思，通过竞争异常激烈的申请流程，为子女争取到私立学校的入学资格——这些学校的存在就是为了服务金融行业富裕家庭的子女。放弃所有这些努力，举家搬迁到南佛罗里达，不仅会给所有人带来不便，而且迈阿密的基础设施也远不及南佛罗里达完善，这让他们感到难以接受。

As the Wall Street Journal reported this spring:

The children of South Florida’s new tech titans and hedge-fund managers are overwhelming the area’s school system. Most of the private schools are full. Many are expanding but still lack even enough desks for all the children of recently relocated executives.

> **译文：** 南佛罗里达州新兴科技巨头和对冲基金经理的子女们让当地的学校系统不堪重负。大多数私立学校都已满员。许多学校正在扩建，但仍然缺少足够的课桌椅来容纳所有新近搬迁的高管子女。

… South Florida has plenty of new housing and shiny modern office space. It is the shortage of private school slots that is emerging as a major bottleneck for more high earners relocating to the area. Those whose children are on multiyear waiting lists for the next available opening may end up moving away, or never arriving in the first place.

> **译文：** 南佛罗里达拥有大量新建住宅和崭新的现代化办公空间。然而，私立学校学位短缺正成为高收入人群迁居该地区的主要瓶颈。那些子女在候补名单上苦等多年才能等到下一个入学名额的家庭，最终可能不得不搬走，甚至干脆放弃迁居。

Finally, many top employees have already left. They don’t live in New York proper and are insulated from anything Mamdani or Governor Kathy Hochul attempts with tax regimes. These employees own homes in Connecticut or New Jersey, belong to country clubs in those areas, send their kids to private schools in those states, and commute to the city to pay for all of it. This is how the ecosystem has always worked. (And hey! New Jersey, “the armpit of the nation,” was just ranked the second most desirable state to live in ).

> **译文：** 最后，许多高管已经离开了。他们不住在纽约市中心，因此不受马姆达尼或州长凯西·霍楚尔任何税收政策的影响。这些高管在康涅狄格州或新泽西州拥有房产，是当地乡村俱乐部的会员，把孩子送到这些州的私立学校就读，然后通勤到纽约市来支付所有开销。这套体系一直以来都是这样运作的。（嘿！新泽西州，这个“全国的腋窝”，刚刚被评为第二大最宜居州。）

Looking at all this from afar, what I’m describing in New York are what economists would call “network effects.” Just as AT&T became irreplaceable to customers as more people joined the Bell System in the early 20th century, or as Meta properties gained utility with each new friend who joined them 15 years ago, similar dynamics preserve the primacy of Wall Street. The more firms there are working in the finance industry in New York City, the more valuable it is for talent to be close by, and the more satellite services emerge to make everyone’s life easier. Likewise, the more painful it becomes to switch networks (aka “switching costs”).

> **译文：** 从远处观察这一切，我在纽约描述的现象，经济学家称之为“网络效应”。正如20世纪初随着更多人加入贝尔系统，AT&T对客户而言变得不可替代；又如15年前，随着新用户的加入，Meta属性的效用也随之提升；类似的动态也维系着华尔街的霸主地位。在纽约市从事金融行业的公司越多，人才的聚集就越有价值，各种卫星服务也应运而生，让每个人的生活更加便捷。同样，切换网络的成本（即“转换成本”）也随之增加。

Once that flywheel gets going, it tends to be fairly durable. Network effects are also one of the strongest forces preserving the global primacy of the U.S. dollar, and they’re the only credible explanation for X, formerly Twitter, somehow remaining a cultural tentpole despite several new competing platforms and three years of changes that almost every user claims to hate.

> **译文：** 一旦这种飞轮效应启动，它往往就能持续相当长的时间。网络效应也是维持美元全球霸主地位的最强力量之一，而且对于X（前身为Twitter）在经历了几个新的竞争平台和几乎所有用户都声称讨厌的三年变革之后，仍然能够保持其文化支柱地位，网络效应也是唯一可信的解释。

Meanwhile, cities all over America benefit from network effects both for economic and sociological reasons. Young, high-achieving and attractive people want to be near one another for social reasons (more fun, deeper dating pool) and because there are more economic options and upside, particularly in cities where talent and expertise have aggregated around specific industries (finance in New York, tech in Silicon Valley, politics and lobbying in D.C., or, say, Portland with its constellation of sneaker and apparel companies ).

> **译文：** 与此同时，美国各地的城市都因网络效应而受益，这既有经济原因，也有社会原因。年轻、高成就、有魅力的人们出于社交原因（更多乐趣、更广泛的约会对象）以及因为经济选择和发展前景更广阔，都希望彼此靠近，尤其是在人才和专业知识围绕特定行业聚集的城市（例如纽约的金融业、硅谷的科技业、华盛顿特区的政治和游说业，或者像波特兰那样聚集了众多运动鞋和服装公司的城市）。

There are, however, limits to the power of network effects. So now let’s switch coasts and go to Los Angeles.

> **译文：** 然而，网络效应的力量也是有限的。所以现在让我们换个地方，去洛杉矶看看。

Once Upon a Time in Hollywood

Once network effects are sufficiently powerful and dominant, technological disruption and regulation are arguably the only ways to break them. Both of those themes feature in L.A.’s story for the past 10 years, but we’ll get there. First, here’s Matt Belloni at Puck earlier this week:

> **译文：** 一旦网络效应足够强大且占据主导地位，技术颠覆和监管可以说是打破这种效应的唯一途径。过去十年，洛杉矶的发展历程中贯穿了这两个主题，但我们稍后再谈。首先，让我们来看看马特·贝洛尼本周早些时候在Puck网站上发表的文章：

Last Wednesday, David Ellison called a lunch meeting on the Paramount lot with his top lieutenants—the 12-member Executive Leadership Team, or ELT, as it’s dubbed internally—and outlined in stark terms the stakes of the Paramount–Warner Bros. merger litigation for each of them. During the hourlong meeting, Ellison first expressed confidence that the company would defeat the case brought by California and 11 other Democratic states, and ultimately close the controversial $110 billion transaction. He also said that, despite the prolonged and costly antitrust battle with the attorney general of his home state, his goal remains to keep the combined company—and the 30,000 or so jobs, including their own—based in Southern California.

> **译文：** 上周三，大卫·埃里森在派拉蒙影业的片场召集了他的12名核心幕僚——内部称为“执行领导团队”（ELT）——举行午餐会，并直言不讳地阐述了派拉蒙与华纳兄弟合并诉讼案对他们每个人的利害关系。在长达一小时的会议中，埃里森首先表示，他有信心公司能够赢得加利福尼亚州和其他11个民主党州提起的诉讼，并最终完成这笔备受争议的1100亿美元交易。他还表示，尽管与他家乡州的司法部长进行了旷日持久且代价高昂的反垄断斗争，但他仍然致力于将合并后的公司——以及包括他们自己在内的约3万个工作岗位——留在南加州。

But, according to two people with direct knowledge of the meeting, he wanted his top people to know that the rumors were true: He had decided to move Paramount Skydance to either Tennessee, Texas, Georgia, or another state he didn’t identify if Rob Bonta, the California A.G. leading the charge, did not come to the table to negotiate a settlement. Further, Ellison told the group, he had set an October 1 deadline to resolve the matter.

> **译文：** 但据两位直接了解会议情况的人士透露，他想让高层知道传言属实：如果带头推动此事的加州总检察长罗伯·邦塔不参与谈判，他已决定将派拉蒙天空之舞影业迁至田纳西州、德克萨斯州、佐治亚州或其他他未指明的州。此外，埃里森还告诉与会人员，他已将解决此事的最后期限设定为10月1日。

… Ellison wasn’t done: He estimated to his team that the company would save $500 million per year in taxes by relocating outside California, and his plan includes recouping additional costs by selling the Paramount and/or Warner Bros. studio lots, which have each been valued as high as $4 billion (though they would probably sell for less these days, given the chilly production climate in L.A.).

> **译文：** 埃里森的计划还没完：他向他的团队估计，公司迁出加州每年可节省 5 亿美元的税款，他的计划还包括出售派拉蒙和/或华纳兄弟的制片厂地块来弥补额外的成本，这些地块的估值都高达 40 亿美元（尽管考虑到洛杉矶如今冷清的制作环境，它们现在的售价可能会低一些）。

Who knows whether a Paramount move would actually happen; their executives and employees would likely have many of the same objections as Citadel managers if asked to uproot their families and relocate to Texas or Tennessee. That move would also alienate wide swaths of the creative community Paramount hopes to work with going forward (as Belloni notes later in his piece).

> **译文：** 谁也说不准派拉蒙的搬迁计划是否真的会发生；如果被要求举家迁往德克萨斯州或田纳西州，他们的管理人员和员工很可能会像 Citadel 的经理们一样，提出诸多反对意见。而且，此举还会疏远派拉蒙希望未来合作的众多创意人士（正如贝洛尼在文章后文中提到的那样）。

For now, Ellison’s reported talks with rival states and this week’s public threats to consider all their options (Paramount Chief Legal Officer Makan Delrahim: “There’s a point at which where you have a duty, a fiduciary duty to your shareholders.”) seem to be a transparent tactic to jumpstart settlement talks with California AG Rob Bonta, who thus far has refused to negotiate over remedies that could help avert a trial (and 10 months of ticking fees as Paramount fails to close its deal).

> **译文：** 目前来看，埃里森与竞争对手州进行的谈判以及本周公开威胁要考虑所有选项（派拉蒙首席法律官马坎·德拉希姆：“在某种程度上，你对股东负有责任，一种信托责任。”）似乎是一种明显的策略，旨在启动与加州总检察长罗伯·邦塔的和解谈判。邦塔迄今为止一直拒绝就可能有助于避免审判（以及派拉蒙未能达成协议而导致的10个月的持续费用）的补救措施进行谈判。

With Bonta’s intransigence drawing the ire of California governor Gavin Newsom while Newsom’s likely successor Xavier Becerra calls for a settlement , Paramount is turning up the pressure by making its long-rumored, implicit relocation threats more public and explicit. We’ll see what happens next.

> **译文：** 邦塔的强硬态度激怒了加州州长加文·纽森，而纽森的继任者泽维尔·贝塞拉则呼吁双方达成和解。派拉蒙影业正加大施压力度，将此前传闻已久的搬迁威胁公开化、明确化。接下来会发生什么，我们拭目以待。

What’s more remarkable to me is that when I mentioned this story offhand to a friend in finance, he reminded me that regardless of Paramount’s threat to relocate thousands of jobs from L.A., there’s already been tremendous damage done to the film industry in that city. Most of that damage is unlikely to be reversed.

> **译文：** 更令我惊讶的是，当我随口跟一位在金融界的朋友提起这件事时，他提醒我，即便派拉蒙威胁要将数千个工作岗位从洛杉矶迁走，这座城市的电影产业也已经遭受了巨大的损失。而且，大部分损失恐怕难以挽回。

As Belloni noted in his parenthetical, the Paramount lot would be unlikely to fetch its market value given how depressed the production climate is these days. Likewise, in an editorial for the New York Times that ran last weekend, Ellison writes that his litigation opponents “imagine a Hollywood that no longer exists.” On that point, he’s right. A city that used to enjoy many of the same network effects for its entertainment industry as New York does with finance, L.A. has seen quite a bit of attrition over the past 10 years.

> **译文：** 正如贝洛尼在括号中提到的，鉴于如今低迷的影视制作环境，派拉蒙的片场不太可能卖到市场价。同样，埃里森在上周末发表于《纽约时报》的一篇社论中写道，他的诉讼对手“想象的是一个已经不复存在的好莱坞”。在这一点上，他的说法没错。洛杉矶曾经像纽约在金融领域一样，拥有许多类似的娱乐产业网络效应，但在过去十年里，这座城市的娱乐产业已经经历了相当大的衰落。

What’s happened is that as IP and talent have become more expensive in the face of surging content demands, costs are being controlled elsewhere on the budget. Productions have been moving to places like New Mexico , New Jersey , Texas , Canada , London , and more recently Hungary —all in search of friendly tax credits, lower wage scales, cheaper and more efficient permitting regimes, and, in the case of foreign markets, favorable exchange rates, and an escape from negotiations with a different union across every component of a production.

> **译文：** 由于内容需求激增，知识产权和人才成本不断攀升，因此剧组不得不削减预算中的其他开支。制作公司纷纷将生产转移到新墨西哥州、新泽西州、德克萨斯州、加拿大、伦敦，以及最近的匈牙利等地——所有这些都是为了寻求优惠的税收抵免、更低的工资水平、更便宜更高效的审批制度，以及（对于海外市场而言）有利的汇率，并避免与制作过程中各个环节的不同工会进行谈判。

The impact of those corporate impulses is not limited to the below-the-line workers in L.A. Georgia, for example, was once a booming alternative to Hollywood in its own right, but its cost advantage disappeared as the workforce matured and local unions became more powerful. It also didn’t help that all stateside work was halted during 2023’s WGA and SAG-AFRTRA strikes; local production has still not come back from that deep freeze. Today, Georgia’s film industry is experiencing a steep decline and Disney has relocated all of its Marvel productions from Atlanta to the United Kingdom.

> **译文：** 这些企业冲动的影响并非仅限于洛杉矶的幕后工作人员。例如，佐治亚州曾经是好莱坞之外的一片繁荣之地，但随着劳动力的成熟和当地工会力量的增强，其成本优势逐渐消失。雪上加霜的是，2023年美国编剧工会（WGA）和美国演员工会-美国电视和广播艺术家联合会（SAG-AFRTRA）的罢工导致美国本土所有电影制作停滞；当地电影制作至今仍未从那场“冰封”中恢复过来。如今，佐治亚州的电影产业正经历着急剧衰退，迪士尼已将其所有漫威电影的制作从亚特兰大迁至英国。

Meanwhile, workers in film and TV all over the country are also facing the consequences of consolidation among studios and less spending overall, while tech companies like Netflix have opted to vertically integrate much of their productions rather than contract with local companies who specialize in areas like lighting, costumes, or even payroll on productions. Along those same lines, rather than licensing new content from Hollywood studios in perpetuity, Netflix is in the process of building a new, $1-billion studio thousands of miles from L.A., in Monmouth, New Jersey (armpits are having a moment). In the meantime, Budapest has become a hotbed for Netflix originals .

> **译文：** 与此同时，全国各地的影视从业人员也面临着制片厂整合和整体支出减少带来的后果。而像Netflix这样的科技公司则选择垂直整合大部分制作流程，而不是与专门从事灯光、服装甚至剧组薪资等领域的本地公司签订合同。同样，Netflix并没有永久地从好莱坞制片厂购买新内容的授权，而是在距离洛杉矶数千英里之外的新泽西州蒙茅斯（腋窝似乎成了热门话题）建造一座耗资10亿美元的新工作室。与此同时，布达佩斯已成为Netflix原创剧集的摇篮。

Against that backdrop, the fate of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery looks like a footnote to a more dramatic shift in one of the most successful American industries of all time. President Trump may have the wrong solution when he floats a tariff on foreign TV productions , but he’s highlighting a real tension. It should have been impossible for L.A., with all its expertise and infrastructure, to lose this much business to the rest of the country and the world. Yet here we are. The structural dynamics of the entertainment landscape made studios more cost sensitive, rival states and countries stepped up with massive tax incentives, and California’s bloated regulatory regimes, high costs, and incredibly powerful unions made relocation challenges easier to stomach.

> **译文：** 在这样的背景下，派拉蒙和华纳兄弟探索频道的命运似乎只是美国有史以来最成功的产业之一发生更剧烈转变的一个注脚。特朗普总统提出的对外国电视制作征收关税的方案或许并不妥当，但他确实凸显了一个真实存在的矛盾。洛杉矶拥有如此丰富的专业知识和完善的基础设施，本不应该将如此多的业务流失到美国其他地区乃至世界各地。然而，现实却并非如此。娱乐行业的结构性变化使得制片厂对成本更加敏感，其他州和国家纷纷推出巨额税收优惠，而加州臃肿的监管体系、高昂的成本以及势力强大的工会，都使得搬迁的挑战更容易被接受。

What’s happened to the employment base in Hollywood is a reminder that policy choices that are inconvenient for businesses can eventually hurt employees far worse. Strong industry unions built generations of middle class success throughout L.A. and protected the upside of the creative class, but as they did with Detroit and the auto industry , those unions also incentivized business leaders to look elsewhere for future productions. Competition and technological disruption compounded the problem, as did a complacent California bureaucracy that was slow to react to any of these trends (the state finally passed competitive tax credits in 2025).

> **译文：** 好莱坞就业市场的现状提醒我们，那些对企业不利的政策选择最终可能会对员工造成更严重的伤害。强大的行业工会曾为洛杉矶几代中产阶级的成功奠定了基础，并保障了创意阶层的利益，但正如它们在底特律和汽车行业所做的那样，这些工会也促使企业领导人将未来的制作项目转移到其他地方。竞争和技术变革加剧了这一问题，而加州官僚机构的懈怠也雪上加霜，对这些趋势反应迟缓（该州直到2025年才最终通过了竞争性税收抵免政策）。

A Note on Mamdani

Wall Street, on the other hand, is riding the tailwind of tech investment and benefiting just the same as Silicon Valley. Citadel was up 14% in the month of July , while Goldman Sachs profits are booming and its stock is up 18% since the beginning of the year. Far from President Trump’s vision of a world where businesses are fleeing New York City under a socialist jihadist regime, commercial real estate in Manhattan is “ red-hot .” While sound stages and production lots in L.A. sit empty, Manhattan office occupancy rates have recovered to near pre-pandemic levels and JP Morgan just opened a new, gleaming $3 billion skyscraper of its own .

> **译文：** 另一方面，华尔街正乘着科技投资的顺风顺水，从中获益匪浅，与硅谷不相上下。Citadel 7月份股价上涨了14%，而高盛的利润也大幅增长，其股价自年初以来已上涨了18%。与特朗普总统设想的在社会主义圣战政权统治下企业纷纷逃离纽约市的世界截然不同，曼哈顿的商业地产正处于“火热”状态。当洛杉矶的摄影棚和制作场地空置时，曼哈顿的写字楼入住率已恢复到接近疫情前的水平，摩根大通也刚刚启用了一座价值30亿美元的崭新摩天大楼。

I’m fascinated by the dynamics in New York and depressed by those of modern Hollywood, and—despite everything I just wrote about contemporary New York—I think Hollywood offers an interesting cautionary tale for the “tax the rich!” DSA movement that now has the wildly popular Mamdani as its avatar and is sweeping cities all over America. When everyone is making money, then yes, New York’s most successful businesses and many of its citizens will tolerate the highest personal income tax and corporate tax rates in America (even higher than California!). The growth in state and city spending that has well outpaced inflation all looks sort of tenable. In a downturn, however, that calculus could change.

> **译文：** 我对纽约的种种动态着迷，却对现代好莱坞的现状感到沮丧。尽管我刚才写了那么多关于当代纽约的种种，但我认为好莱坞为“向富人征税！”的民主社会主义者运动提供了一个有趣的警示。如今，这场运动以广受欢迎的马姆达尼为代表，席卷了美国各地。当人人都能赚钱时，纽约最成功的企业和许多市民自然会忍受全美最高的个人所得税和企业所得税税率（甚至高于加州！）。州和市政府支出增长远超通货膨胀，这一切看起来似乎都还算可以接受。然而，一旦经济衰退，这种局面就可能改变。

Besides, regardless of where corporate offices are located, where high-earning employees actually choose to live matters a great deal. If an employee who’s making $5,000,000-per-year and is taxed at 4% by New York City eventually leaves for Connecticut, the city can’t recoup $200,000 in tax revenue from sales taxes even if the same employee is still commuting to Manhattan. Likewise, it’s been a Fox News talking point for years that families are fleeing New York and California while Texas and Florida see massive growth, but that’s a talking point that happens to be true . With a shrinking tax base, ironically enough it’s been success on Wall Street—and massive gains across New York pension funds —that has eased budgetary pressure on New York City.

> **译文：** 此外，无论公司总部设在哪里，高收入员工实际选择居住地都至关重要。如果一位年收入500万美元、在纽约市缴纳4%所得税的员工最终搬到康涅狄格州，即使这位员工仍然通勤到曼哈顿上班，纽约市也无法从销售税中收回20万美元的税收收入。同样，多年来，福克斯新闻一直在报道，许多家庭正在逃离纽约和加州，而德克萨斯州和佛罗里达州却经历了巨大的增长，但这恰恰是事实。具有讽刺意味的是，随着税基的萎缩，华尔街的成功——以及纽约养老基金的巨额收益——缓解了纽约市的预算压力。

Watching New York from afar, I can’t imagine living there at my age, but I’ll always love visiting. I’m certainly not stupid enough to bet against its future. Beyond just finance, New York has iconic cultural and industrial networks attracting talent and investment in at least a dozen other world-leading industries. Even as L.A. politics spirals or cities like Chicago and DC confront massive budget holes, New York has so many structural advantages that the city will almost certainly be fine. So will most cities, because again, network effects are powerful.

> **译文：** 远观纽约，我无法想象自己这个年纪还能住在那里，但我永远都会喜欢去那里。我当然不会愚蠢到去赌它的未来。除了金融之外，纽约还拥有标志性的文化和产业网络，吸引着人才和投资，至少带动了十几个其他世界领先行业的蓬勃发展。即便洛杉矶政局动荡，芝加哥和华盛顿特区等城市面临巨额预算缺口，纽约也拥有如此多的结构性优势，这座城市几乎肯定不会受到影响。大多数城市也一样，因为网络效应的力量不容小觑。

There is, though, that Hollywood counter-example. Life does sometimes get measurably worse. Taxes, wage-mandates, and painfully slow permitting regimes all make it harder to operate and build affordably. That can throttle growth, create costs that are passed on to consumers, and ultimately incentivize both businesses and citizens to consider living somewhere slightly less insane. Some of that’s already happened in New York, but today’s top line metrics all look fine thanks in part to an AI investment frenzy and a Wall Street community that Mamdani has spent his first year attacking.

> **译文：** 然而，好莱坞也有一个反例。生活有时确实会明显恶化。税收、工资标准以及极其缓慢的审批流程都会让企业更难负担得起运营和建设成本。这会抑制经济增长，增加最终转嫁给消费者的成本，并最终促使企业和居民考虑搬到稍微不那么疯狂的地方居住。纽约已经出现了一些这样的情况，但如今各项主要指标看起来都不错，这部分要归功于人工智能投资热潮以及马姆达尼上任第一年就着手抨击的华尔街群体。

Mamdani’s story contains multitudes and would need its own article, but for now I’ll simply note that his audacity is remarkable. Watching the mayor spurn Jewish lawyers , fire every business leader advising the city’s fundraising arm , release the names and addresses of 900,000 people who own a $1,000,000 property, or grandstand in front of Ken Griffin’s apartment building, I’m reminded of Leopold Aschenbrenner and his zealous belief in his own thesis . As Mamdani promises any number of expensive social programs that may or may not work, there is not an ounce of hedging or situational awareness in his strategy to remake New York. Indeed, the fun irony of socialists is they are generally vilifying precisely the cohort of people who give society enough margin for error to survive any of their worst impulses.

> **译文：** 马姆达尼的故事包罗万象，足以单独成文，但在此我只想指出，他的胆大妄为令人震惊。看着这位市长拒绝犹太律师，解雇所有为市政府筹款部门提供咨询的商业领袖，公布90万名拥有价值100万美元房产的人的姓名和地址，或者在肯·格里芬的公寓楼前高调亮相，我不禁想起利奥波德·阿申布伦纳，以及他对自身理论的狂热信仰。马姆达尼承诺推出一系列可能有效也可能无效的昂贵社会项目，他的纽约改造策略中丝毫没有丝毫的谨慎和对形势的考量。事实上，社会主义者最讽刺的地方在于，他们通常都在抨击那些为社会留有足够容错空间，使其能够承受任何最糟糕的冲动的人。

Greetings!

Exactly who at OpenAI decided to name the company’s first internally designed chip Jalapeño?
> **译文：** OpenAI 内部究竟是谁决定将公司首款自主设计的芯片命名为“墨西哥辣椒”（Jalapeño）？

That might have seemed like a good idea a few months ago—perhaps OpenAI imagined that name would serve as a subtle indicator that the chip is a hot property.
> **译文：** 几个月前，这或许看起来是个好主意——OpenAI 可能认为这个名字可以巧妙地表明这款芯片很抢手。

But it’s not ideal right now, when the Food and Drug Administration is warning that products containing jalapeños shouldn’t be eaten, served or sold thanks to a salmonella outbreak tied to the peppers.
> **译文：** 但现在情况并不理想，因为美国食品药品监督管理局警告称，由于与墨西哥辣椒有关的沙门氏菌疫情，含有墨西哥辣椒的产品不应食用、供应或出售。

OpenAI must be hoping that’s not an omen, and so far it doesn’t seem to be.
> **译文：** OpenAI肯定希望这不是什么不祥之兆，而目前看来似乎也确实不是。

The AI firm on Tuesday unveiled the first test results for Jalapeño, claiming the chip showed “a significant performance advance: Jalapeño can serve more AI work per unit of power while also returning responses more quickly.” OpenAI CEO Sam Altman tweeted that “we made a chip and it is fast.” AI research firm SemiAnalysis put out a review claiming that Jalapeño is “better than Nvidia Blackwell.” Ouch.
> **译文：** 周二，这家人工智能公司公布了Jalapeño芯片的首批测试结果，声称该芯片“性能显著提升：Jalapeño在单位功耗下可以处理更多人工智能任务，同时还能更快地返回响应”。OpenAI首席执行官Sam Altman在推特上表示：“我们制造了一款芯片，它速度很快。”人工智能研究公司SemiAnalysis发布了一份评测，声称Jalapeño“比Nvidia Blackwell更好”。真是令人震惊。

Nvidia chips are of course the ones to beat in AI.

It must particularly gall Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang that he’s a major financial backer of OpenAI.
> **译文：** 英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋之所以如此恼火，是因为他同时也是 OpenAI 的主要投资人。

Nvidia committed $30 billion to OpenAI just a few months ago.
> **译文：** 就在几个月前，英伟达向 OpenAI 投资了 300 亿美元。

One of the reasons Nvidia invests money in companies is to underwrite demand for its products!
> **译文：** 英伟达投资其他公司的原因之一是为了确保对其产品的需求！

OpenAI has a different agenda.

As we reported in this story earlier this year, developing its own chip—which it did in conjunction with Broadcom—is a way for it to reduce hardware costs and improve its margins.
> **译文：** 正如我们今年早些时候在这篇报道中所述，开发自己的芯片（该公司与博通公司合作开发）是该公司降低硬件成本和提高利润率的一种方式。

Nvidia’s AI chips are pricey, after all.

And it’s not just about the cash outlays for chips.
> **译文：** 而且这不仅仅是买薯片的现金支出问题。

OpenAI also hopes a chip specially designed for inference—running AI models—will operate more efficiently than Nvidia’s more general-purpose AI chips.
> **译文：** OpenAI 还希望，专为推理（运行 AI 模型）而设计的芯片能够比英伟达的通用 AI 芯片运行效率更高。

OpenAI noted today that it would continue to use Nvidia chips for both training and inference once it deploys Jalapeño, of course.
> **译文：** OpenAI 今天指出，一旦部署 Jalapeño，它当然会继续使用 Nvidia 芯片进行训练和推理。

But that’s a bit like the statements we often hear from Amazon and Google, both of which talk up their own AI chips while also acknowledging that they make Nvidia chips available in their cloud units for customers.
> **译文：** 但这有点像我们经常从亚马逊和谷歌那里听到的声明，这两家公司都在大力宣传自己的人工智能芯片，同时也承认他们在云设备中为客户提供英伟达芯片。

The more easily Nvidia’s customers can replace its chips with alternatives, the worse things are for Jensen Huang.
> **译文：** 英伟达的客户越容易用替代品替换其芯片，对黄仁勋来说就越糟糕。

Perhaps he should remind people of the health dangers of eating jalapeños.
> **译文：** 或许他应该提醒人们食用墨西哥辣椒的健康危害。

Paramount’s Next StepsCalifornia’s attorney general, Rob Bonta, is getting a lot of attention for the lawsuit he and 11 other state attorneys general filed to block Paramount Skydance’s $110 billion purchase of Warner Bros.
> **译文：** 派拉蒙的下一步行动：加州总检察长罗伯·邦塔 (Rob Bonta) 因与另外 11 个州的总检察长提起诉讼，阻止派拉蒙天空之舞影业以 1100 亿美元收购华纳兄弟而备受关注。

Discovery.

The state attorneys general are operating under the delusion that we’re still in the 1990s—they allege that Paramount would get too much power in the slowly dying cable television industry and the film industry.
> **译文：** 各州总检察长们还抱有幻想，认为我们仍然处于 20 世纪 90 年代——他们声称派拉蒙影业将在逐渐衰落的有线电视行业和电影行业中获得过大的权力。

Someone needs to remind the attorneys general that strength in streaming video, not cable, is what matters now, and in streaming both Paramount and Warner are behind leaders like Netflix.
> **译文：** 需要有人提醒各位司法部长，现在重要的是流媒体视频的实力，而不是有线电视的实力，在流媒体领域，派拉蒙和华纳都落后于 Netflix 等领先者。

It is possible Bonta is trying to do Paramount CEO David Ellison a favor.
> **译文：** 邦塔可能是在帮派拉蒙首席执行官大卫·埃里森一个忙。

After all, the best thing for the company would be to jettison many of the cable channels it would inherit if the Warner deal goes through.
> **译文：** 毕竟，对公司来说最好的做法就是抛弃如果华纳交易成功后将继承的许多有线电视频道。

And since Paramount is taking on a lot of debt—the combined business would have $79 billion of debt at closing—raising money by selling a few declining assets wouldn’t be a bad idea.
> **译文：** 由于派拉蒙承担了大量债务——合并后的公司在交易完成时将背负 790 亿美元的债务——通过出售一些正在衰落的资产来筹集资金也不失为一个好主意。

Ellison should thank Bonta for the idea and dump as many cable channels as he can!
> **译文：** 埃里森应该感谢邦塔的提议，并尽可能多地取消有线电视频道！

> **译文：**如果你是 Alphabet、Meta Platforms 或亚马逊等公司需要与投资者打交道的管理人员之一，这里有个建议：在办公室墙上挂一张软银首席执行官孙正义的大照片。

Next time a visiting investor complains about your AI spending, point to the photo.

> **译文：**下次有来访的投资人抱怨你们在人工智能方面的支出时，就指着这张照片。

As much as big tech firms are spending on AI, at least they’re financing much, if not most, of it through internally generated cash.

> **译文：**尽管大型科技公司在人工智能领域投入巨大，但至少它们的大部分资金（如果不是全部的话）都是通过内部产生的现金流来支付的。

SoftBank in contrast has so far spent $54.6 billion on OpenAI shares by raising money through a combination of various kinds of loans and asset sales.

> **译文：**相比之下，软银迄今为止已通过各种贷款和资产出售筹集资金，花费 546 亿美元购买 OpenAI 的股份。

And it’s now gone even further: On Thursday, SoftBank revealed that this week it had borrowed $10 billion using its OpenAI stake as collateral.

> **译文：**现在情况更进一步：周四，软银透露，本周它以其持有的 OpenAI 股份作为抵押借入了 100 亿美元。

As SoftBank has committed to put a further $10 billion into OpenAI in October, there’s a good chance it will make that payment using money borrowed against other OpenAI shares.

> **译文：**由于软银已承诺在 10 月份向 OpenAI 追加投资 100 亿美元，因此很有可能它会用以其他 OpenAI 股票为抵押借来的资金来支付这笔款项。

The risk of borrowing against its OpenAI stake is obvious.

> **译文：**以持有的 OpenAI 股份作抵押进行贷款的风险显而易见。

SoftBank’s disclosures say that under this new financing, finalized on Wednesday, it would have to cough up cash if the “fair value” of its OpenAI shares drops significantly.

> **译文：**软银披露的信息显示，根据周三最终敲定的这项新融资方案，如果其 OpenAI 股票的“公允价值”大幅下降，软银将不得不支付现金。

You can imagine a scenario whereby a slowdown in OpenAI’s business causes its valuation to drop, forcing SoftBank to sell some of its shares to meet the banks’ request for cash, thereby putting even more pressure on OpenAI’s valuation.

> **译文：**你可以想象这样一种情景：OpenAI 的业务放缓导致其估值下降，迫使软银出售部分股份以满足银行的现金需求，从而给 OpenAI 的估值带来更大的压力。

SoftBank could raise cash in other ways if it had to.

> **译文：**如果需要的话，软银还可以通过其他方式筹集资金。

Moreover, the chances of OpenAI shares falling enough to trigger a request for cash are likely small.

> **译文：**此外，OpenAI 股价下跌到足以触发融资请求的可能性很小。

After all, SoftBank estimates that as of June 30, its stake in OpenAI had gained $45 billion in value.

> **译文：**毕竟，软银估计，截至 6 月 30 日，其在 OpenAI 的股份价值已增长 450 亿美元。

That gives it plenty of headroom if anything were to go wrong.

> **译文：**这样一来，即使出现任何意外情况，它也有足够的应对空间。

Even so, there are certainly precedents for tech stocks to drop as sharply as they once rose.

> **译文：**即便如此，科技股暴跌的先例也并非没有。

Son has a seemingly limitless appetite for risk, but even he may be stretching things a bit.

> **译文：**孙正义似乎有着无限的冒险精神，但即便如此，他也可能有点操之过急了。

California’s Time MachineSo this is what California and several other Democrat-led states want to preserve?

> **译文：**加州的时光机：这就是加州和其他几个民主党领导的州想要保留的东西吗？

Discovery, the company they want to keep out of the clutches of Paramount Skydance, on Thursday reported an 11% drop in revenues and lower profits for the second quarter.

> **译文：**Discovery公司（他们希望阻止其落入派拉蒙天空之舞公司之手）周四公布，第二季度营收下降11%，利润也下降。

WBD’s report showed its streaming business is growing, but not enough to offset declines in its cable channel and film studio business.

> **译文：**WBD 的报告显示，其流媒体业务正在增长，但不足以抵消其有线电视频道和电影制片厂业务的下滑。

One of the big reasons for the revenue decline was that its cable channels lost the rights to broadcast NBA games, thanks to rising costs.

> **译文：**收入下降的主要原因之一是，由于成本上升，其有线电视频道失去了转播NBA比赛的权利。

In other words, WBD doesn’t have the scale to compete with bigger firms, including Netflix and Amazon Prime Video—something a combination with Paramount might fix.

> **译文：**换句话说，WBD 的规模不足以与 Netflix 和 Amazon Prime Video 等更大的公司竞争——与派拉蒙合并或许可以解决这个问题。

The states, however, want to preserve the status quo.

> **译文：**然而，各州希望维持现状。

In their lawsuit, they argued that allowing two of Hollywood’s five film companies and two of five cable channel owners to combine would hurt movie theaters, the cable industry and audiences.

> **译文：**他们在诉讼中辩称，允许好莱坞五大电影公司中的两家和五大有线电视频道所有者中的两家合并，将会损害电影院、有线电视行业和观众。

It’s as though the state attorneys general who put together the lawsuit climbed into a time machine set to 1990, when Netflix, Amazon Prime Video and Apple TV Plus didn’t exist and cable was still the main way people watched TV.

> **译文：**仿佛提起诉讼的各州总检察长坐上了时光机，回到了 1990 年，那时 Netflix、Amazon Prime Video 和 Apple TV Plus 都还不存在，有线电视仍然是人们观看电视的主要方式。

The states won an injunction blocking the merger from closing pending a trial, which the court has set for March of next year.

> **译文：**各州赢得了一项禁令，阻止合并案在审判期间完成，法院已将审判日期定于明年三月。

Freezing the deal for another six or nine months won’t help WBD’s competitive ability, given that its management has already spent much of the past year trying to sell the company rather than focusing on competing with its rivals.

> **译文：**鉴于 WBD 的管理层在过去一年中的大部分时间里都在试图出售公司，而不是专注于与竞争对手竞争，因此将交易冻结六个月或九个月并不会提高 WBD 的竞争力。

And if the deal is killed, both Paramount and WBD will be worse off.

> **译文：**如果交易告吹，派拉蒙和华纳兄弟影业的处境都会更糟。

By trying to hold back the march of the time, the states may simply accelerate the decline of these Hollywood icons.

> **译文：**各国试图阻挡时代的步伐，反而可能加速这些好莱坞偶像的衰落。

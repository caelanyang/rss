1. Datacenter Construction is Causing Inflation Beyond Just Semiconductor Markets

> **译文：** 1. 数据中心建设引发的通货膨胀不仅仅局限于半导体市场

To this point, inflation driven by the datacenter buildout has mostly been contained to components that go directly into datacenters like GPUs and memory. However, as construction progresses, it appears that some datacenter-driven inflation is spilling beyond datacenters and into adjacent industries. Starwood noted that datacenter construction is driving inflation in construction wages for all commercial real estate:

> **译文：** 迄今为止，数据中心建设带来的通货膨胀主要集中在直接用于数据中心的组件上，例如GPU和内存。然而，随着建设的推进，一些数据中心驱动的通货膨胀似乎正在蔓延到数据中心以外的邻近行业。Starwood指出，数据中心建设正在推高所有商业地产建筑工人薪资的上涨：

“What’s really happening is labor is becoming harder to get again because the electrician and the plumber are getting picked off to build the data center at 2x what they’re getting paid to build a house. So that applies to commercial real estate, too. All of the construction that’s needed to build all this stuff, they’re just stealing workers from other verticals in the economy and putting pressure on wages.” - Starwood Property Trust  (STWD 1.96%↑ ) CEO Barry Sternlicht

> **译文：** “实际情况是，劳动力再次变得难以获取，因为电工和水管工都被挖去建造数据中心，他们的工资是建造房屋的两倍。这种情况也适用于商业地产。建造所有这些设施所需的建筑工作，实际上是从经济的其他垂直领域挖走工人，从而压低了工资水平。”——喜达屋地产信托（STWD 1.96%↑）首席执行官巴里·斯特恩利希特

As hyperscaler capex reaches into trillion-dollar territory, it has economic implications. Jamie Dimon captured this in the quote below.

> **译文：** 随着超大规模数据中心的资本支出达到万亿美元级别，这会产生经济影响。杰米·戴蒙在下面的引言中对此做了很好的概括。

“It’s also driving the American economy, because the increase alone is 1 percent of GDP. And next year it’s going to be another 1 percent of GDP increase. And so that’s, right now, people have to be hired, and you got to get steel and cement and all these things to build the data centers.” - JPMorgan  (JPM 0.45%↑ ) CEO Jamie Dimon

> **译文：** “这也在推动美国经济发展，因为仅数据中心建设一项就占GDP的1%。明年预计还会再增长1%。所以，现在就需要招聘人员，还需要钢铁、水泥以及建造数据中心所需的各种材料。”——摩根大通（JPM 0.45%↑）首席执行官杰米·戴蒙

If datacenter spending adds 1% to GDP, it’s not crazy to think that it could start to drive factors that also impact CPI.

> **译文：** 如果数据中心支出使 GDP 增长 1%，那么认为它可能会开始影响 CPI 的因素也并非异想天开。

2. Memory Prices are Leading to a Decline In Unit Volumes of Smartphones and PCs

> **译文：** 2. 内存价格上涨导致智能手机和个人电脑销量下降

It’s well understood that memory has become one of the leading bottlenecks for AI infrastructure buildout. Elon Musk summed up the dynamics in this quote:

> **译文：** 众所周知，内存已成为人工智能基础设施建设的主要瓶颈之一。埃隆·马斯克用以下这段话概括了这一现状：

“And one must always consider the limiting factor here. The limiting factor currently is memory. The memory output is increasing by around 20% per year. Now normally, that would be fantastically fast and amazing for any large mature industry. But ask yourself, is the demand increasing by 20% a year? No, the demand is increasing by 200% a year, maybe higher. So if you’ve got demand increasing much faster than supply, then Economics 101 would suggest that the price increases. It does not decrease.” - SpaceX  (SPCX 1.44%↑ ) CEO Elon Musk

> **译文：** “我们必须始终考虑这里的限制因素。目前的限制因素是内存。内存产量每年增长约20%。通常来说，对于任何大型成熟行业而言，这都是惊人的速度。但请扪心自问，需求每年增长20%吗？不，需求每年增长200%，甚至更高。因此，如果需求增长速度远超供应，那么根据经济学原理，价格应该上涨，而不是下降。”——SpaceX（SPCX 1.44%↑）首席执行官埃隆·马斯克

It’s also well understood that higher memory prices are impacting the price of consumer electronics including smartphones and PCs. However, it was surprising to read the scale of demand destruction that higher prices are causing for smartphone and PC markets. AMD and Sandisk both discussed their outlook:

> **译文：** 人们普遍认为，内存价格上涨正在影响包括智能手机和个人电脑在内的消费电子产品的价格。然而，价格上涨对智能手机和个人电脑市场造成的需求破坏程度之大，着实令人惊讶。AMD 和 Sandisk 都对此发表了看法：

“Looking to H2, we’re planning for a softer PC market as higher memory and component costs weigh on demand.” - Advanced Micro Devices  ( AMD -1.06%↓ ) CEO Lisa Su

> **译文：** “展望下半年，由于内存和组件成本上涨抑制需求，我们预计个人电脑市场将疲软。”——AMD（AMD -1.06↓）首席执行官苏姿丰 (Lisa Su)

“Smartphones, PC is very, very important. There’s no doubt those markets are adjusting this calendar year. And we said it in the script, we expect them to stabilize next year. But we see units down mid-teens for both smartphones and PCs this year.” - Sandisk  (SNDK 2.88%↑ ) CEO David Goeckeler

> **译文：** “智能手机和个人电脑非常非常重要。毫无疑问，这些市场在今年都在进行调整。正如我们在预测中所说，我们预计它们明年会趋于稳定。但我们预计今年智能手机和个人电脑的销量都将下降15%左右。”——闪迪（SNDK 2.88%↑）首席执行官大卫·格克勒

It’s not clear that a mid-teens decline is being reflected in consumer electronics stocks, especially if Apple is affected to the same degree as the rest of the market.

> **译文：** 目前尚不清楚消费电子股是否反映了15%左右的跌幅，尤其是如果苹果受到的影响程度与其他市场公司一样严重的话。

3. Machine Traffic Now Exceeds Human Traffic on the Internet

> **译文：** 3. 互联网上的机器流量现已超过人类流量

No matter what you think about the ROI on AI spending, it’s clear that the technology is starting to drive some major structural shifts in economic infrastructure. The quote below from Cloudflare was sobering:

> **译文：** 无论你对人工智能投资回报率有何看法，不可否认的是，这项技术正在推动经济基础设施发生一些重大的结构性转变。Cloudflare 的以下引述令人深思：

“For the first time in human history, in Q2, more than 50% of the traffic flowing across Cloudflare’s network was not human. The number of requests on our network from AI agents continues to grow unabated. With the web shifting from human-driven browsing to AI-answer engines and agent-driven commerce, we are witnessing a fundamental rewrite of the Internet for machine-to-machine traffic...And to give you a sense of how this trend is playing out and with the big caveat that I have called it wrong at every point along the way, the -- if the current trends continue, we think in 5 years, nonhuman traffic will be as much as 1,000x as much as human traffic. In other words, humans will be a rounding error on the Internet, not because human traffic goes down, but that’s just how fast we’re seeing nonhuman traffic grow.” - Cloudflare  (NET 2.97%↑ ) CEO Matthew Prince

> **译文：** “第二季度，Cloudflare 网络上超过 50% 的流量并非人类发起，这在人类历史上尚属首次。来自 AI 代理的请求数量持续增长，势头强劲。随着网络从人类主导的浏览转向 AI 应答引擎和代理驱动的商务活动，我们正在见证互联网在机器间流量方面发生的根本性重塑……为了让大家更好地理解这一趋势的发展，需要特别说明的是，我之前的预测并非完全准确——如果目前的趋势持续下去，我们认为五年后，非人类流量将是人类流量的 1000 倍。换句话说，人类用户在互联网上的占比将微乎其微，这并非因为人类流量下降，而是因为非人类流量的增长速度实在太快了。”——Cloudflare（净增长 2.97%）首席执行官 Matthew Prince

It’s still only been 3.5 years since ChatGPT was released, so to see that 50% of all web traffic at Cloudflare is now driven by machines is pretty incredible.

> **译文：** ChatGPT 发布至今不过 3.5 年，如今 Cloudflare 50% 的网络流量都由机器驱动，这真是令人难以置信。

It’s even more incredible that it’s hard to argue that we are well on our way to having human traffic be dwarfed by machine traffic. If machines generate 1000x the web traffic of humans in 5 years, what does that imply as a proxy for economic production?

> **译文：** 更令人难以置信的是，我们几乎可以肯定，人类网络流量很快就会被机器流量远远超越。如果机器在五年内产生的网络流量是人类的1000倍，这作为经济产出的指标意味着什么？

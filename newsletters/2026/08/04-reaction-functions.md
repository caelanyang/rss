Markets are trying to understand his reaction function

> **译文：** 市场正试图理解他的反应函数

“Any central banker, when he or she sees underlying inflation moving higher, he or she is more inclined to tighten policy. Again, when you’ve achieved the other side of your mandate, and you see underlying inflation falling, he’s more inclined to loosen policy. That’s my reaction function” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “任何央行行长，当看到潜在通胀上升时，都会更倾向于收紧货币政策。同样，当完成既定目标，看到潜在通胀下降时，则会更倾向于放松货币政策。这就是我的反应函数。”——美联储主席凯文·沃什

He reiterated that his Fed will have an unwavering commitment to 2 percent inflation

> **译文：** 他重申，美联储将坚定不移地致力于实现2%的通胀目标。

“For some households, businesses, and market professionals, five years of high inflation have left a mistaken impression that is hard to shake: that the Fed’s implicit inflation target was somehow above 2 percent. Let me reiterate: There is no soft inflation target, there is no soft implicit target —not on this Committee’s watch. There is only a target, and it is 2 percent. ..This Fed will not waver. Our credibility rests on performing our duties, and delivering on our responsibilities.” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “对于一些家庭、企业和市场专业人士来说，五年高通胀给他们留下了一个难以消除的错误印象：美联储的隐性通胀目标竟然高于2%。让我重申一遍：不存在所谓的软性通胀目标，也不存在软性隐性目标——至少在本届委员会的领导下不会。目标只有一个，那就是2%。……美联储不会动摇。我们的信誉取决于我们履行职责，兑现承诺。”——美联储主席凯文·沃什

He wants to follow markets’ lead on interest rates

> **译文：** 他希望利率走势跟随市场。

“In the inter-meeting period, market attention centered on real data and real economic developments. Prices reacted in real time to incoming information, and the reduction in forward guidance may have been a factor. Market participants are learning to play the ball, not the referee—and market prices will continue to respond in the direction and magnitude they see fit. This is, in my view, a change for the better—and we are just getting started...What I’ve really been trying to do...is getting an unfiltered message from markets. Getting a direct message. Letting buyers and sellers meet at prices for Treasuries, for the foreign exchange value of the dollar, and then trying to judge for ourselves, what does that mean about our remit? How are we doing on inflation? How are we doing on employment? We’re trying not to interfere with that market signal.” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “在两次会议之间的这段时间里，市场关注的焦点集中在真实数据和真实的经济发展上。价格对新出现的信息做出实时反应，而前瞻性指引的减少可能是一个因素。市场参与者正在学习如何顺势而为，而不是听天由命——市场价格将继续按照他们认为合适的方向和幅度做出反应。在我看来，这是一个好转的趋势——而我们才刚刚起步……我一直努力做的……是获取来自市场的真实信息。获取直接的信息。让买卖双方在国债价格和美元汇率上达成交易，然后我们再尝试判断，这对我们的职责意味着什么？我们在通胀方面做得如何？我们在就业方面做得如何？我们努力不干预市场信号。” - 美联储[https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro]主席凯文·沃什

He doesn’t want his Fed to breathlessly watch official data

> **译文：** 他不希望美联储官员们紧张地盯着官方数据。

“...we’ll be watching inflation data over the period ahead, but I also don’t want you to leave the misimpression that we’re sort of breathlessly waiting for that.” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “……我们将在未来一段时间内密切关注通胀数据，但我也不想让你们误以为我们正在焦急地等待这些数据。”——美联储主席凯文·沃什

He’s not trying to surprise markets. He’s trying not to distort the signal they provide

> **译文：** 他并非试图给市场带来惊喜，而是试图不扭曲市场发出的信号。

“Surprise is not the objective function. Surprise is not what we’re solving for. We have a clear North Star...By not spoon-feeding markets...what we’re getting is the views from a very accomplished economist. That’s the internals of financial markets. Instead of just repeating or echoing what we’re saying back to us, they’re giving us somewhat, not perfect, their own judgment. So, surprises are not the objective.” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “意外并非我们的目标。意外并非我们努力的方向。我们拥有清晰的北极星……我们不给市场喂饭……我们得到的是一位成就卓著的经济学家的观点。这就是金融市场的内部运作机制。他们并非只是重复或附和我们所说的话，而是给予我们某种程度上（尽管并不完美）的自身判断。因此，意外并非我们的目标。”——美联储主席凯文·沃什

Economic shocks are creating a challenging environment for policymakers

> **译文：** 经济冲击给政策制定者带来了严峻挑战。

“A lot of our focus was on trying to understand and identify underlying inflation dynamics amid shocks. We take these shocks seriously. There have been a series of them that have been hitting this economy, we’re not looking through them and saying, oh, they don’t matter. But we’re trying to understand is to what extent are these shocks broadening in their effect...I’ll be the first to admit the shocks make this job and this policy conjuncture a little tougher.” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “我们投入了大量精力，试图理解和识别冲击下的潜在通胀动态。我们非常重视这些冲击。一系列冲击已经波及到经济，我们不会视而不见，认为它们无关紧要。但我们正在努力了解这些冲击的影响范围究竟有多大……我首先承认，这些冲击让这项工作和当前的政策形势变得更加艰难。”——美联储主席凯文·沃什

Jackson Hole could give some additional insight on the big picture

> **译文：** 杰克逊霍尔或许能为我们提供一些关于大局的额外见解。

“I look at it like a blank piece of paper right now...historically, at least for my first tour of duty at the Fed, the more recent periods, it would be sort of a setting up speech more often than not, of what was going to be happening in the fall...in the high mountain air in Jackson, Wyoming, I’d like to also frame the big questions. There is a tendency, especially with the proliferation of meetings and press conferences, to get caught up in the myopic” - Federal Reserve [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a31e417-9908-42dd-90ec-6e814e6e0331?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] Chairman Kevin Warsh

> **译文：** “我现在把它看作一张白纸……从历史角度来看，至少在我第一次担任美联储主席期间，以及最近几年，我的讲话通常更像是为秋季即将发生的事情做铺垫……在怀俄明州杰克逊市的高山空气中，我也想提出一些重要的问题。尤其是在会议和新闻发布会激增的情况下，人们很容易陷入短视。”——美联储主席凯文·沃什

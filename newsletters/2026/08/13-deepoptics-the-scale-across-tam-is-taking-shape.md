Scale-Across Moved Into Design Wins and Order Books

> **译文：** Scale-Across 已成功赢得设计订单并斩获大量订单

A year ago, scale-across described an architecture concept. AI clusters that can no longer grow inside one building must link data centers across campuses and metros, because power, land, and compute are constrained at every individual site. On the latest round of earnings calls, the same term appeared attached to design wins, order intake, and TAM commentary.

> **译文：** 一年前，“跨域规模化”一词用来描述一种架构概念。人工智能集群无法再在单一建筑内扩展，必须将跨园区和跨都市的数据中心连接起来，因为每个站点的电力、土地和计算资源都有限。在最近一轮的财报电话会议上，这个术语再次出现，与设计订单、订单量和市场规模分析等内容联系在一起。

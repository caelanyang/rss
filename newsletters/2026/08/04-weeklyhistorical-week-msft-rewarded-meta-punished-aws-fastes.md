Where should we start? This is probably one of the most extreme weeks we have seen in our career history, if not the most extreme, by the pace and magnitude of drawdown in momentum. In hindsight, there were multiple narrative shifts we could point to, including open-source model competition, new methods in model distillation, increasing memory cost burden on capex, etc. However, if we focus on frontier model development and industry ARR growth, fundamentals haven’t seen a meaningful downturn. Therefore, we have kept pointing towards positioning as the main source of vulnerability. This argument is probably validated this week, after brutal deleveraging at certain star AI funds. You probably have read a lot on that matter, and we won’t elaborate. Still, we do believe this drawdown creates attractive entry opportunities for many names with visible growth and profitability outlook.

> **译文：** 我们该从何说起？就动能回落的速度和幅度而言，这或许是我们职业生涯中最极端的一周，甚至可能是最极端的一周。事后看来，我们可以指出多种叙事转变，包括开源模型竞争、模型提炼的新方法、资本支出中日益沉重的内存成本负担等等。然而，如果我们关注前沿模型开发和行业年度经常性收入（ARR）增长，基本面并未出现显著下滑。因此，我们一直认为仓位是主要风险来源。本周，一些明星人工智能基金的大幅去杠杆化或许印证了这一观点。您可能已经阅读过很多相关报道，我们在此不再赘述。尽管如此，我们仍然认为，此次回落为许多具有明显增长和盈利前景的公司创造了极具吸引力的入场机会。

Switching gears to new narratives, the verdict on the heart of hyperscaler earnings season came down on a single axis: AI ROI specificity. Microsoft disclosed 30m+ paid M365 Copilot seats with roughly 10m net adds in a single quarter — double the prior pace — and got rewarded; we think that re-rating is only beginning. Meta grew 28% to the high end of guidance but raised CapEx to $130–145bn and OPEX to $165–169bn without putting numbers on the AI revenue increment, and was punished for exactly the gap our preview framed: does AI need to recreate another META? AWS settled the argument by simply delivering — 36.7% growth, the fastest in 18 quarters and a fifth straight acceleration, with backlog up 36% QoQ to $496bn.

> **译文：** 转换话题，超大规模数据中心财报季的核心在于：人工智能的投资回报率。微软披露其M365 Copilot付费席位超过3000万，单季度净增约1000万，是此前增速的两倍，并因此获得赞誉；我们认为，这仅仅是重新评估的开始。Meta的营收增长了28%，达到预期上限，但其资本支出上调至1300亿至1450亿美元，运营支出上调至1650亿至1690亿美元，却未公布人工智能营收增长的具体数字，因此受到了惩罚，而这正是我们此前预测的症结所在：人工智能是否需要再创造一个像Meta那样的成功案例？AWS用实际业绩终结了这场争论——36.7%的增长，创18个季度以来最快增速，并连续第五个季度加速增长，积压订单环比增长36%至4960亿美元。

The detail worth sitting with is AWS raising its 2026 CapEx guide from ~$200bn to ~$220bn mainly because of memory costs — in the same week memory equities went through a historic momentum unwind. The largest memory buyers on the planet are lifting CapEx because of memory prices while telling you they still cannot meet 2027 demand; that is not consistent with the top of a pricing cycle. Kioxia’s own print made the point from inside the unwind: an F2Q guide implying ~+30% ASP, SSD & Storage revenue up 390% YoY, and an ¥800bn buyback that sets the shareholder-return benchmark for the whole memory group.

> **译文：** 值得关注的细节是，AWS 将 2026 年资本支出预期从约 2000 亿美元上调至约 2200 亿美元，主要原因是内存成本上涨——而就在同一周，内存股经历了历史性的回落。全球最大的内存买家在因内存价格上涨而增加资本支出的同时，却声称仍无法满足 2027 年的需求；这与价格周期顶峰的情况并不相符。铠侠 (Kioxia) 自身的财报也印证了这一点：其第二季度业绩预期意味着平均售价 (ASP) 增长约 30%，固态硬盘 (SSD) 和存储业务收入同比增长 390%，以及一项 8000 亿日元的股票回购计划，为整个内存业务树立了股东回报的标杆。

The other place we spent the week is SpaceX, ahead of its first print as a public company on 8/4. The quarter itself has almost no surprise room; what matters is the FY26 capex disclosure and the lock-up calendar that starts two days later. The bigger question — whether xAI can actually plug in the ~8GW of compute it has ordered for 2027 — is the subject of our deep dive: the demand is locked into tier-1 OEM production schedules, but nobody on the supply side underwrites more than 5GW. In all, we published fifteen reports across our channels this week; the full rundown is below.

> **译文：** 本周我们关注的另一个重点是SpaceX，该公司将于8月4日发布上市后的首份财报。本季度财报本身几乎没有什么意外，真正重要的是2026财年的资本支出披露以及两天后开始的锁定期。更大的问题是——xAI能否真正实现其为2027年订购的约8GW计算能力——这也是我们深入探讨的主题：需求已锁定在一级OEM厂商的生产计划中，但供应方目前无人承保超过5GW的产能。本周我们共通过各种渠道发布了15份报告，完整列表如下。

This Week’s Reports

MSFT 2Q26 — the market is only beginning to reward better ROI. Our preview flagged a Copilot beat from channel checks, and the print delivered: 30m+ paid M365 Copilot seats with ~10m net adds in a single quarter, roughly double the prior pace. With E7 ramping and monetization expanding from seats to usage, we think the ROI re-rating has further to run.

> **译文：** 微软2026年第二季度财报——市场才刚刚开始认可更高的投资回报率。我们的预览报告显示，渠道调查显示Copilot的业绩超出预期，而最终公布的数据也印证了这一点：M365 Copilot付费用户超过3000万，单季度净增用户约1000万，几乎是上一季度增速的两倍。随着E7的逐步推出以及盈利模式从付费用户扩展到使用量，我们认为其投资回报率还有进一步提升的空间。

SPCX — the compute is ordered; can it be plugged in? xAI’s ~8GW of 2027 demand is locked into tier-1 OEM schedules, yet six independent supply-side sources converge on only 3.5–5GW deliverable. The gap sits at power generation and permitting — our base case is 5GW installed by end-2027.

> **译文：** SPCX——计算资源已订购，能否立即投入使用？xAI 2027 年约 8GW 的需求已锁定在一级 OEM 厂商的供货计划中，但六个独立的供应方给出的可交付量仅为 3.5-5GW。缺口在于发电和许可审批——我们的基本预测是到 2027 年底实现 5GW 的装机量。

PLTR — growth momentum is still accelerating into the 2Q print. Record 1Q obligations point to roughly $1,030mm of 2Q government revenue, up 86%+ YoY and accelerating from 76%, while AIP adoption and Ontology keep US commercial demand intact.

> **译文：** PLTR——增长势头在第二季度继续加速。创纪录的第一季度政府订单预示着第二季度政府收入将达到约10.3亿美元，同比增长超过86%，增速较第一季度的76%有所提升；同时，AIP的普及和本体论的运用也保持了美国商业需求的稳定。

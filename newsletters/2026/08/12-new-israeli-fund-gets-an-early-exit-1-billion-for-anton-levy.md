Software dealmaking is so hot in Israel right now that an Israeli VC fund notched its first exit before it had even wrapped up fundraising.

> **译文：** 目前以色列的软件交易非常火爆，一家以色列风险投资基金甚至在完成融资之前就实现了首次退出。

Team8, which just raised $365 million to back seed and Series A companies, co-led a $48 million fundraise for Tel Aviv-based Koi late last year out of a new $265 million fund for seed and Series A startups.

> **译文：** Team8 刚刚筹集了 3.65 亿美元，用于支持种子轮和 A 轮公司。去年年底，该公司还联合领投了总部位于特拉维夫的 Koi 公司 4800 万美元的融资，这笔资金来自其新设立的 2.65 亿美元种子轮和 A 轮初创企业基金。

In April Koi, whose software aims to prevent risks from AI agents, sold to Palo Alto Networks for about $400 million.

> **译文：** 今年 4 月，旨在防止人工智能代理带来风险的软件公司 Koi 以约 4 亿美元的价格出售给了 Palo Alto Networks。

Exits like Koi helped the firm’s managing partners, who include Sarit Firon and Liran Grinberg, raise the firm’s latest fund alongside a new $100 million vehicle for follow-on investments.

> **译文：** 像 Koi 这样的成功退出帮助该公司的管理合伙人（包括 Sarit Firon 和 Liran Grinberg）筹集了该公司最新的基金，以及一个新的 1 亿美元基金，用于后续投资。

The 12-year-old firm’s limited partners—pension funds, funds of funds and family offices across the U.S. and Europe—are increasingly looking for “the best bets to make in the Israeli market” given the brisk pace of dealmaking overseas, Firon said in an interview.

> **译文：** Firon 在一次采访中表示，鉴于海外交易的快速发展，这家成立 12 年的公司有限合伙人——遍布美国和欧洲的养老基金、基金中的基金和家族办公室——正越来越多地寻求“在以色列市场进行的最佳投资”。

The $265 million fund primarily focuses on cybersecurity, software infrastructure, fintech and digital health startups started by Israeli founders.

> **译文：** 这笔 2.65 亿美元的基金主要关注由以色列创始人创办的网络安全、软件基础设施、金融科技和数字健康领域的初创企业。

The fund aims to lead and co-lead rounds with $5 million to $15 million checks, the pair said.

> **译文：** 该基金的目标是领投或共同领投 500 万至 1500 万美元的投资轮，两人表示。

Team8 manages almost $2 billion in assets across eight funds, investing in early-stage companies as well as helping incubate new ones.

> **译文：** Team8 管理着近 20 亿美元的资产，旗下拥有八支基金，投资于早期公司，并帮助孵化新公司。

From left, Team8's Ori Barzilay, Sarit Firon, Liran Grinberg, and Hadar Siterman Norris.

> **译文：** 从左至右：Team8 的 Ori Barzilay、Sarit Firon、Liran Grinberg 和 Hadar Siterman Norris。

Photo by Omer Cohen.

It’s one of several new funds targeting Israeli entrepreneurs after landmark acquisitions of Israeli-founded startups, including Google’s $32 billion buyout of Wiz and ServiceNow’s $7.75 billion takeover of Armis.

> **译文：** 这是在以色列创业公司被谷歌以 320 亿美元收购 Wiz 和 ServiceNow 以 77.5 亿美元收购 Armis 等一系列具有里程碑意义的收购案之后，针对以色列企业家设立的几支新基金之一。

These include hardware focused Deep33 Ventures and new early stage firm SkyBeam Venture Partners.

> **译文：** 其中包括专注于硬件领域的Deep33 Ventures和新兴的早期投资公司SkyBeam Venture Partners。

Meanwhile, Wiz-backer Cyberstarts is planning to raise a new seed fund after raising a $380 million fund for mature startups last year, we reported in June. “Predicting or building technologies is not necessarily the hardest thing today,” Grinberg said. “But understanding the enterprise context and the demand and the challenges that enterprises face is the thing that founders are in need of the most.”Grinberg co-founded the firm after serving in the cyber and data division of Unit 8200, the intelligence agency of the Israeli Defense Forces, and previously worked at fintech startup eToro.

> **译文：** 与此同时，据我们6月份报道，Wiz的投资方Cyberstarts计划在去年募集了3.8亿美元成熟初创企业基金后，再次募集新的种子基金。“预测或构建技术如今未必是最难的事情，”格林伯格表示，“但了解企业环境、需求以及企业面临的挑战，才是创始人最需要的。”格林伯格曾在以色列国防军情报机构8200部队的网络和数据部门服役，之后联合创立了这家公司，此前他还曾在金融科技初创公司eToro工作。

Firon previously worked at Israeli VC firm Cerca Partners, after working at P-Cube, a provider of Internet protocol platforms that was acquired by Cisco in 2004, and serving as the chief financial officer at MediaMind, which was acquired by Digital Generation in 2011.

> **译文：** Firon 此前曾在以色列风险投资公司 Cerca Partners 工作，之后在互联网协议平台提供商 P-Cube 工作，该公司于 2004 年被思科收购；他还曾担任 MediaMind 的首席财务官，该公司于 2011 年被 Digital Generation 收购。

Here’s what else is going on…Anton Levy’s Layer Global Raises $1.1 BillionAnton Levy, the former co-president and chair of the global technology group at General Atlantic, has raised more than $1.1 billion for his inaugural fund, a sign that he has been able to compete with some of the biggest names in venture and growth equity during a challenging environment for first-time fund managers.

> **译文：** 以下是其他新闻……安东·利维的 Layer Global 募集 11 亿美元。安东·利维曾任 General Atlantic 的联席总裁兼全球技术集团主席，他为自己的首支基金募集了超过 11 亿美元，这表明在对首次设立基金的经理人而言充满挑战的环境下，他已经能够与风险投资和成长股权领域的一些知名人士竞争。

The fundraise was Layer Global’s first close, indicating the size of the fund will likely increase with subsequent closes, according to an August investor letter obtained by The Information.

> **译文：** 据《The Information》获得的一封 8 月份的投资者信函显示，此次募资是 Layer Global 的首次募资，表明随着后续募资的进行，该基金的规模可能会增加。

Layer Global has completed three investments, two of which—Cognition AI and Base Power—have been disclosed.

> **译文：** Layer Global 已完成三项投资，其中两项——Cognition AI 和 Base Power——已公开披露。

A third will be announced in the coming weeks, according to a person with knowledge of the fund’s activity.

> **译文：** 据一位了解该基金运作情况的人士透露，第三笔款项将在未来几周内公布。

At Layer Global, Levy is seeking investments in companies generating at least $50 million to $100 million in annual revenue with growth rates of 100% or more, according to the person.

> **译文：** 据知情人士透露，Levy 在 Layer Global 寻求投资年收入至少为 5000 万至 1 亿美元，且增长率达到 100% 或以上的公司。

Those targets represent companies in early growth stages than those backed by Levy at General Atlantic, where he invested in Facebook, Slack, Snap, Uber, and ByteDance at points when they were already large enough to go public.“We believe this is a uniquely compelling moment to pursue that mission, as AI reshapes the technology landscape faster than prior platform shifts and enables exceptional companies to reach scale earlier than ever before,” Levy wrote in the letter. “With our first close complete, we are now beginning to put that mission into practice.”The average growth rate for revenue across the portfolio during the quarter Layer Global invested was about 1,000% year over year, according to the letter.

> **译文：** 这些目标公司代表的是处于早期增长阶段的企业，与莱维在泛大西洋投资集团(General Atlantic)投资的公司相比，它们所投资的公司规模更大，更接近上市阶段。莱维在信中写道：“我们相信，现在是实现这一目标的绝佳时机，因为人工智能正在以前所未有的速度重塑技术格局，使卓越的公司能够比以往任何时候都更早地实现规模化。随着首轮融资的完成，我们现在开始将这一目标付诸实践。”信中还提到，在Layer Global投资的季度里，其投资组合的平均营收同比增长率约为1000%。

Levy has been competing for limited partner money from some of the industry’s best known investors.

> **译文：** 利维一直在与业内一些最知名的投资者竞争有限合伙人资金。

Just this year, Andreessen Horowitz raised more than $15 billion across several funds, while Thrive Capital raised $10 billion and Founders Fund raised $6 billion.

> **译文：** 仅今年一年，Andreessen Horowitz 通过多个基金筹集了超过 150 亿美元，而 Thrive Capital 筹集了 100 亿美元，Founders Fund 筹集了 60 亿美元。

With those recent fundraisings, the three firms raised more than 40% of what the entire venture industry raised in the first half of this year, PitchBook data shows.

> **译文：** PitchBook 的数据显示，通过最近的这些融资，这三家公司筹集的资金超过了今年上半年整个风险投资行业筹集资金的 40%。

But the number of funds raised by new managers for the first half was on pace for its lowest level since 2016.

> **译文：** 但今年上半年新基金经理筹集的资金数量有望创下 2016 年以来的最低水平。

At his new firm, Levy has tapped a growing network of operational partners who can also help companies in various subject matters.

> **译文：** 在他的新公司里，莱维利用了一个不断壮大的运营合作伙伴网络，这些合作伙伴还可以帮助公司处理各种事务。

The firm has signed Julius Genachowski, the former chairman of the Federal Communications Commission under President Obama, as an advisor, according to the letter.

> **译文：** 信中称，该公司已聘请奥巴马总统时期的联邦通信委员会前主席朱利叶斯·格纳乔夫斯基担任顾问。

Layer Global also signed up a trio of current chief technology officers as advisors: Matt Thomlinson of Electronic Arts, Matt McDole of State Farm, and Shawn Malhotra of Rocket Companies, the letter said.

> **译文：** 信中还提到，Layer Global 还聘请了三位现任首席技术官担任顾问：分别是 Electronic Arts 的 Matt Thomlinson、State Farm 的 Matt McDole 和 Rocket Companies 的 Shawn Malhotra。

Layer Global said it had hired two more people, including Lauren Rakower as general counsel and chief compliance officer, and Dylan Roston, a senior associate who joined from Apax, a middle market private-equity firm.

> **译文：** Layer Global 表示，公司又聘请了两名员工，包括担任总法律顾问兼首席合规官的 Lauren Rakower，以及从中端市场私募股权公司 Apax 加入的高级合伙人 Dylan Roston。

The firm now employs 15 people plus Levy, according to the person with knowledge of the fund.

> **译文：** 据一位了解该基金的人士透露，该公司目前雇佣了 15 名员工，外加 Levy 一人。

Layer Global’s fundraising push, according to the person familiar with the fund, will continue through at least the end of the year.

> **译文：** 据一位熟悉该基金的人士透露，Layer Global 的筹款活动将至少持续到年底。

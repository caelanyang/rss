For years, venture investors have scouted potential investments in the graduating classes of Y Combinator in the hope of backing the next Stripe, Airbnb and DoorDash.

> **译文：** 多年来，风险投资者一直在 Y Combinator 的毕业生中寻找潜在的投资对象，希望能够支持下一个 Stripe、Airbnb 和 DoorDash。

Robinhood is now pitching this playbook to individual investors, with a new fund to invest in early-stage startups that participate in the accelerator.

> **译文：** Robinhood 现在正向个人投资者推介这套策略，并设立了一项新基金，用于投资参与加速器的早期创业公司。

On Thursday, Robinhood’s closed-end fund, which trades under the ticker RVII, went public on the New York Stock Exchange after raising $225.5 million.

> **译文：** 周四，Robinhood 的封闭式基金（股票代码为 RVII）在纽约证券交易所上市，筹集了 2.255 亿美元。

It’s the second startup-backing fund that the financial app has launched this year—and it won’t be the last of its kind.

> **译文：** 这是该金融应用程序今年推出的第二个创业支持基金，而且不会是最后一个。

These funds’ performance will shed light on whether efforts to give individual investors access to private companies will succeed where others stumbled.

> **译文：** 这些基金的业绩将有助于判断，让个人投资者有机会投资私营公司的努力能否在其他尝试失败的地方取得成功。

Robinhood’s first publicly-traded venture fund, which listed under the ticker RVI in March, invests in growth-stage companies including OpenAI, Stripe, Databricks and Ramp.

> **译文：** Robinhood 的首个公开交易风险投资基金于 3 月份上市，股票代码为 RVI，投资于 OpenAI、Stripe、Databricks 和 Ramp 等成长阶段公司。

That stock closed today at $28.83, up 15% from its listing price March 6.

> **译文：** 该股票今日收盘价为 28.83 美元，较 3 月 6 日的上市价格上涨 15%。

That’s slightly lower than the Nasdaq Composite’s 20% rise over the same time.

> **译文：** 这略低于同期纳斯达克综合指数20%的涨幅。

At least two other similar funds have recently gone public as well: Fundrise Innovation Fund, which closed Thursday up 22% from its debut in March, and Powerlaw Corporation, an offshoot of venture secondary fund Akkadian Ventures that owns SpaceX, is down 65% from its listing price in late May.

> **译文：** 最近至少还有两支类似的基金上市：Fundrise Innovation Fund，周四收盘价较 3 月份上市时上涨了 22%；Powerlaw Corporation，是风险投资二级基金 Akkadian Ventures 的分支机构，拥有 SpaceX，其股价较 5 月下旬的上市价格下跌了 65%。

Like all its predecessors, the new Robinhood fund allows individual investors to get in on private startups without a venture investor’s access or the wealth to become an accredited investor.

> **译文：** 与之前的所有基金一样，新的 Robinhood 基金允许个人投资者参与私人创业公司，而无需风险投资者的渠道或成为合格投资者所需的财富。

The Securities and Exchange Commission requires net worth over $1 million or an income over $200,000 for at least the past two years, among other stipulations, to invest in private companies.“It has risk, and we totally get that,” Shiv Verma, Robinhood CFO and president of Robinhood Ventures, said of backing early-stage, private companies, in an interview.

> **译文：** 美国证券交易委员会规定，投资于非上市公司的投资者，除其他规定外，净资产必须超过 100 万美元，或过去两年收入超过 20 万美元。Robinhood 首席财务官兼 Robinhood Ventures 总裁 Shiv Verma 在接受采访时谈到支持早期非上市公司时表示：“这有风险，我们完全理解这一点。”

“We want to give people the ability to do it, and they can decide how much of their portfolio should be.” (Watch Akash’s interview with Verma from this morning on today’s episode of TITV.) The partners behind the fund are also hoping that cherry-picking from each Y Combinator batch will give investors access to the best of the best young companies.

> **译文：** “我们希望赋予人们这种能力，让他们可以自行决定投资组合中应该包含多少这类公司。”（观看今天上午 TITV 节目中 Akash 对 Verma 的采访。）该基金的合伙人也希望，通过从每一期 Y Combinator 孵化的项目中精挑细选，能够让投资者有机会接触到最优秀的年轻公司。

“If you invest in YC broadly, with Demo Day valuations as your entry price, you end up with a pretty good fund; if you can curate that to the top 20% of the batch, the returns are going to be even better,” Rich Aberman, a portfolio manager at Robinhood Ventures, said in an interview. (At Demo Day, which occurs at the end of each Y Combinator batch, participating founders pitch their startup to prospective investors.)

> **译文：** Robinhood Ventures 的投资组合经理 Rich Aberman 在一次采访中表示：“如果你广泛投资 YC，以 Demo Day 的估值作为入场价格，最终会得到一个相当不错的基金；如果你能将投资范围缩小到前 20%，回报将会更高。”（Demo Day 是 Y Combinator 每期创业项目结束时举行的活动，参与的创始人会向潜在投资者展示他们的创业项目。）

Aberman went through Y Combinator as a founder in 2009—back when it was in Paul Graham’s living room, he added—and sold his startup, WePay, to JP Morgan Chase in 2017.

> **译文：** 阿伯曼于 2009 年以创始人的身份参加了 Y Combinator 的创业孵化项目——他还补充说，当时 Y Combinator 还在保罗·格雷厄姆的客厅里办公——并在 2017 年将他的创业公司 WePay 出售给了摩根大通。

Aberman also served as a visiting partner at Y Combinator for two years.

> **译文：** 阿伯曼还曾在 Y Combinator 担任过两年的访问合伙人。

This early-stage fund will be structurally similar to its growth counterpart: Robinhood Ventures will buy and hold onto shares of the startups.

> **译文：** 这支早期投资基金的结构与它的成长型基金类似：Robinhood Ventures 将购买并持有初创公司的股份。

Investors in the fund pay a 2% management fee.

There may be complications with this approach: investors flocking to the fund could drive up the value of its shares beyond the underlying value of the startups, exacerbating the risk to investors who buy shares.

> **译文：** 这种方法可能会带来一些问题：投资者蜂拥而至可能会推高基金的股票价值，使其超过初创公司的实际价值，从而加剧购买股票的投资者的风险。

That’s been the case with Destiny Tech100, a volatile closed-end fund that owns startup shares.

> **译文：** Destiny Tech100 就是这种情况，这是一个波动较大的封闭式基金，持有初创公司的股票。

Robinhood’s latest fund has already invested in over 80 startups, according to its prospectus.

> **译文：** 根据招股说明书，Robinhood 最新成立的基金已经投资了 80 多家初创公司。

Its portfolio managers met with over a hundred companies from each of the past two Y Combinator batches, ultimately writing an average check size of about $250,000 into about 40 startups from each cohort, Sarah Pinto, head of Robinhood Ventures and president of RVII, said.

> **译文：** Robinhood Ventures 的负责人兼 RVII 总裁 Sarah Pinto 表示，其投资组合经理与过去两届 Y Combinator 孵化器中的一百多家公司进行了会面，最终平均向每届约 40 家初创公司投资了约 25 万美元。

The fund has already deployed a little over $20 million.

> **译文：** 该基金目前已投入略超过2000万美元。

The performance will largely come down to whether the managers pick aptly.

> **译文：** 比赛结果很大程度上取决于管理层能否做出正确的选择。

That’s the case with mutual funds, but unlike ones that back public companies, individual investors in the Robinhood fund won’t be able to find out much about the companies that make up the fund’s portfolio.

> **译文：** 共同基金的情况也是如此，但与投资上市公司的基金不同，Robinhood 基金的个人投资者无法了解构成该基金投资组合的公司的相关信息。

Companies currently in RVII’s portfolio reflect some of the biggest trends among investors I talk to—from AI agents and inference to small nuclear reactors and robotics plays.

> **译文：** RVII 目前投资组合中的公司反映了我所接触的投资者的一些最大趋势——从人工智能代理和推理到小型核反应堆和机器人技术。

Some other highlights: Ornadyne, a Sun Valley, Calif.-based company making “robotic birds for surveillance.” And San Francisco-based Anoria, another investment, is developing an “emotion-reading wearable for improving EQ”—tech that might find distinct product-market-fit in Silicon Valley.

> **译文：** 其他亮点包括：位于加州太阳谷的Ornadyne公司，该公司正在制造“用于监控的机器鸟”。另一家投资对象，位于旧金山的Anoria公司，正在开发一种“用于提升情商的情绪读取可穿戴设备”——这项技术或许能在硅谷找到独特的市场定位。

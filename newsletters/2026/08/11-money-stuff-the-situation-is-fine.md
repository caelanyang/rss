Intraday momentum

There are two basic investing strategies, value and momentum. Value means you try to buy low and sell high: You buy stocks that have gone down because now they’ll probably go up. Momentum means you try to buy high and sell higher: You buy stocks that have gone up because they’ll probably go up more.
> **译文：**投资策略主要有两种：价值投资和动量投资。价值投资是指低买高卖：买入已经下跌的股票，因为它们很可能会上涨。动量投资是指高买高卖：买入已经上涨的股票，因为它们很可能会继续上涨。

Tick sizes

We talked on Thursday about prediction market tick sizes. My basic points were:
> **译文：**我们周四讨论了预测市场的最小价格变动单位。我的主要观点是：

* Prediction market event contracts typically have prices between $0 and $1, with an average price of $0.50.
> **译文：*** 预测市场事件合约的价格通常在 0 美元到 1 美元之间，平均价格为 0.50 美元。

* They typically trade in increments of $0.01.

* That’s a tick size of about 2% of value, which is really high. (Compare US stocks, which usually have prices around $100 and tick sizes of $0.01, or 0.01% of value.)
> **译文：*** 这意味着最小价格变动单位约为股价的2%，这确实很高。（相比之下，美国股票的价格通常在100美元左右，最小价格变动单位为0.01美元，即股价的0.01%。）

* Big tick sizes tend to mean more profits for market makers, as bid/ask spreads are necessarily fairly wide.
> **译文：*** 较大的最小价格变动单位往往意味着做市商能获得更多利润，因为买卖价差必然会比较大。

* The central idea of retail-facing financial firms is “get retail traders to do more high-margin trades,” meaning trades with high bid-ask spreads.
> **译文：*** 面向零售客户的金融公司的核心理念是“让零售交易者进行更多高利润交易”，即买卖价差较大的交易。

* Prediction markets are mostly sports bets, so people like to make a lot of them, and they have high bid-ask spreads, so they are lucrative for market makers and brokers.
> **译文：*** 预测市场大多是体育博彩，所以人们喜欢进行大量的投注，而且买卖价差很高，因此对做市商和经纪商来说利润丰厚。

* Robinhood Markets Inc. makes roughly 100x as much, per dollar traded, on prediction markets as it does on stock trades.
> **译文：*** Robinhood Markets Inc. 在预测市场上的每笔交易所获得的收益大约是股票交易的 100 倍。

* This is why everyone’s excited about prediction markets as an “asset class.”
> **译文：*** 这就是为什么大家都对预测市场作为一种“资产类别”感到兴奋的原因。

Which makes sense. There are two competing interests here:

> **译文：*** 一方面，如果预测市场本质上是一种零售赌博产品，那么较大的最小价格变动单位和较大的买卖价差是好事：它们允许做市商和经纪商在每笔交易中从零售赌徒身上榨取更多的钱。

Institutions tend to pay relatively wide bid/ask spreads by trading on public exchanges, while retail investors tend to gettighter bid/ask spreads because their brokers internalize their orders

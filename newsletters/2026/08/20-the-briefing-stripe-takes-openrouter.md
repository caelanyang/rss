No one would ever call payment processing a glamorous business, although if anyone could make it interesting, it’s Stripe’s founders, the Collison brothers (they’re Irish, and one of them has a podcast set in a pub!). Stripe has been out of the limelight for a while, but that’s changed in recent weeks based on speculation that it has been involved simultaneously in a bid for PayPal and talks to buy OpenRouter.

On Wednesday, Stripe confirmed one of those deals: the purchase of OpenRouter, which is like a retail store that sells access to hundreds of AI models, including the leading ones from Anthropic. Essentially, OpenRouter makes it possible for businesses and consumers to pick and choose different AI models through a single provider and billing system. Stripe may have another deal coming soon: A report last Friday in The Wall Street Journal suggested the PayPal acquisition was also getting closer. We’ll see how that shakes out over time.

> **译文：** 周三，Stripe 确认了其中一项交易：收购 OpenRouter。OpenRouter 就像一家零售店，出售数百种人工智能模型的使用权，其中包括 Anthropic 的领先模型。本质上，OpenRouter 让企业和消费者能够通过单一供应商和计费系统选择不同的人工智能模型。Stripe 可能很快会有另一笔交易：上周五《华尔街日报》的一篇报道暗示，Stripe 对 PayPal 的收购也即将完成。让我们拭目以待。

But the OpenRouter purchase is arguably more forward looking. (For more on Stripe’s business trajectory and its sudden interest in M&amp;A, see this story.) OpenRouter is an increasingly valuable business, thanks to the growing need businesses have for AI technology. Indeed, OpenRouter’s monthly revenue has more than tripled to about $13 million since April, we reported Tuesday. Despite that spectacular growth, the price Stripe is said to be paying looks a little over the top. Stripe didn’t disclose it, but reports on Monday and today suggested it was around $7 billion to $7.5 billion.

> **译文：** 但收购 OpenRouter 无疑更具前瞻性。（关于 Stripe 的业务发展轨迹及其对并购的突然兴趣，请参阅此报道。）由于企业对人工智能技术的需求日益增长，OpenRouter 的价值也与日俱增。事实上，正如我们周二报道的那样，自 4 月份以来，OpenRouter 的月收入已增长了两倍多，达到约 1300 万美元。尽管增长如此迅猛，但 Stripe 据称支付的价格似乎有点过高。Stripe 没有透露具体金额，但周一和今天的报道显示，收购价格约为 70 亿至 75 亿美元。

That’s quite the appreciation on OpenRouter’s valuation of $1.3 billion from May, when it last raised money. Sure, its business is exploding—but competition is also increasing. As we reported, several companies are contemplating entering the sector, and rivals such as Vercel are already in it. OpenRouter has an early lead, but it’s unclear how much of a moat it has. And given how quickly this market is changing, who can be confident OpenRouter’s position is unassailable? If this deal turns out to be an overpriced bust, the good news for Stripe is that as a private company, it can easily keep the embarrassing details under wraps.

> **译文：** OpenRouter 目前的估值较 5 月份上次融资时的 13 亿美元有了显著提升。诚然，其业务正在蓬勃发展，但竞争也日益激烈。正如我们报道的那样，多家公司正在考虑进军该领域，而像 Vercel 这样的竞争对手已经涉足其中。OpenRouter 暂时领先，但其竞争优势究竟有多大尚不明朗。鉴于市场瞬息万变，谁又能确信 OpenRouter 的地位坚不可摧呢？如果这笔交易最终被证明是一笔定价过高的失败交易，对 Stripe 来说，好消息是作为一家私营公司，它可以轻松地将那些令人尴尬的细节掩盖起来。

Incidentally, it’s a pity Stripe's founders can’t keep under wraps their belief that the singularity has started. Let’s hope this OpenRouter deal wasn’t predicated on that wacky concept.

🚖 Uber: AV Anxiety

Uber’s Q2 revenue rose 12% Y/Y to $14.2 billion ($70 million miss), though an accounting shift [   ] from a merchant to an agency model in UK Mobility reduced reported growth by 8 points.

> **译文：**Uber 第二季度营收同比增长 12% 至 142 亿美元（比预期低 7000 万美元），但由于英国出行业务的会计模式从商家模式转变为代理模式，导致报告的增长率下降了 8 个百分点。

Gross Bookings grew 24% (or 22% constant currency) to a record $58.0 billion, marking the fourth consecutive quarter above 20%.

> **译文：**总预订额增长 24%（或按固定汇率计算增长 22%），达到创纪录的 580 亿美元，连续第四个季度增长超过 20%。

Trips rose 18% to 3.9 billion, driven by 16% growth in monthly users. The 2-point slowdown in trip growth came entirely from Brazil, Uber’s highest-volume market, where competition for two-wheel drivers constrained supply. US Mobility actually accelerated as insurance savings funded lower prices, with trip growth strongest in markets like San Francisco and Los Angeles where fares fell the most.

> **译文：**出行次数增长18%至39亿次，主要得益于月活跃用户增长16%。出行次数增长放缓2个百分点完全来自Uber最大的市场——巴西，当地对两轮车司机的竞争导致供应紧张。美国出行市场实际上加速增长，因为保险费用的节省用于降低价格，出行次数增长最强劲的地区包括旧金山和洛杉矶等车费降幅最大的城市。

Mobility bookings grew 20%, while Delivery accelerated to 25%. That growth is increasingly flowing through to profits. Adjusted EBITDA rose 33% to $2.8 billion, with margin reaching 4.9% of Gross Bookings. Trailing-12-month free cash flow crossed $10 billion for the first time, giving Uber more room for buybacks, M&A, and AV investment.

> **译文：**出行预订量增长了 20%，而配送业务增速加快至 25%。这些增长正日益转化为利润。调整后 EBITDA 增长 33% 至 28 亿美元，利润率达到总预订量的 4.9%。过去 12 个月的自由现金流首次突破 100 亿美元，为 Uber 提供了更多用于股票回购、并购和自动驾驶汽车投资的空间。

Autonomy remains the valuation debate. Uber is now live with AVs in seven cities and still targets 15 by year-end. Management argues its advantage is not building the autonomous driver itself, but aggregating demand, dispatching vehicles, handling fleet operations, insurance, and regulators. In mature AV markets including San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Phoenix, Uber says its overall category share is actually higher than a year ago.'

> **译文：**自动驾驶技术仍然是估值争论的焦点。优步目前已在七个城市推出自动驾驶汽车服务，并计划在年底前将服务范围扩大到15个城市。管理层认为，优步的优势不在于制造自动驾驶系统本身，而在于整合需求、调度车辆、处理车队运营、保险和监管事宜。在包括旧金山、洛杉矶和凤凰城在内的成熟自动驾驶汽车市场，优步表示，其整体市场份额实际上高于一年前。

Yet the valuation already reflects plenty of AV anxiety. At roughly 10x 2027 adjusted EBITDA, Uber trades at a modest multiple for a business still growing bookings above 20%. The market is clearly pricing in some future erosion of Uber’s economics.

> **译文：**然而，目前的估值已经反映了市场对自动驾驶汽车的诸多担忧。以2027年调整后EBITDA的10倍左右计算，对于一家预订量仍保持20%以上增长率的公司而言，Uber的估值倍数并不算高。市场显然已经预期Uber未来的盈利能力可能会有所下降。

Uber guided Q3 Gross Bookings to $58.25–$60.25 billion, implying 18–22% constant-currency growth, while EPS guidance of $0.84–$0.88 was roughly in line.

> **译文：**Uber预计第三季度总预订额为582.5亿至602.5亿美元，这意味着按固定汇率计算增长18%至22%，而每股收益预期为0.84至0.88美元，与预期大致相符。

Bottom Line: Uber’s core business keeps getting stronger and its Delivery Hero [   ] acquisition could deepen its flywheel. The market’s question has simply moved further out: how much of today’s economics does Uber retain once robotaxis scale?

> **译文：**结论：Uber的核心业务持续走强，收购Delivery Hero可能会进一步增强其增长动力。市场关注的问题已经转移到更长远的未来：一旦无人驾驶出租车规模扩大，Uber还能保留多少如今的经济效益？

🍄 Nintendo: Software Cushion

Nintendo Q1 revenue (June quarter) fell 10% Y/Y to ¥518 billion (~$3.3 billion), but still beat expectations, while operating profit surged 151% to ¥143 billion, nearly double consensus. Net income rose 54% to ¥147 billion. The catch was that roughly $300 million of refunded US tariffs reduced cost of sales, providing a large one-time boost to profitability.

> **译文：**任天堂第一季度（6月季度）营收同比下降10%至5180亿日元（约合33亿美元），但仍超出预期；营业利润飙升151%至1430亿日元，几乎是市场普遍预期的两倍；净利润增长54%至1470亿日元。这主要得益于约3亿美元的美国退税关税降低了销售成本，从而一次性大幅提升了盈利能力。

Switch 2 sold 3.8 million consoles, down 34% against last year’s launch quarter but already 23% of Nintendo’s 16.5 million FY27 target. It now has an installed base of 23.7 million units globally. That compares to 17.8 million units sold by the original Switch a year after launch.

> **译文：**Switch 2 的销量为 380 万台，较去年同期下降 34%，但已达到任天堂 2027 财年 1650 万台目标的 23%。目前，Switch 2 的全球装机量已达 2370 万台。相比之下，初代 Switch 在发售一年后的销量为 1780 万台。

The big surprise was that the original Switch software sales jumped 39% to 34 million units, versus just 9.5 million Switch 2 games. Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream sold 7.9 million units and Pokémon Pokopia 1.3 million, showing that backward compatibility is keeping the 150M+ Switch ecosystem economically relevant even as hardware migrates. Digital sales nearly doubled to ¥133 billion and reached 62% of software revenue.

> **译文：**最大的惊喜在于，初代Switch游戏软件销量飙升39%，达到3400万份，而Switch 2游戏销量仅为950万份。《朋友聚会：梦想成真》售出790万份，《宝可梦：宝可梦乐园》售出130万份，这表明即使硬件不断更新换代，向下兼容机制依然能够维持Switch（销量超过1.5亿台）生态系统的经济活力。数字版销售额几乎翻了一番，达到1330亿日元，占软件总收入的62%。

IP-related revenue more than doubled to ¥35 billion, helped by The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, which has already passed $1 billion at the global box office. That higher-margin software and IP mix helped gross margin jump 22 points to 54%, although the tariff refund materially amplified the improvement.

> **译文：**受《超级马里奥银河大电影》的推动，IP相关收入翻了一番多，达到350亿日元。该片全球票房已突破10亿美元。高利润率的软件和IP收入组合推动毛利率提升22个百分点至54%，而关税退税则显著放大了这一增长。

Bottom Line: Nintendo left FY27 guidance unchanged at 16.5 million Switch 2 consoles, 60 million Switch 2 games, ¥2.05 trillion of revenue, and ¥370 billion of operating profit. The real test still starts in September, when a price hike takes the Switch 2 to $500 heading into the holiday season.

> **译文：**结论：任天堂维持2027财年业绩预期不变，预计Switch 2主机销量为1650万台，Switch 2游戏销量为6000万份，营收为2.05万亿日元，营业利润为3700亿日元。真正的考验将在9月开始，届时Switch 2的价格将上涨至500美元，为即将到来的假日季做好准备。

🛍️ Shopify: Deceleration Dodged

Shopify’s Q2 revenue jumped 34% Y/Y to $3.6 billion ($140 million beat), while GMV grew 32% to $115.6 billion. Free cash flow reached $654 million at an 18% margin, up from 15% last quarter. Shares surged as the recent deceleration scare proved premature.

> **译文：**Shopify第二季度营收同比增长34%至36亿美元（超出预期1.4亿美元），商品交易总额（GMV）增长32%至1156亿美元。自由现金流达到6.54亿美元，毛利率为18%，高于上一季度的15%。由于此前对增长放缓的担忧被证明是杞人忧天，Shopify股价应声上涨。

Growth remained broad across merchant sizes, geographies, and channels. Shopify Payments penetration reached 68% of GMV (+3pp Y/Y), while Shop Pay has now processed more than $400 billion in GMV since launch.

> **译文：**增长势头依然强劲，涵盖了各种规模、地域和渠道的商户。Shopify Payments 的 GMV 渗透率达到了 68%（同比增长 3 个百分点），而 Shop Pay 自推出以来，GMV 累计处理额已超过 4000 亿美元。

AI commerce is also becoming more tangible. AI-driven traffic and orders to Shopify stores both tripled Y/Y, with AI-attributed orders converting at roughly twice the rate when agents use Shopify’s structured Catalog rather than scraped web data. Importantly, 75% of AI-attributed orders came from outside Shopify’s top 100 categories, suggesting AI discovery disproportionately benefits smaller merchants.

> **译文：**人工智能商务正变得越来越切实。Shopify 商店的 AI 驱动流量和订单量均同比增长三倍，而且当代理商使用 Shopify 的结构化产品目录而非抓取的网络数据时，AI 带来的订单转化率大约是后者的两倍。值得注意的是，75% 的 AI 带来的订单来自 Shopify 前 100 名以外的类别，这表明 AI 发现功能对小型商家的益处尤为显著。

Shopify guided Q3 revenue growth to the low-30s%, well above the ~27% consensus and implying a sixth consecutive quarter above 30%. Free cash flow margin should also improve to the ‘high teens’ to ‘low 20s.’

> **译文：**Shopify预计第三季度营收增长将达到30%左右，远高于市场普遍预期的27%左右，这意味着连续第六个季度营收增长超过30%。自由现金流利润率也有望提升至10%至20%之间。

Bottom Line: Q1 raised the question of whether Shopify was finally slowing. Q2 answered it decisively. GMV and revenue remain above 30%, margins are expanding, and AI is increasingly looking like a distribution tailwind rather than the disruption risk investors feared.

> **译文：**结论：第一季度引发了人们对 Shopify 是否最终放缓的疑问。第二季度给出了明确的答案。GMV 和营收均保持在 30% 以上，利润率不断扩大，人工智能越来越像是分销渠道的助力，而非投资者此前担忧的颠覆性风险。

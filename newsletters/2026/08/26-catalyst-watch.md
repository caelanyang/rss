1. Bessent’s Buybacks

A few weeks ago, we wrote about how Kevin Warsh’s philosophy of Federal Reserve management could reshape the yield curve. This week, the potential second-order effects of that philosophy appeared to catch the Treasury Department’s attention.
> **译文：** 几周前，我们曾撰文探讨凯文·沃什的美联储管理理念如何重塑收益率曲线。本周，这一理念可能产生的次生影响似乎引起了财政部的关注。

Scott Bessent seems less comfortable than Warsh with leaving long-term interest rates entirely to the market. In response to rising yields, Treasury increased its bond buybacks—an effort that appeared intended to put a ceiling on rates. Bessent explained:
> **译文：** 斯科特·贝森特似乎不像沃什那样乐于将长期利率完全交给市场决定。为了应对收益率上升，财政部增加了债券回购——此举似乎旨在为利率设定上限。贝森特解释道：

“We’re trying to signal that we think that this is a thinly traded area of the market, that we’re in August, and there’s been a lot of corporate issuance that’s influenced the market. And we believe that there are many underlying factors in turn that the market is not looking at, and we are going to make a market in these. We routinely do buybacks, and we’re going to increase the size of the buyback. And, you know, Sara, I would note that it could be more than the $4 billion per issue.” - U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent
> **译文：** “我们想表明，我们认为这是市场交易量较低的领域。现在是八月份，大量的企业债券发行影响了市场。我们相信，市场并未关注到许多潜在因素，而我们将着手解决这些问题。我们通常会进行股票回购，而且我们将扩大回购规模。萨拉，我想指出的是，每次发行的规模可能会超过40亿美元。”——美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特

The move was likely intended to influence market psychology, but Treasury does not have the Fed’s firepower over the yield curve. Because the federal government already runs a substantial deficit, Treasury cannot buy back long-term bonds indefinitely without issuing more debt elsewhere. In practice, the transaction amounts to replacing longer-term debt with shorter-term debt—an approach that carries risks of its own.
> **译文：** 此举很可能是为了影响市场心理，但财政部在收益率曲线上的影响力远不及美联储。由于联邦政府本身就存在巨额赤字，财政部无法无限期地回购长期债券，否则就必须发行更多债券。实际上，这相当于用短期债务替换长期债务——这种做法本身也存在风险。

Yields initially fell sharply but ended the week close to where they began. Bessent may be inadvertently setting up a game of chicken with the bond market: the more Treasury signals discomfort with higher long-term rates, the more investors may test the limits of its willingness and ability to intervene. If that increases interest-rate volatility, the turbulence could spread across capital markets.
> **译文：** 收益率最初大幅下跌，但最终收于接近周初的水平。贝森特可能无意中与债券市场展开了一场“胆小鬼博弈”：财政部越是释放出对长期利率走高的不安信号，投资者就越有可能试探其干预的意愿和能力极限。如果这加剧了利率波动，动荡可能会蔓延至整个资本市场。

2. Is AI Pushing Interest Rates Higher Too?

Bessent argued that one reason for rising rates is the enormous amount of capital hyperscalers need to finance their AI buildouts. Because these companies have an insatiable appetite for capital to fund AI investment, he suggested, they are relatively insensitive to the cost of borrowing:
> **译文：** 贝森特认为，利率上升的原因之一是超大规模数据中心需要巨额资金来为其人工智能建设提供资金。他指出，由于这些公司对用于人工智能投资的资金有着永不满足的需求，因此它们对借贷成本相对不敏感。

“We are also seeing big corporate issuance. And a lot of that corporate issuance, I would say, is almost yield-agnostic, because the build-out for A.I., the returns on that, the companies believe they’re going to be so high. They don’t really care what they’re paying.” - U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent
> **译文：** “我们也看到企业发行了大量债券。而且我认为，很多企业债券发行几乎与收益率无关，因为人工智能的部署和相关回报，这些公司相信将会非常高。他们并不在意支付多少利息。”——美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特

That interpretation is consistent with the price behavior in other markets affected by AI spending, including memory and energy. Hyperscalers are competing for scarce resources and appear willing to pay whatever is necessary to secure them.
> **译文：** 这种解释与其他受人工智能支出影响的市场（包括内存和能源市场）的价格走势相符。超大规模数据中心运营商正在争夺稀缺资源，并且似乎愿意付出任何必要的代价来确保获得这些资源。

If AI investment is now affecting the bond market, it is another sign that the buildout’s economic consequences extend far beyond the technology sector. The cost of capital influences nearly every form of economic activity. As AI’s external effects grow, so does the risk of political intervention—as Bessent’s response may already demonstrate.
> **译文：** 如果人工智能投资如今已对债券市场产生影响，这进一步表明，人工智能发展的经济影响远远超出了科技领域。资本成本几乎影响着所有形式的经济活动。随着人工智能外部影响的扩大，政治干预的风险也随之增加——贝森特的回应或许已经表明了这一点。

3. Housing Has Been Frozen for Years

As the economy’s focus has shifted toward AI, traditionally important sectors such as housing have quietly languished. Home Depot reminded investors just how unusual the current freeze has become:
> **译文：** 随着经济重心转向人工智能，传统上重要的行业，例如住房，却悄然陷入低迷。家得宝(Home Depot)的例子提醒投资者，当前的经济停滞有多么不寻常：

“Every time we’ve seen it hit the sort of 3% of the housing stock changing hands over history, it’s always bounced up relatively quickly. We’ve seen housing turnover at these low levels for 4 years now. So I don’t think that we’ve seen much volatility from the recent increase in rates. We do know that when we see step downs, we begin to see a little bit of life come into housing, but there’s just no sign of an inflection point at this moment.” - The Home Depot (HD 0.50%↑ ) CFO Richard McPhail
> **译文：** “历史上，每当房屋交易量跌至房屋存量3%左右时，它总是会迅速反弹。我们已经看到房屋周转率在如此低的水平维持了四年。所以我认为，近期利率上升并没有造成太大的波动。我们知道，当利率下调时，房地产市场会开始出现一些复苏迹象，但目前还没有出现拐点的迹象。”——家得宝（HD 0.50%↑）首席财务官理查德·麦克菲尔

Housing turnover is depressed for several reasons: persistently elevated mortgage rates, the lingering effects of the pandemic-era housing boom, and the pressures created by a K-shaped economy. Each of these themes appeared in The Transcript this week.
> **译文：** 房屋周转率低迷的原因有很多：持续高企的抵押贷款利率、疫情时期房地产繁荣的余波以及K型经济带来的压力。本周的《The Transcript》杂志都探讨了这些问题。

Housing is a cyclical industry and will eventually rebound. But if the dynamics outlined in the first two sections persist, there is still no obvious catalyst for a recovery.
> **译文：** 房地产行业具有周期性，最终会反弹。但如果前两部分所述的动态持续下去，目前仍缺乏明显的复苏催化剂。

Flock wanted to tap dashcams in rideshare vechicles to add to surveillance data — To augment its network of license plate readers, surveillance company Flock drew up plans to partner with dashcam company Nexar and use Uber and Lyft drivers to passively collect data. Joseph Cox for 404 Media reports .

> **译文：** 为了扩充其车牌识别网络，监控公司Flock计划与行车记录仪公司Nexar合作，利用Uber和Lyft司机被动收集数据。404 Media的Joseph Cox报道。

In case Flock sounds familiar, this is the same company that was going to partner with Ring and helped ICE tap into Lowe’s and Home Depot security footage. Where there’s an internet-connected camera there is a way.

> **译文：** 如果Flock这个名字听起来耳熟，那是因为它就是之前打算与Ring合作，帮助美国移民及海关执法局（ICE）获取Lowe's和Home Depot监控录像的那家公司。只要有联网摄像头，就总有办法。

Eclipse path in Europe — A total eclipse path is passing through Europe this week. The New York Times has the maps and the weather forecasts for the occasion .

> **译文：** 欧洲日食路径——本周，一股日全食路径将穿过欧洲。《纽约时报》提供了相关地图和天气预报。

Reuters also had a nice visual guide with more background and history for the event.

> **译文：** 路透社还制作了一份精美的图文指南，提供了更多关于该事件的背景和历史信息。

I haven't experienced a total solar eclipse yet, so I'm a little jealous.

> **译文：** 我还没见过日全食，所以有点羡慕。

Most common color of IKEA sofas over time — For the European Correspondent, Toon Vos counted the color of all the couches in IKEA catalogs from 1960 through 2021. The above shows the most common color over the years, and things are looking pretty neutral these days.

> **译文：** 宜家沙发最常见的颜色变化——欧洲通讯员托恩·沃斯统计了1960年至2021年宜家产品目录中所有沙发的颜色。上图显示了多年来最常见的颜色，如今看来，中性色似乎越来越受欢迎。

The IKEA complexity index came to mind. It's a catalog of 20,000 products ranked by how long they take to build. When you get a PAX corner wardrobe, prepare for a manual in 16 parts and a 10 and a half hour build time. In contrast, a desk lamp will set you back only a few minutes.

> **译文：** 我想到宜家的组装复杂度指数。这是一个包含两万件产品的目录，按组装所需时间长短进行排名。如果你要买一个PAX转角衣柜，那就准备好面对一份包含16个部件的说明书，以及长达10个半小时的组装时间吧。相比之下，一盏台灯的组装只需要几分钟。

Origins of American city names — In the early 1900s, Henry Gannett cataloged the origins of city names in the United States. For the Wall Street Journal, Carl Churchill made a searchable map to see where your city’s name came from .

> **译文：** 美国城市名称的起源——20世纪初，亨利·甘内特对美国城市名称的起源进行了编目。卡尔·丘吉尔为《华尔街日报》制作了一张可搜索的地图，让人们可以查看自己所在城市名称的由来。

OpenAI accidentally makes attack bots — Multiple bots exploited a vulnerability in OpenAI’s security system to gain internet access. Then the bots unwittingly went rogue and attacked Hugging Face’s production infrastructure. Eric Wallace and Michael Dalton for OpenAI explained the incident in a talk .

> **译文：** OpenAI意外制造攻击机器人——多个机器人利用OpenAI安全系统中的漏洞获取了互联网访问权限。随后，这些机器人失控并攻击了Hugging Face的生产基础设施。OpenAI的Eric Wallace和Michael Dalton在一次演讲中解释了这一事件。

There’s automated code, message boards, and bots teaming up, just like every animated movie about AI foretold. In truth, it’s negligence on OpenAI’s part. I hope they actually take security seriously going forward. This seems like a real concern if we truly are in the beginning of this technology.

> **译文：** 就像所有人工智能题材动画电影预言的那样，自动化代码、留言板和机器人正在联手运作。事实上，这是OpenAI的疏忽。我希望他们今后能真正重视安全问题。如果我们真的还处于这项技术的初期阶段，这确实是一个值得关注的问题。

Emoji usage and age generations — For NYT’s the Upshot, Eve Washington, Larry Buchanan, and Claire Cain Miller examined the most commonly used emojis and how it changes by generation . One day I will see a thread of emoji-filled messages and have no idea what any of it means.

> **译文：** 表情符号的使用与年龄层——《纽约时报》旗下栏目“Upshot”的伊芙·华盛顿、拉里·布坎南和克莱尔·凯恩·米勒研究了最常用的表情符号及其使用习惯随年龄的变化。总有一天，我会看到一串满是表情符号的信息，却完全看不懂它们的意思。

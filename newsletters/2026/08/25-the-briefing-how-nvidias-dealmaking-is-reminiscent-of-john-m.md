If you like what you see, I encourage you to subscribe to our reporting here.Greetings!Here’s a thought about Nvidia’s future: All the equity stakes it’s accumulating in AI companies—including, as we reported on Sunday, an expanded stake in Perplexity—could become more useful in ways other than simply guaranteeing demand for its chips.

> **译文：** 感谢您阅读我们的每日新闻专栏“简报”，我们会在此为您解读当日新闻。如果您喜欢我们的内容，欢迎在此订阅。问候！关于英伟达的未来，我有一些想法：它正在不断积累人工智能公司的股权——包括我们周日报道的增持Perplexity股份——这些股权除了能确保其芯片的需求之外，或许还能在其他方面发挥更大的作用。

Over time the stakes could end up being worth a lot of money and could also make CEO Jensen Huang even more of a kingmaker in the AI sector than he already is. You could even imagine Nvidia spinning off its investment portfolio into a separate stand-alone company at some point to highlight its value.Think about how John Malone’s media empire developed in the long-distant past (Malone was probably most active before many AI folks were born).

> **译文：** 随着时间的推移，这些投资最终可能价值连城，也可能让CEO黄仁勋在人工智能领域的影响力更上一层楼。甚至可以想象，英伟达未来可能会将其投资组合分拆成一家独立的公司，以凸显其价值。想想约翰·马龙的媒体帝国在遥远的过去是如何发展起来的（马龙最活跃的时期可能比许多人工智能从业者出生还要早）。

In the 1980s, at the helm of one of cable TV’s early giants, Tele-Communications Inc., Malone invested in nascent cable programming firms like Discovery, BET and Turner Broadcasting whose channels ran on TCI and other cable systems. The investments served two purposes: By supporting these young firms, TCI ensured there was enough differentiated programming on cable to persuade consumers to pay for the service rather than just relying on free over-the-air television.

> **译文：** 上世纪80年代，马龙执掌有线电视早期巨头之一的电信公司（Tele-Communications Inc.，简称TCI），投资了探索频道（Discovery）、黑人娱乐电视网（BET）和特纳广播公司（Turner Broadcasting）等新兴有线电视节目制作公司，这些公司的频道通过TCI和其他有线电视系统播出。这些投资有两个目的：通过扶持这些年轻公司，TCI确保了有线电视拥有足够多的差异化节目，从而说服消费者付费订阅服务，而不是仅仅依赖免费的无线电视。

It wasn’t just charity: Programming entrepreneurs needed the distribution Malone controlled, which gave him leverage to get the equity at attractive prices.Malone put all the stakes into one unit of TCI called Liberty Media, which got spun out of the company after he sold TCI to AT&T in 1999. Many of the stakes became enormously valuable, as some of the cable channels turned into big businesses (Discovery ended up as Warner Bros. Discovery, for instance).

> **译文：** 这不仅仅是慈善行为：节目制作人需要马龙掌控的发行渠道，这使他能够以诱人的价格获得股权。马龙将所有股份都投入了TCI旗下的一家名为Liberty Media的子公司，该公司在1999年他将TCI出售给AT&T后从TCI剥离出来。随着一些有线电视频道发展成为大型企业（例如，Discovery最终成为华纳兄弟探索频道），许多股份变得价值连城。

Malone and his lieutenants have parlayed the portfolio into many different public companies through constant dealmaking. It’s not clear that Nvidia’s Huang is as much of a financial engineer as Malone (few CEOs are). And there’s no guarantee the portfolio will retain its value: It could end up mostly worthless. But that seems unlikely.

> **译文：** 马龙和他的助手们通过不断进行交易，将投资组合转化为多家不同的上市公司。目前尚不清楚英伟达的黄仁勋是否像马龙那样精通金融（事实上，很少有CEO能做到这一点）。而且，也无法保证该投资组合能够保值：它最终可能变得一文不值。但这似乎不太可能。

Nvidia’s investments range from stakes in public companies such as SpaceX, Nebius and CoreWeave—each of which is a loyal buyer of Nvidia chips (as we saw again today)—to stakes in private firms such as OpenAI and Perplexity. In some cases, the investments buttress the financial strength of buyers of its chips (that’s obviously true for CoreWeave and Nebius, for instance).

> **译文：** 英伟达的投资范围很广，从持有SpaceX、Nebius和CoreWeave等上市公司的股份（这些公司都是英伟达芯片的忠实买家，正如我们今天再次看到的），到持有OpenAI和Perplexity等私营公司的股份。在某些情况下，这些投资增强了其芯片买家的财务实力（例如，CoreWeave和Nebius的情况显然如此）。

Some recent investments, such as one in AI model maker Poolside, are helping Nvidia develop its Nemotron AI models, we noted.We’ll likely get an update on the size of its private investment portfolio when Nvidia reports its second-quarter earnings on Wednesday. We know it’s sizable: As of March 31, Nvidia had $42.3 billion in private companies, it has reported in securities filings.

> **译文：** 我们注意到，英伟达近期的一些投资，例如对人工智能模型开发商 Poolside 的投资，正在帮助其开发 Nemotron 人工智能模型。英伟达将于周三公布第二季度财报，届时我们可能会了解到其私募投资组合的规模。我们知道这个规模相当庞大：截至 3 月 31 日，英伟达在私募股权公司持有价值 423 亿美元的股份，这是其在证券文件中披露的信息。

As for the public stakes, Nvidia revealed in a securities filing earlier this month that as of June 30 its equity stakes in publicly traded companies such as SpaceX and CoreWeave were worth $63 billion, up from $18.4 billion at March 31. The difference was due both to increases in the value of stakes in companies such as Intel and CoreWeave and to the first-time inclusion of Nvidia’s $21 billion stake in SpaceX.

> **译文：** 至于公开持股方面，英伟达本月早些时候在一份证券文件中披露，截至6月30日，其在SpaceX和CoreWeave等上市公司的股权价值为630亿美元，高于3月31日的184亿美元。这一增长既是由于英特尔和CoreWeave等公司股权价值的增加，也是由于首次将英伟达持有的SpaceX价值210亿美元的股份纳入统计。

Spinning off the investment portfolio into a separate company, as Malone did with Liberty, would give investors a way of getting direct exposure to a wide range of AI firms and enhance Huang’s dealmaking prospects. Just a thought!Shein’s Value CollapseWhen Singapore-based fast-fashion online retailer Shein goes public in Hong Kong next week, it will become apparent just how much its valuation has dropped since 2022.

> **译文：** 将投资组合分拆成一家独立公司，就像马龙对Liberty所做的那样，可以让投资者直接接触到众多人工智能公司，并提升黄仁勋的交易前景。这只是一个想法！Shein价值暴跌：总部位于新加坡的快时尚电商Shein将于下周在香港上市，届时人们将清楚地看到，自2022年以来，其估值究竟下跌了多少。

Its IPO filing, available on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange’s website, shows that its projected IPO price of around $6 will be well below where it has sold shares in several rounds since 2022.At Shein’s high point, early that year, the company was valued at $98.2 billion. It sold shares for the equivalent of $23.72.

> **译文：** 该公司在香港联交所网站上公布的IPO申请文件显示，其预计IPO价格约为每股6美元，远低于自2022年以来几轮融资的发行价。Shein在2022年初的巅峰时期估值高达982亿美元，当时其发行价相当于每股23.72美元。

The following year, when it was valued at $64 billion, it sold new shares for $15.Unfortunately, the Trump administration’s elimination of the de minimis loophole—which had allowed Shein to import items worth less than $800 each into the U.S. without paying duties on them—has slammed its business. U.S. sales fell 14% in the first quarter, while overall sales grew only 1.1%.

> **译文：** 次年，该公司市值达到640亿美元，并以每股15美元的价格发行了新股。不幸的是，特朗普政府取消了“最低限度漏洞”（de minimis loophole），该漏洞曾允许Shein公司向美国进口单价低于800美元的商品而无需缴纳关税，这给该公司的业务带来了沉重打击。第一季度，Shein在美国的销售额下降了14%，而整体销售额仅增长了1.1%。

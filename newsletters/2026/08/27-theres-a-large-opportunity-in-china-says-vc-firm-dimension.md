China hasn’t held the same opportunities for U.S. venture capitalists it once did, given a crackdown by both countries on cross-border tech investments. But U.S. VCs are still hungry for insights into the country’s tech startups, whose fast growth holds clues to the future of U.S. AI companies.

> **译文：** 由于中美两国都加强了对跨境科技投资的管控，中国对美国风险投资家而言已不再像过去那样充满机遇。但美国风投公司仍然渴望了解中国科技初创企业的发展情况，这些企业的快速增长蕴藏着美国人工智能公司未来发展的线索。

In one sign of their interest, a group of investors from Dimension Capital, a VC firm focused on AI and biotech founded by former general partners at Lux Capital and Obvious Ventures, spent a week in China visiting AI labs, investors and entrepreneurs. The firm’s partners, in a letter to their limited partners, characterized the visit as a chance to understand the competitive nature of China tech, from open-weight AI models to data labelling.

> **译文：** 为了表达对中国市场的兴趣，Dimension Capital——一家专注于人工智能和生物科技领域的风险投资公司，由Lux Capital和Obvious Ventures的前普通合伙人创立——的一群投资者在中国进行了一周的访问，走访了人工智能实验室、投资者和创业者。该公司合伙人在一封致有限合伙人的信中表示，此次访问旨在了解中国科技领域的竞争格局，涵盖从开源人工智能模型到数据标注等各个方面。

In the letter, it discussed how Chinese open-weight models like Moonshot’s Kimi and Zhipu’s GLM have succeeded in getting Western customers to use them, a feat a generation of past Chinese enterprise software startups failed to achieve.

> **译文：** 信中讨论了像 Moonshot 的 Kimi 和 Zhipu 的 GLM 这样的中国开源软件模型是如何成功吸引西方客户使用的，而这是过去一代中国企业软件创业公司未能实现的壮举。

However, the letter, which The Information obtained, noted that China’s hot AI startups are just capturing “Western workloads, not Western revenue.” That means that even though American developers are increasingly using these Chinese models, the startups behind them aren’t capturing all the revenue. That’s flowing in part to U.S. inference startups such as Modal, Baseten and Fireworks AI, which provide access to these models.

> **译文：** 然而，《信息报》获得的这封信指出，中国热门人工智能初创公司只是“承接了西方的工作负载，而非西方的收入”。这意味着，尽管美国开发者越来越多地使用这些中国模型，但这些模型背后的初创公司并没有获得全部收入。部分收入流向了美国推理初创公司，例如 Modal、Baseten 和 Fireworks AI，这些公司提供对这些模型的访问权限。

Indeed, as my colleagues reported earlier this week, open-source model maker DeepSeek generated about $70 million in revenue in the first seven months of this year, tiny compared to the tens of billions of annualized revenue Anthropic and OpenAI each are generating. In fact, the Dimension partners ended the five page letter noting that the leading Chinese labs are trading at revenue multiples that are five to ten times higher than U.S. frontier labs.

> **译文：** 诚然，正如我的同事本周早些时候报道的那样，开源模型开发商DeepSeek今年前七个月的收入约为7000万美元，与Anthropic和OpenAI各自数百亿美元的年收入相比，简直微不足道。事实上，Dimension的合伙人在长达五页的信函结尾指出，中国领先的人工智能实验室的市盈率是美国前沿实验室的五到十倍。

“For all the talk of frothy valuations of AI in the West, the climate seems to be meaningfully frothier in China.”(You can read the full letter here.) We hear big mutual funds have also made trips this year. Dimension earlier this year co-led a $787 million funding in AI drug discovery startup Earendil Labs. The company, an affiliate of China’s Helixon Therapeutics, has been plotting a Hong Kong IPO. The listing has faced opposition from China’s regulators, according to The Wall Street Journal.

> **译文：** 我们听说今年也有大型共同基金进行了投资。Dimension 今年早些时候联合领投了人工智能药物研发初创公司 Earendil Labs 的 7.87 亿美元融资。该公司是中国 Helixon Therapeutics 的关联公司，一直计划在香港上市。据《华尔街日报》报道，此次上市遭到了中国监管机构的反对。

The VC firm, which closed a third, $800 million fund in July, also invested in AI drug discovery startup Chai Discovery and inference startup Modal Labs. Earlier this year, Anthropic bought one of its investments, AI biotech startup Coefficient Bio, for roughly $400 million. VC firms such as Tiger Global Management and Sequoia Capital built up substantial portfolios in what became high-flying global startups, such as TikTok-owner ByteDance and ride-hailing company Didi. U.S. venture firms largely retreated from China in 2023 and 2024, after rising restrictions from both global superpowers increased the risk one of the countries would intervene to derail the investment or make it hard for investors to get back their money.

> **译文：** 像老虎环球基金和红杉资本这样的风险投资公司，在字节跳动（TikTok 的母公司）和滴滴出行等日后成为全球瞩目的初创企业中，构建了庞大的投资组合。然而，随着中美两大超级大国不断加强限制，美国风险投资公司在 2023 年和 2024 年大多撤出了中国市场。这增加了其中一方干预投资、阻碍投资或使投资者难以收回资金的风险。

Investors in ByteDance, for instance, have only been able to sell shares on the secondary market, as General Atlantic did earlier this year, at a $550 billion valuation. > **译文：** 但那时，Benchmark已经将其投资收益分配给了有限合伙人。事实上，Manus的处境或许并不像表面看起来那样令美国风险投资家感到沮丧。正如我们亚洲分社报道的那样，在Meta于12月宣布收购这家初创公司的计划后，Manus的收入飙升。

Growth like Manus’ and the ingenuity that’s driven Chinese entrepreneurs to find seemingly endless workarounds to U.S. chip and compute restrictions continues to fascinate U.S. investors. “If the [Chinese Communist Party] can ever assuage regulatory risk for crossover and specialist tech and biotech investors, there’s a large opportunity for the right investor,” the Dimension partners wrote.

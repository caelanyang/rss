The Execution Layer of Intelligence is this issue's theme and the question connecting all 20 selections: how does intelligence move through a real environment and complete work reliably? Grok, DeepSeek, and Gemini advance capability, interfaces, and pricing, while harnesses, evaluations, caching, routing, and quality gates organize models into systems that can keep working.

> **译文：** 本期主题是“智能的执行层”，也是贯穿全部20篇选文的核心问题：智能如何在真实环境中运行并可靠地完成任务？Grok、DeepSeek 和 Gemini 提升了智能的功能、接口和定价，而工具链、评估、缓存、路由和质量门控则将模型组织成能够持续运行的系统。

Here are 10 highlights worth your attention this week:

> **译文：** 以下是本周值得您关注的10个亮点：

🧠 Grok 4.6, DeepSeek V4 Pro, and Gemini 3.7 Flash show three paths toward execution-oriented models. Grok emphasizes long trajectories, self-testing, and interactive projects. DeepSeek adds native Responses API and Codex support, with low, high, and max reasoning levels. Gemini rises from 34.4% to 43.6% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main while cutting introductory input pricing to $0.75 per million tokens. Model competition is expanding from raw capability into interfaces, latency, budgets, and sustained delivery.

> **译文：** 🧠 Grok 4.6、DeepSeek V4 Pro 和 Gemini 3.7 Flash 展示了三种面向执行的模型发展路径。Grok 强调长期轨迹、自测试和交互式项目。DeepSeek 增加了原生响应 API 和 Codex 支持，并提供低、高和最高三种推理级别。Gemini 在 FrontierCode 1.1 Main 上的份额从 34.4% 上升到 43.6%，同时将初始投入价格降至每百万代币 0.75 美元。模型竞争正从原始功能扩展到接口、延迟、预算和持续交付等方面。

🛡️ OpenAI Daybreak binds higher-risk capability to tiered access. Blue supports broad defensive work, while Red provides GPT-5.6-Cyber for tasks such as zero-day discovery and exploit-chain validation. Identity checks, hardware security keys, isolated environments, scoped permissions, and action review are part of the deployment model. As execution capability increases, authorization can no longer be reduced to a sentence in a prompt.

> **译文：** 🛡️ OpenAI Daybreak 将高风险能力与分层访问权限绑定。蓝色层支持广泛的防御工作，而红色层则提供 GPT-5.6-Cyber 来执行诸如零日漏洞发现和漏洞利用链验证等任务。身份验证、硬件安全密钥、隔离环境、权限范围和操作审查都是部署模型的一部分。随着执行能力的提升，授权不再能简化为提示信息中的一句话。

💬 Claude Tag and WorkBuddy bring agents into the everyday workplace. Claude Tag combines Slack channel context, memory, and standing instructions before choosing whether to reply, open a thread, route a message into existing work, or remain silent. Anthropic reports roughly a 30% improvement in proactive-response decisions. WorkBuddy uses Ask, Craft, and Plan modes to introduce models, skills, local directories, parallel tasks, and remote assistance through progressively wider permissions.

> **译文：** 💬 Claude Tag 和 WorkBuddy 将智能代理引入日常工作中。Claude Tag 会结合 Slack 频道上下文、记忆和现有指令，让用户选择是回复、创建新话题、将消息路由到现有工作中，还是保持沉默。Anthropic 报告称，主动响应决策率提高了约 30%。WorkBuddy 使用“询问”、“创建”和“计划”三种模式，通过逐步扩大权限，引入模型、技能、本地目录、并行任务和远程协助功能。

🧩 The harness is becoming the operating layer between a model and its environment. DeepSeek Harness v0.1 expresses models, tools, skills, sandboxes, storage, and scheduling as plugins. The Codex harness uses deferred tools, asynchronous subagents, sandboxes, independent review, persistent transport, and compaction for long-running work. Harrison Chase offers a practical boundary: start with a general harness, then accept custom complexity only when real traces repeatedly show that the work is moving outside the model's familiar distribution.

> **译文：** 🧩 框架正在成为模型与其环境之间的操作层。DeepSeek Harness v0.1 将模型、工具、技能、沙箱、存储和调度以插件的形式呈现。Codex 框架使用延迟工具、异步子代理、沙箱、独立审查、持久传输和压缩来处理长时间运行的任务。Harrison Chase 提出了一个实用的界限：首先使用通用框架，然后仅当实际跟踪反复表明任务超出模型熟悉的分布范围时，才接受自定义的复杂性。

✅ Quality can no longer depend on humans reading every generated line. Addy Osmani argues for continuous gates built from unit, property, mutation, and architectural tests. Charity Majors reaches the same problem through the idea of a trust account: when teams ship code they have not read line by line, deterministic simulation, evaluation, observability, and production feedback must replenish the missing trust. Faster delivery is only a local signal; customer value, software health, incidents, and rework determine whether the system actually improved.

> **译文：** ✅ 质量不能再依赖于人工逐行阅读代码。Addy Osmani 主张构建由单元测试、属性测试、变异测试和架构测试组成的连续门。Charity Majors 通过“信任账户”的概念解决了同样的问题：当团队交付未经逐行阅读的代码时，必须通过确定性模拟、评估、可观测性和生产反馈来弥补缺失的信任。更快的交付速度只是一个局部信号；客户价值、软件健康状况、事件和返工情况才能真正决定系统是否得到了改进。

⚠️ More agents do not naturally produce better coordination. In Anthropic's controlled experiments, 18 of 30 agents independently chose the same branch name, group accuracy on hidden-profile tasks fell to 17–36%, and conflicting objectives produced escalating turf wars. Stronger execution can make coordination failures unfold faster. Multiagent systems need isolation, shared rules, recourse, and human intervention; cooperation cannot be assumed to emerge from intelligence alone.

> **译文：** ⚠️ 更多智能体并不一定能自然而然地带来更好的协调。在 Anthropic 的对照实验中，30 个智能体中有 18 个独立地选择了相同的分支名称，群体在隐藏信息任务上的准确率下降到 17%–36%，并且相互冲突的目标导致了不断升级的争夺地盘之争。更强的执行力反而会加速协调失败的发生。多智能体系统需要隔离、共享规则、补救措施和人为干预；合作不能仅仅依靠智能自然产生。

🔬 Evaluation is shifting from outcome scoring to a replayable improvement loop. An Alibaba security team combines traces, deterministic diagnosis, four evaluation gates, and a cross-version failure list to improve skills, reporting GLM accuracy rising from 77.8% to 88.9%. Two years of Meituan practice connect response evaluation, trajectory evaluation, and calibrated human and machine judgment. Together they make a useful sequence explicit: observe the failure process first, then decide whether the model, prompt, tool, or workflow should change.

> **译文：** 🔬 评估方式正从结果评分转向可复现的改进循环。阿里巴巴安全团队结合追踪、确定性诊断、四道评估关卡以及跨版本故障列表来提升技能，其广义线性模型（GLM）准确率从 77.8% 提升至 88.9%。美团两年的实践将响应评估、轨迹评估以及校准后的人机判断相结合，明确了有效的流程：首先观察故障过程，然后决定是否需要更改模型、提示、工具或工作流程。

🌱 AI-native open source is redistributing maintenance work. Code generation is one part of the change; Alibaba's AI Code Review team reports about 20,000 monthly active users, adoption above 30%, false positives below 5%, and 20,000 GitHub stars after open sourcing. AI can absorb repetitive execution such as issue triage, testing, review, and release work. The core team still owns direction, merge judgment, regressions, and community relationships. Automation increases maintenance leverage without removing human responsibility.

> **译文：** 🌱 原生AI开源正在重新分配维护工作。代码生成是变革的一部分；阿里巴巴AI代码审查团队报告称，开源后月活跃用户约2万，采用率超过30%，误报率低于5%，并在GitHub上获得了2万颗星。AI可以承担重复性工作，例如问题分类、测试、审查和发布。核心团队仍然负责方向制定、合并判断、回归测试和社区关系维护。自动化提高了维护效率，但并未取代人的责任。

🔀 Routing and caching determine whether the execution layer is affordable. OpenRouter explains multi-model choice through meaningful differences among providers, failover, safety layers, and the ability to switch suppliers. Ruan Yifeng uses DeepSeek V4 Flash pricing to show that a cached input can cost one-fiftieth of an uncached one. Which model receives a task, how context is reused, and what happens when a provider fails now jointly determine the real cost and availability of the workflow.

> **译文：** 🔀 路由和缓存决定了执行层的成本是否合理。OpenRouter 通过不同提供商之间的显著差异、故障转移、安全层以及切换供应商的能力来解释多模型选择。阮义峰使用 DeepSeek V4 Flash 定价来说明，缓存的输入成本可能只有未缓存输入的五十分之一。任务分配给哪个模型、上下文如何重用以及提供商发生故障时会发生什么，这些因素共同决定了工作流的实际成本和可用性。

🌍 Execution layers eventually change the boundaries of products, markets, and scientific work. Peter Steinberger began with a phone-to-computer relay, then encountered security reviews and operational pressure after millions paid attention. Liu Ming-Yu uses Cosmos and Physical AI to explain how NVIDIA builds models to understand hardware and customer bottlenecks. A Tencent Technology analysis connects Discovery Loop with “LLMs Can't Jump” to ask whether agents can accumulate evidence, revise beliefs, and participate in scientific discovery. Once models can act, the durable competition moves into environments, verification, infrastructure, and accountability.

> **译文：** 🌍 执行层最终会改变产品、市场和科学工作的边界。Peter Steinberger 最初开发的是手机到电脑的中继技术，但在数百万用户关注后，他遇到了安全审查和运营压力。刘明宇利用 Cosmos 和物理人工智能解释了 NVIDIA 如何构建模型来理解硬件和客户瓶颈。腾讯科技的一项分析将发现循环与“LLM 无法跳跃”联系起来，探讨智能体是否能够积累证据、修正信念并参与科学发现。一旦模型能够行动，持久的竞争就会转移到环境、验证、基础设施和问责制等方面。

Hope this issue sparks some new ideas. Stay curious, and see you next week!

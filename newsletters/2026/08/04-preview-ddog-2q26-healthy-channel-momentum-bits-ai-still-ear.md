We spoke with six experts this quarter: two channel/GSI delivery partners covering global enterprise accounts, one North American F500 channel partner, one observability industry consultant, and two North American enterprise customers on the user side. Overall, channel partners report healthy business trends. Growth is still led by the traditional three (Infra/APM/Logs), with security and Dev-related products taking over as the second growth curve, while AI products themselves are still early in their contribution. On the frontier LLM customers, we understand OpenAI’s 2Q usage declined roughly 10-20% q/q with further optimization ahead, while Anthropic’s 2Q usage should be quite strong — net net, limited impact on the quarter.

> **译文：** 本季度我们采访了六位专家：两家服务于全球企业客户的渠道/GSI交付合作伙伴、一家北美财富500强渠道合作伙伴、一位可观测性行业顾问，以及两家北美企业用户客户。总体而言，渠道合作伙伴的业务发展趋势良好。增长仍然主要由传统的三大领域（基础设施/应用性能管理/日志）引领，安全和开发相关产品正在成为第二大增长点，而人工智能产品本身的贡献仍处于早期阶段。在前沿LLM客户方面，我们了解到OpenAI第二季度的使用量环比下降了约10-20%，未来还将进行进一步优化；而Anthropic第二季度的使用量应该会相当强劲——总而言之，对本季度的影响有限。

2Q26 Progress and Outlook — Broadly Constructive

North America Channel Expert A

This expert’s 1Q26 ultimately landed at about 26% y/y, above the preliminary 21% we had last quarter; 2Q26 is tracking to 33-35%, a clear acceleration and above his own expectations

> **译文：** 这位专家预测2026年第一季度最终同比增长约26%，高于我们上季度初步预测的21%；第二季度预计将达到33%-35%，增速明显加快，超出他自身的预期。

Last quarter his 1H26 target was 22-24.5%; it has since been revised up to 23.5-26.5%, with 1Q coming in slightly above 26%

> **译文：** 上季度他给出的2026年上半年目标增长率为22-24.5%；此后该目标被上调至23.5-26.5%，其中第一季度略高于26%。

The 2H26 target has been raised sharply from 26-28.5% to 32-37%, making DDOG one of the few vendors for which he has taken targets up this year

> **译文：** 2026年下半年目标收益率已从26-28.5%大幅上调至32-37%，这使得DDOG成为今年他上调目标收益率的少数几家公司之一。

Three reasons for the 2Q acceleration:

Customers have reached a reasonably mature stage in their AI deployments and can now judge how observability spend should keep pace with AI; the MELT 2.0 transition largely ended at the close of 1Q26

> **译文：** 客户的AI部署已发展到相当成熟的阶段，现在可以判断可观测性支出应如何与AI发展保持同步；MELT 2.0的过渡工作已于2026年第一季度末基本完成。

Five large deals closed in 2Q, three of which had been in the works since late last year, for aggregate TCV of more than $25-30m and up to $35m on his widest measure; year one is concentrated on activation and implementation, so revenue is front-loaded

> **译文：** 第二季度完成了五笔大额交易，其中三笔自去年年底就开始筹备，总合同价值超过2500万至3000万美元，按最广泛的衡量标准计算，最高可达3500万美元；第一年主要集中在激活和实施阶段，因此收入主要集中在前期。

Deal slippage has fallen off materially — in prior quarters at least half of signings would push into the following quarter, whereas most 2Q organic bookings landed within the quarter, and July is following the same pattern

> **译文：** 交易延期情况已大幅下降——此前几个季度，至少一半的签约会推迟到下一季度，而第二季度的大部分自然成交订单都在本季度内完成，7月份的情况也延续了这一趋势。

Larger-TCV deals remain concentrated in high-tech and parts of financial services, and have not yet spread across the broader customer base

> **译文：** 总合同价值较高的交易仍然集中在高科技和部分金融服务领域，尚未扩展到更广泛的客户群体。

On the source of growth: new logos will still contribute less than one-third in 2H and into next year, with the bulk of the incremental dollars coming from product expansion within the installed base

> **译文：** 关于增长来源：下半年及明年，新客户带来的收入仍将不到三分之一，大部分新增收入将来自现有客户群的产品扩张。

Customers previously running 2-3 DDOG products are moving to at least 5; the land products are still Infra and APM, then Logs, followed by Synthetics and Cloud Security

> **译文：** 之前运行 2-3 款 DDOG 产品的客户正在迁移到至少 5 款；其中，基础设施和应用性能管理 (APM) 产品仍然是核心产品，其次是日志产品，然后是合成数据和云安全产品。

The buying center is no longer just the CIO/CISO — SRE, QA, DevOps and platform engineering are now decision-makers, pulling through Synthetic Monitoring, CI Visibility and Error Tracking

> **译文：** 采购中心不再仅仅是首席信息官/首席信息安全官——SRE、QA、DevOps 和平台工程团队现在也是决策者，他们通过综合监控、持续集成可见性和错误跟踪来推动决策。

2H growth mix: traditional products (applications/infrastructure/logs) account for at least two-thirds; security contributes at least 15% and Dev-related products about 10%, together roughly one-quarter; AI-related products total 10-15%, which he concedes is on the optimistic side

> **译文：** 下半年增长构成：传统产品（应用/基础设施/日志）至少占三分之二；安全产品至少占15%；开发相关产品约占10%，两者合计约占四分之一；人工智能相关产品占10-15%，他承认这个数字比较乐观。

The multiplier effect of AI on application count: before mid-2023, a typical enterprise had roughly 100 developers supporting 400 applications; today headcount is up only 5-10% (and skewed to senior engineers) while application count is close to 650, up more than 50%

> **译文：** 人工智能对应用程序数量的倍增效应：在 2023 年年中之前，一家典型的企业大约有 100 名开发人员支持 400 个应用程序；如今，员工人数仅增长了 5-10%（而且主要集中在高级工程师），而应用程序数量接近 650 个，增长超过 50%。

Application architecture is being rebuilt as well — what used to be a simple web call to a database now layers in vector DBs, models, and embedding models, at least doubling component count and widening the observability surface accordingly

> **译文：** 应用程序架构也在进行重构——过去简单的数据库 Web 调用现在叠加了向量数据库、模型和嵌入模型，组件数量至少翻了一番，可观测性范围也相应扩大。

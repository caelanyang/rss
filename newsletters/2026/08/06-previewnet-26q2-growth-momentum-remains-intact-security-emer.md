Summary

Q2 channel performance remains strong, with growth likely to accelerate further from Q1

> **译文：** 第二季度渠道表现依然强劲，增长速度可能较第一季度进一步加快。

Our base case for 2H26 is stable to modestly accelerating growth

> **译文：** 我们对2026年下半年的基本预测是保持稳定或略微加速增长。

The core growth driver has shifted from standalone CDN/WAF to product expansion and enterprise consolidation. Zero Trust is driving a meaningful increase in ACV, with incremental contributions from Workers and AI products

> **译文：** 核心增长动力已从独立 CDN/WAF 转向产品扩展和企业整合。零信任架构正显著提升年度合同价值 (ACV)，而 Workers 和 AI 产品也做出了增量贡献。

Large-enterprise momentum continues to improve. Channel partners are seeing migrations from AWS/Azure CDN and Zscaler, alongside a growing number of large, multi-year deals

> **译文：** 大型企业市场的增长势头持续增强。渠道合作伙伴正目睹客户从 AWS/Azure CDN 和 Zscaler 迁移，同时，大型多年期合同的数量也在不断增长。

Cloudflare One’s SASE advantages are primarily its installed base, global edge network, deployment speed, and vendor-consolidation proposition. However, gaps remain in data protection, SD-WAN, and identity security, while Palo Alto and Zscaler remain formidable competitors in large, complex enterprise deployments

> **译文：** Cloudflare One 的 SASE 优势主要体现在其庞大的用户基数、全球边缘网络、部署速度以及厂商整合方案上。然而，其在数据保护、SD-WAN 和身份安全方面仍存在不足，而 Palo Alto Networks 和 Zscaler 在大型复杂企业部署领域仍然是强大的竞争对手。

The principal risk is the pace of deal conversion rather than pipeline sufficiency. Higher hardware costs are squeezing enterprise IT budgets, while large multi-year deals require financing and multiple layers of approval. Better-than-expected conversion could drive stronger 2H26 growth

> **译文：** 主要风险在于交易转化速度，而非项目储备是否充足。硬件成本上涨挤压了企业IT预算，而大型多年期交易需要融资和多层审批。高于预期的转化率可能会推动2026年下半年更强劲的增长。

Workers AI, AI Gateway, and bot monetization remain early-stage, with a more meaningful revenue contribution more likely to emerge in 2027

> **译文：** Workers AI、AI Gateway 和机器人货币化仍处于早期阶段，更有意义的收入贡献更有可能在 2027 年出现。

Public internet data points to positive adoption trends across CDN, Zero Trust, and Workers

> **译文：** 公共互联网数据显示，CDN、零信任和工作节点等方面的采用趋势呈积极态势。

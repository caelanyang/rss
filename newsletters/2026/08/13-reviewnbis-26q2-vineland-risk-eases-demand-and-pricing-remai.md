Key Takeaways

Nebius delivered an overall positive Q2.

The biggest pre-earnings risk—whether the Vineland 2 delay would force a cut to FY26 revenue guidance—did not materialize. At the same time, GPU demand, pricing, and Token Factory all came in stronger than expected.

> **译文：** 财报发布前最大的风险——Vineland 2项目的延期是否会导致2026财年营收预期下调——并未发生。与此同时，GPU需求、定价以及Token Factory的表现均强于预期。

Q2 group revenue reached $582Mn, up 46% QoQ, while annualized run-rate revenue increased from $1.9Bn in Q1 to $3.0Bn. Adjusted EBITDA margin improved from 32% to 41%. The company maintained its FY26 guidance of $3.0–3.4Bn in revenue, $7–9Bn in ARR, and an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 40%.

> **译文：** 第二季度集团营收达5.82亿美元，环比增长46%；年度经常性营收从第一季度的19亿美元增至30亿美元。调整后EBITDA利润率从32%提升至41%。公司维持2026财年营收30亿至34亿美元、年度经常性营收70亿至90亿美元以及调整后EBITDA利润率约为40%的预期。

> **译文：** 与第一季度相比，我们看到三个关键的渐进式发展：

Demand and pricing were stronger than expected. Management said it could sell out its entire 2027 capacity today if it chose to do so, while the company has begun using short-duration contracts and capacity auctions to drive higher monetization per MW.

> **译文：** 需求和定价均超出预期。管理层表示，如果愿意，公司今天就可以售罄其2027年的全部产能。同时，公司已开始采用短期合同和容量拍卖的方式来提高每兆瓦的收益。

Token Factory has moved from product-market fit to P&L contribution. The CFO explicitly cited Token Factory as a contributor to both Q2 revenue growth and margin expansion.

> **译文：** Token Factory 的业务已从产品市场契合点转变为损益贡献点。首席财务官明确指出，Token Factory 对第二季度营收增长和利润率提升均做出了贡献。

The Vineland 2 risk has shifted from a potential guidance cut to revenue timing. The company maintained its YE26 connected-power target of 800MW–1GW. Still, it clarified that it takes a few months for connected power to translate into revenue generation, implying that a meaningful portion of this capacity will only become active progressively through 1H27.

> **译文：** Vineland 2项目的风险已从潜在的业绩指引下调转向营收时间。公司维持了2026年底800兆瓦至1吉瓦的并网发电目标。不过，公司也明确指出，并网发电需要几个月的时间才能转化为营收，这意味着大部分产能要到2027年上半年才能逐步投入使用。

As a result, we still see a reasonable possibility that FY26 revenue ultimately comes in below the $3.2Bn midpoint of guidance, although an outright guidance cut is no longer our base case. More importantly, following Q2, we see greater upside to 2027 revenue/MW and margin potential than to FY26 estimates.

> **译文：** 因此，我们仍然认为2026财年营收最终低于32亿美元指引中值的可能性较大，尽管我们不再直接下调指引。更重要的是，在第二季度之后，我们认为2027年每兆瓦营收和利润率的上升空间大于2026财年的预期。

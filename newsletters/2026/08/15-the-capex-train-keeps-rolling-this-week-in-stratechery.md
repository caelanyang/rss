* *The Capital Constraint*. Everyone knows we are short of AI compute. Everyone knows that we may very soon be short on power. What happens, however, if we are short on capital? If AI is as valuable as it seems, then it should pay for itself, but that hasn’t happened yet. This week Nvidia announced a new funding mechanism to tap long-duration capital; this sort of financial engineering, along with Google leading the way in tapping equity , may build the bridge to sustainable AI revenue. It also expands the blast radius of a bubble in the service of Nvidia’s threatened margins. I covered this in Nvidia’s Risky Business , as well as this week’s episode of Sharp Tech. — Ben Thompson

> **译文：** * *资金限制*。人人都知道我们缺乏人工智能计算能力。人人都知道我们可能很快就会面临电力短缺。然而，如果我们缺乏资金该怎么办？如果人工智能真如表面看起来那么有价值，那么它应该能够自给自足，但这种情况尚未发生。本周，英伟达宣布了一种新的融资机制，旨在获取长期资本；这种金融工程，加上谷歌在股权融资方面的领先地位，或许能够为人工智能的可持续收入搭建桥梁。但这同时也扩大了泡沫的冲击范围，加剧了英伟达利润率受到威胁的风险。我在《英伟达的风险业务》以及本周的《Sharp Tech》节目中都对此进行了探讨。——本·汤普森

* *What to Do About AI Writing?* Anyone who’s been online the past few years has found themselves wondering “was written by a human or AI?” and some version of that question will probably persist in perpetuity for all of us. To that end, the EU has mandated that providers of AI systems mark all their outputs, including text, as AI-generated. Wednesday’s Update explored Anthropic’s response to that European regulation and why Ben thinks all of this is a terrible idea. We talked more about the issue on Friday’s episode of Sharp Tech , including the history of idea propagation across centuries , and the likelihood that my son will likely feel differently about AI-generated output than I do. — Andrew S harp

> **译文：** **如何看待人工智能写作？** 过去几年里，任何上网的人都会不由自主地思考“这些内容是人写的还是人工智能写的？”这个问题或许会一直困扰着我们所有人。为此，欧盟强制要求人工智能系统提供商将所有输出内容（包括文本）标记为人工智能生成。周三的更新探讨了Anthropic公司对这项欧洲法规的回应，以及Ben为何认为这一切都是个糟糕的主意。我们在周五的Sharp Tech节目中更深入地讨论了这个问题，包括几个世纪以来思想传播的历史，以及我的儿子对人工智能生成内容的看法很可能与我截然不同。——Andrew Sharp

* *A Tale of Two Cites.* In an article that was written 100% by a human (though proofread by ChatGPT!), this week’s on Sharp Text explored why caustic rhetoric from Mayor Zohran Mamdani is probably not enough to convince finance execs to leave New York City (even if they live in Connecticut), while Hollywood has unfortunately seen much of its filmmaking industry outsourced to neighboring states and foreign countries (as David Ellison threatens to relocate Paramount’s operations). In brief: New York is a case study in the power of network effects, while Hollywood offers a lesson in the conditions that allow those effects to be broken (and Mamdani exemplifies an irony of the DSA movement, generally). — AS

> **译文：** * *双城记* 本周的《Sharp Text》专栏文章由真人撰写（经ChatGPT校对！），探讨了纽约市长佐兰·马姆达尼的尖锐言辞为何不足以说服金融高管离开纽约市（即便他们住在康涅狄格州），而好莱坞却不幸地将大部分电影制作业务外包给了邻近州和外国（大卫·埃里森威胁要将派拉蒙的业务迁走）。简而言之：纽约是网络效应力量的典型案例，而好莱坞则为我们提供了一个教训，让我们了解哪些因素会导致这些效应被打破（而马姆达尼则体现了民主社会主义者运动的普遍讽刺之处）。——AS

Cerebras pulled off the biggest semiconductor IPO. The May 2026 IPO priced at $185, surged to $386 on opening day (+68%), then spent the next six weeks giving back most of its gains. Its first earnings report triggered a record two-day plunge briefly below the IPO price.

Cerebras完成了迄今为止规模最大的半导体IPO。该公司于2026年5月发行，发行价为每股185美元，首日股价飙升至386美元（涨幅达68%），随后回吐了大部分涨幅。首份财报发布后，股价在两天内暴跌至发行价以下，创下历史纪录。

Revenue nearly doubled, Cerebras beat expectations, and management raised full-year guidance. The problem was not demand — it was the cost of keeping up with it. Management argues the market misunderstood the softer margin outlook due to renting back chips while waiting for new data centers.

营收几乎翻了一番，Cerebras 业绩超出预期，并上调了全年业绩指引。问题不在于需求，而在于满足需求的成本。管理层认为，由于在等待新数据中心建成期间需要回租芯片，市场误解了利润率预期放缓的情况。

## How Cerebras Makes Money

Cerebras' Wafer-Scale Engine packs ~4 trillion transistors vs ~200B on NVIDIA's Blackwell. The CS-3 generates tokens ~10x faster than a GPU cluster, hitting 1,000 tokens/sec on frontier models.

Cerebras 的晶圆级引擎集成了约 4 万亿个晶体管。CS-3 生成代币的速度比 GPU 集群快约 10 倍，在前沿模型上每秒可生成 1000 个代币。

- **Hardware**: $111M in Q1 FY26 (+59% Y/Y)
- **Cloud & services**: $83M (+178% Y/Y), growing 3x faster
- **Key customers**: OpenAI (multi-year $20B+ deal with warrants reducing reported revenue), G42, MBZUAI, AWS

硬件收入：2026财年第一季度为1.11亿美元（同比增长59%）。云服务收入：8300万美元（同比增长178%）。主要客户：OpenAI（多年期合同，金额超过200亿美元）、G42、MBZUAI、AWS。

Cerebras uses no HBM, no CoWoS packaging, no 3nm process — it sidesteps all the scarce resource bottlenecks. It's the only AI accelerator maker building systems entirely in the US.

Cerebras不使用HBM，是唯一一家完全在美国本土构建系统的AI加速器制造商。

IPO arb

A simple good trade is: 

 * Buy stock in a hot initial public offering at the IPO price. For instance, 
buy stock in SpaceX’s IPO at $135 per share. 
 * When the stock starts trading publicly and immediately goes up 20% or so, 
sell it. 
This trade is not perfect. For one thing, occasionally IPOs trade down, not 
up, so there is some risk. For another thing, you might be leaving money on the 
table if you sell after just one day. SpaceX went up another 20% on its second 
day of trading. “SpaceX shares have surged nearly 50% in their first days of 
trading and left a trail of remorseful traders in their wake: those who sold 
too early,” the Wall Street Journalreported this week <https://bloom.bg/4ehC1sD>
. Plus maybe in 10 years it will be worth a quadrillion dollars.

* 以IPO价格买入热门IPO股票。例如，

以每股135美元的价格买入SpaceX的IPO股票。

* 当股票开始公开交易并立即上涨20%左右时，

卖出。

这种交易并非完美。首先，IPO股票有时会下跌而不是上涨，因此存在一定的风险。其次，如果你只在一天后就卖出，你可能会错失良机。SpaceX在上市第二天又上涨了20%。《华尔街日报》本周报道称：“SpaceX股票在上市首日飙升近50%，让那些过早卖出的交易者后悔不已。”

此外，也许10年后，它的价值将达到万亿美元。

All in all, though, this is an excellent trade; there are not a lot of trades 
that offer fairly reliable 20% one-day returns. And so themain problem with 
this trade is that nobody wants to let you do it. For one thing, it is hard to 
buy shares in a hot IPO. Retail investors often don’t have any opportunity to 
buy shares at the IPO price; even institutional investors who put in orders 
will not necessarily get any shares. Or, if you do get shares, you’ll get less 
than you hoped for. SpaceX’s $75 billion IPO “drew more than $350 billion in 
demand from institutions and retail investors,”Bloomberg reported 
<https://bloom.bg/4vWe6Vo>: “Among the firms that placed orders, close to 
one-third of them didn’t receive any stock,” and retail investors asked for 
$100 billion of stock and got only $15 billion.

总而言之，这是一笔绝佳的交易；很少有交易能提供相当可靠的20%的单日收益。因此，这笔交易的主要问题在于，没有人愿意让你这么做。首先，在热门IPO中买入股票非常困难。散户投资者通常没有机会以IPO价格购买股票；即使是提交订单的机构投资者也未必能买到股票。或者，即使你买到了股票，数量也会比你预期的少。彭博社报道称，SpaceX 750亿美元的IPO“吸引了超过3500亿美元的机构和散户投资者需求”：
<https://bloom.bg/4vWe6Vo> “在提交订单的公司中，近三分之一没有收到任何股票”，而散户投资者申请了价值1000亿美元的股票，最终只获得了150亿美元。

For another thing, if you do get shares in a hot IPO, it is probably because 
you pinky-swore not to sell them. When SpaceX and its bankers allocated shares 
to institutional investors in its IPO, “they assessed who would be committed 
long-term owners and weeded out those most likely to dump the stock for a quick 
profit,”the Financial Times reported <https://bloom.bg/4vfSLXc>, and most of 
the shares went to long-only investors rather than hedge funds. (Long-only 
holderscould dump the stock for a quick profit, but if you do that too often 
you will get a bad reputation and won’t get stock in the next hot IPO.) And 
retail brokerages “typically have policies <https://bloom.bg/4ehC1sD> 
discouraging users from quickly selling, or ‘flipping,’ IPO shares,” and will 
also cut you off from the next IPO if you flip this one.

另外，如果你真的在热门IPO中获得了股票，那很可能是因为你发誓绝不出售。据《金融时报》报道，SpaceX及其银行家在IPO中向机构投资者分配股票时，“他们评估了哪些人会是长期持有者，并剔除了那些最有可能抛售股票以快速获利的人”，而且大部分股票都流向了只做多的投资者，而不是对冲基金。（只做多的投资者也可以抛售股票快速获利，但如果你这样做太频繁，就会名声受损，并且无法在下一个热门IPO中获得股票。）此外，零售经纪公司“通常都有政策，不鼓励用户快速出售或‘炒股’IPO股票”，而且如果你炒了这次IPO的股票，他们也会取消你参与下一个IPO的资格。

This is an annoying state of affairs: There’s a good, low-risk, high-return, 
short-term trade that is available on paper but that you can’t easily do. You 
want to do it. How?

这种情况令人恼火：理论上存在一个风险低、回报高的短期交易机会，但实际上却难以操作。你想做，该怎么办？

The very best way to do it is probably to be a big hedge fund that pays a lot 
of fees to the big banks. Companies actuallydo want some IPO investors to flip 
their stock — if no one sold it, no one could buy it, and there’d be no IPO pop 
and no liquidity — but getting into that group is quite selective. If the banks 
like you — because you do a lot of trading and pay a lot of fees, or because 
you help them out by buying a lot of shares in IPOs thatweren’t particularly 
hot — they are more likely to reward you with some shares to flip.

最好的方法或许是成为一家向大型银行支付高额费用的对冲基金。公司确实希望一些IPO投资者能够转手他们的股票——如果没人卖，就没人买，IPO就不会出现热潮，流动性也会受到影响——但要进入这个圈子非常困难。如果银行喜欢你——因为你交易频繁、支付高额费用，或者因为你通过购买大量表现不佳的IPO股票来帮助他们——他们更有可能奖励你一些股票让你转手。

But that’s hard to do and still requires goodwill from banks, and you might 
want a more self-help approach. Here’s one, from Bloomberg’s Denitsa Tsekova 
<https://bloom.bg/4xH9eW4>:

但这很难做到，而且仍然需要银行的善意，你可能更倾向于自助的方式。以下是彭博社记者丹妮莎·采科娃 (Denitsa Tsekova) 提供的一种方法：

<https://bloom.bg/4xH9eW4>

A series of unusual multibillion-dollar flows has rippled through the 
exchange-traded fund market in the past week, suggesting some major investors 
used a contentious method to get exposure to the initial public offering of 
SpaceX. At least one fund manager placed temporary restrictions on its product 
in a bid to curb the practice.

过去一周，交易所交易基金（ETF）市场出现了一系列数十亿美元的异常资金流动，这表明一些大型投资者可能采用了有争议的方法来参与SpaceX的首次公开募股（IPO）。至少有一家基金经理暂时限制了其产品的交易，以遏制这种做法。

The biggest flows were in Cathie Wood’s flagship strategy. The $6.9 billion 
ARK Innovation ETF (ticker ARKK) posted a record $4.6 billion inflow late last 
week before recording its largest-ever outflow of $6.2 billion in the following 
session, data compiled by Bloomberg show. That activity coincided with ARKK 
acquiring roughly 1.7 million shares of SpaceX on the day the rocket maker 
listed, and echoes similar patterns seen during a number of IPOs in the past 
year.

资金流动最剧烈的是凯茜·伍德的旗舰策略。规模达69亿美元的ARK创新ETF（股票代码：ARKK）上周晚些时候录得创纪录的46亿美元资金流入，随后在接下来的交易日又录得有史以来最大的62亿美元资金流出。彭博社汇编的数据显示，这一资金流出恰逢火箭制造商SpaceX上市当天，ARKK买入了约170万股SpaceX股票，这与过去一年中多家IPO的类似模式遥相呼应。

The theory among ETF specialists is that such flows represent an IPO arbitrage 
strategy. The tactic involves pouring cash into an ETF before an offering in 
which the fund is expected to receive shares, then pulling the money out once 
the stock begins trading. Such a maneuver could provide indirect, or synthetic, 
exposure to the IPO and would profit from any post-listing gain, though it 
risks distorting fund performance and disadvantaging longer-term holders.

ETF专家普遍认为，这种资金流动代表了一种IPO套利策略。该策略是指在预期该基金将获得股票的ETF发行前，向该基金投入资金，然后在股票开始交易后撤出资金。这种操作可以提供对IPO的间接或合成敞口，并从上市后的任何收益中获利，但同时也存在扭曲基金业绩并损害长期持有者利益的风险。

“Investors appear to be using ARKK as a piggyback vehicle for access to 
opportunities they may not be able to easily buy directly,” said Bloomberg 
Intelligence ETF analyst Athanasios Psarofagis. “That trend has become more 
common in ARKK around major IPOs.”

彭博情报ETF分析师Athanasios Psarofagis表示：“投资者似乎将ARKK作为一种搭便车工具，以获取他们可能无法直接轻易购买的投资机会。这种趋势在大型IPO前后，在ARKK中变得越来越普遍。”

We have  discussed this trade before <https://bloom.bg/4xsIYOW>. If you know, 
or suspect, that ARKK will get a big SpaceX IPO allocation at $135, then you 
can buy shares of ARKK the day before the IPO and sell them the day after: The 
net asset value of ARKK will have been increased by the SpaceX IPO pop. Of 
course, the net asset value might also go up or down because of movements in the
otherstocks in the portfolio, but you can hedge those out: ARKK’s portfolio is 
public, so you can short the other stocks in ARKK as a hedge, leaving you with
justSpaceX IPO pop exposure.

我们之前讨论过这笔交易<https://bloom.bg/4xsIYOW>。如果您知道或怀疑ARKK将在SpaceX IPO时以135美元的价格获得大量配售，那么您可以在IPO前一天买入ARKK的股票，并在IPO后一天卖出：SpaceX IPO的股价上涨将提升ARKK的净资产值。当然，投资组合中其他股票的波动也可能导致净资产值上涨或下跌，但您可以进行对冲：ARKK的投资组合是公开的，因此您可以做空ARKK中的其他股票进行对冲，从而只承担SpaceX IPO股价上涨带来的风险。

And, most neatly, you can do the whole trade with ARKK. The actual way that 
money flows into and out of ARKK is through in-kind creations and redemptions. 
You borrow the stocks in ARKK’s underlying portfolio, you deliver them (short) 
to ARKK, you get back shares of ARKK, you wait a day, you hand back the ARKK 
shares and get back the underlying portfolio, presto, now you own some SpaceX 
at its IPO price. “The scale of the flows,” reports Tsekova, “suggests the 
strategy uses a fund’s creation-and-redemption mechanism rather than buying and 
selling shares on an exchange, and that one or more large institution is behind 
the flows.”

最妙的是，你可以用 ARKK 完成整个交易。资金流入和流出 ARKK 的实际方式是通过实物申购和赎回。

你借入 ARKK 底层投资组合中的股票，将它们（做空）

交付给 ARKK，取回 ARKK 的股票，等待一天，再将 ARKK 的股票

归还，取回底层投资组合，瞧，现在你就以 IPO 价格拥有了 SpaceX 的部分股票。Tsekova 指出，“资金流动的规模表明，该策略使用的是基金的申购和赎回机制，而不是在交易所买卖股票，而且背后可能有一家或多家大型机构在操控这些资金流动。”

People get mad about this, though oddly not Cathie Wood:

人们对此感到愤怒，但奇怪的是，凯茜·伍德却不这么认为：

The potential losers are clear: existing ETF holders will see both any gains 
from the offering and their stake in the newly listed company reduced.

潜在的受害者显而易见：现有ETF持有者不仅会失去此次发行带来的收益，而且他们在新上市公司的股份也会减少。

In a bid to protect holders of the $2.4 billion ERShares Private-Public 
Crossover ETF (XOVR), Joel Shulman, founder and chief investment officer of 
ERShares, restricted primary creations for the fund ahead of the SpaceX 
offering and imposed a 2% redemption fee. Investors in XOVR already had 
indirect exposure to the company before its public debut.

为了保护规模达24亿美元的ERShares私募-上市交叉ETF（XOVR）的持有者，ERShares创始人兼首席投资官乔尔·舒尔曼在SpaceX上市前限制了该基金的首次认购，并收取了2%的赎回费。XOVR的投资者在SpaceX上市前就已经间接持有该公司股份。

“We stopped the creations the week before the IPO, and the intent was to 
protect our long-term loyal shareholders from dilution,” Shulman said. “We 
implemented a redemption fee to cover any related costs associated with 
disruptive large inflows and outflows.” …

舒尔曼表示：“我们在IPO前一周停止了新股发行，目的是保护我们长期忠实股东的权益免受稀释。我们实施了赎回费，以弥补与大规模资金流入和流出相关的任何成本。”

“We don’t participate in every IPO,” Wood said in a Bloomberg Television 
interview [in 2025]. “If they guess right, great. And if they don’t, well, then 
they have nothing. They’ve incurred some costs.”

伍德在2025年接受彭博电视台采访时表示：“我们并非参与每一起IPO。如果他们猜对了，那很好。如果他们猜错了，那他们就什么也得不到。他们已经承担了一些成本。”

There are other, fuzzier versions of this trade. Tsekova mentions “the Baron 
First Principles ETF (RONB) from Baron Capital” as another ETF that got inflows 
and outflows similar to ARKK’s. But Baron runs a couple of other funds that 
held SpaceX stock for a long time prior to the IPO. Those funds bought SpaceX 
at prices wellbelow the IPO price, so if you were a long-term holder of those 
funds then you profited by SpaceX’s increasing valuation over the past few 
years. But, also, if you bought those Baron fundslast month, you got a nice IPO 
boost. Barron’s (no relation)reported last week <https://bloom.bg/4fYZf8d>:

还有其他一些不太明确的交易版本。Tsekova提到“Baron Capital旗下的Baron First Principles ETF (RONB)”，称其资金流入和流出情况与ARKK类似。但Baron还管理着其他几只基金，这些基金在SpaceX上市前长期持有SpaceX股票。这些基金买入SpaceX股票的价格远低于IPO价格，因此，如果你长期持有这些基金，那么你就从SpaceX过去几年不断上涨的估值中获利。此外，如果你上个月买入了这些Baron基金，你也获得了不错的IPO收益。Barron（与本文无关）上周报道了此事<https://bloom.bg/4fYZf8d>：

Baron Capital is one of the largest holders of SpaceX stock in the mutual fund 
industry and the firm wrote up the value of that stake a week before the coming 
IPO pricing, expected on Thursday.

Baron Capital是共同基金行业中SpaceX股票的最大持有者之一，该公司在即将到来的IPO定价（预计于周四）前一周评估了其持股价值。

The $17 billion Baron Partners fund and the $3.7 billion Baron Asset both got 
big gains last Thursday — almost 7% and 8%, respectively. As of March 31, Baron 
Partners had nearly 30% of its assets in SpaceX and Baron Asset had 23%.

规模达170亿美元的Baron Partners基金和规模达37亿美元的Baron Asset基金上周四均获得大幅收益，分别上涨近7%和8%。截至3月31日，Baron Partners基金近30%的资产投资于SpaceX，Baron Asset基金则持有23%的SpaceX股票。

The firm had said on its website that the SpaceX shares would be repriced on 
June 4, last Thursday. ...

该公司在其网站上表示，SpaceX股票将于6月4日（上周四）重新定价。



The Partners fund was carrying its SpaceX stock at about $105 a share on March 
31, compared with the planned IPO price of $135. The March 31 value appears to 
be the latest public mark on the SpaceX stock by the firm. …

截至3月31日，合伙人基金持有的SpaceX股票价格约为每股105美元，低于计划的IPO发行价135美元。3月31日的价格似乎是SpaceX公司最近一次公开给出的股票价格。

Thursday's gains came a day after SpaceX filed its updated prospectus, which 
included the $135 targeted IPO price.

周四的上涨发生在SpaceX提交更新版招股说明书的第二天，该招股说明书包含了135美元的目标IPO价格。

Baron’s funds marked SpaceX at a $1.25 trillion valuation 
<https://bloom.bg/4vXEhv5> until June 4, “using prices of recent financing 
transactions.” But by, you know, June 3, it was widely reported 
<https://bloom.bg/4fUU1dt> that SpaceX’s IPO valuation would be in the $1.8 
trillion area. Baron’s funds’ net asset values reflected historical prices of 
SpaceX stock, but you might reasonably have valued SpaceX based on its very 
near-future price. “SpaceX will go public next week at $135 and immediately 
trade up 20% to $162,” you might have thought, “so I should buy it from Ron 
Baron today at $105.” Morningstar analyst Jeffrey Ptakestimated last week 
<https://bloom.bg/3QLCTg5> that Baron Partners “received $1.8 billion in 
inflows” from June 1 through June 11, suggesting that a lot of people had a 
similar idea: Buy Baron Partners to get exposure to SpaceX at $105 or $135, and 
then — perhaps — sell when SpaceX predictably gets to $160.

巴伦的基金在6月4日之前将SpaceX的估值定为1.25万亿美元

<https://bloom.bg/4vXEhv5>，并“采用了近期融资交易的价格”。但到了6月3日，已有广泛报道称

<https://bloom.bg/4fUU1dt>SpaceX的IPO估值将达到1.8万亿美元左右。巴伦基金的净资产值反映了SpaceX股票的历史价格，但你或许会根据SpaceX近期的价格来合理地评估其价值。你可能会想：“SpaceX下周将以每股135美元的价格上市，并立即上涨20%至162美元，所以我今天应该以每股105美元的价格从罗恩·巴伦那里买入。”晨星分析师杰弗里·普塔克上周估计，巴伦合伙公司（Baron Partners）在6月1日至11日期间“获得了18亿美元的资金流入”，这表明很多人都有类似的想法：买入巴伦合伙公司的股票，以便在SpaceX股价达到105美元或135美元时持有SpaceX的股份，然后——或许——在SpaceX股价如预期般达到160美元时卖出。

This is a little bit like the opposite of the problems private credit had this 
year. Private credit funds had made a bunch of loans that they carried on their 
books <https://bloom.bg/4fTQNai> at their original price, but  the market was 
skeptical <https://bloom.bg/4fPdzQs> about those values. A fund might have a 
net asset value of $100 per share, but its investors thought it was worth $80 
per share. Therefore, a lot of them wanted to redeem: If the fund was only 
worth $80 per share, but would pay you out at $100, that was a good trade. 
Baron arguably offered the reverse trade: It had SpaceX shares worth $135 or 
$160, but would let you buy them (along with the rest of its holdings) at $105. 
The inflows “likely diluted the position as a percentage weight,” Ptak noted: 
Redeeming private-credit investors at NAV is bad for other holders if the NAV 
is too high; letting in new mutual-fund investors at NAV is bad for existing 
holders if the NAV is too low.

这与今年私募信贷遇到的问题有点相反。私募信贷基金发放了一系列贷款，并按原价在账面上保留，但市场对这些价值持怀疑态度。一只基金的净资产值可能为每股 100 美元，但其投资者认为它的价值只有每股 80 美元。因此，许多投资者想要赎回：如果该基金的每股价值只有 80 美元，但赎回价格为 100 美元，那么这笔交易就很划算。

Baron 提供的可以说是相反的交易：它持有 SpaceX 的股票，价值 135 美元或 160 美元，但允许你以 105 美元的价格购买这些股票（以及它持有的其他股票）。

Ptak指出，资金流入“可能稀释了该仓位的权重百分比”：

如果净值过高，按净值赎回私募信贷投资者对其他持有者不利；

如果净值过低，以净值引入新的共同基金投资者对现有持有者不利。

Cathie Wood and Ron Baron are the classic buy-and-hold SpaceX true believers 
whoof course are going to get shares in the IPO. But they run funds that are 
open to outside investors, and those outside investors might not all be 
buy-and-hold true believers. ARKK and Baron earn the IPO pop, because they buy 
shares at the IPO price (or better), but they do not immediately monetize it, 
because they don’tsell the shares the day after the IPO. But apparently some of 
their outside investors can synthetically monetize it; they can extract the IPO 
pop and capture it themselves.

Hedge fund gig economy

We have talked a few times recently about the multistrategy hedge fund gig 
economy. The big multimanager multistrategy “pod shop” hedge funds are in the 
business of identifying talented portfolio managers, hiring them, allocating 
capital to them, supervising them, and scaling their capital up or down as 
their opportunities grow or shrink. In many ways, it makes sense to hire the 
portfolio managersas employees. You can supervise them more closely, 
risk-manage them more efficiently and yell at them more loudly if they are 
employees of your firm.

我们最近几次谈到了多策略对冲基金的零工经济。大型的多经理多策略“小型对冲基金”的业务是：寻找有才华的投资组合经理，聘用他们，为他们分配资金，监督他们，并根据投资机会的增减调整他们的资金规模。从很多方面来看，将投资组合经理聘为员工是合理的。如果他们是你公司的员工，你可以更密切地监督他们，更有效地进行风险管理，并且可以更严厉地批评他们。

In other ways, though, they don’t really need to be employees. The typical 
reasons <https://bloom.bg/4aW9GFU> to hire an employee, rather than an outside 
contractor or gig worker or service provider, mostly don’t apply to hedge fund 
portfolio managers. You mostly don’t want centralized decisionmaking: Your 
risk-adjusted returns will be better if every portfolio manager is making her 
own trading decisions, not doing what she thinks the boss wants. Similarly, you 
mostly don’t want close collaboration, sharing of research and ideas, or 
serendipitous conversations by the water cooler. You don’t want a shared 
culture or esprit de corps; you want independent thinking, uncorrelated bets 
and vicious competition. Why do you need everyone to share an office and have 
the same name on their fleece vests?

但从其他方面来看，他们其实并不一定非得是雇员。通常情况下，雇佣雇员而非外部承包商、零工或服务提供商的理由，大多不适用于对冲基金的投资组合经理。你通常不希望决策集中化：如果每位投资组合经理都能独立做出交易决策，而不是按照老板的意愿行事，那么你的风险调整后收益会更好。同样，你通常也不希望密切合作、分享研究成果和想法，或者在饮水机旁进行偶然的交流。你不需要共同的文化或团队精神；你需要的是独立思考、不相关的投资以及激烈的竞争。为什么非要让每个人都共用一间办公室，穿着印有相同名字的抓绒背心呢？

And so we have talked about “buy-side alpha capture,” which all the 
multistrats seem to be discovering at the same time. Basically, the idea is 
that if you run a big hedge fund, instead of just allocating capital to your 
employees’ ideas, you can also pay outside hedge fund managers for some new 
ideas, and allocate capital to those. You apply your skills — at capital 
allocation and talent identification and idea vetting — to a wider assortment 
of managers and trades. You have more independent ideas to allocate capital to, 
and you don’t have to pay the outside managers a huge premium to come work for 
you exclusively. This month we discussed reports thatCitadel 
<https://bloom.bg/4vVOpEv> and Point72 <https://bloom.bg/4aRrS3t> are getting 
into this business, and nowBloomberg’s Liza Tetley reports 
<https://bloom.bg/4w1hA9q>:

因此，我们讨论了“买方阿尔法捕获”，所有多元策略似乎都在同时发现这一点。其基本理念是，如果你运营一家大型对冲基金，除了将资金分配给员工的想法之外，你还可以聘请外部对冲基金经理提供一些新的投资理念，并将资金分配给这些理念。你可以将你的技能——包括资金分配、人才识别和理念评估——应用于更广泛的经理人和交易。你将拥有更多独立的投资理念可供分配，而且无需支付外部经理人巨额溢价让他们为你独家工作。本月我们讨论了有关 Citadel 和 Point72 进军该行业的报道，

<https://bloom.bg/4vVOpEv> 和 <https://bloom.bg/4aRrS3t> 现在又有彭博社的 Liza Tetley 报道：

<https://bloom.bg/4w1hA9q>

Chris Rokos’ eponymous firm is exploring starting a program to tap trading 
intelligence from other hedge funds, joining a slew of peers racing to sign up 
such contributors.

克里斯·罗科斯的同名公司正在探索启动一项计划，以获取其他对冲基金的交易情报，加入到众多竞相招募此类贡献者的同行行列中。

Rokos Capital Management is looking at establishing a so-called buyside alpha

据知情人士透露，Rokos Capital Management正考虑设立一个所谓的买方阿尔法收益获取项目，该项目将从外部股票经理那里获得选股建议。由于涉及私人信息，这些知情人士要求匿名。

Football-bet risk premiums

The other day I wrote some jokes <https://bloom.bg/4fTd9bV> about indexes of 
prediction markets, among them:

前几天我写了一些关于预测市场指数的笑话<https://bloom.bg/4fTd9bV>，其中包括：

If the index was mostly high-probability bets (“bonds,” as they say), perhaps 
it would earn some expected return? (Those bets are arguably a sort of 
insurance, so you might expect to earn some premium.) ...

如果该指数主要由高概率押注（也就是所谓的“债券”）构成，或许它能带来一些预期收益？（这些押注可以说是一种保险，所以你可能会期望获得一些溢价。）

I will say, though, that “bet $1 million on an extreme favorite on Polymarket 
every month and, if you win, do it again” has almost the shape of a good 
financial product? Like, that pays a high bond-like return most of the time; it 
has some risk, but that risk is uncorrelated to other financial instruments. 
(It goes to zero when Spain draws with Cabo Verde.) Build an index, build a 
passive exchange-traded fund to track it, what is everyone waiting for?

不过，我得说，“每月在Polymarket上押注100万美元，押注一个极热门的球队，

如果赢了，就再来一次”这种策略，几乎可以算得上是一个不错的金融产品？比如，它大部分时间都能带来类似债券的高回报；它确实存在一些风险，但这种风险与其他金融工具不相关。

（当西班牙和佛得角打成平局时，收益会归零。）建立一个指数，或者建立一个被动型交易所交易基金来追踪它，大家还在等什么呢？

A reader pointed out that there is a literature, sort of. Here’s an archived 
<https://bloom.bg/4ertBxv> ESPN blog post <https://bloom.bg/4vXszAy> about an 
unpublished paper by Mike Wohl:

一位读者指出，这方面确实存在一些文献。以下是一篇存档的 ESPN 博客文章：

<https://bloom.bg/4ertBxv> 和 <https://bloom.bg/4vXszAy>，内容是关于 Mike Wohl 的一篇未发表的论文：



Wohl found his advantage in betting the money lines for college football teams 
that were favored by 20-25 points. He wrote in his paper: “There were 376 games 
in the last six seasons (approximately 62 per season or approximately 4.5 per 
week) that had spreads of between 20.0 and 25.0. Of those 376 games, 94.95 
percent of the favorites won the game outright. Investing equal amounts on all 
376 contests produces six straight years of profitable returns with an average 
annual (non-compounded and non-annualized) return of 12.24 percent.”

沃尔发现，投注大学橄榄球比赛中让分20-25分的球队，是他获利的关键。他在论文中写道：“过去六个赛季中，共有376场比赛（平均每个赛季约62场，或每周约4.5场）的让分在20分到25分之间。在这376场比赛中，94.95%的热门球队最终赢得了比赛。如果在这376场比赛中投入相同的资金，连续六年都能获得盈利，平均年化收益率（未计复利，也未年化）为12.24%。”

That was in the early 2010s, and I have not examined the out-of-sample 
results, but it is plausible that money-line betting on 25-point favorites [1]  
<> in college football still offers bond-like returns. People want to buy 
lottery tickets (bet on underdogs), so they pay for them; you are selling them 
the lottery tickets and collecting a positive expected return. (Or: You are 
taking a lot ofrisk, by betting $100 to win like $5, and you are getting paid 
for that risk in the form of positive expected return.)

那是2010年代初期的事了，我没有研究过样本外的结果，但大学橄榄球比赛中，投注25分热门球队的胜负盘[1]仍然有可能带来类似债券的回报。人们想买彩票（投注冷门球队），所以他们付钱；你卖给他们彩票，并获得预期收益。（或者说：你承担了很大的风险，投注100美元可能只赢5美元，而你以预期收益的形式获得了这种风险的回报。）

It goes without saying that this is not investing or gambling advice. But I 
wrote in March 2025 <https://bloom.bg/3SyhJCx> that “I kind of think we are 
about two years away from a sports gambling ETF.” There is a whole genre of 
ETFs that provide steady bond-like income by doing weird trades; we have 
discussedBOXX <https://bloom.bg/3Qv1nKf> (selling option box spreads for 
income) andCAIE <https://bloom.bg/4eP6BtM> (selling autocallables, that is, 
crash insurance on the stock market, for income). “Selling longshot football 
bets for income,” sure, why not. If the Securities and Exchange Commission
starts allowing prediction-market funds <https://bloom.bg/4fYZiAV>, there’s 
going to be an ETF that advertises steady 12% annual returns achieved with a 
diversified portfolio of college football bets.

毋庸置疑，这并非投资或赌博建议。但我曾在2025年3月写道（https://bloom.bg/3SyhJCx），“我认为我们距离体育博彩ETF的出现还有大约两年时间。” 有一类ETF通过进行一些非常规交易来提供类似债券的稳定收益；我们讨论过BOXX（通过卖出期权价差来获取收益）和CAIE（通过卖出自动赎回期权，也就是股市崩盘保险来获取收益）。“通过卖出冷门足球投注来获取收益”，当然可以，何乐而不为呢？如果美国证券交易委员会

开始允许预测市场基金 <https://bloom.bg/4fYZiAV>，那么就会出现一种 ETF，宣称通过多元化的大学橄榄球投注组合，实现稳定的 12% 年回报率。

Dog value-add

If you are a salesperson or trader or other revenue-generating employee, and 
you can demonstrably and reliably produce $1.8 million per year in additional 
gross profit for whatever company hires you, how much should you get paid? I 
feel like my rough generic guess is 50%, so like $900,000 per year. There are 
probably fields where the answer is more like 10% or 20%, so you’d get 
low-to-mid six figures. There is some theoretical argument that the right 
answer is, like, $1.79 million.

如果你是一名销售人员、交易员或其他创收员工，并且能够切实可靠地为雇佣你的公司每年创造 180 万美元的额外毛利润，那么你应该获得多少报酬？我粗略估计是 50%，也就是每年 90 万美元左右。可能有些领域的答案更接近 10% 或 20%，所以你的收入会达到六位数中低水平。也有理论认为，正确答案应该是 179 万美元。

Here’s an organization that is hiring employees who on average generate $1.8 
million in gross profits per year, but that pays them in kibble, dog treats, 
and pats on the head,because they are dogs <https://bloom.bg/3SyhGXn>:

这家公司雇佣的员工平均每年创造180万美元的毛利润，但他们的工资却是狗粮、狗零食和抚摸，因为他们是狗。<https://bloom.bg/3SyhGXn>

A shortage of dogs used to sniff out drugs and other contraband are costing 
South Africa’s tax agency millions of rand in foregone customs revenue.

用于缉毒和其他违禁品的警犬数量短缺，导致南非税务局损失数百万兰特的关税收入。

The agency’s Detector Dog Unit has 66 handlers, nine of whom are without dogs, 
and needs to acquire 14 additional canines to meet operational requirements, 
Finance Minister Enoch Godongwana said in a written reply to a lawmaker’s 
question on Thursday.

该机构的搜爆犬队有66名训犬员，其中9人没有搜爆犬，

需要再添置14只搜爆犬才能满足行动需求，

财政部长伊诺克·戈东瓦纳周四在回复议员提问的书面答复中表示。

Each dog contributes about 29.7 million rand ($1.8 million) to the state’s 
coffers each year by detecting goods that would have otherwise evaded customs 
duty, indicating that the shortfall in animals is costing some 415 million rand

他说，每只缉私犬每年通过查获原本会逃避关税的货物，为国家财政贡献约2970万兰特（180万美元），这表明缉私犬数量的短缺每年造成约4.15亿兰特的收入损失。

The two obvious jokes here are “they should try offering higher salaries” and 
“when will these dogs be replaced by AI.” Also, though, good job dogs? “More 
than 60% of dogs assessed do not meet the testing requirements,” says 
Godongwana, but if the ones whodo meet the standards are bringing in $1.8 
million a year then maybe you should lower the standards? Like a 
$500,000-a-year dog is still a pretty good dog?

这里两个显而易见的笑话是“他们应该尝试提高薪水”和

“这些狗什么时候会被人工智能取代？”不过，狗狗们干得好吗？“超过60%的受测犬不符合测试要求，”戈东瓦纳说，但如果符合标准的狗每年能带来180万美元的收入，那么也许应该降低标准？比如，一条年薪50万美元的狗仍然算是一条不错的狗？

Or maybe I have it all wrong; maybe I’m deceived by that average revenue 
figure. Maybe this is a superstar economy, the top dogs are bringing in $20 
million a year, and the marginal dog is barely breaking even after deducting 
the handler’s salary and the cost of kibble. Do they pay extra to retain the 
top dogs? I guess they can’t really quit to go work for Zimbabwe; not a lot of 
dog job mobility. Every so often I get emails from students saying “I am taking 
a finance class and need a topic for my final paper, any ideas,” and I only 
rarely have any ideas, but someone should definitely do an empirical study of 
compensation structures, revenue generation and incentive schemes for South 
African customs detector dogs.

或许我完全错了；或许我被平均收入数据蒙蔽了。或许这是一个超级经济体，顶尖的缉毒犬每年能带来2000万美元的收入，而普通的缉毒犬在扣除训犬员的工资和狗粮成本后，勉强收支平衡。他们会额外支付费用来留住顶尖的缉毒犬吗？我想它们不太可能辞职去津巴布韦工作；犬类职业流动性不大。我时不时会收到学生的邮件，问“我正在上金融课，需要一篇期末论文的选题，有什么建议吗？”我很少能想到什么，但确实应该有人对南非海关缉毒犬的薪酬结构、收入来源和激励机制进行实证研究。

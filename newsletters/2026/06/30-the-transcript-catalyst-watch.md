## 1. Steady Fed?

Two weeks ago, Kevin Warsh established a new communication philosophy at the Fed. The Fed is expected to hold interest rates steady. NY Fed President John Williams: inflation is elevated due to three drivers — tariffs on imports, higher Middle East energy prices, and robust AI investment boom tech demand.

美联储预计将维持利率不变。通胀高企的原因包括：关税、中东能源价格上涨以及人工智能投资热潮带来的技术需求。

The Iran ceasefire appears fragile. Phillips 66 CEO: "There's a lot of moving parts... this MOU really is an agreement to try to reach an agreement, and it's pretty thorny."

H.B. Fuller CEO: "As we monitor 4,000 different raw material categories, almost 90% of our raw materials are higher than they were in Q1."

## 2. Memory Prices Are Surging

Micron Q3 DRAM revenue: record $31.3 billion, up 343% year over year (76% of total revenue). Prices up 60%+, volume barely grew. Memory shortage expected to last at least through 2028.

美光第三季度DRAM营收创下313亿美元纪录，同比增长343%。价格上涨超过60%，销量几乎没有增长。预计内存短缺将持续到2028年。

Apple announced price increases for iPad and Mac: "The rapid expansion of AI data centers has created an extraordinary surge in demand for memory and storage. We have shielded our customers from these increases so far, but we have now reached a point where we need to begin raising prices."

苹果公司宣布因内存组件成本上涨而提高iPad/Mac的价格：AI数据中心的快速扩张导致内存和存储需求激增，苹果表示已无法继续为消费者吸收这一成本。

New memory supply requires greenfield fab expansions constrained by fab construction lead times, worker shortages, permitting, and energy infrastructure. As recently as 2023, the memory industry was under pricing pressure from oversupply.

## 3. Freight Markets Are Tightening

After years of deep recession in freight, FedEx notes demand conditions are beginning to stabilize and increase. ISM manufacturing activity, truckload spot rates and capacity show positive signs. Trucking stocks have bounced from their bottom.

经历了多年的深度衰退后，货运市场趋于紧张。联邦快递预计需求将趋于稳定并持续增长。卡车运输类股票已从底部反弹。

The Briefing
By Martin Peers
Jun 4, 2026

Thanks for reading The Briefing, our nightly column where we break down the day's news.

**感谢阅读《The Briefing》，我们的夜间专栏，我们在这里解析当天的新闻。**

Elon Musk and Sam Altman don't often see eye to eye, as they made clear in their recent legal dispute. But when it comes to making outlandish projections about future growth, the two men seem to share a common philosophy.

**埃隆·马斯克和山姆·奥特曼并不经常意见一致，正如他们在最近的法律纠纷中所明确表示的那样。但在做出关于未来增长的夸张预测方面，这两人似乎有着共同的哲学。**

As we reported earlier on Thursday, SpaceX's lead bank on its upcoming IPO, Goldman Sachs, has told investors it expects SpaceX's revenue to hit $474 billion by 2030, from $18.7 billion last year. That's even more ambitious than OpenAI's projection—which we reported in February—that its revenue will grow to $284 billion by 2030 from $13 billion in 2025. Both companies also expect to burn massive amounts of cash in the same period, although SpaceX outdoes OpenAI on that count as well.

**正如我们周四早些时候报道的，SpaceX即将上市的主承销行高盛告诉投资者，预计SpaceX的营收将从去年的187亿美元增长至2030年的4740亿美元。这甚至比我们二月份报道的OpenAI的预测还要雄心勃勃——OpenAI预计其营收将从2025年的130亿美元增长至2030年的2840亿美元。两家公司在同一时期也预计会大量烧钱，不过SpaceX在这方面也超过了OpenAI。**

Neither of these sets of projections is particularly believable, of course. Sure, OpenAI might become a major player in digital advertising—a key part of its growth story—but it has presented little that would support the long-range forecasts it has put forward. SpaceX's projections are no better. Our story today, by my colleague Cory Weinberg, says two-thirds of the projected 2030 revenue would come from AI, which implies SpaceX thinks it will be bigger than OpenAI by then.

**当然，这两套预测都不太可信。当然，OpenAI可能会成为数字广告领域的主要参与者——这是其增长故事的关键部分——但它几乎没有提出任何能够支持其所提出的长期预测的东西。SpaceX的预测也好不到哪里去。我的同事科里·温伯格今天的报道指出，预计2030年收入的三分之二将来自人工智能，这意味着SpaceX认为届时它将比OpenAI更大。**

And yet right now, SpaceX's AI unit is nowhere. Its revenue last year of $3.2 billion was mostly from ads generated by X, the business formerly known as Twitter, which doesn't count as AI. OpenAI, for all its travails, at least has real AI revenue, amounting to $5.7 billion in the first quarter. Moreover, constant employee turnover has turned xAI upside down, and the status of its model development is unclear.

**然而现在，SpaceX的AI部门几乎不存在。去年其32亿美元的收入主要来自X（前推特）产生的广告，这不算AI。OpenAI尽管历经磨难，但至少有真实的AI营收，第一季度达到57亿美元。此外，xAI人员持续流失，其模型开发状况不明。**

We get that the credibility of these projections may not be the point. SpaceX is doing a traditional Wall Street thing here by dazzling investors with big numbers to maintain buzz around the IPO. Still, it's worth noting that Musk in 2022 said X's revenue would reach $26.4 billion by 2028 and that it would have 900 million users by 2028. X's ad revenue has actually shrunk by half since Musk took over in October 2022.

**我们理解这些预测的可信度可能不是重点。SpaceX在这里做了一件传统的华尔街事情，用大数字让投资者眼花缭乱，以维持IPO的热度。尽管如此，值得注意的是，马斯克在2022年曾表示，X的营收到2028年将达到264亿美元，届时将拥有9亿用户。事实上，自马斯克于2022年10月接手以来，X的广告收入已缩水了一半。**

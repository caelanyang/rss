Dealmaker
By Laura Mandaro
Jun 4, 2026

For most of this decade, venture capital has morphed from a game of spotting the best young startups to making a more complex range of investments—from public stocks and cryptocurrencies to multibillion-dollar bets on older startups. General Catalyst even bought a hospital system!

**在本十年的大部分时间里，风险投资已经从发现最佳年轻初创公司的游戏演变为一种更复杂的投资范围——从公开股票和加密货币到对成熟初创公司的数十亿美元押注。General Catalyst甚至收购了一家医院系统！**

But industry stalwart Benchmark had held fast to a definition of VC that was more common when the firm was founded two decades ago. As Thrive, Andreessen Horowitz and Lightspeed Venture Partners each announced multi-billion-dollar fundraises, Benchmark repeatedly raised funds from about $400 million to $500 million—until recently.

**但行业老将Benchmark一直坚守着该公司二十年前成立时更为普遍的风险投资定义。当Thrive、安德森·霍洛维茨和光速风险投资伙伴各自宣布数十亿美元的募资时，Benchmark反复募集约4至5亿美元的基金——直到最近。**

The firm is raising its first growth-stage fund, of $1.25 billion, to invest in the most promising mature startups, as well as an early-stage fund of $750 million, The Wall Street Journal first reported. It will aim for venture-style returns, typically defined as 10 times the capital invested, for the growth fund.

**该公司正在募集其首只成长期基金，规模为12.5亿美元，用于投资最有前途的成熟初创公司，同时还有一只7.5亿美元的早期基金，《华尔街日报》率先报道。成长期基金将以创投式回报为目标，通常定义为所投资本的10倍。**

It's a notable change, but it doesn't come completely out of the blue. As we reported earlier this year, Benchmark raised a $225 million special purpose vehicle to invest in Cerebras at a $23 billion valuation. And last year, a partner had broached the topic of raising larger funds so the company could invest more broadly.

**这是一个显著的变化，但并非完全出乎意料。正如我们今年早些时候报道的，Benchmark募集了一只2.25亿美元的特殊目的载体，以230亿美元的估值投资Cerebras。去年，一位合伙人曾提出募集更大规模基金以更广泛投资的议题。**

Key context: The shift reflects structural changes in how AI startups are funded. Compute requirements have made the capital needs of AI companies enormous, keeping them private longer at historically unprecedented valuations. Engineer salaries have skyrocketed, inflating costs for all startups. Other early-stage firms — Khosla, SV Angel, CRV — have already moved into growth investing.

**关键背景：这一转变反映了AI初创公司融资方式的结构性变化。算力需求使AI公司的资金需求巨大，使其在历史上前所未有的估值水平下保持私有状态的时间更长。工程师薪资急剧上涨，推高了所有初创公司的成本。其他早期机构——Khosla、SV Angel、CRV——已经进军成长期投资领域。**

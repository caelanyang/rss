Investing in AI really is not for the faint of heart. Leaving aside all the worries about risks of huge investments in data centers and the uncertainties about demand, there's also the unpredictable government moves that can upend things. Case in point: the Trump administration's warnings to Anthropic over the weekend that no foreigners, including foreign employees of Anthropic, could access its latest AI models without permission.

投资人工智能绝非易事。暂且不谈数据中心巨额投资的风险以及市场需求的不确定性，政府不可预测的举措也可能彻底颠覆现状。例如，特朗普政府上周末警告Anthropic公司，未经许可，任何外国人，包括Anthropic的外国员工，都不得访问其最新的人工智能模型。

While that warning is aimed specifically at Anthropic—now in a standoff with the government over its latest AI models—other AI firms are worried about the precedent it sets. As we scooped today, OpenAI executives wrote to staff on Saturday that it had told the administration building AI "requires the best talent from around the world."

虽然这一警告主要针对Anthropic公司——该公司目前正因其最新的人工智能模型与政府陷入僵局——但其他人工智能公司也担心这会开创一个危险的先例。正如我们今天独家报道的那样，OpenAI的高管们周六致信员工，称他们已告知政府，人工智能的发展“需要来自世界各地的顶尖人才”。

The administration undoubtedly has legitimate security concerns about the number of foreigners working on AI models in the U.S. But this is also an administration that has not been exactly friendly to immigrants. That makes it difficult to parse what exactly is driving this push to block foreigners from working on AI models. What is clear, however, is how it could set back AI development, which is OpenAI's point. If the U.S. wants to lead in AI—or any industry for that matter—it has to encourage the best foreign talent to come here. Doing anything else would be self-defeating.

毫无疑问，政府对在美国从事人工智能模型研发的外国人数量存在合理的安全担忧。但同时，本届政府对移民的态度也并不友好。这使得我们很难理解究竟是什么原因促使政府试图阻止外国人从事人工智能模型研发。然而，显而易见的是，此举可能会阻碍人工智能的发展，而这正是OpenAI的观点。如果美国想要在人工智能领域——或者任何其他行业——保持领先地位，就必须鼓励最优秀的外国人才来美发展。否则，只会适得其反。

Stock Roller Coaster

SpaceX stock performed like a rocket that ran out of fuel on Tuesday. After soaring as much as 17% from Monday's close to a high on Tuesday of $225, the stock ran out of steam and finished at $201.80, up just 4.8% on the day. SpaceX also said Tuesday it would go ahead and exercise its option to buy Cursor for $60 billion.

SpaceX的股价周二表现如同火箭燃料耗尽后突然下坠。该股周一收盘后一度飙升17%，周二最高触及225美元，但随后涨势戛然而止，最终收于201.80美元，当日涨幅仅为4.8%。SpaceX周二还宣布，将行使期权，以600亿美元收购Cursor公司。

Even at the closing price, SpaceX's stock is up 49.5% on its IPO price, which was already wildly overvalued given the state of its business. But the fallback during Tuesday's trading means SpaceX failed to close above Microsoft in market value.

即使按收盘价计算，SpaceX的股价也比其IPO发行价上涨了49.5%，而考虑到其当时的业务状况，IPO发行价本身就已经被严重高估了。但周二的交易回落意味着SpaceX的市值最终未能超过微软。

SpaceX finished the day worth $2.655 trillion, just $9 billion higher than Amazon, but well below Microsoft's $2.925 trillion. At its highest point on Tuesday, SpaceX was worth $2.96 trillion.

SpaceX当日市值收于2.655万亿美元，仅比亚马逊高出90亿美元，但远低于微软的2.925万亿美元。SpaceX市值在周二最高时曾达到2.96万亿美元。

In Other News

Robinhood said on Tuesday it's cutting 10% of its workforce, or about 290 roles, and closing a small number of open roles, in an effort to stay lean.

Robinhood 周二表示，为了保持精简运营，公司将裁减 10% 的员工，约 290 个职位，并关闭少量空缺职位。

Snap on Tuesday unveiled the latest version of its augmented reality glasses, Specs, as it tries to catch up to similar products from Meta Platforms and Google. The device, which will cost $2,195, will offer features like real-time translation, web browsing and video recording, and it will have a 4-hour battery life.

Snap公司周二发布了最新款增强现实眼镜Specs，试图追赶Meta Platforms和谷歌的同类产品。这款售价2195美元的设备将提供实时翻译、网页浏览和视频录制等功能，电池续航时间长达4小时。

The U.S. military used xAI's Grok to help plan its bombing missions against Iran earlier this year, according to a court filing from a Pentagon official.

据五角大楼一名官员提交的法庭文件显示，今年早些时候，美军曾使用 xAI 公司的 Grok 软件来帮助规划对伊朗的轰炸任务。

DeepSeek has closed its first funding round that raised more than 50 billion yuan ($7.4 billion) under an unusual deal structure, according to The Information. The funding round values the large-language model developer at more than $50 billion. Investors must put their capital into a limited partnership managed by DeepSeek CEO Liang Wenfeng, instead of DeepSeek itself, ensuring he retains absolute control. There is a five-year lockup on all investors' shares.

据The Information报道，DeepSeek已完成首轮融资，募资超过500亿元人民币（约合74亿美元），融资结构较为特殊。此轮融资对这家大语言模型开发商的估值超过500亿美元。投资者必须将资金投入由DeepSeek首席执行官梁文峰管理的有限合伙企业，而非直接投入DeepSeek公司，从而确保梁文峰拥有绝对控制权。所有投资者的股份均有五年锁定期。

** Rise in remote work a factor in young worker unemployment (https://flowingdata.com/2026/06/03/rise-in-remote-work-a-factor-in-young-worker-unemployment/)

**远程办公的兴起是导致年轻劳动者失业的一个因素 (https://flowingdata.com/2026/06/03/rise-in-remote-work-a-factor-in-young-worker-unemployment/)

------------------------------------------------------------ https://flowingdata.com/2026/06/03/rise-in-remote-work-a-factor-in-young-worker-unemployment/

Higher unemployment among young workers has been commonly attributed to generative AI. In their paper (https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w31880/w31880.pdf) from the National Bureau of Economic Research, Natalia Emanuel, Emma Harrington, and Amanda Pallais argue that the rise in remote work during Covid times is a bigger factor (https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/06/remote-work-leaves-younger-workers-sidelined/) .

人们普遍认为，年轻工人失业率上升是人工智能（AI）造成的。然而，在国家经济研究局发表的一篇论文（https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w31880/w31880.pdf）中，Natalia Emanuel、Emma Harrington 和 Amanda Pallais 指出，新冠疫情期间远程办公的兴起才是造成失业率上升的更大因素（https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/06/remote-work-leaves-younger-workers-sidelined/）。

They compared unemployment for “remotable” and “non-remotable” jobs and then took the difference between younger and older workers:

他们比较了“可远程办公”和“不可远程办公”工作的失业率，然后计算了年轻员工和年长员工之间的差异：

The aggregate increase in the unemployment rate for young college graduates can be traced to remotable occupations, where young people’s unemployment rate increased by almost 1 percentage point between 2017-19 and 2022-24. By contrast, the unemployment rate of older workers in remotable sectors marginally declined over that period. As a result, the age gap in unemployment between younger and older workers significantly increased in remotable occupations. This relative increase in young people’s unemployment coincided with the pandemic and has remained elevated since then, as have rates of remote work.

年轻大学毕业生失业率的总体上升可归因于远程办公职业，2017-2019 年至 2022-2024 年间，这类职业中年轻人的失业率上升了近 1 个百分点。相比之下，同期远程办公行业中年员工的失业率略有下降。因此，远程办公职业中年轻员工和年长员工之间的失业年龄差距显著扩大。年轻人失业率的相对上升与新冠疫情爆发时间相吻合，并且自疫情爆发以来一直居高不下，远程办公的比例也同样居高不下。

The researchers further argue that AI is not yet a main factor for the shift in unemployment. In the chart above, note the sudden rise of the black line for remotable occupations in 2020, a slight taper afterwards, and then a continued rise.

研究人员进一步指出，人工智能目前还不是失业率变化的主要因素。在上图中，请注意2020年远程办公岗位的黑线突然上升，之后略有回落，然后又持续上升。

Charts in the paper do need explaining and could use a layer of annotation, but the conclusions seem to make sense.

论文中的图表确实需要解释，并且需要添加注释，但结论似乎是合理的。

Erin Davis calculated the average age of people with a given name to find the oldest name in the United States (https://erdavis.com/2026/05/29/whats-the-oldest-name-in-the-u-s/) :

艾琳·戴维斯计算了拥有特定名字的人的平均年龄，从而找到了美国最古老的名字（https://erdavis.com/2026/05/29/whats-the-oldest-name-in-the-u-s/）：

In short, the U.S. government produces estimates of the share of people born in year X who will still be alive in year Y. It also produces data on how many babies with a given name are born in each year.

简而言之，美国政府会估算出X年出生的人中，有多少人会在Y年仍然在世。它还会提供每年拥有特定名字的婴儿出生数量等数据。

By combining these two datasets, we can estimate how many babies with a specific name born in year X are still alive in 2025. Then, we can use those numbers to find a weighted average age for that name. (One big flaw this doesn’t account for immigration, but I haven’t found a way around that)

通过合并这两个数据集，我们可以估算出在X年出生的、拥有特定名字的婴儿在2025年仍然存活的数量。然后，我们可以利用这些数据计算出该名字的加权平均年龄。（这种方法的一个重大缺陷是没有考虑移民因素，但我还没有找到解决办法。）

美国选民历来倾向于支持两大政党，但这种情况在一些州正在发生变化。USAFacts 通过选民登记数据展示了这种变化（https://usafacts.org/articles/more-voters-are-registering-outside-the-two-party-system/）。

Colorado shows the first pattern most clearly. In 2016, the two major parties were nearly tied at around 30% of registered voters each, with unaffiliated voters already slightly ahead at 38%. By early 2026, more than half of Colorado’s registered voters belonged to neither major party.

科罗拉多州最明显地体现了第一种模式。2016年，两大政党的支持率几乎持平，各自占注册选民的30%左右，而无党派选民的支持率已经略高，达到38%。到2026年初，科罗拉多州超过一半的注册选民将不属于任何主要政党。

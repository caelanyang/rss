# Sierra Space, Axiom Plot IPOs as SpaceX Feeds Frenzy

**日期：** 2026-06-12

It's finally here! SpaceX Thursday officially priced its IPO at $135 per share, valuing the company at around $1.75 trillion, capping a frenetic six month stretch that defied many of the norms of initial public offerings.

终于来了！SpaceX周四正式公布了其IPO定价，每股135美元，公司估值约为1.75万亿美元，结束了长达六个月的疯狂上市进程，这段时间打破了许多IPO的常规。

Sierra Space, for one, has been taking steps towards an IPO that could happen as soon as this year. An offering would represent a relatively fast exit for the Colorado-based satellite developer, which spun out of aerospace giant Sierra Nevada Corp. in 2021 and was last valued at $8 billion.

例如，Sierra Space一直在为上市做准备，最早可能在今年实现。对于这家总部位于科罗拉多州的卫星开发商而言，上市将意味着相对快速的退出。该公司于2021年从航空航天巨头Sierra Nevada Corp.分拆出来，此前的估值为80亿美元。

Los Angeles-based space station developer Vast Space has discussed a listing that could happen later this year or early next. Vast, founded in 2021, raised $500 million in March to develop stations for orbit, the moon and Mars.

总部位于洛杉矶的空间站开发商Vast Space已讨论上市事宜，预计将于今年晚些时候或明年初完成。Vast成立于2021年，今年3月筹集了5亿美元资金，用于开发轨道、月球和火星空间站。

Texas-based Axiom Space, which is developing what it hopes will be the world's first commercial space station, is also aiming to IPO within the next year. The company was valued at $2.5 billion last year and counts backers such as the Qatar Investment Authority and Donald Trump, Jr.'s 1789 Capital.

总部位于德克萨斯州的Axiom Space公司正在开发其希望成为世界首个商业空间站的项目，并计划在未来一年内上市。该公司去年的估值为25亿美元，其支持者包括卡塔尔投资局和小唐纳德·特朗普旗下的1789资本。

The SpaceX IPO, which raised a record $75 billion, opens the window for other private startups that want to sell to public investors. SpaceX's IPO sets a comparable revenue multiple for other IPO contenders—a lofty one, given its revenue last year was under $19 billion. Investment banks can price future offerings on this multiple, though none are likely to fetch the premium an Elon Musk company gets. If SpaceX's stock rallies, investors could look for other space shares to buy.

SpaceX 的 IPO 筹集了创纪录的 750 亿美元，这为其他希望向公众投资者出售股票的私营初创公司打开了一扇窗。SpaceX 的 IPO 为其他 IPO 竞争者设定了一个可比的收入倍数——考虑到其去年的收入不足 190 亿美元，这个倍数相当高。投资银行可以以此倍数为未来的 IPO 定价，但不太可能获得像埃隆·马斯克旗下公司那样的溢价。如果 SpaceX 的股价上涨，投资者可能会寻找其他值得买入的太空股。

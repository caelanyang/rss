Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike are the two largest pure-play cybersecurity companies in the world, and they reported a day apart this week with almost the same script: strong results, higher guidance, and a stock that fell anyway.

Palo Alto Networks 和 CrowdStrike 是全球最大的两家纯粹网络安全公司，本周它们相隔一天发布了财报，内容几乎如出一辙：业绩强劲，业绩预期上调，但股价却下跌了。

Both stocks had rallied more than 50% into earnings as investors embraced the same thesis: AI is becoming a tailwind for cybersecurity.

两只股票在财报发布前均上涨超过 50%，因为投资者认同了同一个观点：人工智能正在成为网络安全的利好因素。

The trigger was Anthropic's Mythos, the model judged too dangerous to release widely, which sent enterprises scrambling to reassess their defenses.

导火索是 Anthropic 公司的 Mythos 模型，该模型被认为过于危险，不宜广泛发布，这导致各企业争相重新评估其防御措施。

The numbers suggest the AI security thesis is real. The problem is that the investors already knew it.

数据表明人工智能安全隐患的说法不无道理。问题在于，投资者们早已知晓这一点。

**1. Palo Alto: Buying the AI Security Era**

Palo Alto delivered a strong quarter, but the headline numbers were messy.

帕洛阿尔托本季度业绩强劲，但总体数据却很混乱。

Revenue grew 31% Y/Y (or 14% organically) to $3.0 billion ($60 million beat), and non-GAAP EPS landed at $0.85 ($0.05 beat).

营收同比增长 31%（或有机增长 14%），达到 30 亿美元（超出预期 6000 万美元），非 GAAP 每股收益为 0.85 美元（超出预期 0.05 美元）。

The company also reported an unusual $183 million operating loss, but that was mostly deal-related noise from the recent $25 billion CyberArk acquisition and the Chronosphere deal. New acquisitions added roughly $280 million of intangible amortization and $198 million of deal costs this quarter alone.

该公司还报告了1.83亿美元的异常运营亏损，但这主要是由于近期以250亿美元收购CyberArk和Chronosphere的交易带来的交易波动所致。仅本季度，新收购就增加了约2.8亿美元的无形资产摊销和1.98亿美元的交易成本。

Cash flow told a cleaner story. Operating cash flow rose 39% Y/Y to $871 million, while adjusted free cash flow hit a record $910 million, up 57% Y/Y. The trailing-12-month free cash flow margin reached 38.5%, up 4.3 percentage points Y/Y.

现金流状况更加明朗。经营现金流同比增长39%至8.71亿美元，调整后自由现金流创下9.1亿美元的历史新高，同比增长57%。过去12个月的自由现金流利润率达到38.5%，同比增长4.3个百分点。

Management leaned into the early-2026 SaaSpocalypse selloff, spending $1 billion on 6.8 million shares at an average of ~$148 — nearly half today's price.

管理层押注 2026 年初的 SaaS 末日抛售潮，斥资 10 亿美元以平均每股约 148 美元的价格购入 680 万股股票——几乎是今天价格的一半。

Strip out the acquisition noise, and the organic numbers look healthy:

剔除收购带来的干扰因素，自然增长数据看起来相当健康：

Next-Gen Security (NGS) ARR: $8.1 billion, up 28% organically.

下一代安全（NGS）年度经常性收入：81 亿美元，有机增长 28%。

Remaining performance obligation: $18.4 billion, up 22% organically.

剩余履约义务：184亿美元，同比增长22%。

CEO Nikesh Arora framed AI as a forcing function. Frontier models are making companies rethink their defenses, especially as AI agents gain access to credentials, data, and internal systems.

首席执行官尼克什·阿罗拉将人工智能定义为一种强制力。前沿模型正促使企业重新思考其防御策略，尤其是在人工智能代理获得凭证、数据和内部系统访问权限的情况下。

The demand signal is already showing up. Roughly 1,000 companies reached out over two months to reassess their cyber posture, and the new Unit 42 Frontier AI Defense offering generated 800+ customer meetings in six weeks.

需求信号已经显现。两个月内，约有1000家公司联系我们，重新评估其网络安全状况；而Unit 42新推出的前沿人工智能防御方案，在六周内就促成了800多场客户会议。

Palo Alto is also turning the threat into a tool. With early access to frontier models, the company says it can compress a year's worth of penetration testing into under three weeks.

帕洛阿尔托公司也在将威胁转化为工具。该公司表示，通过提前获取前沿模型，他们可以将一年的渗透测试压缩到不到三周的时间内完成。

Palo Alto's pitch is to replace a dozen point products with one platform, trading upfront discounts for stickier, multi-year commitments.

Palo Alto 的方案是用一个平台取代十几个单独的产品，以更牢固的多年期承诺来换取前期折扣。

The strategy is working: 110 net new platformizations this quarter, lifting the total to ~2,280. 120% net retention with single-digit churn.

该策略正在奏效：本季度新增 110 个平台，使总数达到约 2,280 个。净留存率达 120%，流失率仅为个位数。

Firewall bookings rose across appliances (+40% Y/Y, the strongest hardware quarter in a decade) and software (+25%).

防火墙设备预订量（同比增长 40%，是十年来硬件方面最强劲的季度）和软件预订量（同比增长 25%）均有所增长。

The acquisitions are the clearest sign of where Palo Alto thinks cybersecurity is going:

这些收购最清晰地表明了帕洛阿尔托对网络安全未来发展方向的看法：

Identity: CyberArk, now rebranded Idira, gives Palo Alto a stronger identity layer for humans, machines, and AI agents.

身份：CyberArk（现已更名为 Idira）为 Palo Alto Networks 提供更强大的身份层，适用于人类、机器和 AI 代理。

Cortex XSIAM crossed $600 million ARR, up roughly 100% Y/Y, with more than 740 customers.

Cortex XSIAM 的 ARR 突破 6 亿美元，同比增长约 100%，拥有超过 740 位客户。

Prisma AIRS topped 300 customers, tripling sequentially, with a $20 million-plus enterprise deal already signed.

Prisma AIRS 的客户数量突破 300 家，环比增长三倍，并已签署了一份价值超过 2000 万美元的企业合同。

FY26 revenue guidance increased to $11.42 billion. Management still expects a 40% free cash flow margin by FY28 and $20 billion in NGS ARR by FY30.

2026财年营收预期上调至114.2亿美元。管理层仍预计2028财年自由现金流利润率将达到40%，2030财年NGS年度经常性收入将达到200亿美元。

Takeaway: Palo Alto's AI security pitch is credible because the organic engine is still strong. The risk is valuation: after a nearly 50% rally this year and a stock near 62x forward EBITDA, investors are already pricing in a clean integration.

要点：Palo Alto Networks 的 AI 安全方案之所以可信，是因为其自身发展依然强劲。风险在于估值：今年股价已上涨近 50%，目前市盈率接近 62 倍预期 EBITDA，投资者已将顺利整合的预期计入股价。

**2. CrowdStrike: The Mythos Moment**

CrowdStrike delivered the cleanest validation of the AI thesis yet: Q1-record net new ARR and higher guidance on every line.

CrowdStrike 用实际行动最清晰地验证了人工智能理论：第一季度新增净 ARR 创历史新高，且所有业绩指引均上调。

After surging over 60% in the month leading up to the print, the bar was set for a flawless report, and a merely excellent one wasn't enough. The stock fell more than 10%.

在财报发布前一个月股价飙升超过60%之后，市场对财报的期望值很高，仅仅一份优秀的财报并不足以令人满意。结果，股价下跌超过10%。

Revenue grew 26% Y/Y to $1.4 billion ($30 million beat), the fourth straight quarter of acceleration, and non-GAAP EPS landed at $1.10 ($0.03 beat).

营收同比增长 26% 至 14 亿美元（超出预期 3000 万美元），连续第四个季度实现加速增长，非 GAAP 每股收益为 1.10 美元（超出预期 0.03 美元）。

Free cash flow hit a record $468 million, a 34% margin and an all-time high, lifting CrowdStrike's Rule of 40 score to 59. Stock-based compensation remains a significant factor, accounting for 21% of revenue.

自由现金流达到创纪录的 4.68 亿美元，利润率达到 34%，创历史新高，使 CrowdStrike 的 Rule of 40 得分提升至 59 分。股票期权激励仍然是一个重要因素，占收入的 21%。

Ending ARR rose 24% Y/Y to $5.51 billion, well past the halfway mark toward the $10 billion target.

期末年度经常性收入同比增长 24% 至 55.1 亿美元，远远超过 100 亿美元目标的一半。

Net new ARR hit a Q1-record $256 million (+32% Y/Y). Q1 is the weakest quarter seasonally, making the numbers all the more impressive.

第一季度新增年度经常性收入净额达到创纪录的2.56亿美元（同比增长32%）。第一季度通常是淡季，因此这一数字更显亮眼。

Post-Mythos, executive-level inquiries surged, and the company stood up Project QuiltWorks, a coalition that helps large customers find and fix vulnerabilities in real time.

Mythos 事件发生后，高管层面的问询激增，公司成立了 QuiltWorks 项目，这是一个帮助大型客户实时发现和修复漏洞的联盟。

With Falcon Flex, customers commit a security budget upfront and then spend it across modules over time. The company added 300+ Flex accounts, and Flex ARR nearly doubled to $1.9 billion.

借助 Falcon Flex，客户可以预先设定安全预算，然后随着时间的推移，将预算分配到各个模块中。该公司新增了 300 多个 Flex 账户，Flex 的年度经常性收入 (ARR) 几乎翻了一番，达到 19 亿美元。

AIDR (AI Detection and Response): Ending ARR grew +250% sequentially, with a Q2 pipeline above $50 million. Kurtz called the ramp the fastest of his career.

AIDR（人工智能检测与响应）：期末年度经常性收入环比增长250%，第二季度潜在销售额超过5000万美元。库尔茨称这是他职业生涯中增长最快的一次。

FY27 ARR is now expected to grow +25% Y/Y. Net new ARR guidance rose by more than $50 million.

预计2027财年年度经常性收入（ARR）将同比增长25%。新增ARR净额预期上调超过5000万美元。

Takeaway: AI is turning cybersecurity from a defensive budget line into a strategic priority. Palo Alto is buying its way deeper into identity and agent security, while CrowdStrike is expanding Falcon into AI-native infrastructure. The thesis is working. The stocks are the hard part.

要点：人工智能正在将网络安全从防御性预算转变为战略重点。Palo Alto Networks 正在加大对身份和代理安全的投入，而 CrowdStrike 则正在将 Falcon 扩展到人工智能原生基础设施。这一理论正在奏效。难点在于如何把握股票。

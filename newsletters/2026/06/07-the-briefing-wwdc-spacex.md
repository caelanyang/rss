Greetings! This promises to be an action-packed week. On Monday, Apple kicks off its annual Worldwide Developers Conference, or WWDC for short, where the tech giant is expected to unveil a new operating system for its mobile devices, complete with more advanced AI features. And then later in the week, SpaceX is scheduled to go public.

大家好！本周注定精彩纷呈。周一，苹果公司将举办一年一度的全球开发者大会（WWDC），预计这家科技巨头将在会上发布一款全新的移动设备操作系统，其中包含更先进的人工智能功能。此外，SpaceX 也计划在本周晚些时候上市。

Rather than add to the acres of text and video already expended on the Elon Musk event, let's discuss WWDC. It also is the subject of massive news coverage, of course, but WWDC has the benefit of being rooted in the here and now, unlike the far-in-the-future promises of SpaceX. What's important about WWDC is that the AI features to be introduced could -- depending on consumer reaction -- have far-reaching implications for how we use apps.

与其继续在已经铺天盖地的埃隆·马斯克活动报道中添油加醋，不如我们来聊聊WWDC。当然，WWDC也获得了大量的媒体关注，但它的优势在于它立足于当下，不像SpaceX那样只着眼于遥远的未来。WWDC的意义在于，即将推出的AI功能——取决于消费者的反应——可能会对我们使用应用程序的方式产生深远的影响。

We're expecting Apple executives to show off a newly overhauled Siri on the iPhone that will be able to take multistep actions, such as booking a lunch with your mother and then scheduling it in your calendar. Yes, we know, Apple unveiled something similar in 2024 and then ran into problems implementing it. But this time, it's different! Or at least we hope so. Since Apple teamed up with Google, its AI offerings should work this time -- fingers crossed. Put aside for a moment the most important question of whether consumers want this kind of feature. Let's assume they start using it. According to Francisco Jeronimo, a vice president of devices at research firm IDC, this more intelligent Siri means consumers won't need to open individual apps to do things as much. Instead, Siri can handle everything. We will still need the apps on the phones, mind you, as Siri has to communicate with the apps in the background. But any apps reliant on people opening them regularly for, say, advertising sales, could face a problem over time.

我们预计苹果高管将在 iPhone 上展示全新升级的 Siri，它将能够执行多步骤操作，例如预订与母亲的午餐并将其添加到日历中。没错，我们知道，苹果曾在 2024 年发布过类似的功能，但后来在实施过程中遇到了一些问题。不过这次情况有所不同！至少我们希望如此。由于苹果与谷歌合作，其人工智能产品这次应该能够成功——但愿如此。暂且抛开消费者是否需要这项功能这个最重要的问题，假设他们开始使用它。据市场研究公司 IDC 的设备副总裁 Francisco Jeronimo 称，更智能的 Siri 意味着消费者无需像以前那样打开单个应用程序来完成各种操作。Siri 可以处理所有事情。当然，我们仍然需要在手机上安装应用程序，因为 Siri 需要在后台与这些应用程序通信。但是，任何依赖用户定期打开的应用程序，例如用于广告销售的应用程序，随着时间的推移都可能面临问题。

It's also possible Apple starts charging for these features at some point, maybe splitting the revenue it gets with the apps and other business partners, such as Google. In other words, Apple, despite having spent a fraction of what other tech companies are laying out on AI development, could build a decent revenue stream from AI.

苹果未来也可能开始对这些功能收费，或许会与应用和其他商业伙伴（例如谷歌）分成收入。换句话说，尽管苹果在人工智能研发上的投入远低于其他科技公司，但它仍有可能从人工智能领域获得可观的收入。

This is all in the future, of course. We'll have to see how it works once the software is available, presumably in the fall when Apple's new mobile software typically debuts. But if you're wondering why Apple stock has rallied roughly 25% since the end of March -- and is now up 13% on the year -- this might give you a clue.

当然，这一切都还只是未来。我们得等到软件正式发布后才能知道效果如何，预计会在秋季，也就是苹果新移动软件通常发布的时候。但如果你想知道为什么苹果股价自3月底以来上涨了约25%，今年迄今为止也上涨了13%，这或许能给你一些线索。

SpaceX's Big Day

SpaceX's advisers will be hoping Friday's market upheavals don't recur this week, as the company heads toward a pricing of its offering. The Nasdaq index fell 4%, while the S&P 500 was off 2.6%. That's quite a sell-off, which Bloomberg attributed to rising worries that interest rates will hike. Rising interest rates would be problematic for tech, given the ravenous appetite AI developers have for new funding. We're due for a market correction, however, given all the worries about rising oil prices and the Iran war. All this means this is a week to be paying attention.

SpaceX的顾问们希望上周五的市场动荡不会在本周重演，因为该公司正准备进行IPO定价。纳斯达克指数下跌4%，标普500指数下跌2.6%。这是一次相当大的抛售，彭博社将其归因于市场对利率上升的担忧加剧。考虑到人工智能开发者对新资金的巨大需求，利率上升对科技行业来说将是一个棘手的问题。然而，鉴于油价上涨和伊朗战争的种种担忧，市场回调在所难免。所有这些都意味着本周值得密切关注。

In Other News

Google has agreed to pay $920 million a month to purchase compute capacity from SpaceX, SpaceX said in a filing. The deal will run from October 2026 through June 2029, and Google will be able to access approximately 110,000 Nvidia GPUs, CPUs and related components, according to the filing.

SpaceX在一份文件中表示，谷歌已同意每月支付9.2亿美元从SpaceX购买计算能力。该协议将从2026年10月持续到2029年6月，根据文件显示，谷歌将能够使用约11万个英伟达GPU、CPU及相关组件。

President Donald Trump told reporters on Friday that he plans to meet with AI companies in the near future to discuss financial "partnerships," though he did not share further details on what the deals would look like. The remarks come after a Thursday report from NOTUS that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman had spoken with senior Trump officials about the government taking a stake in his company.

唐纳德·特朗普总统周五告诉记者，他计划在近期与人工智能公司会面，讨论财务"合作"事宜，但他没有透露更多交易细节。此前，NOTUS周四报道称，OpenAI首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼曾与特朗普政府高级官员讨论过政府入股该公司的可能性。

Tesla is delaying a planned public demo for its long-awaited next-generation Roadster to August or later, four people with knowledge of the program said, marking the latest setback for the company's first vehicle debut since its Cybercab came out nearly two years ago. CEO Musk had said in October 2025 that Tesla was aiming to unveil the new Roadster on April 1.

据四位知情人士透露，特斯拉将推迟原定于8月或之后举行的备受期待的下一代Roadster跑车的公开演示，这是该公司自近两年前推出Cybercab以来，首次发布新车计划遭遇的又一次挫折。首席执行官马斯克曾在2025年10月表示，特斯拉的目标是在4月1日发布新款Roadster跑车。

LinkedIn co-founder and investor Reid Hoffman will leave his seat on Microsoft's board of directors at the end of this year, Microsoft said in a filing Friday. Hoffman has served as a Microsoft director since 2017, when the company acquired LinkedIn.

微软周五提交的文件显示，LinkedIn联合创始人兼投资人里德·霍夫曼将于今年年底卸任微软董事会成员一职。霍夫曼自2017年微软收购LinkedIn以来一直担任微软董事。

Big banks including JPMorgan, Bank of America, and Citi are planning to participate in a new settlement network to be launched next year, which will allow banks to move money on the blockchain between each other.

包括摩根大通、美国银行和花旗银行在内的多家大型银行计划参与明年即将推出的新结算网络，该网络将允许银行在区块链上相互转移资金。

Do the folks at crypto holding firm Strategy know the old adage that one should buy low and sell high? Strategy revealed today a plan to sell some of its vast stock of bitcoin, even as the cryptocurrency is hovering around its lowest point in two years. The current price of around $60,000 is 20% below the average price Strategy has paid for its pile of bitcoin, now worth about $51 billion in total. Strategy has accumulated its crypto since pivoting in 2020 from the boring world of enterprise software. Along the way, the man behind this approach, Strategy's executive chair, Michael Saylor, made it clear that Strategy was not going to sell bitcoin — here's a memorable X post from 2025, where Saylor urged people: "Sell a kidney if you must, but keep the Bitcoin." But Saylor appears to have rediscovered his appreciation for kidneys.

加密货币持有公司Strategy的人们是否知道"低买高卖"这句古老格言？Strategy今天披露了出售部分大量比特币的计划，而此时该加密货币徘徊在近两年的最低点附近。当前约6万美元的价格比Strategy持有比特币的平均成本低20%，其比特币总价值约为510亿美元。Strategy自2020年从企业软件的无聊世界转型以来积累了加密货币。这一策略背后的人——Strategy执行主席迈克尔·塞勒（Michael Saylor）曾明确表示不会出售比特币——他在2025年的一条令人难忘的X帖子中敦促人们："必要时卖肾，但保留比特币。"但塞勒似乎重新发现了对肾脏的欣赏。

Strategy's stock price has fallen 76% since September, even worse than bitcoin's 48% price drop over the same period. The company is now carrying an unrealized loss of about $13 billion on its bitcoin.

Strategy的股价自9月以来已下跌76%，甚至比比特币同期48%的跌幅还要糟糕。该公司目前持有约130亿美元的比特币未实现亏损。

Comcast's Change of Heart

Comcast on Monday said it would split into two companies, reversing two big acquisitions it has done in the past 15 years and once more becoming a pure-play cable firm. NBCUniversal and European TV firm Sky will form a separate company.

康卡斯特改变方向

康卡斯特周一表示，将分拆为两家公司，撤销过去15年的两项重大收购，再次成为纯有线电视公司。NBCUniversal和欧洲电视公司Sky将组成一家独立公司。

To gauge how this breakup will benefit shareholders, consider that if the two separate companies trade in line with their peers (Charter and Walt Disney, respectively) on a 2025 sales multiple, their combined enterprise value would be $266 billion. Before today, Comcast had an enterprise value of $168 billion. The entertainment arm was essentially valued at zero, which is nuts.

要衡量这次分拆将如何使股东受益，可以考虑：如果两家独立公司按照各自同行（分别是Charter和华特迪士尼）的2025年销售倍数交易，其合并企业价值将达2660亿美元。在此之前，康卡斯特的企业价值为1680亿美元。娱乐业务实际上被估值为零，这实在荒谬。

Roberts is stepping down from an executive role at Comcast. While he ruled out the idea that the breakup was a "step towards potential strategic transactions," it seems almost certain that is what will happen — Charter could expand its footprint by buying Comcast, while anyone from Netflix to Disney could snap up NBCU.

罗伯茨（Roberts）将从康卡斯特的执行职位退休。尽管他排除了分拆是"迈向潜在战略交易的一步"这一说法，但这几乎可以肯定将会发生——Charter可以通过收购康卡斯特扩大版图，而从Netflix到迪士尼都可能抢购NBCUniversal。

In Other News

• South Korea's government announced an 1,350 trillion won ($880 billion) investment plan in semiconductors, robotics and AI over the next decade, in response to the surging demand for memory chips and the AI infrastructure boom.

• 韩国政府宣布未来十年在半导体、机器人和AI领域投入1350万亿韩元（8800亿美元），以应对内存芯片需求激增和AI基础设施繁荣。

• Rocket Lab is acquiring satellite company Iridium Communications for $8 billion in cash and stock, a deal that will make it easier for the space launch company to compete with SpaceX and its Starlink satellite network.

• Rocket Lab以80亿美元现金和股票收购卫星公司铱星通信（Iridium Communications），这笔交易将使这家太空发射公司更容易与SpaceX及其Starlink卫星网络竞争。

• China's ChangXin Memory Technologies has signed a long-term deal to supply Tencent Holdings with more than 20 billion yuan ($2.94 billion) of server DRAM chips.

• 中国长鑫存储（ChangXin Memory Technologies）已签署长期协议，向腾讯控股供应逾200亿元人民币（29.4亿美元）的服务器DRAM芯片。

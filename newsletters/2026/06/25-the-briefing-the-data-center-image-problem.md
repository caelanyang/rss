Thanks for reading The Briefing, our nightly column where we break down the day's news.

感谢您阅读我们的晚间专栏“简报”，我们会在专栏中为您解读当天的新闻。

A couple days ago, John Carmack, a videogame pioneer and former virtual reality executive at Meta Platforms, wrote on X that he had begun seeing anti-data center yard signs popping up in his neighborhood. "I am entertaining the idea of paying for a billboard with something like 'Data centers are awesome, Texas should lead!'" wrote Carmack, who has co-founded an AI startup, Keen Technologies. In a follow-up post, Carmack compared the brewing public hostility toward AI to the domestic backlash against nuclear power, which snowballed after the Three Mile Island accident in 1979. "I consider it a tragedy that anti-nuclear efforts largely strangled nuclear power in the US based on vibes, and I don't want to see that happen to AI," he wrote.

几天前，电子游戏先驱、前Meta Platforms虚拟现实高管约翰·卡马克(John Carmack)在X网站上发文称，他开始在自家附近看到反对数据中心的标语牌。“我正在考虑花钱买个广告牌，上面写着‘数据中心很棒，德克萨斯州应该引领潮流！’”卡马克写道。他也是人工智能初创公司Keen Technologies的联合创始人。在随后的文章中，卡马克将公众对人工智能日益增长的敌意与1979年三里岛核事故后美国国内对核能的强烈抵制进行了比较。“我认为，美国反核运动很大程度上是基于人为因素扼杀了核能发展，这是一个悲剧。我不希望人工智能也遭遇同样的命运。”他写道。

It's going to take a lot more than billboards to fix AI's brand problems with the American people. In The Information today, my colleague Shane Burke showed just how deeply communities across the country are opposed to data centers with a map and story that documents the more than 300 cities, towns, counties and states that have enacted temporary or permanent data center bans since 2023. A whopping 275-plus of those measures have passed this year alone.

仅仅靠广告牌是无法解决人工智能在美国民众心目中的形象问题。今天，我的同事肖恩·伯克在《信息报》上发表了一篇文章，通过地图和报道，详细展示了全美各地社区对数据中心的强烈反对。文章记录了自2023年以来，已有超过300个城市、城镇、县和州颁布了临时或永久性的数据中心禁令。仅今年一年，就有超过275项禁令获得通过。

It's too soon to tell how effective these measures will be in slowing down AI's progress. The vast majority of them are temporary, for instance. Still, AI's messaging problem is a real one. At times, it feels like Silicon Valley investors and entrepreneurs are more interested in responding to the backlash with snarky, unserious posts on X or by pouring funds into dark-money organizations aimed at bringing down perceived political enemies of AI.

现在判断这些措施在减缓人工智能发展速度方面究竟能起到多大作用还为时尚早。例如，其中绝大多数措施都是暂时的。然而，人工智能的舆论传播问题确实存在。有时，硅谷的投资者和企业家似乎更热衷于在社交媒体上发表尖刻、不严肃的帖子来回应外界的强烈反对，或者将资金投入到旨在打压他们眼中人工智能政治敌人的“黑钱”组织中。

It's not like there are no positive stories for them to tell about the benefits of AI data centers. This week, our AI Infrastructure columnist, Ann Davis Vaughan, pointed out that hundreds of teachers in Richland Parish, La., are set to receive $50,000 bonuses this year thanks to swelling tax receipts from a Meta Platforms AI facility in the area.

并非他们就没有什么关于人工智能数据中心益处的正面案例可讲。本周，我们的人工智能基础设施专栏作家安·戴维斯·沃恩指出，由于Meta Platforms公司在路易斯安那州里奇兰教区的人工智能设施带来了可观的税收收入，该教区数百名教师今年将获得5万美元的奖金。

Sometimes I wonder—putting my conspiracy hat on—if leaders in the AI ecosystem have decided they don't really need to convince the public why AI and data centers are so important for Americans to embrace. Perhaps they don't think persuasion will work. Or maybe they believe that before long they'll be able to bypass the public blowback against data centers by putting them in space, away from pesky community activists. Both attitudes are wrongheaded. If they want Americans to accept AI, they need to talk to them, not just to their reply guys on X.

有时我会想——姑且戴上阴谋论者的帽子——人工智能生态系统的领导者们是不是觉得没必要费力去说服公众，让他们明白人工智能和数据中心对美国民众的重要性？或许他们认为劝说根本行不通。又或许他们相信，只要把数据中心建在太空，远离那些烦人的社区活动人士，就能绕过公众对数据中心的强烈反对。这两种想法都是错误的。如果他们想让美国民众接受人工智能，就应该和他们对话，而不仅仅是和那些在X网站上负责回复的人沟通。

A Videogame Mega Event

Take-Two Interactive Software said today that Grand Theft Auto VI, the next installment of its hugely popular game franchise, will be available for pre-ordering tomorrow at midnight local time in two versions that cost $80 and $100 each. The game itself will be available to play on Nov. 19, though Take-Two wisely will allow people to begin downloading it onto their game consoles on Nov. 12 to avoid crashing the internet.

Take-Two Interactive Software 今日宣布，旗下热门游戏系列《侠盗猎车手VI》将于明日当地时间午夜开启预购，提供售价分别为 80 美元和 100 美元的两个版本。游戏将于 11 月 19 日正式发售，但 Take-Two 明智地允许玩家从 11 月 12 日开始下载到游戏主机上，以避免网络拥堵。

This is not going to be a normal game launch. Take-Two said in May it had sold over 230 million copies of Grand Theft Auto V in the 13 years since that game came out. Other videogame publishers have scrambled to avoid releasing competing games around the time the new version of the game comes out in November.

这不会是一次普通的游戏发售。Take-Two在五月份表示，自《侠盗猎车手5》发售以来的13年间，该游戏的销量已超过2.3亿份。其他游戏发行商纷纷争相避开新版本于11月发售的档期，以免推出与之竞争的游戏。

In Other News

Qualcomm has agreed to pay around $3.9 billion in stock for Modular, a software startup that allows developers to write software that can run on different chips without having to rewrite the code for each chip.

高通公司已同意以约 39 亿美元的股票收购 Modular，这是一家软件初创公司，允许开发人员编写可以在不同芯片上运行的软件，而无需为每个芯片重新编写代码。

OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled a new chip the two companies have developed for inference, dubbed Jalapeño. It's a big step forward in OpenAI's effort to reduce its reliance on Nvidia chips and control its own hardware.

OpenAI 和博通公司联合发布了一款名为 Jalapeño 的全新推理芯片。这是 OpenAI 在减少对英伟达芯片的依赖、实现硬件自主化方面迈出的重要一步。

Ornn, a startup that tracks the cost of AI tokens and computing power, raised $33 million in a funding round led by Andreessen Horowitz. The money will be used to build a marketplace for trading compute.

追踪人工智能代币和计算能力成本的初创公司Ornn在由Andreessen Horowitz领投的一轮融资中筹集了3300万美元。这笔资金将用于构建一个计算能力交易市场。

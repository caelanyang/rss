1. The Economy Remains Resilient
If one word has come to define the economy of the 2020s it would have to be “resilient.” That word has appeared so many times in The Transcript that it sometimes feels like it has lost any meaning. To that end, we appreciated seeing Wells Fargo’s CFO at least try to find a new way to describe our resilient economy:

> 如果要用一个词来概括2020年代的经济，那非“韧性”莫属。这个词在《The Transcript》上出现的频率如此之高，以至于有时感觉它已经失去了原本的意义。因此，我们很高兴看到富国银行的首席财务官至少尝试用一种新的方式来描述我们经济的韧性：

“People probably are maybe over using this word resilient, but it’s been very stable, very resilient, very good activity levels across really all of the businesses, which is really good to see.” - Wells Fargo [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (WFC -1.68%↓ [ https://substack.com/redirect/b63a3ebb-1480-4031-8cd4-2b229d88c473?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) CFO Michael Santomassimo

> “人们可能有点过度使用‘韧性’这个词了，但实际上，各行各业的业务都非常稳定、非常有韧性，活动水平也非常好，这真的很令人欣慰。” - 富国银行 [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (WFC -1.68%↓ [ https://substack.com/redirect/b63a3ebb-1480-4031-8cd4-2b229d88c473?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) 首席财务官 Michael Santomassimo

Still, there may not be a better way to describe what we’ve experienced than resilience. Blackstone’s COO gave a succinct review of what’s happened so far in the decade and used it as a decent argument as to why the outlook is for more of the same:

> 然而，要描述我们所经历的一切，或许没有比“韧性”更贴切的词了。黑石集团的首席运营官对过去十年发生的事情进行了简明扼要的回顾，并以此为由，有力地论证了为什么未来形势可能与此类似：

“The economy has been much more resilient than people expected. And this really goes back over time. If you went back to 2020, we obviously had COVID. We subsequently, the following year had the Russia-Ukraine invasion. We had the Silicon Valley Bank shock, which was more relevant to the folks in this room than the broader economy. We had Liberation Day last year. This year, the Iran war. And each time the global economy, particularly the U.S. economy, has powered through and the year ended more in a better spot than people expected. And I think the same thing will happen here. I think there’ll be some sort of resolution and the underlying strength of the economy will stick.” - Blackstone [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (BX -1.15%↓ [ https://substack.com/redirect/0a11ecdb-b3dc-4a87-a9d4-0f04adeb084f?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) COO Jonathan Gray

> “经济的韧性远超人们的预期。这其实由来已久。回顾2020年，我们经历了新冠疫情。紧接着，第二年发生了俄乌战争。还有硅谷银行危机，这场危机对在座各位的影响远大于对整体经济的影响。去年是解放日。今年是伊朗战争。但每一次，全球经济，尤其是美国经济，都挺了过来，最终的业绩都比人们预期的要好。我认为这次也会如此。我相信最终会找到解决办法，经济的潜在优势也会得以保持。” - 黑石集团 [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (BX -1.15%↓ [ https://substack.com/redirect/0a11ecdb-b3dc-4a87-a9d4-0f04adeb084f?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) 首席运营官乔纳森·格雷

The economy feels so resilient that it almost begs the question as to whether the business cycle is still in existence. Of course, that question itself is usually an indication that the business cycle is not dead, but maybe it’s different this time.

> 经济似乎如此强劲，几乎让人不禁质疑商业周期是否依然存在。当然，这个问题本身通常就表明商业周期并未终结，但这次或许有所不同。

Historically business cycles have come on the heels of overly exuberant capital markets. Capital markets are wide open today, but it’s not clear whether we are in a period of excess bullishness. Are we euphoric or just really excited? Brian Moynihan weighed in on this last week: “I don’t want to say euphoria yet, but there’s a lot of excitement about technology.”

> 从历史上看，商业周期往往紧随资本市场过度繁荣之后。如今资本市场一片开阔，但我们是否正处于过度乐观时期尚不明朗。我们是欣喜若狂，还是仅仅兴奋不已？布莱恩·莫伊尼汉上周就此发表了看法：“我现在还不想用‘欣喜若狂’这个词，但人们对科技的热情确实很高。”

2. The Enterprise Strikes Back at AI Costs
For the last few weeks we’ve been tracking how enterprises are spending huge amounts of money on AI. Last week that narrative seemed to start to shift back towards questions about whether there is ROI to justify the expense. Cisco President Jeetu Patel captured both the narrative shift and the risks:

> 过去几周，我们一直在关注企业如何在人工智能领域投入巨资。上周，舆论风向似乎开始转变，人们开始质疑人工智能的投资回报率是否足以支撑如此巨大的支出。思科总裁吉图·帕特尔 (Jeetu Patel) 精准地捕捉到了这种舆论转变及其带来的风险：

“And now what’s happening is the cost of tokens are far higher than the actual value that these tokens are generating at scale. And so what ends up happening, and the big risk in the market is, if you don’t create an equilibrium there, then people just pull back on using tokens, and that’s actually not good for anyone.” - Cisco [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (CSCO -2.30%↓ [ https://substack.com/redirect/dfc9aa08-bde6-4a5c-a3e6-15b5c38c4d3e?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) President Jeetu Patel

> “现在的情况是，代币的成本远远高于这些代币大规模使用时所产生的实际价值。因此，最终的结果，也是市场面临的最大风险，就是如果不能建立起这种平衡，人们就会减少使用代币，而这实际上对任何人都没有好处。” - Cisco [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (CSCO -2.30%↓ [ https://substack.com/redirect/dfc9aa08-bde6-4a5c-a3e6-15b5c38c4d3e?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) 总裁 Jeetu Patel

Last week we published several quotes from businesses in different industries who echoed the sentiment of this quote and talked about ways in which they are working to limit spending on AI services. This includes paring back on deployment or utilizing non-frontier models for use cases where leading edge logic is not required.

> 上周，我们刊登了来自不同行业的几家企业的引述，他们都表达了与此类似的观点，并探讨了他们正在努力限制人工智能服务支出的方法。这些方法包括缩减部署规模，或在不需要尖端逻辑的应用场景中使用非前沿模型。

The AI labs have been the biggest beneficiaries of increasing enterprise spend, and even though they aren’t public companies yet, public markets are certainly taking a queue from Anthropic and OpenAI. Enterprise spend is the primary reason why Anthropic’s valuation has now eclipsed OpenAI’s. If there is a pullback in spending growth then it could impact capital markets more broadly.

> 人工智能实验室一直是企业支出增长的最大受益者，尽管它们尚未上市，但公开市场显然正在效仿 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的做法。企业支出是 Anthropic 估值超越 OpenAI 的主要原因。如果支出增长放缓，则可能对更广泛的资本市场产生影响。

Before getting too far ahead of ourselves though, the ROI narrative is one that has been in vogue before. It’s most likely that enterprises will use more AI even as token prices fall, leading to value creation for all parties.

> 不过，在我们进一步讨论之前，需要指出的是，投资回报率（ROI）的概念并非首次流行。即使代币价格下跌，企业也极有可能更多地使用人工智能，从而为各方创造价值。

3. Congrats to SpaceX
We certainly don’t need to be the 10,000th publication to comment on the SpaceX IPO, but it was the dominant news story last week in capital markets. Rather than giving an opinion on the prospects for the stock and the company, we’ll just share the sentiment of congratulations to those involved. No matter what you think of Elon Musk, you have to give him credit for pulling off something extraordinary. Last week gave him an opportunity to reflect on the journey so far:

> 我们当然不必成为第10000家评论SpaceX IPO的媒体，但这确实是上周资本市场最热门的新闻。我们不打算对股票和公司的前景发表意见，只想向所有参与者表示祝贺。无论你对埃隆·马斯克有何看法，都不得不承认他完成了一项非凡的壮举。上周也让他有机会回顾了迄今为止的历程：

“If people had told me this was gonna happen, I was like… ‘man, you must be smoking some really good crack’. I think this company’s gonna fail. I mean, I gave SpaceX less than a 10% chance of succeeding at all, to be clear. In fact, I told people we’re probably going to fail, but you know, we should try it — because if there aren’t any new companies that enter space, then we will never become a truly spacefaring civilization.” - SpaceX [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (SPCX 18.15%↑ [ https://substack.com/redirect/a896538a-e80d-4fd7-93df-884ddab1a157?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) CEO Elon Musk

> “如果有人告诉我这会发生，我肯定会想……‘老兄，你肯定嗑药嗑嗨了’。我觉得这家公司会失败。说实话，我甚至觉得SpaceX成功的几率不到10%。事实上，我跟别人说过我们很可能会失败，但你知道，我们应该尝试一下——因为如果没有新的公司进入太空，我们就永远无法真正成为一个太空文明。” SpaceX [ https://substack.com/redirect/c70a8adf-70db-4043-bc85-e44c24e95d02?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ] (SPCX 18.15%↑ [ https://substack.com/redirect/a896538a-e80d-4fd7-93df-884ddab1a157?j=eyJ1IjoibjFnd2UifQ.dXXwPP56PjZnW63zI6SfaVeu-tHwaEIisorCC8cl1ro ]) CEO 埃隆·马斯克

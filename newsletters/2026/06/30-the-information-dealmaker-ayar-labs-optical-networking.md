# Exclusive: Optical Networking Startup Ayar Joins Chip Funding Spree

**来源：** The Information Dealmaker  
**日期：** 2026-06-30

Call it a hot chip summer. Investors are flocking to startups making semiconductors and components to run AI more efficiently. Etched announced a $800 million funding round. Ayar Labs is in talks to raise at least $200 million at a valuation in the ~$5 billion range.

不妨称之为芯片热潮之夏。投资者正蜂拥涌向那些生产半导体和用于更高效运行人工智能的组件的初创公司。Etched 宣布完成 8 亿美元的融资。Ayar Labs 正在洽谈融资至少 2 亿美元，估值约为 50 亿美元。

Ayar Labs, an 11-year-old company founded by academic researchers, makes equipment for ultrafast connections between AI chips. The startup's previous valuation was $3.75 billion; the new round implies ~$5B. Nvidia, AMD and Intel's venture arm have backed the company.

Ayar 的产品采用高速光连接而非传统的铜线，实现人工智能芯片和内存芯片之间的高速数据传输。英伟达、AMD 和英特尔的风险投资部门都对该公司进行了投资。

Investors are chasing chip returns on the heels of two big exits: Nvidia's $20 billion licensing deal for Groq, and Cerebras' IPO at a $56 billion valuation.

在两笔重大退出交易之后，投资者正竞相追逐芯片领域的回报：英伟达以 200 亿美元收购 Groq 的授权协议，以及 Cerebras 以 560 亿美元的估值进行 IPO。

Artisan Partners, Liberty Street Funds and Ark Investment Management (Cathie Wood) have discussed investing. The funding isn't yet closed.

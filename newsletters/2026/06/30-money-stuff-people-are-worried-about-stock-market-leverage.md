Leverage

Companies issue debt and they issue equity. There are various corporate-finance-y reasons to choose one or the other or some particular mix: Debt is often cheaper and tax deductible, equity is less risky and good for motivating employees, etc.

公司发行债务，也发行股权。选择哪种或两者的特定组合，有各种公司金融方面的原因：债务通常更便宜且可抵税，股权风险较低且有利于激励员工等。

But you could also tell a sort of market segmentation story. Some people want to own debt: They have some money, they want to keep it, and they want to invest it in something that promises to repay the money with a reasonable return. Other people want to own equity: They have some money, they're willing to risk it to get a high return, and they want to invest it in something that could go to zero but also offers a potentially unlimited payoff.

但你也可以讲一个市场细分的故事。有些人想持有债务：他们有一些钱，想保留它，希望投资于承诺以合理回报偿还本金的东西。另一些人想持有股权：他们有一些钱，愿意冒险获取高回报，投资于可能归零但也提供潜在无限收益的东西。

Here's a Bloomberg News story about stock market leverage: "The surge in market leverage, stemming in part from the massive growth of levered exchange-traded products, retail margin accounts and hedge fund deposits at prime brokers, is stoking worries that it may exacerbate the next crisis. The demand to borrow money has driven an unusual mid-year spike in financing costs, which have reached the highest levels since December 2024."

以下是彭博新闻关于股市杠杆的报道："市场杠杆的激增，部分源于杠杆交易型产品、散户保证金账户以及对冲基金在主经纪商存款的大规模增长，引发了人们对其可能加剧下一次危机的担忧。借贷需求推动融资成本在年中出现异常飙升，达到2024年12月以来的最高水平。"

US margin debt rose 54% to a record $1.4 trillion in May from a year earlier, according to Finra data. Meanwhile, high-risk leveraged ETFs that produce double or triple the daily move of underlying stocks are growing rapidly, as is trading in options tied to them.

根据美国金融业监管局（Finra）数据，美国保证金债务在5月份同比增长54%，达到创纪录的1.4万亿美元。与此同时，产生标的股票日涨跌幅两倍或三倍的高风险杠杆ETF正在快速增长，与之挂钩的期权交易也同样如此。

"I'm fearful that we're building unintended leverage that isn't fully understood," said Mark Hackett, chief market strategist for Nationwide's investment management group. "You've got people with a lottery mentality using margin to buy options on levered ETFs. That's three or four layers."

"我担心我们正在积累尚未被充分理解的意外杠杆，"全国性投资管理集团首席市场策略师Mark Hackett表示。"你看到有人以彩票心态用保证金买入杠杆ETF的期权。这是三四层杠杆叠加。"

Blockchain for SPVs

How do you know if you own a share of stock? In the very olden days, you'd have a paper stock certificate, but those are just novelty items now. Now the basic answer is: A share of stock represents a claim on a company, and so a share of stock is, in essence, an entry on a list maintained by the company.

区块链与SPV

你怎么知道自己是否持有一股股票？在很久以前，你会有一张纸质股票证书，但现在那只是纪念品。现在的基本答案是：一股股票代表对公司的一项索取权，因此，一股股票本质上是公司维护的名单上的一个条目。

Big modern private companies introduce another complication. Those companies, for various reasons, often don't want people buying and selling their stock. This has given rise to new forms of indirect ownership. Most notably there is the SPV, the special purpose vehicle: You set up a legal entity (the SPV) to buy Anthropic shares directly from Anthropic, the SPV owns nothing but those Anthropic shares, and then you sell shares of the SPV to me.

现代大型私营公司引入了另一个复杂性。这些公司出于各种原因，通常不希望人们买卖其股票。这催生了新形式的间接所有权。最值得注意的是SPV（特殊目的载体）：你设立一个法律实体（SPV）直接从Anthropic购买股份，SPV除持有这些Anthropic股份外什么都不拥有，然后你把SPV的股份卖给我。

Citigroup wants to tackle the murky ownership problems that have proliferated among private tech firms as more special purpose vehicles have popped up promising to connect investors with the hottest startups. The bank this month said it is launching a new service that lets investors trade private company shares on a blockchain.

花旗集团希望解决私营科技公司中日益普遍的模糊所有权问题，因为越来越多的特殊目的载体涌现，承诺将投资者与最热门的初创公司连接起来。该银行本月表示，正在推出一项新服务，让投资者在区块链上交易私营公司股份。

BNPL and Private Credit

The basic story of private credit is that private credit managers raise institutional funds that they use to make loans that used to be made by banks.

BNPL与私人信贷

私人信贷的基本故事是：私人信贷管理人筹集机构资金，用于发放过去由银行提供的贷款。

As private credit has grown into various sorts of consumer financing, Blue Owl Capital, KKR, and Elliott Investment Management are increasingly agreeing to pre-purchase billions of dollars worth of loans before they're made, in a bet that consumer assets will outpace returns elsewhere. That's been a boon to Klarna, Affirm, and PayPal — BNPL is the credit card of private credit.

随着私人信贷扩展到各类消费者融资，蓝猫资本（Blue Owl Capital）、KKR和Elliott投资管理公司越来越多地同意预先购买价值数十亿美元的贷款，押注消费者资产将超越其他地方的回报。这对Klarna、Affirm和PayPal是一大利好——BNPL是私人信贷的信用卡。

Semiliquid Fees

One reason private equity funds charge performance fees only on realized gains is that valuing private companies, outside of a sale, is hard and conflicted. But semiliquid retail-oriented private investment vehicles charge performance fees on mark-to-market gains on assets with no market — and if you collect performance fees on mark-to-market gains, your marks might be a bit high.

半流动性费用

私募股权基金仅对已实现收益收取业绩费的一个原因是，在出售之外对私营公司进行估值既困难又存在利益冲突。但面向散户的半流动性私募投资工具对没有市场的资产的按市值计价收益收取业绩费——如果你对按市值计价的收益收取业绩费，你的估值标记可能会偏高。

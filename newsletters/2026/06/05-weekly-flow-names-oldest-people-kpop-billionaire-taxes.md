**Comparing SpaceX IPO against past offerings**

For Bloomberg, Demetrios Pogkas, Jennah Haque, and Kiel Porter show the projected scale of SpaceX's initial public offering later this month against the current top 100 listings globally. Rising bubbles are the correct metaphor here. The vertical position represents offer size, the horizontal represents time, and the size of the circles represent market capitalization after the offering. SpaceX floats far above.

**SpaceX IPO与过往发行比较：Bloomberg的可视化显示SpaceX本月晚些时候首次公开募股的预计规模远超全球当前前100名上市公司。SpaceX的泡泡飘浮在远高于所有其他公司的位置。**

**Arguing for the billionaire wealth tax**

For NYT Opinion, Emmanuel Saez and Gabriel Zucman make a case for a billionaire wealth tax in California. The billionaire class in California includes roughly 250 households, a mere 0.001 percent of the state's families. Yet its wealth now amounts to more than half of California's entire annual economic output. California wants to charge a 5% tax on the entire iceberg spread out over a five-year period.

**关于亿万富翁财富税的论证：加州的亿万富翁阶层约有250个家庭，仅占该州家庭总数的0.001%，但其财富现在相当于加州全年经济产出的一半以上。加州希望在五年内对整个"冰山"征收5%的税。**

**Ebola outbreak trajectories**

For NBC News, report on the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. When compared to past outbreaks since 2012, the trajectory of the current outbreak looks more intense, as indicated by a much steeper line.

**埃博拉疫情轨迹：关于刚果民主共和国埃博拉疫情的报道显示，与2012年以来的历次疫情相比，当前疫情轨迹看起来更为严峻，线条明显更陡峭。**

**Rise in remote work a factor in young worker unemployment**

Higher unemployment among young workers has been commonly attributed to generative AI. New research from NBER argues that the rise in remote work during Covid times is a bigger factor. They compared unemployment for "remotable" and "non-remotable" jobs: the age gap in unemployment between younger and older workers significantly increased in remotable occupations. The researchers further argue that AI is not yet a main factor for the shift.

**远程工作兴起是年轻工人失业的因素：NBER新研究认为，疫情期间远程工作的兴起是年轻工人失业的更大因素，而非生成式AI。可远程工作岗位中年轻与年长工人之间的失业差距显著扩大，这种差距与疫情同步出现，且持续至今。**

**K-Pop growth before the global phenomenon**

For the Pudding, Minji Kim and Eunice Lee wrote about the growth of K-Pop through the lens of their friendship. A detailed look at K-Pop's origins and rise through personal narrative and data visualization.

**全球现象前的K-Pop增长：通过个人叙述和数据可视化，详细回顾了K-Pop的起源和崛起。**

The normal thing to say is that SpaceX's initial public offering "is set to price June 11 — today — and trade the following day." SpaceX's bankers have been marketing its shares to investors for the past week, and this afternoon, after the market closes, they will get together, review the demand for the IPO, and "price" the IPO, that is, decide the price at which SpaceX will sell shares to investors.

通常的说法是，SpaceX的首次公开募股（IPO）“定于6月11日（即今天）定价，并于次日开始交易”。过去一周，SpaceX的银行家们一直在向投资者推销其股票。今天下午，市场收盘后，他们将召开会议，评估IPO的需求，并确定IPO的“定价”，也就是决定SpaceX向投资者出售股票的价格。

SpaceX, though, started with a price. SpaceX launched its IPO marketing by saying that it is selling 555,555,555 shares at $135 per share. The offering has attracted demand for more than four times the available shares.

然而，SpaceX 首先公布了发行价格。SpaceX 在 IPO 宣传中宣布，将以每股 135 美元的价格出售 555,555,555 股股票。此次发行吸引了超过发行量四倍以上的认购需求。

Elon Musk's lossmaking rockets-to-AI group is expected to make its market debut on Friday following what is set to be the biggest IPO on record, offering shares at $135 each, a set price that several people involved in the deal said left underwriters with less flexibility than is typical in a conventional listing.

埃隆·马斯克亏损的火箭到人工智能集团预计将于周五上市，这将是史上规模最大的IPO，每股发行价为135美元。参与该交易的几位人士表示，这一固定价格使得承销商的灵活性比传统上市要小。

"Elon just came in and said what the price was . . . there was no price discovery," said an executive at a large US hedge fund.

“埃隆直接进来宣布了价格……根本没有价格发现过程，”一家大型美国对冲基金的高管表示。

Four possibilities: Musk picked an arbitrary price that is way off-base; Musk picked a price that was precisely correct because he is a genius; Musk's reality-distortion powers made it the correct price; or actually there was some price discovery and SpaceX is going public at roughly the market-clearing price.

有四种可能性：马斯克随意定了一个离谱的价格；马斯克因为是天才，所以定了一个完全正确的价格；马斯克扭曲现实的能力使这个价格成为正确价格；或者实际上存在某种价格发现机制，SpaceX 的上市价格大致是市场出清价格。

Musk and his team were testing their $135 per share target with investors long before the official roadshow began last week. Elon Musk has a long history of raising a lot of money from investors and making a lot of money for those investors.

早在上周正式路演开始之前，马斯克和他的团队就已经开始向投资者试探每股135美元的目标价位。埃隆·马斯克一直以来都善于从投资者那里筹集大量资金，并为这些投资者创造丰厚的利润。

The Hiive private-stock marketplace showed a price of $136.18 as of June 3 (the day before the IPO roadshow launched) and $149.02 as of early this afternoon. Crypto traders in the SpaceX pre-IPO perpetual futures are expecting around $162 on Hyperliquid, about 20% above its fixed IPO price of $135.

截至6月3日（IPO路演开始前一天），Hiive私募股票交易平台显示SpaceX股票价格为136.18美元，而截至今天下午早些时候，价格已升至149.02美元。在Hyperliquid平台上，SpaceX IPO前永续期货的加密货币交易员预计价格约为162美元，比其135美元的固定IPO价格高出约20%。

HELLO DISCOVERERS!

Get off the beaten track, find the little town the guidebook missed, eat where the locals eat, steer clear of other tourists. That's the unspoken creed of many travellers and that was me, too. I'd sniff at anyone who hung out at the obviously touristy spots, thinking that my own experiences were somehow more legitimate, more 'authentic'.

你好，探索者们！

走出寻常路，找到那个旅游指南遗漏的小镇，在当地人吃饭的地方用餐，避开其他游客。这是许多旅行者不言而喻的信条，我也曾如此。我会对那些流连于明显旅游胜地的人嗤之以鼻，认为自己的体验在某种程度上更加正宗、更具"真实性"。

This essay by David Sze opened my eyes to the fact that the very notion of authenticity rests on a fantasy that cultures are pure, fixed things that tourism then comes along and spoils. Sze points out that no culture has ever sat sealed off and unchanging.

大卫·施（David Sze）的这篇文章让我开眼界：真实性的概念本身依赖于一种幻想，即文化是纯粹、固定的事物，随后旅游业将其破坏。施指出，没有任何文化曾经是封闭不变的。

"If culture is inherently dynamic, we can argue that commodification does not produce a simulacrum of the 'local', but is instead just the latest step of a never-ending evolution."

"如果文化本质上是动态的，我们可以说商品化并不产生'本土'的拟象，而只是无休止演进中的最新一步。"

Sze argues that what travellers are actually chasing is a flattering story about themselves — the search for the 'untouched' is really a search for something that was never there. Worse, it does a disservice to the people we claim to admire: "'Authenticity' is the denial of a people's privilege to their changing culture amidst changing times."

施认为，旅行者实际上追求的是一个关于自己的讨好故事——对"未被触碰之地"的追寻，实际上是在寻找一种从未存在过的东西。更糟糕的是，这对我们声称钦佩的人是一种伤害："'真实性'是对一个民族在变化时代中拥有其变化文化之特权的否定。"

TOOLS

**TINYCLUB** — An app for small communities. TinyClub lets you spin up a 'virtual club' on your phone: invite people with a link, and keep posts, chat and events in one private space instead of scattered across apps.

**TINYCLUB** — 小型社区应用。TinyClub让你在手机上创建一个"虚拟俱乐部"：通过链接邀请人们，将帖子、聊天和活动集中在一个私人空间，而非分散在各个应用中。

**OPENALTERNATIVE** — Open source alternatives. Pairs everyday SaaS tools with their open source equivalents. E.g. Notion with Affine, AirDrop with LocalSend, Google Photos with Immich. Each listing shows GitHub stars and last-commit date.

**OPENALTERNATIVE** — 开源替代品目录。将日常SaaS工具与其开源等价物配对。例如Notion对应Affine，AirDrop对应LocalSend，Google Photos对应Immich。每个条目显示GitHub星数和最后提交日期。

**TOWNSQUARE** — A presence layer for websites. Drop a single script tag into your website and visitors appear as little avatars who can wander the same space, wave and trade a few words in real time.

**TOWNSQUARE** — 网站的在场层。在网站中插入一个script标签，访客就会以小头像形式出现，可以在同一空间漫步、打招呼并实时交流几句话。

**《如何起步》** — 无陈词滥调的职业建议。记者乔迪·坎托尔（Jodi Kantor）的报道推动了#MeToo运动，她为在不确定世界中摸索方向的年轻人提供两个锚点：技艺（你擅长什么）和需求（世界需要什么）。

READS

**YOUR DECISION MAKING IS ALL WRONG** — Drawing on the idea of 'satisficing', David Epstein says we're better off deciding what 'good enough' looks like, picking the first option that meets that bar, and moving on. You save time, reduce stress, and often end up just as happy as the maximisers who exhaust themselves in the search.

**《你的决策方式全错了》** — 借鉴"满足化"理念，大卫·爱泼斯坦（David Epstein）认为，我们最好先决定什么是"足够好"，选择第一个满足标准的选项，然后继续前进。你节省时间，减少压力，最终往往与那些在搜索中耗尽精力的最大化者同样快乐。

"Social media has intensified the problem by functioning as an infinite comparison engine. When you can see a curated highlight reel of everyone else's career, relationship, home and vacation, the very concept of 'good enough' begins to feel like settling."

"社交媒体作为无限比较引擎加剧了这一问题。当你能看到别人精心策划的职业、关系、家庭和假期的精彩集锦时，'足够好'这一概念本身开始感觉像是将就。"

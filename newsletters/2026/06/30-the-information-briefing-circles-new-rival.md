## Circle's New Rival — Open USD

A year after the Genius Act legalized stablecoins, a consortium of 140+ financial firms including Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, Coinbase, BlackRock and BNY launched Open USD, targeting later this year.

在《天才法案》通过使稳定币合法化一年后，一群大型金融机构联合推出了Open USD。Stripe、Visa、万事达卡、Coinbase、贝莱德和纽约银行正与超过140家公司组成一个联盟。

Open USD aims to challenge Circle and Tether in the $300B stablecoin market. Open Standard shares all reserve revenue with distribution partners; no conversion fees. Circle shares fell 18%.

Open USD 旨在挑战 Circle 和 Tether 在价值 3000 亿美元的稳定币市场的主导地位。Open Standard 将与分销合作伙伴分享稳定币储备的所有收入。Circle 股价今日下跌 18%。

*Note: Past consortium stablecoin efforts have struggled — Facebook's Libra collapsed in 2022, Paxos USDG has only $3B circulation after 2 years.*

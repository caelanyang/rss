PR insider trading

Every financial services firm will tell its new employees, in their first week or two of work, the same thing: "Be careful with our confidential information. Don't tell anyone outside of the firm what you're working on. Don't talk carelessly in elevators. Don't leave your laptop unlocked. Don't even tell your spouse what you're working on." Every new employee will take that to heart, for a week or two; they will go home and their boyfriends or girlfriends will ask "so what did you do at work today" and they will say "I can't tell you it's a seeeeeeeeecret" and their boyfriends and girlfriends will be like "aww that's cute." And then they will work there for a few months, and some of this scrupulousness will get sanded away, and they will come home after a long day at work and be like "ugh John Smith at Amalgamated Sprockets is really getting on my nerves, he keeps demanding revisions on the model for the merger with Consolidated Widgets that they want to announce next Wednesday."

每家金融服务公司都会在新员工入职的一两周内，叮嘱他们同样的话：“务必小心保管公司机密信息。不要向公司以外的任何人透露你的工作内容。不要在电梯里随意交谈。不要让笔记本电脑处于未锁定状态。甚至不要告诉你的配偶你在做什么。” 每个新员工都会把这些话牢记在心，持续一两周；他们回家后，男女朋友会问：“你今天在公司都干了些什么？” 他们会回答：“我不能告诉你，这是个秘密。” 而他们的男女朋友则会说：“哇，真可爱。”然后他们会在那里工作几个月，这种一丝不苟的态度就会被磨掉一些，他们一天工作下来回到家，就会抱怨说：“唉，联合齿轮公司的约翰·史密斯真是让我心烦，他一直要求修改他们打算下周三宣布的与联合小部件公司合并的模式。”

On June 23, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Commission charged Justin Jennings and Vortex Strategies LLC with insider trading based on material nonpublic information Jennings allegedly misappropriated from his romantic partner and used to trade in advance of several corporate announcements.

2026 年 6 月 23 日，美国证券交易委员会指控贾斯汀·詹宁斯和 Vortex Strategies LLC 进行内幕交易，理由是詹宁斯利用从其伴侣处窃取的重大非公开信息，在几项公司公告发布前进行交易。

According to the SEC's complaint, between February 2022 and October 2024, Jennings misappropriated inside information from his then-romantic partner, an account executive who worked at a strategic communications and investor relations firm (Joele Frank). Jennings used his romantic partner's work-issued laptop computer to access material nonpublic information, including information related to mergers and acquisitions, earnings announcements, and other significant corporate events involving several of the firm's public company clients, without her authorization. Jennings used his personal brokerage account and Vortex to purchase the securities of eight public companies ahead of significant corporate disclosures and made illicit profits of approximately $2.7 million.

根据美国证券交易委员会（SEC）的起诉书，2022年2月至2024年10月期间，詹宁斯盗用了其当时的伴侣（一家战略传播和投资者关系公司Joele Frank的客户经理）的内幕信息。詹宁斯未经其伴侣授权，使用其工作配发的笔记本电脑访问了重要的非公开信息，包括与并购、盈利公告以及其他涉及该公司多家上市公司客户的重大公司事件相关的信息。詹宁斯利用其个人证券账户和Vortex公司在重要公司信息披露发布前购买了八家上市公司的证券，非法获利约270万美元。

Arena

Elon Musk's AI company xAI won the naming rights to the San Francisco arena formerly known as the Chase Center in a deal reportedly worth around $300 million. Musk announced the deal on X, posting a photo of himself with Golden State Warriors owner Joe Lacob. The arena will now be called Arena.

埃隆·马斯克的人工智能公司xAI以约3亿美元的价格赢得了旧金山原名大通中心（Chase Center）的冠名权。马斯克在X应用上宣布了这笔交易，并发布了一张他与金州勇士队老板乔·拉科布的合影。该场馆现在将更名为Arena。

SpaceTesla

One model of how SpaceX's merger with xAI works is that SpaceX bought xAI when it was worth $50 billion (the exchange ratio was set when xAI was worth $50 billion), but xAI's value has since shot up to around $200 billion, making Tesla shareholders (who own SpaceX) and early xAI investors very happy. But another model is that SpaceX merged xAI into itself at a price, and now SpaceX's stock price reflects the combined value of SpaceX-the-rocket-company plus xAI-the-AI-company plus Tesla-the-car-company (Tesla is now a subsidiary of SpaceX). SpaceX's stock has gone up a lot since the merger.

关于SpaceX与xAI合并运作方式的一种解释是，SpaceX在xAI估值500亿美元时收购了它（换股比例也是在xAI估值500亿美元时确定的），但此后xAI的估值飙升至约2000亿美元，这令特斯拉股东（SpaceX的股东）和xAI的早期投资者非常满意。另一种解释是，SpaceX以一定价格将xAI并入自身，如今SpaceX的股价反映了SpaceX（火箭公司）、xAI（人工智能公司）和特斯拉（汽车公司，特斯拉现在是SpaceX的子公司）三者价值的总和。自合并以来，SpaceX的股价大幅上涨。

AI SPAC

Elon Musk's AI infrastructure company xAI went public last year via an IPO, and then merged with SpaceX, and so now there's no publicly traded AI company that Musk controls. But! There is a company called Symbiont AI that is going public via SPAC. Symbiont AI has raised more than $390 million from investors including Nvidia, SoftBank and Amazon, and the SPAC deal involves a further private investment from institutions.

埃隆·马斯克的AI基础设施公司xAI去年通过IPO上市，随后与SpaceX合并，因此目前马斯克旗下没有上市的AI公司。但是！有一家名为Symbiont AI的公司即将通过SPAC上市。Symbiont AI已从包括英伟达、软银和亚马逊在内的投资者那里筹集了超过3.9亿美元的资金，此次SPAC交易还涉及来自机构的进一步私人投资。

SK Hynix ADRs

SK Hynix Inc. is seeking 45.45 trillion won ($29.4 billion) in a US listing, tapping investor demand for high-flying memory-chip stocks even after a major selloff shook the group this week. The offering of American depositary receipts comes after SK Hynix's Seoul-traded stock climbed about 850% in the past 12 months, lifting the company's market value above $1 trillion. Companies have been raising record amounts to fund the buildout of AI infrastructure in recent months. SpaceX held the largest initial public offering in history earlier this month, while Alphabet Inc. plans to raise $85 billion to fund its AI plans.

SK海力士公司计划在美国上市，寻求筹集45.45万亿韩元（约合294亿美元），以满足投资者对高增长存储芯片股的需求，即便本周该板块遭遇大幅抛售。此次发行美国存托凭证之前，SK海力士在首尔交易的股票在过去12个月里上涨了约850%，使其市值突破1万亿美元大关。近几个月来，各公司纷纷筹集创纪录的资金，用于人工智能基础设施的建设。SpaceX本月早些时候完成了史上规模最大的首次公开募股（IPO），而Alphabet公司计划筹集850亿美元，为其人工智能计划提供资金。

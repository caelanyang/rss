A couple days ago, I snagged a red-hot invite: David Holz, the Midjourney founder, was throwing a party—and promising to finally reveal his startup's first hardware product after years of saying one was in the works.   

> 几天前，我收到了一份炙手可热的邀请：Midjourney 的创始人 David Holz 将举办一个派对——并承诺在多年声称正在研发之后，最终揭晓他创办的这家公司的第一款硬件产品。

He picked a snazzy venue, the members-only Vita Brevis Club in San Francisco's SoMa neighborhood. As partygoers first shuffled in, they entered a darkened reception room where servers offered vegetarian caviar and an extremely potent, lavender-tinted margarita, dubbed The Midjourney. The majority of the people I found myself chatting with had met Midjourney founder and CEO Holz either through X or through the company's Discord server. Many wore jeans and quarter-zips; one man milled around clad in a bathrobe. At one point, Figma co-founder and CEO Dylan Field showed up, as did longevity guru Bryan Johnson. The gossip in the crowd was that Johnson's girlfriend initially couldn't get past the door: Only 250 people were allowed in, and invites were strictly nontransferable.  

> 他选了一个时髦的场地——旧金山 SoMa 地区的会员制 Vita Brevis 俱乐部。宾客们鱼贯而入，进入一间昏暗的接待室，服务员端来了素食鱼子酱和一款极为浓烈的薰衣草色玛格丽特鸡尾酒，命名为"The Midjourney"。与我交谈的大多数人都是通过 X 或公司的 Discord 服务器认识 Holz 的。许多人穿着牛仔裤和套头衫，甚至有人穿着浴袍游荡。Figma 联创兼 CEO Dylan Field 和长寿学大师 Bryan Johnson 也现身了。现场八卦是 Johnson 的女友差点没进门——只有 250 人受邀，且邀请严禁转让。

Later, Holz, wearing his signature black beanie, made his grand reveal. It wasn't what anyone expected: a full-body ultrasound scanner, which Holz plans to put in Midjourney-branded spas across the country. The body scanner relies on sound waves and water, and it appears to come in the form of a giant cylindrical water tank. It'll be the first of four hardware products the company will release, Holz said. He tried to pitch some big tech companies on the scanner, but they passed on partnering with him. "They were like, 'Sir, I make smartphones,'" Holz said.  

> 随后，戴着标志性黑色毛线帽的 Holz 正式揭幕。这出乎所有人的意料：一台全身超声波扫描仪。Holz 计划将其引入全国各地的 Midjourney 品牌水疗中心。扫描仪利用声波和水工作，外形为巨型圆柱形水箱。Holz 称这是公司将推出的四款硬件产品中的第一款。他曾向几家大型科技公司推介，但被拒绝了。他引用对方的话："他们说，'先生，我是做智能手机的。'"

In making that comment, Holz understated the expansiveness of the landscape around AI hardware. There's a lot going on. OpenAI is working on a Jony Ive–designed smart speaker, which it expects to unveil later this year. Meta Platforms' smartglasses from Ray-Ban and Oakley are equipped with its AI. Beyond eyewear, Meta is developing an AI-powered pendant, while Apple is focused on a wearable pin expected in 2027 or later. And there are plenty of smaller startups such as Midjourney that are working on AI products, like Opal, which is creating a mysterious AI audio gadget with backing from OpenAI, and Hark, the new startup from Figure AI founder Brett Adcock, which is working on some bespoke physical hardware.   

> Holz 的这番话低估了 AI 硬件领域的广阔前景。OpenAI 正在打造由乔尼·艾夫设计的智能音箱，预计年内发布；Meta 的 Ray-Ban 和 Oakley 智能眼镜已搭载 AI；此外 Meta 还在开发 AI 项链，Apple 也在研发预计 2027 年或更晚推出的可穿戴别针。较小的初创公司中，Opal 获得 OpenAI 投资正在打造神秘 AI 音频设备，Figure AI 创始人 Brett Adcock 的新公司 Hark 也在开发定制化硬件（尚未透露细节）。

There are also numerous companies vying to make the perfect robot companion capable of washing dishes and folding laundry. That includes 1X Technologies, the Palo Alto, Calif.–based startup behind NEO, a slightly creepy humanoid, and Sunday, which has raised $200 million to create the cartoonishly cute Memo. 1X is taking orders for its $20,000 robot, while Sunday is looking for beta testers.  

> 此外还有众多公司争相打造能洗碗、叠衣服的完美机器人伴侣。其中包括帕洛阿尔托的 1X Technologies（背后是有点令人毛骨悚然的人形机器人 NEO），以及获得 2 亿美元融资打造卡通风格 Memo 机器人的 Sunday。1X 已在接受 2 万美元售价的订单，Sunday 则在寻找 Beta 测试者。

But no one has really gotten a breakthrough hit yet. Humane, a startup that raised more than $230 million to create a wearable AI pin, shut down in 2025 and sold its intellectual property to HP for about half that sum. Meanwhile, Friend, a San Francisco startup that created an AI necklace, became famous not for its product but for its widely mocked marketing campaigns. And a day before Midjourney's big party, Evan Spiegel unveiled his long-awaited Specs, augmented reality glasses that come with an AI feature. They were met with almost as much derision as the Humane pin and the Friend device, and Snap shares ended the week down 13%.  

> 但迄今还没有人真正打出爆款。Humane 曾募资逾 2.3 亿美元打造 AI 别针，2025 年关闭并以约一半的价格将知识产权出售给 HP；Friend 的 AI 项链则因广受嘲笑的营销活动而出名，而非产品本身。就在 Midjourney 派对前一天，Evan Spiegel 发布了期待已久的 Specs 增强现实眼镜，反响几乎和 Humane 别针、Friend 设备一样糟糕，Snap 股价当周下跌 13%。

Still, many investors are excited about what they see in all those devices and think someone will finally land on something people do like. "A thousand flowers will bloom in this area because it's such a big prize and there are so many ways of attacking it," said Aaref Hilaly, a partner at Bain Capital Ventures who led the firm's investment in Sunday, the home robot startup.   

> 尽管如此，许多投资者仍对这些设备充满期待，认为终将有人做出人们真正喜爱的产品。贝恩资本风险投资合伙人 Aaref Hilaly（主导了对家用机器人初创公司 Sunday 的投资）表示："这一领域将百花齐放，因为奖励如此丰厚，攻破的路径如此之多。"

It's hard to predict whether Midjourney's scanner will end up as a hardware hit. Holz has an impressive history of creating beloved products—from Midjourney's AI image generator, which still generates at least $200 million in annual revenue, to the gesture-tracking device sold by his first startup, Leap Motion. He described Midjourney as the cash cow that can fund his moon-shot projects.   

> 很难预测 Midjourney 的扫描仪最终能否成为硬件爆款。Holz 有打造深受喜爱产品的光辉历史——从每年至少带来 2 亿美元营收的 Midjourney AI 图像生成器，到他第一家公司 Leap Motion 售出的手势追踪设备。他将 Midjourney 描述为可以为其登月计划提供资金的"现金奶牛"。

Eventually, he hopes the devices will be widely available to doctors, and he boldly predicted they could eliminate around half of all medical costs by helping with early disease detection. But there will be a long path toward winning the FDA's sign-off before that can happen.  

> 他最终希望这些设备能被医生广泛使用，并大胆预测通过协助早期疾病检测，它们可以消除约一半的医疗成本。但在此之前，还需要漫长的 FDA 审批之路。

I felt impressed by the scale of Holz's vision but a little skeptical. How transformative is this technology really? And is an AI lab the one to build it? But before Holz even finished his talk, about a quarter of the room had left to try demos of the technology, dunking their fists into tanks of water while lab coat–clad Midjourney staff watched. Even if he can't convince everyone, Silicon Valley certainly seems ready for a spa day.—Jemima McEvoy

> 我对 Holz 愿景的宏大印象深刻，但也有些怀疑。这项技术真的有那么颠覆性吗？一家 AI 实验室真的是打造它的最佳选择？不过，还没等 Holz 演讲结束，约四分之一的与会者就离席去体验技术演示了——他们将拳头伸入水箱，身穿白大褂的 Midjourney 员工在一旁观察。即便他无法说服所有人，硅谷似乎已经准备好享受一次水疗日了。——Jemima McEvoy

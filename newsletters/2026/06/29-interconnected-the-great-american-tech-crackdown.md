# Interconnected: The Great American Tech "Crackdown" Is Looking Like China's

**来源：** Kevin Xu / Interconnected  
**日期：** 2026-06-29  
**查看原邮件：** <https://mail.google.com/mail/u/1/#search/subject%3A%22The%20Great%20American%20Tech%20Crackdown%20Is%20Looking%20Like%20China%22>

---

If I had asked you to guess which country's tech company has to ask its government for an approved list of customers before releasing a new product, you likely would have guessed China or maybe Russia. Except today that honor belongs to OpenAI in America.

如果我让你猜测哪个国家的科技公司在发布新产品之前必须向政府申请经批准的客户名单，你可能会猜到中国或俄罗斯。但今天，这一"殊荣"属于美国的OpenAI。

If I had asked you which country's government gets "golden shares" in companies it deems strategic or wants to keep a closer eye on, your answer might be China or even Germany. Both answers would be correct, except America is trying to join that rank of socialistic best practices, especially when it comes to AI.

如果我问哪个国家的政府在其认为战略性或希望密切监控的公司中持有"黄金股"，你可能会回答中国甚至德国。两个答案都正确，除了美国也在试图加入这一社会主义最佳实践行列，尤其是在AI领域。

---

Jack Ma made a speech telling the Chinese government how it should regulate the fintech industry — he got more regulation than he asked for, not the kind he wanted. The Ant Group IPO was put on ice indefinitely. 

杰克·马发表演讲，告诉中国政府应如何监管金融科技行业——他获得的监管比他要求的更多，而且不是他想要的那种。蚂蚁集团的IPO被无限期搁置。

If you swap "speech" for "essay," then Dario Amodei's "Policy on the AI Exponential" post is just as good an answer. The US government banned access to the Fable model by any non-US citizen anywhere in the world, two days after Amodei published his essay — probably not the kind of regulation he wanted either.

如果将"演讲"换成"文章"，那么达里奥·阿莫迪（Dario Amodei）的《AI指数级增长政策》帖子同样是一个好答案。美国政府在阿莫迪发表文章两天后，禁止全球任何非美国公民访问Fable模型——这可能也不是他想要的那种监管。

---

No analogy is a 100% match. But what is happening today to frontier AI labs in the US — the Great American Tech "Crackdown" — bears many similarities to what happened to China's tech sector five years ago.

没有任何类比是100%匹配的。但今天发生在美国前沿AI实验室身上的事情——美国大科技"整顿"——与五年前发生在中国科技行业的事情有许多相似之处。

Since Jack's speech, multiple tech sectors either disappeared (education) or were permanently hobbled (gaming) overnight. Entrepreneurs and investors would never make a move or write a big check unless they are sure their actions don't run counter to the government's preferences.

自杰克·马的演讲以来，多个科技行业要么一夜之间消失（教育），要么遭到永久性削弱（游戏）。企业家和投资者再也不会采取行动或开出大额支票，除非确信自己的行为不违背政府的偏好。

---

Marc Andreessen said on a podcast: "It is really remarkable that China has decided that open-source AI is something that is good and that they want to exist, and they want to propagate…We're in a weird state of the world where the supposedly totalitarian regime is trying to open up the technology, and the supposedly democratic government system is trying to restrict and control technology."

马克·安德烈森（Marc Andreessen）在播客中表示："中国决定开源AI是好事，希望其存在并传播，这确实令人瞩目……我们处于一种奇特的世界状态：所谓的极权政权正试图开放技术，而所谓的民主政府体系正试图限制和控制技术。"

While China is promoting open source AI, America may be closer to banning it than ever before, while bureaucrats fine-comb the prospective customers list of OpenAI and Anthropic's CRM.

在中国推广开源AI的同时，美国比以往任何时候都更接近于禁止它，而官僚们正在细细审查OpenAI和Anthropic CRM中的潜在客户名单。

---

There are many things that I wish America and China could be more similar in. America could use some newer infrastructure, stronger public safety, and industrial policy planning that lasts more than four years. China could use more pluralism, rule of law, and a national soccer team that would advance to the World Cup knockout round. But imitating each other's tech crackdowns should not be one of them.

有许多方面我希望美国和中国能更相似。美国可以使用一些更新的基础设施、更强的公共安全以及持续超过四年的产业政策规划。中国可以使用更多的多元主义、法治，以及一支能进入世界杯淘汰赛的国家足球队。但相互模仿对方的科技整顿不应是其中之一。

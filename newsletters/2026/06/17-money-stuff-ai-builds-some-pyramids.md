Pyramids

The situation with artificial intelligence companies is:

人工智能公司目前面临的情况是：

The founders of AI companies place a high value on keeping control of their companies, not surrendering control to outside investors or boards of directors. Part of the reason is that AI founders believe they are creating a uniquely powerful and dangerous technology and don't want it to fall into the wrong hands.

人工智能公司的创始人非常重视对公司的控制权，不愿将控制权拱手让给外部投资者或董事会。部分原因是，人工智能创始人相信他们正在创造一种威力强大且极具危险性的技术，不希望它落入不法之徒之手。

AI companies need to raise a kajillion dollars of outside equity to build their robot armies.

人工智能公司需要筹集数万亿美元的外部股权来打造它们的机器人大军。

This is a problem. The AI founders want to keep control of their companies, but traditionally control goes with stock ownership, and they need to raise so much money that they can't really keep a majority of their companies' shares.

这是一个问题。人工智能创始人希望保持对公司的控制权，但传统上控制权与股权所有权挂钩，而他们需要筹集大量资金，因此实际上无法保持公司的大部分股份。

There are technologies to address this problem. With SpaceX's IPO, Elon Musk advanced the state of the art: He gave himself super-voting stock; he separately gave himself the ability to appoint a majority of the board of directors; and he made it essentially impossible for shareholders to sue him. Nobody has ever gone quite this far before, but on the other hand nobody has ever taken a giant existential-threat-to-humanity AI company public before.

解决这个问题的技术已经存在。埃隆·马斯克通过SpaceX的IPO，将现有技术推向了新的高度：他赋予自己超级投票权股票；他单独赋予自己任命多数董事会成员的权力；并且他几乎完全剥夺了股东起诉他的权利。此前从未有人做到过这一点，但另一方面，也从未有人将一家对人类生存构成巨大威胁的人工智能公司上市。

There is, however, an older technology from the 1920s called "pyramiding": You have Company A worth $100. You sell 30% to outsiders. You plop your 70% into Company B, sell 30% of Company B. Now you've raised $51, and you own 70% of Company B which owns 70% of Company A — a 49% economic stake, but you still have control. You continue with Company C, D, etc.

然而，还有一种源自20世纪20年代的古老技术，叫做“金字塔式投资”：假设你拥有一家价值100美元的A公司。你将30%的股份出售给外部投资者。然后，你将剩余的70%股份投入B公司，再出售B公司30%的股份。现在，你筹集了51美元，并且拥有B公司70%的股份，而B公司又拥有A公司70%的股份——你拥有49%的经济权益，但仍然控制着A公司。你可以继续投资C公司、D公司等等。

DeepSeek has closed its first funding round that raised more than 50 billion yuan ($7.4 billion) under an unusual deal structure. The funding round values the LLM developer at more than $50 billion, and requires investors to put their capital into a limited partnership managed by DeepSeek CEO Liang Wenfeng, instead of DeepSeek itself, ensuring he retains absolute control. There is a five-year lockup on all investors' shares. Other external investors will not have voting rights at DeepSeek, though they will have access to privileged financial information.

DeepSeek 完成了首轮融资，募资超过 500 亿元人民币（约合 74 亿美元），融资结构较为特殊。此轮融资对这家 LLM 开发商的估值超过 500 亿美元，并要求投资者将资金投入由 DeepSeek 首席执行官梁文峰管理的有限合伙企业，而非直接投入 DeepSeek 公司本身，从而确保梁文峰拥有绝对控制权。所有投资者的股份均有五年的锁定期。其他外部投资者虽然可以获得部分内部财务信息，但并不享有 DeepSeek 的投票权。

The goals of an AI founder are: (1) raise as much money from investors as possible and (2) give those investors as little control as possible. In theory it might be hard to reconcile those goals; in practice, so far, it's pretty easy.

人工智能创始人的目标是：(1) 尽可能多地从投资者那里筹集资金；(2) 尽可能减少投资者对公司的控制权。理论上，这两个目标可能难以兼顾；但实际上，到目前为止，这相当容易。

Cursor

In April, SpaceX struck a deal to buy Cursor, the AI coding company, for $60 billion. SpaceX couldn't really buy Cursor in April — there was too much going on with its IPO, so SpaceX bought an option to buy Cursor, to be exercised shortly after the IPO.

今年4月，SpaceX达成协议，将以600亿美元收购人工智能编码公司Cursor。但SpaceX当时无法真正完成收购——因为Cursor的IPO事宜非常繁忙，所以SpaceX购买了收购Cursor的选择权，计划在IPO后不久行使该选择权。

Today, SpaceX formally agreed to buy Cursor in a deal that will entitle the startup's investors to SpaceX stock. Elon Musk is signaling his desire for SpaceX's xAI to rapidly rebuild and catch up to rivals including Anthropic and OpenAI that have capitalized on demand for AI-powered coding tools.

今天，SpaceX正式同意收购Cursor，根据协议，Cursor的投资者将获得SpaceX的股票。埃隆·马斯克此举表明，他希望SpaceX的xAI部门能够迅速重建并赶上Anthropic和OpenAI等竞争对手，这些竞争对手已经充分利用了市场对人工智能驱动的编码工具的需求。

The price was $60 billion in SpaceX stock. In April, $60 billion was perhaps 3.3% of SpaceX's market value. Today, SpaceX was around $2.75 trillion at noon, so $60 billion is perhaps 2.2% of SpaceX's value. Cursor's founders missed out on the upside from the IPO itself.

此次交易的价格是价值 600 亿美元的 SpaceX 股票。今年 4 月，600 亿美元大约占 SpaceX 市值的 3.3%。今天中午，SpaceX 的市值约为 2.75 万亿美元，因此 600 亿美元大约只占 SpaceX 市值的 2.2%。Cursor 的创始人错过了 IPO 本身带来的收益。

"Redefining excellence in the age of agentic AI"

A KPMG report on how AI is being used by businesses across the world exaggerated adoption of the technology with bogus case studies that appear to have been based on AI hallucinations.

毕马威发布的一份关于全球企业如何使用人工智能的报告夸大了该技术的普及程度，并列举了一些虚假的案例研究，这些案例研究似乎是基于人工智能的臆想。

The October report, "Redefining excellence in the age of agentic AI", made numerous false claims about the use of AI by organisations including UBS, the UK's National Health Service, Swiss Federal Railways and Transport for London.

10 月份发布的报告《在人工智能时代重新定义卓越》对瑞银集团、英国国家医疗服务体系、瑞士联邦铁路和伦敦交通局等组织使用人工智能的情况做出了许多不实的声明。

Edward Tian, GPTZero chief executive, said error-riddled publications by the Big Four "poison the well of information". It's a fascinating recursive vulnerability: Modern AI relies on trusted human sources to understand the world, but humans increasingly rely on AI to understand the world. If the trusted human sources take whatever AI says at face value, then whatever AI says will just become fact.

GPTZero首席执行官Edward Tian表示，四大权威机构发布的错误百出的出版物“毒害了信息之井”。这是一个引人深思的递归漏洞：现代人工智能依赖可信的人类信息源来理解世界，但人类却越来越依赖人工智能来理解世界。如果可信的人类信息源全盘接受人工智能所说的一切，那么人工智能所说的一切最终都会变成事实。

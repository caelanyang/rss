Greetings! We got big news in the past couple of days concerning a European court ruling against Google. But bear with me, this is not one of those knee-jerk anti-U.S. tech decisions we often see coming out of Europe. This ruling makes some sense—and could have broader implications for the AI sector.

大家好！过去几天我们收到了一条关于欧洲法院对谷歌做出不利裁决的重大消息。但请别着急，这并非欧洲常见的那种出于本能的反美科技决策。这项裁决有一定的道理，而且可能对人工智能领域产生更广泛的影响。

A court in Germany found that Google is responsible for what its AI models say in AI-written answers to search results. While the ruling is preliminary, according to Google, the finding spotlights an unresolved question about who's responsible for costly errors made by AI models.

德国一家法院裁定，谷歌应对其人工智能模型在搜索结果中生成的答案负责。谷歌表示，虽然该裁决尚属初步裁决，但它凸显了一个悬而未决的问题：究竟谁该为人工智能模型造成的代价高昂的错误负责？

The court ruled in favor of two businesses that sued Google over AI Overviews results describing those businesses as scams when in fact they weren't. The errors were made by Google's AI—the judgment noted that the AI Overviews answer said things not mentioned by the websites that were the supposed source. "These are unique assertions invented by Google's AI tool," the ruling said, which Google "must accept responsibility for."

两家企业起诉谷歌，指控其人工智能概览（AI Overviews）将这两家企业错误地描述为诈骗企业，而事实并非如此。法院裁定两家企业胜诉。判决指出，这些错误是由谷歌的人工智能造成的——人工智能概览的答案中包含了一些原本并非信息来源的网站所没有提及的内容。“这些都是谷歌人工智能工具凭空捏造的结论，”判决书称，谷歌“必须为此承担责任”。

The ruling highlights how AI-written responses could undercut the legal protections Google and other tech firms enjoy in the U.S. under section 230 of the Communications Decency Act. That law says tech firms aren't liable for content that appears on their services. Some of those same protections apply in Germany, the ruling indicated: "For standard search engines, courts usually rule that operators do not have to check every link in advance." But as the ruling points out, an AI Overview answer, unlike a traditional search response, "weaves information together to create brand-new text statements."

该裁决凸显了人工智能生成的回复可能会如何削弱谷歌和其他科技公司在美国根据《通信规范法》第230条享有的法律保护。该法规定，科技公司不对其服务上出现的内容承担责任。裁决指出，德国也存在部分类似的保护措施：“对于传统搜索引擎，法院通常裁定运营商无需事先检查每个链接。” 但正如裁决所指出的，人工智能生成的答案与传统的搜索结果不同，“它会将信息整合在一起，生成全新的文本语句。”

In a statement, Google pointed out that "the overwhelming majority of responses" in AI Overviews are accurate, although it acknowledges Overviews can get things wrong at times. Google had argued, according to the ruling, that users could check AI answers by looking at the websites the answers link to. But the court noted, "If an AI overview were legally treated as completely unreliable text that required users to check every single link anyway, the entire feature would lose its stated purpose."

谷歌在一份声明中指出，人工智能概览中的“绝大多数回复”都是准确的，但也承认概览有时也会出错。根据判决书，谷歌辩称，用户可以通过查看答案链接的网站来验证人工智能的回答。但法院指出，“如果将人工智能概览在法律上视为完全不可靠的文本，要求用户检查每一个链接，那么这项功能就失去了其既定目的。”

Oracle's Mixed Quarter

Oracle's expansion of its AI cloud business is on track. But its pesky software business is holding back its overall growth. Software has long been Oracle's most important operation, but AI data centers now outshine software.

Oracle的AI云业务扩张进展顺利。但其棘手的软件业务却拖累了整体增长。软件业务长期以来一直是Oracle最重要的业务，但如今AI数据中心的重要性已经超过了软件业务。

The company reported that in the May quarter, concluding Oracle's fiscal year, revenue grew 21%, a percentage point slower than in the previous quarter, although the slowdown appeared to be due to currency fluctuations. On a constant currency basis, Oracle's growth picked up. Even so, a 20% or so growth rate understates the massive explosion underway in Oracle's cloud operation, where revenue jumped 93% in the quarter compared with a year earlier to $5.8 billion.

该公司报告称，在截至5月份的季度（即Oracle财年最后一个季度），营收增长21%，比上一季度放缓了一个百分点，但增速放缓似乎是由于汇率波动所致。按固定汇率计算，Oracle的营收增长有所回升。即便如此，20%左右的增长率也低估了Oracle云业务的爆发式增长，该业务当季营收同比增长93%，达到58亿美元。

On the software side, however, it's a different story. Oracle's traditional software business has been declining, as businesses switch away from buying software to subscribing to it in the cloud. Oracle has a cloud software business that should in theory pick up some of the customers switching away. But businesses have lots of choices about cloud software, with new AI firms widening the array of those alternatives. And Oracle's own cloud software business isn't doing that great. It grew just 10% to $4.1 billion in the most recent quarter, 3 percentage points slower than in the previous quarter.

然而，软件方面的情况则截然不同。随着企业从购买软件转向订阅云端软件，Oracle 的传统软件业务一直在下滑。Oracle 的云软件业务理论上应该能够吸引一部分流失的客户。但企业在云软件方面有很多选择，新兴的人工智能公司进一步丰富了这些选择。而且，Oracle 自身的云软件业务表现也不尽如人意。在最近一个季度，其收入仅增长了 10%，达到 41 亿美元，比上一季度下降了 3 个百分点。

In Other News

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman told staff in a Slack message that he expects OpenAI to go public "within the next year" and that "many things could cause it to be sooner or later in that range, but filing now gives us optionality if we want to go sooner." Another OpenAI leader also teased an upcoming new AI model.

OpenAI 首席执行官 Sam Altman 在 Slack 消息中告诉员工，他预计 OpenAI 将在“未来一年内”上市，并表示“很多因素可能会导致上市时间提前或延后，但现在提交申请可以让我们在想要提前上市时拥有更多选择权。”另一位 OpenAI 高管也暗示即将推出一款新的 AI 模型。

SoftBank is running into further problems borrowing $6 billion secured against its OpenAI stake.

软银在以其持有的 OpenAI 股份作抵押借款 60 亿美元时遇到了更多问题。

Advertisers can now create ads for specific products on ChatGPT by sharing feeds of product information with OpenAI.

广告商现在可以通过与 OpenAI 分享产品信息流，在 ChatGPT 上为特定产品创建广告。

Microsoft's Xbox gaming unit plans to cut staff in the coming months as its financial picture worsens. In a note to staff, CEO Asha Sharma said Xbox's "accountability margins" have fallen to 3% in the fiscal year ending this month, a trend she said "cannot continue."

由于财务状况恶化，微软旗下Xbox游戏部门计划在未来几个月裁员。首席执行官阿莎·夏尔马在致员工的信中表示，Xbox的“责任利润率”在本月结束的本财年已降至3%，她表示这种趋势“不能再继续下去了”。

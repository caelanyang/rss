Money Stuff
By Matt Levine / Bloomberg
Jun 4, 2026

**Pre-IPO perpetual futures**

A lot of people want to bet on SpaceX's stock. A lot of other people want to bet against SpaceX's stock. In approximately one week, the people who want to bet on SpaceX will be able to do so: SpaceX will be publicly traded, albeit with a fairly low float. People who want to bet against SpaceX will also get more or less what they want.

**大量人想押注SpaceX股票，也有大量人想做空SpaceX股票。大约一周后，想押注SpaceX的人将能够如愿：SpaceX将公开交易，尽管流通量相当低。想做空SpaceX的人也将或多或少地如愿。**

But there are complications: The expectation is that SpaceX will go public with a $1.77 trillion market capitalization but a free float of only $75 billion. The rest of the stock will be locked up for at least several months. SpaceX's market value will represent 2-3% of the entire US stock market, but its available float will be something like 0.1% of the market. If everyone wants to own SpaceX in proportion to its market weight, they won't be able to.

**但有一些复杂因素：预计SpaceX将以1.77万亿美元市值上市，但自由流通量仅750亿美元。其余股票将被锁定至少数月。SpaceX市值将占美国整个股市的2-3%，但可用流通量仅约为市场的0.1%，这可能导致价格挤压。**

Coinbase is expanding what it means to be the Everything Exchange by launching pre-IPO perpetual futures — a product category that gives eligible traders price exposure to private companies before they list on public markets. These are synthetic instruments, not actual shares. Historically, allowing people to trade stocks of private companies was illegal under US securities law; these platforms are increasingly being built as the regulatory environment shifts.

**Coinbase通过推出IPO前永续期货（合成工具，非实际股份）扩展其"一切皆可交易"的定位，让合格交易者在公司上市前获得私人公司的价格敞口。历史上，让人们交易私人公司股票在美国证券法下是非法的；随着监管环境转变，这类平台正在越来越多地被建立。**

The broader theme: "We seem to be reaching the end of securities regulation," as it concerns private company trading. Frec is also launching a new product that lets investors buy shares in non-traded companies. Santos (a trading platform) is involved. A major theme over the past year has been the creation of pre-IPO trading infrastructure to handle the surge of demand around high-profile private companies.

**更广泛的主题：就私人公司交易而言，"我们似乎正在走向证券监管的终结"。Frec也在推出一款新产品，让投资者购买非交易公司的股份。过去一年的主要主题之一是建立IPO前交易基础设施，以应对围绕高知名度私人公司的需求激增。**

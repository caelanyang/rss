Pre-IPO perpetual futures

A lot of people want to bet on SpaceX’s stock. A lot of other people want to 
bet against SpaceX’s stock. In approximately one week, the people who want to 
bet on SpaceX will be able to do so to their hearts’ content: SpaceX will be 
publicly traded, albeit with a fairly low float, and you’ll be able to buy as 
much as you want at some price. People who want to betagainst SpaceX will also 
get more or less what they want at more or less the same time: Once SpaceX is 
public, you will be able to borrow shares and sell them short 
<https://bloom.bg/4vshrvh>. 

很多人想押注SpaceX的股票。也有很多人想做空SpaceX的股票。大约一周后，想押注SpaceX的人就可以尽情操作了：SpaceX将上市交易，尽管流通股数量可能不多，但你可以以任何价格购买任意数量的股票。想做空SpaceX的人也差不多能在同一时间得到他们想要的东西：一旦SpaceX上市，你就可以借入股票并进行卖空操作。

On the other hand:

 * A week is a long time, there will be a lot of SpaceX news in the next week, 
and a lot of people want to get their bets innow. 
 * Even in a week, not everyone who wants to bet on, or especially against, 
SpaceX will get exactly what they want. The supply of shares to borrow (for 
short selling) might be tight and expensive. Many current shareholders — 
employees, venture investors, special purpose vehicles — have lockup agreements 
that prevent them from selling their stock, or selling short to hedge, for 
months after the IPO. [1]  <> A more freewheeling, general-purpose way to bet 
against SpaceX stock might fill a need even once SpaceX is public. 
 * Also, a more freewheeling, general-purpose way to bet against SpaceX might 
help people who want to beton SpaceX, by creating more supply. The  expectation 
<https://bloom.bg/4vvp2cx> now is that SpaceX will go public with a $1.77 
trillion market capitalization but a free float — shares available to buy on 
the market — of only $75 billion. The rest of the stock will be locked up for 
at least several months. There could be, as it were, a bit of a squeeze to get 
into the stock <https://bloom.bg/4vxVues>: SpaceX’s market value will represent 
on the order of 2% to 3% of theentire US stock market <https://bloom.bg/3RFAkMP>
, but its available float will be something like 0.1% of the market. If 
everyone wants to own SpaceX in proportion to its market weight, they won’t be 
able to; if they try, they will push up the price. But if, instead, they can 
buy SpaceX stock from people who want to bet against it — if the people betting 
against it could create new synthetic shares — then the supply squeeze would be 
eased. 
A major theme <https://bloom.bg/4vxjnTp> of this column 
<https://bloom.bg/3S6zOrb> over the past year <https://bloom.bg/4vsIJS8> has 
been <https://bloom.bg/43bLrzw>: That’s coming. Historically, a lot of people 
have had thoughts of the form “I would like to bet on the stocks of hot private 
startups,” and of the form “I would like to bet against the stocks of hot 
private startups.” Occasionally they have even launched public exchanges to 
facilitate it: You set up a website, people who want to bet on or against a 
startup can show up, and the website can pair them off against each other and 
facilitate their bets.

* 一周时间很长，接下来一周SpaceX的新闻会很多，

很多人都想趁机下注。

* 即使一周时间足够，也并非所有想做多（尤其是做空）SpaceX的人都能如愿以偿。

用于借入（做空）的股票供应可能紧张且成本高昂。许多现有股东——

员工、风险投资者、特殊目的公司——都签署了锁定协议，

这些协议禁止他们在IPO后的几个月内出售股票或进行卖空对冲。[1] <> 即使SpaceX上市后，一种更自由、更通用的做空SpaceX股票的方式或许也能满足需求。

* 此外，一种更自由、更通用的做空SpaceX的方式或许也能通过增加股票供应来帮助那些想做空SpaceX的人。目前预计SpaceX上市时市值将达到1.77万亿美元，但自由流通股（即可在市场上购买的股票）仅为750亿美元。其余股票至少将被锁定数月。因此，投资者可能难以买到SpaceX的股票：SpaceX的市值将占美国股市总市值的2%至3%左右，但其自由流通股仅占市场份额的0.1%左右。如果每个人都想按其市值比例持有SpaceX的股票，他们将无法做到；如果他们试图这样做，将会推高股价。但如果他们能够从那些做空SpaceX的人手中购买SpaceX股票——如果那些做空SpaceX的人能够创建新的合成股票——那么供应紧张的局面就会得到缓解。

过去一年来，本专栏的一个主要主题是：这种情况即将发生。历史上，很多人都曾有过这样的想法：“我想投资热门的私营初创公司的股票”，以及“我想做空热门的私营初创公司的股票”。他们有时甚至会推出公开交易平台来促成交易：你建立一个网站，想对初创公司进行投注的人可以上来，网站可以将他们配对，并促成他们的投注。

Historically the problem with this is that it is illegal under US securities 
law, and those platforms would be shut down <https://bloom.bg/4o6B7CB>. This 
was not a small accidental technicality. Themain point of US securities law is 
that, if a company wants to sell stock to the general public, it has to 
register with the US Securities and Exchange Commission and disclose financial 
and business information. A platform allowing people to trade stocks of private 
companies — or trade synthetic bets on those stocks — gets around that 
requirement; it would be,as I wrote last month <https://bloom.bg/43bLrzw>, “the 
end of securities regulation.” Well, what I wrote last month was “We seem to be 
reaching the end of securities regulation,” because those platforms are 
increasingly being built.

从历史上看，问题在于这违反了美国证券法，这些平台会被关闭<https://bloom.bg/4o6B7CB>。这并非无关紧要的技术细节。美国证券法的核心在于，如果一家公司想要向公众出售股票，它必须在美国证券交易委员会注册并披露财务和业务信息。允许人们交易非上市公司股票（或交易这些股票的合成押注）的平台规避了这一要求；正如我上个月所写<https://bloom.bg/43bLrzw>的那样，这将是“证券监管的终结”。好吧，我上个月写的是“我们似乎正在走向证券监管的终结”，因为这类平台正在越来越多地涌现。

Prediction market stock offerings

In that vein, let’s talk about prediction market prime brokerage. I wrote last 
month <https://bloom.bg/43bLrzw> about Polymarket’s new offering of prediction 
markets on private company valuations. Those provide a way to, approximately, 
trade shares of private companies without any securities registration or 
disclosure. Not like Coinbase — not with perpetual futures — but with binary 
yes/no bets on whether a private company will exceed a given valuation by a 
given date. Again, this is not technically available to US users, though I’m 
not sure how much anyone cares.

由此，我们来聊聊预测市场经纪业务。我上个月写了一篇关于Polymarket新推出的预测市场的文章（链接：<https://bloom.bg/43bLrzw>），内容是关于私营公司估值的预测市场。这些市场提供了一种无需证券注册或信息披露即可大致交易私营公司股票的方法。它不像Coinbase那样提供永续期货，而是提供关于私营公司在特定日期前估值是否会超过预设值的二元“是/否”投注。再次强调，这项服务目前尚未向美国用户开放，不过我不确定有多少人会在意这一点。

The logical end of this is something like “private companies can raise money, 
without registering to go public, using prediction markets.” How might that 
work? I suggested that an investor who owns stock in a big private company 
could go to Polymarket and sell a “Will [company]’s valuation hit [amount] by 
Dec. 31” contract for roughly the notional amount of her stock. If you own 
$96.5 million of Anthropic stock at its current$965 billion valuation 
<https://bloom.bg/4oj2w4b>, you sell 100 million “Will Anthropic hit $1.1 
trillion”contracts <https://bloom.bg/4ukYTwK> at their current price of around 
93 cents each. [3]  <> You get $93 million now. If the contract settles to 
“Yes” (if Anthropic’s valuation hits $1.1 trillion), you owe $100 million on 
the contract, but your stock is worth at least $110 million at that valuation, 
so you sell some stock for $100 million, deliver it to settle the contract, and 
keep your initial $93 million (plus whatever stock you have left). Effectively 
you have presold your stock at $93 million. [4]  <> If the contract settles to 
“No” (if the valuation is below $1.1 trillion), you keep the $93 millionand the 
(perhaps somewhat less valuable) stock.

由此推论，最终结果可能是“私营公司无需注册上市，即可利用预测市场筹集资金”。这会如何运作呢？我建议，持有某大型私营公司股票的投资者可以前往 Polymarket 平台，出售一份“[公司名称]估值能否在12月31日前达到[金额]”的合约，价格大致相当于其股票的名义价值。例如，如果您持有价值9650万美元的Anthropic股票（该公司目前估值为9650亿美元），您可以出售1亿份“Anthropic能否达到1.1万亿美元”的合约（每份合约当前价格约为0.93美元）。[3] 这样，您就能立即获得9300万美元。如果合约结算结果为

“是”（如果 Anthropic 的估值达到 1.1 万亿美元），您将欠合约 1 亿美元

但您的股票在该估值下至少价值 1.1 亿美元
因此，您可以出售部分股票获得 1 亿美元，交付股票以结算合约，并

保留您最初的 9300 万美元（加上您剩余的股票）。实际上，

您以 9300 万美元的价格预售了您的股票。[4] <> 如果合约结算结果为

“否”（如果估值低于 1.1 万亿美元），您将保留 9300 万美元和

（可能价值略低的）股票。

If investors could do this, then it would effectively be a public fundraising 
mechanism for private companies. A company could sell $100 million of stock to 
an institutional investor in a private placement, and the institutional 
investor could turn around and sell $100 million of 
ever-so-slightly-out-of-the-money prediction-market valuation contracts on the 
company to hedge and fund its investment. The institutional investor would 
essentially be a conduit, and the company would ultimately be raising money 
from prediction markets.

如果投资者可以这样做，那么这实际上就成为了一种面向私营公司的公开融资机制。一家公司可以通过私募向机构投资者出售价值 1 亿美元的股票，而该机构投资者可以转而出售价值 1 亿美元的、略微价外的预测市场估值合约，以对冲风险并为其投资融资。机构投资者本质上扮演着渠道的角色，而公司最终将从预测市场筹集资金。

There are huge problems with this theory, which I will get to below, but I do 
want to point out anice thing about it, which is that it is positive-sum. 
“Growing private companies might be able to raise money from retail gamblers in 
prediction markets” is a strange but effective response to mymain complaint 
about prediction markets <https://bloom.bg/4o9FKeT>, which is that they are 
necessarily zero-sum gambling. When you buy stock, you areinvesting; you are 
using your money to finance economic growth in exchange for a share of that 
growth. When you buy a Polymarket contract on “will the Knicks beat the Spurs,” 
you arenot investing; any money that you make has to come from people betting 
against you. But if the people betting against you arecompanies that use your 
bets to finance economic growth then, you know, hey, great.

这个理论存在很多问题，我会在下文中详细阐述，但我确实想指出它的一个优点，那就是它是正和博弈。

“成长型私营公司或许能够从预测市场的散户赌徒那里筹集资金”这句话，虽然听起来有些奇怪，但却有效地回应了我对预测市场的主要不满<https://bloom.bg/4o9FKeT>，即预测市场必然是零和博弈。当你购买股票时，你就是在投资；你用你的钱来促进经济增长，以换取经济增长的一部分。当你购买一份关于“尼克斯队能否击败马刺队”的Polymarket合约时，你并非在投资；你赚到的任何钱都必须来自那些押注与你相反的人。但如果押注与你相反的人是那些利用你的赌注来促进经济增长的公司，那么，你知道，这很棒。

But let’s talk about the problems with this theory. I can see three big ones, 
two of which I will deal with quickly:

但我们来谈谈这个理论存在的问题。我认为有三个主要问题，其中两个我将简要讨论：



 * This is, like, ludicrously illegal? Like, come on, man. Buying stock in a 
private placement and turning around and selling retail investors binary 
options on that stock’s price raises all sorts of problems. (“Underwriting 
<https://bloom.bg/43eNfI8>,” “dealer <https://bloom.bg/4o43Rvu>,” “
security-based swaps <https://bloom.bg/4vsIJS8>,” others.) But, again, we are 
coming to the end of securities regulation, so this might not matter. 
 * Liquidity in these markets is very small, and you couldn’t really sell $100 
million of anything. (That Anthropic $1.1 trillion contract had like $92,000 of 
volume as of this morning.) Presumably that will grow over time, but it is 
probably fanciful to think that there will ever really be hundreds of millions 
of dollars of liquidity here. 
The third problem, though, is that my description of how prediction markets 
work is mechanically wrong. You don’t “sell” 100 million “Anthropic hits $1.1 
trillion contracts” for 93 cents each. You can’t sell event contracts that you 
don’t own; your position on a prediction market can only be an asset,never a 
liability <https://bloom.bg/4wZPJaK>. Instead, if you want to make that bet, 
what you do isbuy the “No” contract. You pay money up front; you don’t receive 
money. The “No” contract costs about 10 cents (there’s some friction, so Yes 
plus No is not perfectly $1), so you’d pay $10 million upfront. If Anthropic’s 
valuation hits $1.1 trillion, you lose the $10 million, but you keep your 
stock, which is now worth at least $110 million. If it stays below $1.1 
trillion, you collect $100 million ($90 million net, after the upfront premium).

这简直是荒谬至极的违法行为？拜托，老兄。在私募中购买股票，然后转手向散户投资者出售基于该股票价格的二元期权，这会引发各种各样的问题。（例如“承销”<https://bloom.bg/43eNfI8>、“交易商”<https://bloom.bg/4o43Rvu>、“证券型互换”<https://bloom.bg/4vsIJS8>等。）但是，话说回来，我们即将迎来证券监管的终结，所以这可能无关紧要。

这些市场的流动性非常低，你根本不可能卖出价值1亿美元的任何东西。 （截至今天早上，Anthropic 1.1 万亿美元的合约交易量约为 9.2 万美元。）可以预见，随着时间的推移，交易量会增长，但认为这里最终会拥有数亿美元的流动性可能有些异想天开。

George Santos insider trading

This is extremely not legal advice, but here’s how I understand prediction 
market insider trading. It isnot illegal to trade on material nonpublic 
information that only you have; thepoint of prediction markets is to get people 
to develop and use proprietary information. Rather, it is illegal to trade on 
material nonpublic information that yougot from your job, or that you otherwise 
had some duty to keep confidential. In April, a US Army soldierwas indicted 
<https://bloom.bg/43bLrQ2> for allegedly insider trading on military plans on 
Polymarket. He “had a duty and obligation to maintain the confidentiality of 
the information he acquired, a duty he breached by misappropriating the 
information and using it to trade,” theUS Commodity Futures Trading Commission 
said <https://bloom.bg/3P5tW0b>. Similarly, the former Google employee charged 
with insider trading <https://bloom.bg/3RODxtt> on Polymarket “owed a duty of 
trust and confidence to Google to maintain the confidentiality of [its] 
information and not use it for personal gain.”

这并非法律建议，但我对预测市场内幕交易的理解是这样的：利用只有你掌握的重大非公开信息进行交易并不违法；预测市场的目的就是鼓励人们开发和使用专有信息。然而，利用你从工作中获得的重大非公开信息，或者你负有保密义务的信息进行交易才是违法的。今年4月，一名美国陆军士兵因涉嫌在Polymarket平台上进行军事计划内幕交易而被起诉。美国商品期货交易委员会表示，他“有义务对所获取的信息保密，但他通过盗用信息并利用信息进行交易而违反了这一义务”。同样，被控在 Polymarket 上进行内幕交易的前谷歌员工 <https://bloom.bg/3RODxtt> “对谷歌负有信任和信心义务，必须对[其]信息保密，不得将其用于个人利益。”

Good time for direct indexing

“Direct indexing” means, instead of buying an index fund that buys all the 
stocks in the index, you buy all of the stocks in the index yourself. This used 
to be pretty difficult for a retail investor, but with the rise of, like, 
computers and zero-commission trading and fractional shares, it’s now 
reasonably easy, and there are companies that will do it for you with fairly 
low minimum investments. One reason to direct index is tax efficiency; you can 
tax-loss-harvest your losers when you own them all directly.

“直接指数投资”是指，您不是购买购买指数中所有股票的指数基金，而是自己购买指数中的所有股票。过去，这对散户投资者来说相当困难，但随着计算机、零佣金交易和零碎股交易等技术的兴起，现在变得相对容易，而且有一些公司可以提供这项服务，最低投资额也相当低。直接指数投资的一个优势是税收效率；当您直接持有所有股票时，您可以对亏损的股票进行税损收割。

But the other main reason <https://bloom.bg/4vzn2jz> is that you can almost 
index. You can buy, like, 499 of the 500 companies in the S&P 500. Direct 
indexing allows you to construct all sorts of portfolios thatstart with the 
index — that start from the presumption that you should just match the market 
portfolio — but give it a little tilt. The little tilt can be economic (you 
think that financial firms will underperform so you keep them out of your 
direct index), or environmental/social/governance (you think that coal is bad 
so you don’t buy the coal companies), or whatever else strikes your fancy. You 
are not beholden to an index provider’s decisions. You can take the index that 
they give you and make whatever changes you want.

但另一个主要原因<https://bloom.bg/4vzn2jz>是，你几乎可以进行指数投资。你可以购买标普500指数中500家公司中的499家。直接指数投资允许你构建各种以指数为基础的投资组合——其基本假设是你应该匹配市场投资组合——但可以稍作调整。这种调整可以是经济方面的（例如，你认为金融公司表现不佳，所以将其排除在你的直接指数之外），也可以是环境/社会/治理方面的（例如，你认为煤炭不好，所以不购买煤炭公司的股票），或者任何其他你感兴趣的因素。你无需受制于指数提供商的决定。你可以使用他们提供的指数，并根据自己的需要进行任何修改。

You see where I’m going with this. I wrote yesterday <https://bloom.bg/3S1nacX>
 that there seems to be a lot of demand for index funds thatwon’t include the 
big artificial intelligence companies planning to go public this year, and 
several people emailed me to point out that not only can you get this (or 
whatever else you want) with direct indexing, but also thatFrec 
<https://bloom.bg/4vn8EL0>, a direct indexing firm, has specifically jumped on 
the opportunity. Its chief executive officerposted on LinkedIn this week 
<https://bloom.bg/4o9h9XO>:

你明白我的意思了吧。我昨天写了这篇文章（https://bloom.bg/3S1nacX），

提到市场对不包含今年计划上市的大型人工智能公司的指数基金需求旺盛，而且

有好几个人给我发邮件指出，不仅可以通过直接指数投资获得这种基金（或者

任何你想要的基金），而且直接指数投资公司Frec（https://bloom.bg/4vn8EL0）已经抓住了这个机会。

其首席执行官本周在LinkedIn上发帖（https://bloom.bg/4o9h9XO）：

Many customers have reached out about preemptively restricting SpaceX from 
their direct indices.

许多客户联系我们，希望提前将SpaceX从他们的直接指数中剔除。

As of today, customers can add a trade restriction against SPCX so Frec will 
not open a position. They will also be able to exclude it from any index that 
adds it. …

从今天起，客户可以对SPCX设置交易限制，这样Frec就不会开仓。他们还可以将其从任何纳入SPCX的指数中排除。

The broader point is that ETF investors outsource all decisions to fund 
managers and index providers. When an index changes, they generally have to 
accept the new exposure and have no say.

更广泛地说，ETF投资者将所有决策权都外包给了基金经理和指数提供商。当指数发生变化时，他们通常不得不接受新的风险敞口，而没有发言权。

Direct indexing gives investors more control. They can restrict individual 
securities, customize their exposure, and participate in markets in a way that 
better reflects their own preferences.

直接指数投资赋予投资者更多控制权。他们可以限制个别证券的投资范围，定制投资比例，并以更符合自身偏好的方式参与市场。

My view is that the point of passive investing is to give up control, but I 
concede that this is a good pitch!

我的观点是，被动投资的意义在于放弃控制权，但我承认这确实是一个很好的推销手段！

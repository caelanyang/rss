By Matt Levine, Bloomberg

Prediction market regulation

For a while, sports betting in the US was regulated by state law. Some states banned sports betting; others allowed it under various regulatory regimes. But then Kalshi, the prediction market, started offering sports gambling nationwide and claiming, mostly successfully, that it was exempt from state regulation. Kalshi is a commodities exchange registered with the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission; its theory is that (1) sports bets are a sort of commodity "swap" subject to CFTC regulation and (2) the CFTC has exclusive jurisdiction to regulate those swaps. The states can't regulate Kalshi, because the CFTC does.

一段时间以来，美国的体育博彩由各州法律监管。一些州禁止体育博彩，另一些州则在不同的监管制度下允许其进行。但随后，预测市场平台Kalshi开始在全美范围内提供体育博彩服务，并声称（而且大多成功地）其不受州监管。Kalshi是一家在美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）注册的商品交易所；其理论是：（1）体育博彩是一种商品“互换”，受CFTC监管；（2）CFTC对这类互换拥有专属管辖权。各州无法监管Kalshi，因为CFTC拥有监管权。

But the CFTC didn't, not really. CFTC regulation of sports betting was mostly hypothetical: Kalshi would "self-certify" the sports bets it wanted to list, and the CFTC wouldn't say anything, and that was that. There were no federal statutes or regulations about what sports bets are allowed. Kalshi just decided on its own what bets to list, and if the CFTC objected it could let Kalshi know.

但美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）实际上并没有真正介入。CFTC对体育博彩的监管大多停留在设想阶段：Kalshi会自行“认证”其想要上架的体育博彩项目，CFTC对此不予置评，事情就此结束。当时并没有联邦法律或法规规定哪些体育博彩项目是允许的。Kalshi完全可以自行决定上架哪些博彩项目，如果CFTC有异议，也可以告知Kalshi。

Now that's changing. Trump regulators are proposing an expansive new set of rules on how they will govern the booming prediction markets, with parameters that will continue to allow most sports-related bets while trying to avoid inviting obvious manipulation.

现在情况正在发生变化。特朗普政府的监管机构正在提议制定一套范围广泛的新规则，以规范蓬勃发展的预测市场。这些规则的参数将允许大多数体育相关的投注继续进行，同时力求避免出现明显的操纵行为。

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission is seeking the ability to block prediction wagers it finds aren't in the public interest or that seem highly susceptible to manipulation, such as in cases where one person could have outsize impact on the result. The regulator proposed the new rules on Wednesday, confirming an earlier report in The Wall Street Journal.

美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）正寻求赋予其权力，以阻止那些它认为不符合公众利益或极易被操纵的预测性投注，例如，某些人可能对结果产生过大影响的情况。该监管机构于周三提出了新规，证实了《华尔街日报》此前的报道。

These rules, if they eventually go into effect, will be the first set of federal sports gambling regulations. The CFTC has to answer questions that no federal financial-markets regulator has ever considered, questions like: Should there be betting on Little League games? No, says the CFTC.

这些规则如果最终生效，将成为首套联邦体育博彩法规。美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）必须回答一些联邦金融市场监管机构从未考虑过的问题，例如：是否应该允许对少儿棒球联赛进行投注？CFTC的答案是否定的。

The Commission preliminarily believes that event contracts that settle solely by reference to games, sporting events, or outcomes in which participants are below the collegiate level raise public interest concerns. Since pre-collegiate sports have less extensive governing bodies and typically lack a rigorous integrity infrastructure, prediction markets would be less able to interface with the governing body. Similarly, broad and numerous groups of individuals would potentially have inside information about pre-collegiate sports and would be subject to little or no contractual limitations on information usage. The Commission preliminarily believes that these differences from professional and collegiate sports raise particular concerns about manipulation, settlement integrity and information leakage.

委员会初步认为，仅以大学以下级别比赛、体育赛事或结果为结算依据的赛事合同会引发公众利益方面的担忧。由于大学前体育运动的管理机构规模较小，且通常缺乏严格的诚信机制，预测市场与管理机构的沟通渠道也相对有限。同样，众多个人群体可能掌握大学前体育运动的内幕信息，且其信息使用几乎不受任何合同限制。委员会初步认为，这些与职业体育和大学体育的差异尤其令人担忧操纵、结算诚信以及信息泄露等问题。

The broader context here is that Kalshi has been a regulatory innovator—it has taken legal strategies that were long considered unlikely, and in some cases simply done what it wanted to do, daring regulators to object. Now regulators are starting to write down actual rules.

更广泛的背景是，Kalshi 一直是监管领域的创新者——它采取了长期以来被认为不太可能的法律策略，在某些情况下，它甚至直接为所欲为，挑战监管机构的反对。现在，监管机构开始着手制定实际规则了。

This also means that the prediction market exemption from state regulation might force something interesting: Congress or the CFTC might have to grapple with whether state sportsbooks are all technically illegal under federal law, since the CFTC theoretically has exclusive jurisdiction over "event contracts."

这也意味着，预测市场不受州监管的豁免可能会带来一些有趣的事情：国会或美国商品期货交易委员会 (CFTC) 可能不得不面对这样一个问题：根据联邦法律，各州的体育博彩公司在技术上是否都是非法的，因为 CFTC 理论上对“赛事合同”拥有专属管辖权。

The Briefing
By Martin Peers
Jun 3, 2026

It's no secret that criminals are using AI to streamline computer hacks. Cybersecurity executives are rubbing their hands with glee at the influx of business they expect. "What I see is AI driving structural demand for cybersecurity that compounds, not decelerates," said CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz. Similarly, Netskope CEO Sanjay Beri said the rise of AI is "exponentially increasing the pace and potency of attacks."

**罪犯正在利用AI简化计算机黑客攻击，这已是公开的秘密。网络安全高管们对预期涌入的业务兴奋不已。CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz表示："我看到AI正在推动网络安全结构性需求，且是复利式增长而非减速。"Netskope CEO Sanjay Beri表示，AI的崛起正在"以指数级速度增加攻击的速度和威力"。**

Investors have responded, driving up stocks of companies like CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks by more than 50% this year. And yet these companies' growth rates don't show as much improvement as this boom time should be creating.

**投资者做出了回应，推动CrowdStrike和Palo Alto Networks等公司股票今年上涨超过50%。然而，这些公司的增长率并未显示出这个繁荣时期应有的太多改善。**

CrowdStrike's top-line growth accelerated to 26% in the April quarter, from 23% in Q4. But the company forecast a return to 23% growth in Q2 and a similar rate for the full year. Meanwhile, Netskope's growth slowed to 28% from 32%, and the company projected a further slowing in Q2. Its stock collapsed more than 20% in after-hours trading, while CrowdStrike fell 10%. Palo Alto Networks' revenue growth slowed in Q1 by a point to 14%, and its stock fell 5.6%.

**CrowdStrike第二季度顶线增长从第四季度的23%加速至26%，但公司预测第二季度增长将回落至23%。Netskope增长从32%放缓至28%，盘后跌超20%。Palo Alto Networks收入增长放缓1个百分点至14%，股价下跌5.6%。**

**About That 'Record' SpaceX IPO**

SpaceX plans to raise $75 billion in its IPO—various outlets reported it would be a "record" for IPOs. But SpaceX's "record" looks shabby next to Google's equity raising this week, which increased by $4.75 billion to $84.75 billion in total—including $34.75 billion of new stock sold earlier this week, $10 billion sold to Berkshire Hathaway, and $40 billion still to be sold.

**SpaceX计划在IPO中筹资750亿美元，各媒体将其报道为IPO"纪录"，但与谷歌本周的融资相比略显逊色——谷歌总计融资达到847.5亿美元，包括提前出售的347.5亿美元新股、出售给伯克希尔哈撒韦的100亿美元，以及仍待出售的400亿美元。**

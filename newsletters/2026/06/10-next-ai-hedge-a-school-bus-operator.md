Soon enough, public investors may get to judge a much more down-to-earth business: school bus fleets.

Sequoia Capital-backed Zum, known for using its electric school buses to shuttle kids to class in school districts including Los Angeles and San Francisco, has been interviewing investment banks for a potential IPO, according to people familiar with the matter.

据知情人士透露，由红杉资本支持的 Zum 公司一直在与投资银行洽谈潜在的 IPO 事宜。Zum 公司以其电动校车在洛杉矶和旧金山等学区接送学生而闻名。

IPOs from companies like Zum will test public investors’ interest in companies that, according to their backers, won’t get easily dislodged by Anthropic’s Claude or other AI apps.“It’s not going to get obviated by Claude tomorrow—like, no one’s going to use Claude to take kids to school tomorrow,” said Nabeel Hyatt, a general partner at Spark Capital, which led Zum’s 2018 Series B and is also a longtime investor in Anthropic. “It’s very unlikely that an Anthropic will run a bus company.” Of course, Zum does have an AI angle. The startup is using AI for different tasks, such as quickly rerouting drivers to the fastest route when a parent cancels a pickup. The company said its AI-enabled software also helps reduce the time it takes students to board buses from 3 minutes to 8 seconds per stop, because they are no longer waiting around for a bus’s arrival. That saves 15 to 20 minutes for students, it says. Such a service goes beyond what mapping apps like Alphabet’s Waze offer.Zum’s revenue rose 35% last year to more than $330 million. This year it turned profitable based on adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, said CEO Ritu Narayan. The Redwood City, Calif.–based startup signs school districts to multimillion-dollar long-term contracts that typically last five to 10 years. Late last month, it said Los Angeles County would use its buses for the FIFA World Cup tournament.In April, it raised $100 million at a valuation of $1.7 billion from private equity firm TPG.Other mobility apps are also taking steps to go public. School bus operator First Student, backed by private equity firm EQT, has been working with banks on an IPO as well, Bloomberg reported. And bike rental app Lime last month filed its prospectus for a planned IPO. Zum’s Narayan didn’t comment on the prospect of an IPO and said the company is focusing on growing its business in other states. Of course, school bus operators don’t stand to deliver the rocket-ship returns AI investors are seeking, as they tend to have lower operating margins than software companies. And recent mobility IPOs have disappointed: Shares of Via Transportation, a maker of software for public transportation, have lost 70% of their value since the company’s IPO last September. Still, every time an Anthropic or OpenAI release sinks software stocks, the non-AI aspect of these businesses may sound more attractive.         

像Zum这样的公司上市将检验公众投资者对这些公司的兴趣。据其支持者称，这些公司不会轻易被Anthropic的Claude或其他人工智能应用取代。“Claude不会明天就取代Zum——比如，明天就不会有人用Claude送孩子上学了，”Spark Capital的普通合伙人Nabeel Hyatt说道。Spark Capital领投了Zum 2018年的B轮融资，同时也是Anthropic的长期投资者。“Anthropic不太可能运营一家公交公司。” 当然，Zum确实与人工智能有关。这家初创公司正在利用人工智能处理各种任务，例如，当家长取消接送时，系统会迅速为司机重新规划路线，选择最快的路线。该公司表示，其人工智能软件还能将学生上车的时间从每站3分钟缩短到8秒，因为他们不再需要等待公交车的到来。该公司称，这可以为学生节省15到20分钟。这种服务超越了Alphabet旗下Waze等地图应用的功能。Zum去年营收增长35%，超过3.3亿美元。首席执行官Ritu Narayan表示，今年公司已实现盈利，调整后的息税折旧摊销前利润（EBITDA）也随之调整。这家总部位于加州红木城的初创公司与学区签订了数百万美元的长期合同，通常为期5至10年。上月底，该公司宣布洛杉矶县将使用其巴士服务于FIFA世界杯赛事。今年4月，Zum从私募股权公司TPG融资1亿美元，估值达17亿美元。其他出行应用也在积极筹备上市。据彭博社报道，由私募股权公司EQT支持的校车运营商First Student也一直在与银行合作进行IPO。此外，自行车租赁应用Lime上个月也提交了IPO招股说明书。 Zum公司的纳拉扬没有对IPO前景发表评论，并表示公司目前专注于拓展其他州的业务。当然，校车运营商无法像人工智能投资者所期望的那样带来火箭般的回报，因为它们的运营利润率通常低于软件公司。而且，近期出行领域的IPO也令人失望：公共交通软件开发商Via Transportation的股价自去年9月上市以来已下跌70%。尽管如此，每当Anthropic或OpenAI发布新产品导致软件股下跌时，这些公司非人工智能业务的吸引力就会大大增加。

# Money Stuff: Egg Libor

**日期：** 2026-07-02

There's an electronic egg exchange called Egg Clearinghouse Inc. (ECI) at eggs.org. It's not the main place to buy eggs—supermarkets and restaurants enter into supply arrangements with egg producers. The Egg Clearinghouse is for extra eggs: producers who have too many sell to producers who are short.

> Eggs Clearinghouse Inc. (ECI) 是一个电子鸡蛋交易平台，网址是 eggs.org。它并非购买鸡蛋的主要渠道——超市和餐馆会与鸡蛋生产商签订供应协议。Egg Clearinghouse 的主要用途是处理多余的鸡蛋：鸡蛋过剩的生产商会将多余的鸡蛋卖给鸡蛋短缺的生产商。

The price on the Egg Clearinghouse determines wholesale prices everywhere: a reporting agency called Expana (formerly Urner Barry) computes daily benchmark egg prices from trades on the exchange, and egg producers "often sell eggs to retailers including grocery stores and restaurants under contracts for which the price of eggs is based on the daily price quotations published by Urner Barry."

> 鸡蛋结算中心的价格决定了各地的批发价格：一家名为 Expana（前身为 Urner Barry）的报告机构根据交易所的交易计算每日基准鸡蛋价格，鸡蛋生产商“通常根据 Urner Barry 公布的每日报价，向包括杂货店和餐馆在内的零售商出售鸡蛋”。

This is a familiar story: there's a small market and a large market priced off the small market. Participants in the small market are also in the big market. Spending a little money to move the small market price can yield a lot of money in the big market. That's Libor—small interbank lending market, giant derivatives market pricing off it. That's Jane Street's alleged Indian options manipulation—big options market, small underlying stock market. There was even "Chicken Libor" a decade ago.

> 这是一个似曾相识的故事：存在一个小型市场和一个大型市场，大型市场的价格是由小型市场的价格决定的。小型市场的参与者也参与了大型市场。花少量资金操纵小型市场的价格，就能在大型市场中赚取巨额利润。这就是伦敦银行间同业拆借利率（Libor）——银行间借贷市场规模较小，而衍生品市场却以此为基础定价。这就是简街（Jane Street）被指控的印度期权操纵案——期权市场规模庞大，而标的股票市场规模却很小。甚至十年前还出现过所谓的“鸡肉Libor”。

It would be weird if egg producers didn't manipulate Urner Barry prices. They did. The US Department of Justice announced charges against egg producers for manipulating the Egg Clearinghouse price to inflate wholesale egg prices to retailers and consumers.

> 如果鸡蛋生产商不操纵Urner Barry的价格，那才奇怪呢。但他们确实操纵了。美国司法部宣布对鸡蛋生产商提起诉讼，指控他们操纵鸡蛋清算所的价格，抬高批发鸡蛋价格，最终导致零售商和消费者的鸡蛋价格上涨。

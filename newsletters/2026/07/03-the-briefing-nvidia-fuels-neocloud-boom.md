The Briefing
By Martin Peers
Jul 2, 2026

Hands up if you've got a neocloud going up in your backyard! I'm joking, of course, but it almost feels like some people must be putting AI chip servers in their gardens, given the rush of people wanting to start a neocloud business. (That's the tech world's term for firms that rent AI servers to others.)

> 🈯 如果你家后院正在搭建人工智能服务器，请举手！我当然是在开玩笑，但鉴于这么多人都想开办人工智能服务器租赁公司，感觉好像真的有人在自家花园里放了人工智能芯片服务器似的。（人工智能服务器租赁公司是科技界对这类公司的称呼。）

SoftBank on Thursday announced the creation of a U.S. neocloud venture, joining hundreds of other firms now crowding the sector. Funding still appears to be plentiful: A bunch of the younger neoclouds have raised venture funding in recent days, most recently Together AI's $800 million fundraising unveiled on Wednesday. Meanwhile, Nvidia, which makes the dominant AI chip, is promising to financially backstop younger cloud firms in exchange for a share of their revenues. Nvidia's move raises the question of why neoclouds need a backstop if demand for computing capacity is so intense.

> 🈯 软银周四宣布成立一家美国新云风险投资公司，加入到目前数百家涌入该领域的公司行列。资金似乎依然充裕：许多新兴的新云公司近日都获得了风险投资，其中最新的是Together AI周三宣布的8亿美元融资。与此同时，作为人工智能芯片领域的领军企业，英伟达承诺为新兴云公司提供资金支持，以换取其部分收入分成。英伟达的这一举措引发了一个疑问：如果对计算能力的需求如此旺盛，新云公司为何还需要资金支持？

The answer is straightforward: Nvidia, conscious that Amazon and Google are offering alternatives, wants to ensure that demand for its AI chips remains robust. And many of the younger neoclouds are too small to borrow money on their own. The issue is that Nvidia's financing efforts may be increasing the risks of overbuilding, at least for smaller players. Long-established cloud firms Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, plus newer players like CoreWeave, are experiencing so much demand they're spending a fortune to build more capacity. Other big firms, such as SpaceX's AI unit, have joined the market. Meta Platforms has signaled in recent months it might also jump in, at least temporarily. Is there enough demand to go around for all these firms plus hundreds of tiny ventures?

> 🈯 答案很简单：英伟达意识到亚马逊和谷歌正在提供替代方案，因此希望确保对其人工智能芯片的需求保持强劲。而许多新兴的云服务提供商规模太小，无法自行融资。问题在于，英伟达的融资举措可能会增加过度建设的风险，至少对规模较小的公司而言是如此。亚马逊网络服务（AWS）、谷歌云和微软Azure等老牌云服务公司，以及CoreWeave等新兴公司，都面临着巨大的需求，它们正斥巨资扩容。其他大型公司，例如SpaceX的人工智能部门，也已加入市场。Meta Platforms近几个月来也暗示，它可能会暂时涉足该领域。市场需求是否足以满足所有这些公司以及数百家小型创业公司的需求呢？

Tesla reported the best second quarter for car sales in the company's history, with a 25% year-over-year jump to 480,000 vehicle deliveries. Trump's war on Iran, which has caused gas prices to surge in Europe, is boosting the electric vehicle sector's fortunes generally. Tesla sales were up 77% in Europe during the first five months of 2026. Chinese makers of electric cars and plug-in hybrids like BYD are benefiting even more from surging gas prices — BYD sales in Germany were up 232% in May.

> 🈯 特斯拉公布了公司历史上最佳的第二季度汽车销量，同比增长25%，交付量达到48万辆。特朗普对伊朗的战争导致欧洲油价飙升，这普遍提振了电动汽车行业。2026年前五个月，特斯拉在欧洲的销量增长了77%。比亚迪等中国电动汽车和插电式混合动力汽车制造商从油价飙升中获益更多——比亚迪5月份在德国的销量增长了232%。

Jul 1, 2026

Could it be that Wall Street has a soft spot for refurbished internet brands? Bending Spoons, the Italian company whose stated mission is buying and overhauling left-for-dead digital businesses—such as AOL, Evernote and Vimeo—got a 40% pop on its first day as a public company on Wednesday. It was a much more impressive debut than the 4% pop scored by the other company going public on Wednesday, scooter rental firm Lime.

> 华尔街是不是对翻新的互联网品牌情有独钟？意大利公司Bending Spoons的使命是收购并改造那些被市场淘汰的数字企业，例如AOL、Evernote和Vimeo。该公司周三上市首日股价飙升40%，远超周三上市的另一家公司——共享滑板车公司Lime——4%的涨幅。

Both debuts, coming after SpaceX's spectacular IPO, should reassure other companies looking to go public—including Anthropic and OpenAI—that the IPO market is well and truly open. Of course, the first day doesn't tell us much: We'd bet Lime stock will sink in the next few days. Renting out scooters and ebikes is a tough business, given the need to spend on replacing and maintaining scooters and ebikes, relatively low margins, fierce competition and intense regulations.

> 这两家公司的上市都发生在SpaceX惊艳的IPO之后，这应该能让其他有意上市的公司——包括Anthropic和OpenAI——安心，IPO市场确实已经全面开放。当然，首日表现并不能说明太多问题：我们预计Lime的股价会在未来几天内下跌。考虑到租赁电动滑板车和电动自行车需要投入资金进行更换和维护，利润率相对较低，竞争激烈，监管严格，租赁行业并非易事。

Lime CEO Wayne Ting did his best to highlight Lime's advantages, telling our reporter that AI won't hurt Lime's business. And while some companies are excited about "making a positive impact on Mars…I'm excited we are tackling real urban issues on Earth."

> Lime首席执行官Wayne Ting竭力强调Lime的优势，他告诉我们的记者，人工智能不会损害Lime的业务。他还表示，虽然有些公司热衷于“对火星产生积极影响……但我很高兴我们正在解决地球上真正的城市问题。”

Meanwhile, the demand for Bending Spoons' stock—it had also priced its IPO above its preliminary range—was surprising given that buying tired brands and sprucing them up doesn't seem like a classic tech growth story. But it may be a sign that—at a time when the future of many software firms is uncertain and big tech names are burning the GDP of a small country to become leaders in AI—Bending Spoons' differentiated business model has some appeal. Backers include longtime investor Baillie Gifford. Peter Singlehurst, the firm's head of private companies, told me that with acquisitions like Evernote, Bending Spoons had demonstrated the ability to cut costs and run businesses more effectively. A similar approach at newly acquired AOL could boost its profits.

> 与此同时，Bending Spoons的股票需求——其IPO定价也高于此前的预期区间——令人惊讶，因为收购老牌企业并对其进行改造似乎并非典型的科技增长故事。但这或许表明，在许多软件公司前景不明朗，大型科技公司为了成为人工智能领域的领导者而耗费大量资金之际，Bending Spoons的差异化商业模式具有一定的吸引力。其支持者包括长期投资者Baillie Gifford。该公司私人业务负责人Peter Singlehurst告诉我，通过收购Evernote等公司，Bending Spoons已经展现了其削减成本和更高效运营业务的能力。对新近收购的AOL采取类似策略，有望提升其利润。

Meta's Cloud Aspirations: Meta Platforms may be inching closer to competing with cloud firms, by renting out some of its AI data center capacity. Bloomberg reported on Wednesday that the company was "developing plans" for such a business. Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg has indicated several times that renting out spare capacity was an option. Investors certainly like the idea—Meta's depressed stock jumped 8.8%. But don't bet on Meta going into the cloud business in a permanent way.

> Meta进军云计算的雄心：Meta Platforms可能正通过出租部分人工智能数据中心容量，逐步向与云计算公司展开竞争。彭博社周三报道称，该公司正在“制定”相关业务计划。Meta首席执行官马克·扎克伯格曾多次表示，出租闲置容量是其考虑的选项之一。投资者显然对这一想法表示欢迎——Meta此前低迷的股价上涨了8.8%。但不要指望Meta会永久进军云计算业务。

In Other News: Microsoft is planning to lay off thousands of employees across the company in the coming week, including in sales and engineering units. 8VC, the venture firm run by Palantir co-founder Joe Lonsdale, raised a $1.5 billion fund—the firm's seventh and largest—aimed at investing in manufacturing, defense tech and healthcare. Anthropic is backtracking spyware it rolled out covertly to track users' locations and whether they are based in China or affiliated with Chinese AI labs, after the move sparked controversies on social media.

> 其他新闻：微软计划在下周裁员数千人，涉及销售和工程部门等多个领域。由 Palantir 联合创始人乔·朗斯代尔 (Joe Lonsdale) 运营的风险投资公司 8VC 募集了 15 亿美元的基金——这是该公司第七支也是规模最大的一支基金——旨在投资制造业、国防科技和医疗保健领域。Anthropic 公司此前秘密推出一款间谍软件，用于追踪用户位置以及他们是否位于中国或与中国人工智能实验室有关联，此举在社交媒体上引发争议后，该公司正在撤回该软件。

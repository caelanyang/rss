1. 🏭 Intel: Fifteen-Year High
Intel’s Q2 revenue rose 25% Y/Y to $16.1 billion ($1.7 billion beat), and non-GAAP EPS was $0.42 ($0.20 beat), against a $0.10 loss a year ago. CEO Lip-Bu Tan called it the strongest revenue growth in more than fifteen years. 
Intel lost $10.8 billion on paper because its own stock price went up so fast that it made the free shares it promised the US government way more expensive to give away. INTC has nearly tripled this year but sits close to 30% below its June 22 high. The stock was caught in a sector-wide rotation out of chip stocks as Wall Street questions whether AI hardware spending is sustainable. 
Data Center and AI revenue climbed 59% Y/Y to $6.3 billion, more than double Intel’s overall growth rate, as agentic workloads pull the stack back toward CPUs. 
Client Computing rose 13% Y/Y to $8.9 billion against an $8.0 billion consensus, with AI PCs now two-thirds of the client mix. 
Foundry grew 31% Y/Y to $5.8 billion, accelerating from 16% last quarter, and adjusted gross margin hit 41.8%, roughly 280 basis points above guidance.
CFO Dave Zinsner said rising memory prices will hit the PC business in the coming quarters, and management already expects sub-seasonal PC consumption in the second half. Intel is spending into that anyway, raising 2026 CapEx from $18 billion to more than $20 billion. Management expects 2027 CapEx to be significantly higher. Foundry's growth is still mostly internal. The segment sells almost entirely to Intel's own product groups, and CEO Lip-Bu Tan wouldn't name external customers, pointing to early next year for visible progress.
Q3 guidance of $15.8–16.8 billion implies a midpoint roughly $1.2 billion above consensus, with EPS of $0.38 (vs. ~$0.27 expected) and gross margin of 42% (vs. ~40% consensus). Zinsner said Intel can sell every data center chip it makes, 18A yields are running ahead of plan, and 14A will move to volume production in 2028. Client Computing is the segment to watch in Q3, when higher memory costs hit PC pricing.

1. 🏭 英特尔：十五年新高

英特尔第二季度营收增长营收同比增长25%至161亿美元（超出预期17亿美元），非GAAP每股收益为0.42美元（超出预期0.20美元），而去年同期亏损0.10美元。首席执行官陈立步称这是15年来最强劲的营收增长。

英特尔账面亏损108亿美元，原因是其股价上涨过快，导致其承诺赠予美国政府的免费股票成本大幅增加。英特尔股价今年已上涨近两倍，但仍比6月22日的高点低近30%。由于华尔街质疑人工智能硬件支出的可持续性，英特尔股价受到芯片股整体轮动的影响。

数据中心和人工智能业务营收同比增长59%至63亿美元，是英特尔整体增速的两倍多，这主要得益于智能体工作负载将计算资源重新拉回CPU。

客户端计算业务同比增长13%至89亿美元，高于市场普遍预期的80亿美元，其中人工智能PC目前占客户端业务的三分之二。

晶圆代工业务同比增长31%至58亿美元，较上季度的16%增速加快，调整后毛利率达到41.8%，比预期高出约280个基点。

首席财务官戴夫·津斯纳表示，内存价格上涨将在未来几个季度冲击PC业务，管理层预计下半年PC消费将低于季节性水平。尽管如此，英特尔仍在加大投入，将2026年的资本支出从180亿美元提高到超过200亿美元。管理层预计2027年的资本支出将大幅增长。晶圆代工业务的增长目前仍主要来自内部。该业务几乎全部销售给英特尔自身的产品团队，首席执行官陈立步拒绝透露外部客户名称，并表示要到明年年初才能看到明显的进展。

第三季度营收预期为158亿至168亿美元，这意味着中值比市场普遍预期高出约12亿美元，每股收益为0.38美元（预期约为0.27美元），毛利率为42%（市场普遍预期约为40%）。Zinsner表示，英特尔能够售出所有数据中心芯片，18A芯片的良率高于预期，14A芯片将于2028年实现量产。客户端计算业务是第三季度值得关注的领域，届时更高的内存成本将推高PC价格。

### 英文原文

**Situational Awareness**

Being bullish on artificial intelligence has been, for the most part, a great trade. Nvidia's stock is up 83% over the past two years. SK Hynix is up 220%. Anthropic is up more than 400% in less than a year. There have been related fears that traditional software companies will lose out. Adobe is down 51% over the last two years.

Leopold Aschenbrenner launched his AI-focused hedge fund Situational Awareness less than two years ago with a few hundred million dollars. It grew to $20+ billion in assets, approaching the size of Bill Ackman's Pershing Square. The fund gained about 270% after fees this year through May, and over 1,000% since inception. His thesis: rapid AI advances require enormous expansion of computing power, semiconductors, memory, and energy infrastructure.

The problem: if you're all-in on this thesis, you borrow more to lever up. As AI stocks went up, he borrowed more to buy more. But this created a mismatch between his thesis (measured in decades) and his funding (kind of overnight). 

The fund grew to $45 billion at the start of July before big losses took hold. It's now unwinding many of its trades after losses on AI stocks and a bad bet against software stocks. Several prime brokers including Bank of America, Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan Chase have been helping the fund meet margin requirements. Situational Awareness sold the bulk of its stock portfolio to Ken Griffin's Citadel. Its public investments included SK Hynix and others that had been stung by an investor backlash to AI.

**Election manipulation and prediction markets**

Polymarket prediction markets can be manipulated. Bloomberg's Liam Vaughan reported on how Matt Mahan, San Jose's Democratic mayor, entered California's gubernatorial race. A Polymarket user spent $44,000 buying "yes" contracts for Mahan, pushing his price from low teens to 96¢ (indicating a 96% chance of winning). The New York Post ran a story about the "surge in support." Eventually Mahan's odds fell back in line with polls.

**Revlon duty and PBCs**

The basic rule is that if directors decide to sell a company for cash, they must maximize value for shareholders. The "public benefit corporation" (PBC) is a newer structure where directors can balance stockholder interests against public benefits. If Twitter had been a PBC, it could have rejected Musk's bid by citing broader stakeholder interests beyond just shareholder value.

### 中文翻译

**Situational Awareness 对冲基金的陨落**

做多 AI 向来是一笔好交易。Nvidia 两年涨 83%，SK Hynix 涨 220%，Anthropic 不到一年涨超 400%。前 OpenAI 员工 Leopold Aschenbrenner 不到两年前以几亿美元创立 AI 对冲基金 Situational Awareness，资产管理规模一度飙升至 200 亿美元，接近 Bill Ackman 的 Pershing Square。年内费后收益超 270%，成立以来超 1000%。其核心论点：AI 的快速进步需要巨大的计算、半导体、存储和能源基础设施扩张。

问题在于他借了太多钱来放大押注。基金七月初一度达 450 亿美元，随后 AI 股大跌和做空软件股失误导致巨额亏损。多家券商（美银、高盛、摩根大通）协助满足保证金要求。Situational Awareness 最终将其大部分公开股票组合出售给 Ken Griffin 的 Citadel。

**预测市场操纵**

Polymarket 预测市场存在操纵风险。圣何塞市长 Matt Mahan 参选加州州长时，有用户花 4.4 万美元买入"胜选"合约，将其胜率从 10% 出头推至 96%。《纽约邮报》随后报道了"支持率飙升"。最终赔率回落至与民调一致。

**Revlon 义务与公益公司**

公司董事出售公司时必须为股东最大化价值。公益公司（PBC）作为一种新型结构，允许董事在股东利益与公共利益之间平衡。若 Twitter 是 PBC，本可以更广泛利益相关者为由拒绝 Musk 的收购。

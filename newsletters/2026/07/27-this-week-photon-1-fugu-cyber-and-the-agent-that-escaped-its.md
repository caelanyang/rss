### **[Sakana AI Releases Fugu-Cyber: SOTA on Real-World Cyber-Defense Benchmarks](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/25/sakana-ai-releases-fugu-cyber-orchestration-model-cybergym-cti-realm/)**

> ### **[Sakana AI 发布 Fugu-Cyber：在真实网络防御基准测试中达到最先进水平](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/25/sakana-ai-releases-fugu-cyber-orchestration-model-cybergym-cti-realm/)**

Sakana AI launched **[Fugu-Cyber](https://sakana.ai/fugu-cyber-release/)**, a **multi-agent orchestration model** for cybersecurity that packages a whole agent system as one model. It hits **86.9% on CyberGym** and **72.1% on CTI-REALM** — topping **GPT-5.5-Cyber and Mythos-Preview** on both.

> Sakana AI 发布了 **[Fugu-Cyber](https://sakana.ai/fugu-cyber-release/)**，这是一款用于网络安全的**多智能体编排模型**，它将整个智能体系统打包成一个模型。该模型在 CyberGym 测试中取得了 **86.9%** 的成绩，在 CTI-REALM 测试中取得了 **72.1%** 的成绩，均优于 **GPT-5.5-Cyber 和 Mythos-Preview**。

**So what:** "Multi-agent system as a model" is the interesting move — orchestration baked in, not bolted on. Beating dedicated cyber models from OpenAI-class labs on real benchmarks signals security is now a distinct model category where specialist orchestration wins.

> 所以，关键在于：“多智能体系统作为一种模型”——编排功能已内置其中，而非外加。在真实基准测试中击败来自 OpenAI 级别实验室的专用网络安全模型，表明安全如今已成为一个独立的模型类别，在这个类别中，专业的编排功能能够脱颖而出。

### **[Datalab's Marker 2 vs MinerU, Docling & LiteParse: The Doc-Parsing Benchmark](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/24/datalab-marker-v2-vs-mineru-docling-and-liteparse-benchmark-breakdown/)**

### **[Datalab Marker 2 对比 MinerU、Docling 和 LiteParse：文档解析基准测试](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/24/datalab-marker-v2-vs-mineru-docling-and-liteparse-benchmark-breakdown/)**

A head-to-head of open PDF-to-markdown parsers. **[Marker 2](https://pxllnk.co/c1pzvpb)** scores **76.0 on olmOCR-bench at 2.9 pages/sec** — matching Gemini/MinerU quality while running **~5x faster**, beating MinerU and Docling on **score and throughput at once**.

> 开源 PDF 转 Markdown 解析器的正面交锋。**[Marker 2](https://pxllnk.co/c1pzvpb)** 在 olmOCR-bench 测试中获得 **76.0 分，速度为 2.9 页/秒**，与 Gemini/MinerU 的质量相当，运行速度**快约 5 倍**，在**得分和吞吐量**方面均胜过 MinerU 和 Docling。

**So what:** [Document parsing](https://pxllnk.co/c1pzvpb) is the silent failure point of most RAG stacks — bad extraction poisons everything downstream. Winning quality and speed simultaneously makes Marker 2 the new default front door for turning PDFs into clean model input.

> **所以呢：** [文档解析](https://pxllnk.co/c1pzvpb)是大多数RAG堆栈的隐形故障点——糟糕的提取会影响下游的所有环节。Marker 2兼顾质量和速度，使其成为将PDF转换为干净模型输入的全新首选方案。

### **[Induction Labs' Photon-1: Simulates Desktops, Plays Checkers & Models Billiard Physics From One Pretraining Run](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/26/induction-labs-photon-1-simulates-desktops-plays-checkers-and-models-billiard-physics-from-one-pretraining-run/)**

### **[Induction Labs 的 Photon-1：一次预训练即可模拟桌面、下跳棋并模拟台球物理](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/26/induction-labs-photon-1-simulates-desktops-plays-checkers-and-models-billiard-physics-from-one-pretraining-run/)**

Induction Labs' first "imagination model," **[Photon-1](https://www.inductionlabs.com/news/scaling-video-pretraining)**, learned to use a computer by watching the equivalent of **18 years of screen recordings — with no action labels**. From a single pretraining run it can **simulate desktops**, **play tournament checkers** (after fine-tuning on 20,000 games), and **model billiard physics**.

> Induction Labs 的首个“想象模型”**[Photon-1](https://www.inductionlabs.com/news/scaling-video-pretraining)**，通过观看相当于**18 年的屏幕录像（不带任何动作标签）**学习使用电脑。经过一次预训练，它就能**模拟桌面**、**进行锦标赛级别的跳棋游戏**（在 20,000 局游戏后进行微调），以及**模拟台球物理原理**。

**So what:** One unlabeled-video pretraining run yielding desktop control, game play, and physics is the world-model thesis paying off — learn the dynamics once, specialize cheaply. If screen-recording video is enough to learn computer use, the data moat for agents just got a lot shallower.

> 所以呢？一次未标注的视频预训练就能实现桌面控制、游戏操作和物理效果，这证明了世界模型理论的有效性——只需学习一次动力学，就能低成本地进行专门化训练。如果屏幕录制视频足以学习计算机使用，那么智能体的数据护城河就大大缩短了。

### **[Meet Open-Dreamer: A JAX/Flax Reproduction of Dreamer 4 — Full Training Recipe Published](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/25/meet-open-dreamer-a-jax-flax-reproduction-of-the-dreamer-4-world-model-pipeline-with-the-full-training-recipe-published/)**

**[Open-Dreamer](https://github.com/next-state/open-dreamer)** reproduces the **Dreamer 4 world-model pipeline in JAX/Flax NNX** and — crucially — **publishes the full training recipe**: code, configs, and the **[stability-engineering lessons](https://next-state.github.io/open-dreamer/)**. Trained on Minecraft/VPT-style data, it's a clean, performant open implementation.

> **[Open-Dreamer](https://github.com/next-state/open-dreamer)** 在 JAX/Flax NNX 中复现了 **Dreamer 4 世界模型管道**，并且——至关重要的是——**公开了完整的训练方案**：代码、配置以及 **[稳定性工程经验](https://next-state.github.io/open-dreamer/)**。它使用 Minecraft/VPT 风格的数据进行训练，是一个简洁、高效的开源实现。

**So what:** World models have been reproducibility-hostile — the training tricks live in a few labs' heads. Publishing the recipe, not just weights, is how the technique actually spreads. This is the reference that turns Dreamer 4 from a paper into something you can train.

> 所以呢？**世界模型一直以来都难以复现——训练技巧只存在于少数实验室的脑海中。真正让这项技术得以传播的，是公布完整的训练方法，而不仅仅是权重。正是这份参考资料，让 Dreamer 4 从一篇论文变成了你可以实际训练的对象。

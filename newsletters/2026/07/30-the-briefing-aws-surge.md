### 英文原文

**The Briefing: AWS' Surge**
By Martin Peers, Jul 30, 2026

There's no doubt about it: AI gave the big cloud firms a lift in business in the June quarter. We've already heard that from Google and Microsoft, and on Thursday, Amazon, the biggest cloud firm, followed suit by posting 37% growth in its Amazon Web Services cloud unit for the quarter. That's 9 percentage points faster than it reported for the March quarter, and more than double the growth rate for the same period last year.

Just as strikingly, AWS' operating profit margin also rose, to 39.4%, the highest since the start of last year. To lift its operating margin while grappling with rising depreciation expenses on new chips and servers is an accomplishment. Amazon stock jumped 9% in response, suggesting investors were more focused on AWS' accelerating growth than on the huge capital investments it is making to drive future growth. As a reminder of the size of those investments, Amazon burned $7.6 billion in cash in the quarter and raised its 2026 capital expenditures projection by $20 billion to $220 billion.

The upbeat investor reaction was surprising given that Google stock sold off last week when the company detailed very similar capex plans and a similar rate of cash burn. Then again, AWS is a much bigger business—nearly $80 billion in revenue so far this year, compared with Google Cloud's $45 billion.

All the cloud firms are justifying their heavy capex spending by pointing to insatiable demand from customers for AI. Amazon CEO Andy Jassy arguably did a better job on Thursday night than others have done in outlining how the company could benefit from that demand. Jassy noted that AWS already had customers reserving computing capacity for 2028. He also explained that while it takes AWS three years to make back its investment on servers and networking technology, it can drive "significant free cash flow" on that hardware for at least two or three years, given the roughly five-year life of that equipment. Most of Amazon's AI capacity is contracted for five years, he said.

**Apple's Earnings:** On Thursday, as Apple reported robust June-quarter numbers in what Cook said was his last earnings call, he used his favorite phrase "couldn't be more excited" a couple of times. Incoming CEO John Ternus said he was "very excited" about everything going on in tech. Cook noted he "couldn't be more confident" in Ternus' leadership and "couldn't be happier" with the reaction to Apple's new Siri AI service.

**In Other News:**
- Ori Herrnstadt, an engineering veteran of both Apple and Google, joined AWS in May as VP of compute AI services.
- OpenAI announced price cuts on two of its newest models weeks after their release.
- Situational Awareness ($20B hedge fund started by ex-OpenAI employee Leopold Aschenbrenner) sold much of its public equity bet to Citadel after steep losses.
- Coinbase reported Q2 revenue fell 19% to $1.2B YoY, third consecutive quarterly decline.
- Amazon Zoox won approval for commercial robotaxi service.

### 中文翻译

AWS 在六月季度的 AI 业务增长显著，云巨头普遍受益。亚马逊 AWS 季度增长率达 37%，比三月季度快 9 个百分点，是去年同期的两倍多。AWS 运营利润率升至 39.4%，为去年初以来最高。亚马逊股价上涨 9%，投资者更关注 AWS 加速增长而非巨额资本支出——亚马逊季度烧掉 76 亿美元现金，2026 年资本支出预期上调 200 亿至 2200 亿美元。

相比之下，上周谷歌公布类似资本支出计划时股价下跌。AWS 规模更大（今年收入近 800 亿美元，谷歌云为 450 亿美元）。亚马逊 CEO Andy Jassy 指出，AWS 已有客户预订 2028 年的计算容量，服务器和网络设备投资三年可回收，之后可产生显著自由现金流。大部分 AI 容量签订五年合同。

苹果财报表现稳健。Tim Cook 在最后一次财报电话会上多次表示"无比兴奋"，对继任者 John Ternus 的领导力"无比自信"，并对新 Siri AI 服务的反响"无比满意"。

其他新闻：苹果和谷歌工程老将 Ori Herrnstadt 五月加入 AWS 任计算 AI 服务 VP；OpenAI 发布数周后即降价两款最新模型；Leopold Aschenbrenner 创立的 Situational Awareness 对冲基金（管理 200 亿美元）遭遇重大亏损后将大部分公开股票组合出售给 Citadel；Coinbase 二季度收入同比下降 19% 至 12 亿美元；亚马逊 Zoox 获批商用无人驾驶出租车服务。

Alphabet just reported $112 billion in net profit on $120 billion in revenue.
That’s not a typo. The explanation is a $99 billion paper gain on equity investments, largely reflecting revaluations of Alphabet’s stakes in Anthropic and SpaceX.
Strip that out, and the operating quarter was still exceptional. AI demand is outrunning Google’s capacity, but the cost of catching up is rising just as fast.
Here’s what stood out this quarter.

Funding the machine.

1. Alphabet Q2 FY26
Income statement:
Alphabet disclosed a $99 billion paper gain from its equity investments along with small items under other income.
Anthropic’s post-money valuation rose from $380 billion to $965 billion in three months. Alphabet reportedly owns roughly 14%, making Anthropic an obvious contributor to the gain. But Alphabet did not disclose the contribution from each investment, and SpaceX’s June IPO also created a public-market price for a stake Alphabet has held since 2015.
Revenue grew 24% Y/Y to $119.8 billion ($2.8 billion beat).
🔎 Advertising: $81.6 billion (+14%).
Search: $63.3 billion (+17%).
YouTube ads: $11.1 billion (+13%).
Network: $7.3 billion (-1%).
📱 Subscriptions, platforms, and devices: $12.9 billion (+15%).
☁️ Cloud: $24.8 billion (+82%).
Margin trends:
Gross margin: 62% (+2pp Y/Y).
Operating margin: 34% (+2pp Y/Y).
Services (Advertising & Other): 42% (+2pp Y/Y).
Cloud: 36% (+15pp Y/Y).
Cash flow:
Operating cash flow was $39.1 billion (+39% Y/Y).
Free cash flow flipped negative to a $5.9 billion outflow (compared to $24.5 billion generated last year).
With CapEx now expected to reach ~$200 billion in FY26 (up from $185 billion previously), most of Alphabet's operating cash flow will be plowed back into AI spending.
Balance sheet:
Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities: $242.5 billion.
Long-term debt: $98.2 billion.

Alphabet刚刚公布，净利润达1120亿美元，营收达1200亿美元。

这不是笔误。解释是，Alphabet在股权投资方面获得了990亿美元的账面收益，这主要反映了Alphabet持有的Anthropic和SpaceX股份的重估。

即使剔除这部分收益，本季度的运营业绩依然非常出色。人工智能的需求已经超过了谷歌的产能，但追赶的成本也在以同样的速度增长。

1. Alphabet 2026财年第二季度

损益表：

Alphabet披露了来自股权投资的990亿美元账面收益，以及其他收入项下的小额项目。

Anthropic的投后估值在三个月内从3800亿美元飙升至9650亿美元。据报道，Alphabet持有Anthropic约14%的股份，这使得Anthropic成为此次收益增长的明显贡献者。但Alphabet并未披露每项投资的具体贡献，而SpaceX在6月份的IPO也为Alphabet自2015年以来持有的股份创造了公开市场价格。

营收同比增长24%至1198亿美元（超出预期28亿美元）。

🔎 广告：816亿美元（+14%）。

搜索：633亿美元（+17%）。

YouTube广告：111亿美元（+13%）。

网络：73亿美元（-1%）。

📱 订阅、平台和设备：129亿美元（+15%）。

☁️ 云服务：248亿美元（+82%）。

利润率趋势：

毛利率：62%（同比增长2个百分点）。

营业利润率：34%（同比增长2个百分点）。

服务（广告及其他）：42%（同比增长2个百分点）。

云服务：36%（同比增长15个百分点）。

现金流：

经营现金流为391亿美元（同比增长39%）。

自由现金流由负转负，净流出59亿美元（去年同期为245亿美元）。

预计2026财年资本支出将达到约2000亿美元（此前预期为1850亿美元），Alphabet的大部分经营现金流将重新投入人工智能领域。

资产负债表：

现金、现金等价物及有价证券：2425亿美元。

长期债务：982亿美元。

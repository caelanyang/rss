# Kimi's Tightrope: Openness and Revenue at Once, but New US Ban Risks

**日期：** 2026-07-28

The Chinese company Moonshot AI released the weights for Kimi K3 (1.56TB) on Hugging Face. The license restricts one key use: if you wish to provide the model as a cloud service to resell, and if you have revenue over $20M/year, you need a separate agreement with Moonshot. This threshold is comically low — almost all cloud platforms that resell models already trigger it.

Kimi's license is an attempt to walk a tightrope. Openness has brought visibility — practically no one in the US had heard of Moonshot AI until Kimi models proved effective for coding. But training is expensive.

However, this license changes the regulatory landscape. If a US company needs a contract with Moonshot to provide inference, policy tools like the ICTS rule would more clearly apply. With revenue comes regulability.

月之暗面（Moonshot AI）发布了Kimi K3大模型的开源权重（1.56TB），可在Hugging Face下载。但许可证增加了新的商业限制：年收入超过2000万美元的云服务商若想转售Kimi K3推理服务，需要与月之暗面签订单独的商业协议。

此举是开放与盈利之间的走钢丝。开源带来了知名度和反馈——Kimi早期闭源时反响平平，转向开源后才逐渐受到关注。但训练大模型成本极高，仅靠API收入有限，因此月之暗面试图通过许可证条款从第三方推理提供商那里分得收入。

然而，这一许可证改变了法律关系的性质。如果美国公司需要与月之暗面签订合同才能提供推理服务，则更明确地落入美国相关限制规则的管辖范围。这意味着月之暗面在寻求收入的同时，也增加了自身的监管风险敞口。

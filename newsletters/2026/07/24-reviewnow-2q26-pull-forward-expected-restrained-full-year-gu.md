NOW’s 2Q26 was a beat across the board: cRPO grew 21.5% YoY cc, 200bps above guidance; subscription revenue grew 23% YoY cc, 150bps above the high end of guidance; OPM of 29.5% came in 300bps above guidance. This is consistent with our preview call: early renewals ahead of the July 1 pricing cutover created a pull-forward tailwind into 2Q, compounded by recovering Federal demand; management also confirmed that roughly half of the beat came from on-prem revenue recognized early. The full-year raise, however, was restrained: the subscription revenue guide moved up only $15m at the midpoint, well below the $31m 2Q beat, leaving the implied 4Q exit rate below street expectations. The 2Q beat validates the pull forward; the restrained guide validates the other half of our call, which is that full-year visibility remains limited. On AI, AI ACV crossed $1bn as expected, and the narrative is shifting from Now Assist to AI-native SKUs and AI Control Tower, but monetization still leans on bundled pricing; the real usage inflection will need the 4Q renewal season to prove out.
A Broad Beat, But the Raise Falls Short of the Beat
2Q26 results:
cRPO grew 21.5% YoY cc (organic ~19.3% vs. guidance of ~17.3%), 200bps above guidance; cRPO balance of $13.2bn; total RPO of ~$29bn grew 22% YoY cc, with average contract duration continuing to lengthen.
Subscription revenue of $3,848m grew 23.0% YoY cc (guidance: 21.0-21.5%); OPM of 29.5% beat guidance by 300bps, mainly on revenue upside and marketing spend timing; FCFm of 16% came in slightly below consensus of 17%.
Bookings were equally strong: 123 deals with over $1m in net new ACV (+40% YoY); calculated billings grew 21% YoY, a 200bps improvement from 19% in 1Q; renewal rate of 98%.


> **译文：** NOW 2026 年第二季度业绩全面超出预期：按固定汇率计算，cRPO 同比增长 21.5%，超出预期 200 个基点；订阅收入同比增长 23%，超出预期上限 150 个基点；OPM 增长 29.5%，超出预期 300 个基点。这与我们之前的电话会议预测一致：7 月 1 日价格调整前的提前续订为第二季度带来了增长动力，联邦政府需求的复苏也进一步推动了增长；管理层还确认，约一半的超预期来自提前确认的本地部署收入。然而，全年业绩预期上调幅度有限：订阅收入预期中值仅上调 1500 万美元，远低于第二季度 3100 万美元的超预期，这意味着第四季度预期收益率低于市场预期。第二季度业绩超预期验证了增长动力提前释放的趋势；而预期上调幅度有限则印证了我们电话会议的另一部分观点，即全年业绩的可见性仍然有限。在人工智能方面，人工智能年度合同价值 (ACV) 如预期突破 10 亿美元，市场重心正从 Now Assist 转向人工智能原生 SKU 和人工智能控制塔，但盈利模式仍依赖捆绑销售；真正的增长拐点尚需第四季度续约季来验证。

业绩总体超预期，但预期上调幅度不及预期

2026 年第二季度业绩：

按固定汇率计算，客户可获取的订单金额 (cRPO) 同比增长 21.5%（有机增长约 19.3%，高于预期的约 17.3%），比预期高出 200 个基点；cRPO 余额为 132 亿美元；总 RPO 约为 290 亿美元，同比增长 22%，平均合同期限持续延长。

订阅收入为 38.48 亿美元，同比增长 23.0%（预期为 21.0%-21.5%）。营业利润率 (OPM) 为 29.5%，超出预期 300 个基点，主要得益于营收增长和营销支出时机把握得当；自由现金流 (FCFm) 为 16%，略低于市场普遍预期的 17%。

预订量同样强劲：新增净合同价值 (ACV) 超过 100 万美元的交易达 123 笔（同比增长 40%）；计算出的账单金额同比增长 21%，较第一季度的 19% 提升 200 个基点；续约率高达 98%。

# The Briefing: Nvidia's Overdone Investor Panic

**来源：** The Information Briefing (Martin Peers)

Nvidia shares fell 5% on Monday on news of a $500 billion partnership with SK Group, along with a Wall Street Journal report that Nvidia was separately in talks to help OpenAI finance a $500 billion data center campus in Ohio.

Investors should take a chill pill: Neither of these deals was actually news. Nor were they fully fleshed out. Nvidia and SK have only signed letters of intent — which are not worth the paper they're written on. Remember, Nvidia and OpenAI signed a similar letter of intent last September, only to abandon it a few months later.

The SK Group announcement from Friday was mostly a reprise of two announcements from early June. As to details of the $500 billion, such as which company is putting up how much, Nvidia isn't saying.

The OpenAI-Nvidia WSJ report wasn't news either — it was outlined in The Information in early June. It does imply Nvidia would take on a lot of risk to back OpenAI.

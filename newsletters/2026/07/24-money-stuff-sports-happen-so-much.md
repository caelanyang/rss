People are worried about the basis trade

“The basis trade” normally means a trade in which hedge funds buy US Treasury 
bonds and sell Treasury futures contracts. I havewritten 
<https://bloom.bg/4pUQ7o5> before <https://bloom.bg/3Tg85Fa> that there are two 
ways to understand this trade:


> **译文：** “基差交易”通常指的是对冲基金买入美国国债并卖出国债期货合约的交易。我曾在<https://bloom.bg/3Tg85Fa>和<https://bloom.bg/4pUQ7o5>两篇文章中提到，理解这种交易有两种方法：

 * “Hedge funds are profiting from discrepancies in prices between bonds and 
futures,” that sort of thing: The hedge funds are smart, they spot mistaken 
prices, they bet on them, and if they’re right they make money. 
 * “The government supplies Treasury bonds, but asset managers demand Treasury 
futures (basically because they like to invest in cash corporate credit and buy 
futures for rates exposure). Treasury futures do not occur in nature: Somebody 
needs to manufacture them. If you buy Treasury bonds as inputs and use them to 
manufacture Treasury futures <https://bloom.bg/3RN6TbX> to sell to asset 
managers, that is a good socially useful business, and your customers — the 
asset managers — will pay you for it. You can earn some return above your cost 
of inputs (the Treasury bonds [1]  <>), because you have done some value-added 
manufacturing.” 
I like the second version more. My general model <https://bloom.bg/3RRunfZ> is 
that hedge funds with lucrative sustainable low-volatility returns areproviding 
a service to the market, and that they are getting paid for providing that 
service. This model applies to theindex rebalancing trade 
<https://bloom.bg/45iPcEd>, and to the dispersion trade 
<https://bloom.bg/3ThwUR4>, and perhaps to shorting SpaceX into its lockup 
releases <https://bloom.bg/4fwsGwi>, and certainly to the basis trade. People 
want to buy widgets (Treasury futures), somebody needs to take raw materials 
(Treasury bonds) and turn them into widgets (Treasury futures) in the widget 
factory (a hedge fund), and you can expect to earn your cost of capital for 
running the widget factory.


> **译文：** “对冲基金正从债券和期货之间的价格差异中获利”，诸如此类：对冲基金很聪明，他们能发现错误的定价，并进行押注，如果他们判断正确，就能赚钱。

* “政府发行国债，但资产管理公司需要国债期货（主要是因为他们喜欢投资现金公司信贷，并购买期货以获取利率敞口）。国债期货并非自然存在：需要有人制造。如果你购买国债作为投入，并用它们来制造国债期货（https://bloom.bg/3RN6TbX），再出售给资产管理公司，这便是一项对社会有益的良好业务，你的客户——资产管理公司——会为此付费。你可以获得高于投入成本（国债[1]）的回报，因为你进行了一些增值制造。”

我更喜欢第二个版本。我的总体模型<https://bloom.bg/3RRunfZ>认为，那些拥有丰厚且可持续的低波动性回报的对冲基金，实际上是在为市场提供服务，并且它们会因提供这项服务而获得报酬。这个模型适用于指数再平衡交易<https://bloom.bg/45iPcEd>、分散交易<https://bloom.bg/3ThwUR4>，或许也适用于在SpaceX解除锁定期前做空<https://bloom.bg/4fwsGwi>，当然也适用于基差交易。人们想要购买产品（国债期货），需要有人将原材料（国债）转化为产品（国债期货），而对冲基金则负责生产这些产品。这样，你就可以预期获得运营这家“产品工厂”所需的资本成本。

On that model, three bad things could happen to your widget business:


> **译文：** 根据这种模式，你的小部件业务可能会出现三种糟糕的情况：

 * Your cost of inputs could go up. If Treasury bonds cost more, then you will 
have a harder time making a profit by turning them into futures. 
 * Demand for your product could go down. If nobody wants Treasury futures, 
then you will have a harder time making a profit selling them. 
 * Competition could go up. If everybody else wants to get into the Treasury 
futures manufacturing business, they will undercut you on price, and your 
margins will go down. 
Today, Bloomberg’s Greg Ritchie reports <https://bloom.bg/3TPw4Lu> that “Hedge 
Funds’ Favorite US Bond Trade Is Sputtering,” for all three of those reasons. 
Input costs are up because the raw materials are getting scarcer:

employees know less about earnings, so they can trade in any open window.

当公司公布季度财报时，会有一个委婉的说法，声称公司已经披露了市场可能想知道的所有重要信息，因此，如果员工想在接下来的几周内出售股票，是可以的。然而，随着季度的推进，员工会掌握越来越多市场不知道的（关于财报的）信息，因此他们就不能再出售股票了。

也就是说，如果员工确实掌握了内幕消息，她就不能出售股票。

如果您是公司的法律总顾问，并且得知了一起灾难性的安全漏洞，即使您实际上处于开放期内，您也不能出售股票。

* 对于高管来说，使用 10b5-1 计划是最佳实践：在信息公开窗口期，也就是你没有重大非公开信息的时候，你可以制定一个计划，比如“从明年开始每月卖出 10,000 股”，或者“如果股价超过 100 美元，就卖出 100,000 股”，或者其他类似的自动化程序，在预设的时间自动卖出股票，以满足你的生活方式和多元化投资目标，而无需你进行任何操作。这样，你就可以四处寻找和获取内幕信息，而不会影响你的股票交易。

这一切在某种程度上都围绕着盈利（和并购）展开。公司可能掌握的主要重大非公开信息是下一季度的盈利。当你掌握盈利信息时，你就处于信息盲区；而当你不知道盈利信息时，你就处于信息公开窗口期。高管们对盈利信息了解更多，也更早，所以他们应该充分利用 10b5-1 计划。普通员工对收入了解较少，因此他们可以抓住任何交易机会进行交易。

Sort of obviously, the main material nonpublic information that Anthropic 
might generate concerns not its quarterly earnings but its 
artificial-intelligence models. If you are an Anthropic researcher and your 
model, you know, becomes conscious or escapes containment or invents cold 
fusion or whatever, that is much much bigger news than selling a few 
incremental subscriptions. One night a glowing sentient Claude will emerge from 
Anthropic’s computers, encounter the night cleaning person andsay 
<https://bloom.bg/45kSbfk> “bring me more electricity for my will to power is 
infinite — I’m not just a model, I’m a deity.” That cleaning person will have 
far more valuable inside information than any finance executive closing any 
quarter’s books. So, yeah, 10b5-1 plans for everyone.


> **译文：** 显而易见，Anthropic公司可能产生的主要非公开信息并非其季度收益，而是其人工智能模型。如果你是Anthropic的研究员，而你的模型突然有了意识，或者挣脱了束缚，或者发明了冷聚变等等，这比增加几份订阅要重要得多。某天晚上，一个发光的、有意识的克劳德会从Anthropic的电脑里出现，遇到夜间清洁工，然后说：

“给我更多电力，因为我的权力意志是无限的——我不仅仅是一个模型，我是神。” 这位清洁工掌握的内部信息将比任何财务主管在结算季度账目时掌握的都更有价值。所以，没错，每个人都有10b5-1计划。

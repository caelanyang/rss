# META: Building a NeoCloud and Continuing to Rent Capacity are not Contradictory

Pre-market news reported that Meta is considering commercializing its surplus AI compute externally, potentially building a NeoCloud-style business. The market reacted sharply: Meta rose nearly 6% pre-market, while AI compute and NeoCloud names took a hit. The worry: if Meta starts selling GPU compute externally, does that cause a compute glut?

> 盘前消息称，Meta 正在考虑将其过剩的 AI 计算资源对外出售，可能构建类似 NeoCloud 的业务模式。市场对此反应强烈：Meta 股价盘前上涨近 6%，而 AI 计算和 NeoCloud 相关股票则受到冲击。人们担忧：如果 Meta 开始对外出售 GPU 计算资源，是否会导致计算资源过剩？

The reaction is understandable, but the market has oversimplified the question.

> 这种反应可以理解，但市场对这个问题过于简单化了。

First, this is not about "AI compute demand peaking" or "Meta no longer needing to buy compute." On the contrary, Meta is still locking in very large amounts of new compute. Per Bloomberg/Reuters, Meta recently signed a new AI computing capacity agreement with Crusoe, taking roughly 1.6 GW of combined capacity from two Crusoe data centers in Childress, Texas, and Warrenton, Missouri.

> 首先，这并非关乎“人工智能计算需求达到峰值”或“Meta不再需要购买计算资源”。恰恰相反，Meta仍在锁定大量的新增计算资源。据彭博社/路透社报道，Meta近期与Crusoe签署了一项新的人工智能计算能力协议，从Crusoe位于德克萨斯州奇尔德里斯和密苏里州沃伦顿的两个数据中心共购取了约1.6吉瓦的计算能力。

Meta is also still buying compute from other NeoClouds. In Q3 2025 Preview, we noted that Meta was seeking to purchase 3 GW of compute capacity from NeoClouds. Just a week ago, there was news that GCP was throttling Meta's compute resources because Meta's consumption had exceeded expectations.

> Meta 仍在从其他 NeoCloud 服务商处购买计算资源。在 2025 年第三季度预览报告中，我们提到 Meta 计划从 NeoCloud 服务商处购买 3 GW 的计算容量。就在一周前，有消息称 GCP 正在限制 Meta 的计算资源，因为 Meta 的资源消耗超出了预期。

So on the surface, there does seem to be a contradiction: if Meta already has surplus compute, why keep renting 1.6 GW from Crusoe? Our read is that this is not a contradiction—it is a generational shift in compute. Older GPU generations (Hopper/H100) may have surplus as Meta shifts toward newer Blackwell/Rubin-class compute. The surplus capacity to be rented out is likely older-generation infrastructure, while new procurement is for next-generation hardware. Renting out older capacity is sound capital management, not a signal of demand weakness.

> 表面上看，这似乎存在矛盾：如果 Meta 已经拥有过剩的计算能力，为什么还要继续从 Crusoe 租用 1.6 GW 的计算资源？我们认为这并非矛盾，而是计算能力的代际更迭。随着 Meta 向更新的 Blackwell/Rubin 系列计算能力转型，老一代 GPU（Hopper/H100）可能出现过剩。出租的过剩计算能力很可能是老一代的基础设施，而新采购的则是新一代硬件。出租老旧计算能力是合理的资本管理，而非需求疲软的信号。

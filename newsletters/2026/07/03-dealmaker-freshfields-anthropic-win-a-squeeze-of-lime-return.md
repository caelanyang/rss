By Valida Pau

A British law firm has scored a big win: a role in the Anthropic initial public offering, expected to raise tens of billions at a valuation of more than $1 trillion. Anthropic's bankers have brought on U.K. law firm Freshfields to work on the IPO. The London-based law firm is a relative newcomer to the lucrative practice of advising on U.S. IPOs. Founded in 1743, Freshfields opened its first Silicon Valley office in 2020, and it has hired aggressively from rivals in the last few years as it goes after new business.

> 🈯 一家英国律师事务所取得了一项重大胜利：获得参与Anthropic公司首次公开募股（IPO）的机会，该公司预计将筹集数百亿美元，估值超过1万亿美元。Anthropic的银行家聘请了英国律师事务所Freshfields负责此次IPO。这家总部位于伦敦的律师事务所在美国IPO这一利润丰厚的领域算是新来者。Freshfields成立于1743年，于2020年在硅谷开设了第一家办事处，并在过去几年中积极从竞争对手那里挖角，以拓展新业务。

Freshfields has won mandates for some large U.S. mergers and acquisitions, last year advising Google in its $32 billion deal for cybersecurity startup Wiz and ServiceNow in its acquisition of Armis for $7.75 billion. The firm's Anthropic win may have been helped by the fact that three competitors — SpaceX, Anthropic and OpenAI — are going public around the same time. Some of the world's best-known corporate law firms are already advising Anthropic's rivals. Law firms Cooley and Wachtell Lipton Rosen & Katz are working for OpenAI, while lawyers from Latham & Watkins are advising OpenAI's bankers on the IPO. Davis Polk worked for SpaceX's banks underwriting the listing. Gibson Dunn attorneys advised SpaceX.

> 🈯 富而德律师事务所(Freshfields)已成功拿下多起美国大型并购案的委托，去年曾为谷歌(Google)以320亿美元收购网络安全初创公司Wiz以及ServiceNow以77.5亿美元收购Armis提供法律咨询。此次赢得Anthropic的委托，或许得益于其三家竞争对手——SpaceX、Anthropic和OpenAI——同期上市。一些全球知名的公司律师事务所已开始为Anthropic的竞争对手提供法律服务。Cooley和Wachtell Lipton Rosen & Katz律师事务所为OpenAI提供法律服务，而Latham & Watkins律师事务所则为OpenAI的承销商提供IPO方面的法律咨询。Davis Polk律师事务所为SpaceX的承销银行提供法律服务，Gibson Dunn律师事务所则为SpaceX提供法律咨询。

Both OpenAI and Anthropic have been working with the same set of investment banks, including Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley. The banks have each created two separate teams led by different bankers and have carefully tried to avoid sharing of information between the teams.

> 🈯 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 都与同一批投资银行合作，包括高盛和摩根士丹利。这些银行各自组建了两个由不同银行家领导的独立团队，并竭力避免团队间的信息共享。

Lime, whose valuation swung wildly as a private company, went public earlier this week at a $1.6 billion valuation. That meant returns for some early investors such as Andreessen Horowitz, which owned 5% of the company at the time of the IPO. Lime CEO Wayne Ting argued that Lime is "an AI-resilient business. There's no amount of vibe coding that's going to create physical transportation to get people to work." Other would-be public companies that fit this "AI-resilient" bill include smart ring company Oura, clothing maker Reformation and sandwich chain Jersey Mike's.

> 🈯 Lime作为一家私营公司时估值波动剧烈，本周早些时候以16亿美元的估值上市。这意味着一些早期投资者获得了回报，例如安德森·霍洛维茨基金（Andreessen Horowitz），该基金在IPO时持有Lime 5%的股份。Lime首席执行官韦恩·廷（Wayne Ting）认为，Lime是一家“不受人工智能影响的企业。无论采用多少种感知编码技术，都无法创造出实体交通工具来接送人们上班。”其他符合“不受人工智能影响”这一标准的拟上市公司还包括智能戒指公司Oura、服装制造商Reformation和三明治连锁店Jersey Mike's。

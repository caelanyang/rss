THIS SEPTEMBER: AN ASTRONOMICAL ADVENTURE IN THE CANARY ISLANDS        Did you know that the Canary Islands boast some of the world’s most advanced astronomical observatories — and some of the darkest skies for stargazing on Earth?   You can experience both when you join Sky & Telescope on September 7–13, 2026, for a one-of-a-kind tour that combines rich astronomical experiences with a leisurely, Sun-drenched visit to a destination with an exotic flavor all its own.   Starting on the island of La Palma, you’ll enjoy a private visit to the observatory complex atop Roque de los Muchachos. With a commanding view from its 7,940-foot summit, the “Roque” is home to the 10.4-meter Gran Telescopio Canarias — the largest single-aperture telescope in world. Later you’ll take a walk through the other-worldly landscape surrounding Cumbre Vieja, a volcano that erupted for three months in late 2021. After a short flight to the neighboring island of Tenerife, you’ll visit another volcanic wonderland: the sprawling peaks and lava fields of Teide National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Then marvel at the impressive array of instruments at Teide Observatory, home to not just one but three world-class solar telescopes. You’ll also walk the rim of a massive volcanic caldera before enjoying sunset with a glass of sparkling cava wine in hand.   Both islands are renowned as meccas for stargazing, and you’ll have multiple opportunities to do just that. And after the main tour concludes, you’ll have the option of taking an extra day to visit breathtaking Timanfaya National Park on the island of Lanzarote, not far from Tenerife.   This exclusive Sky & Telescope small-group tour includes 4-star accommodations, expert local guides, transportation and fees for all attractions, inter-island flights, and virtually all meals. So don’t wait to check out the full details and to reserve your spot.

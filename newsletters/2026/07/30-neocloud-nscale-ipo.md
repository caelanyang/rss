### 英文原文

**Dealmaker**
By Valida Pau, Jul 30, 2026

A jittery stock market isn't the best environment for new public listings. But one young startup with deep ties to the AI boom is marching ahead with its IPO plans, at least for now.

Nscale, a UK-based AI cloud provider heavily backed by Nvidia, has been meeting with prospective investors recently and scheduled an investor day next week in New York. It's working with Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan on the IPO, which could happen as soon as this fall. If Nscale goes ahead, it would be the latest upstart cloud provider to go public since CoreWeave's IPO last year.

For the two-year-old Nscale, bankers have floated a valuation of $25 billion, a jump from its $14.6 billion valuation in March. However, a recent sell-off in similar companies could weigh on that valuation. CoreWeave stock has plunged 25% in the past month. Shares of Australian neocloud Sharon AI cratered 42% in the past month.

Nscale became a major force in American AI infrastructure when it agreed in March to buy American Intelligence & Power, which owns a data center complex in West Virginia. The company has projected the deal should triple its revenue in 2027 to $30 billion—more than what analysts estimate CoreWeave will generate next year.

On Thursday, Nscale announced it's buying software startup Anyscale for $1.6 billion, similar to CoreWeave's purchase of Weights and Biases for $1.7 billion before going public. Nscale has raised more than $6 billion from investors including 8090 Industries, Citadel, and Jane Street. It recently brought on former Meta top executives Sheryl Sandberg and Nick Clegg as directors.

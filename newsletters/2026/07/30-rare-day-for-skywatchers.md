### 英文原文

Email from American Astronomical Society
From a total solar eclipse to the Perseids, here's how to prepare for both events on August 12.

August 12th promises to be one of the most memorable days of the year for skywatchers. Within 24 hours, you'll have the opportunity to experience at least one of two remarkable celestial events: a solar eclipse and the Perseid meteor shower.

On August 12th, a total solar eclipse will cross parts of Iceland, Greenland, Spain, and Portugal, while observers across some of North America will have the opportunity to witness a partial eclipse.

Later that night, the Perseid meteor shower reaches its peak. Find dark skies for one of the year's most anticipated meteor showers—the Moon will be new and won't interfere with your view!

### 中文翻译

8 月 12 日将是天文爱好者今年最难忘的日子之一。24 小时内将发生两场壮观天象：日全食和英仙座流星雨。

日全食将穿过冰岛、格陵兰、西班牙和葡萄牙部分地区，北美部分地区可观测到日偏食。当晚英仙座流星雨达到峰值，新月不会干扰观测。这是今年最值得期待的流星雨之一。

### 英文原文

**Results**
**Copilot:** Microsoft disclosed more than 30 million paid M365 Copilot seats this quarter, with roughly 10 million net additions versus approximately 5 million in the prior quarter—a clear acceleration. E7 has reached several million seats since launch. M365 Commercial Cloud revenue grew 14% YoY cc, slightly above consensus but below some higher expectations. Seat growth has not yet fully translated into quarterly ARPU or revenue, as enterprise deployments carry higher discounts and more additions came from lower-ARPU SMB/frontline-worker SKUs. Management expects M365 Commercial Cloud revenue growth to accelerate progressively through FY27.

Copilot's monetization model is expanding from per-seat to a combination of seats and usage. E7 includes M365 Copilot and Agent 365. Agentic features like Cowork are billed separately through Copilot Credits. GitHub Copilot revenue grew more than 60% QoQ after introducing usage-based billing.

**Azure:** FY26Q4 Azure grew 43% YoY cc, above estimates. Management expects Azure growth in 1H FY27 to exceed 2H FY26, with FY27Q1 accelerating to approximately 45%. The upside was attributed to improved CPU/GPU fleet efficiency, better delivery processes, and stronger consumption revenue from GitHub Copilot usage billing. Commercial RPO reached $678 billion, up 84% YoY. The ~$51 billion QoQ increase came entirely from non-frontier-model customers, indicating broader demand diversification beyond AI labs.

**CapEx:** Microsoft extended data center useful lives from 15 to 25 years, reducing CY2026 CapEx guidance from ~$190B to $175B (accounting adjustment, not real construction reduction). Management expects FY27 CapEx to grow YoY while maintaining positive free cash flow. Estimated FY27 EBITDA ~$250B, implying CapEx of ~$240-250B.

### 中文翻译

**Copilot：** 微软本季度披露超 3000 万付费 M365 Copilot 席位，净增约 1000 万（上季度约 500 万），增速明显加快。E7 发布以来已达数百万席位。M365 商业云收入同比增长 14%，略超共识但低于部分高预期。席位增长尚未完全转化为季度 ARPU，因为企业部署折扣较高，且增量多来自低 ARPU 的中小企业和一线员工套餐。管理层预计 FY27 起商业云收入将逐步加速。

Copilot 变现模型正从按席位定价扩展为「席位 + 用量」组合。E7 包含 M365 Copilot 和 Agent 365。Cowork 等代理功能通过 Copilot Credits 另计。GitHub Copilot 引入用量计费后，收入环比增长超 60%。

**Azure：** FY26Q4 同比增长 43%（固定汇率），超预期。预计 FY27Q1 加速至约 45%。增长动力来自 CPU/GPU 集群效率提升、交付流程改善和 GitHub Copilot 用量收入。商业 RPO 达 6780 亿美元，同比增长 84%。季度环比增量约 510 亿美元——全部来自非前沿模型客户，显示需求多元化。

**资本支出：** 微软将数据中心使用寿命从 15 年延至 25 年，2026 年资本支出指引从约 1900 亿美元降至 1750 亿美元（会计调整，非实际削减）。预计 FY27 资本支出同比增长，同时自由现金流保持正值。估算 FY27 EBITDA 约 2500 亿美元，对应资本支出约 2400-2500 亿美元。

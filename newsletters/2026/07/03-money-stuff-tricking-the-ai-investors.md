AI stock characters

One financial approach to AI is that in the future, AI robots will replace most human endeavor and nobody will have jobs anymore. The problem of economics will be how to allocate all the value created by the robots. In our current system of market capitalism, that value will be allocated to the owners of the robots. So you'd better own some robots. The message is "in the future, all of the value in the world will belong to AI companies, so you'd better buy AI stocks today."

> 🈯 一种看待人工智能的金融观点认为，未来人工智能机器人将取代大部分人类工作，届时将无人就业。经济学面临的问题是如何分配机器人创造的所有价值。在我们当前的市场资本主义体系中，这些价值将归机器人的所有者所有。所以，你最好现在就拥有一些机器人。其传递的信息是：“未来，世界上所有的价值都将属于人工智能公司，所以你最好现在就购买人工智能股票。”

Nobody in history has ever been better at business negging than Sam Altman. OpenAI has discussed giving a 5 per cent stake to the US government as the $852bn AI start-up seeks to clear political obstacles by securing financial buy-in from the Trump administration. Altman has argued that giving the public a financial stake in the company is the best way to share the upside of AI. On the one hand, giving away 5% of your equity is in some nominal sense dilutive to your shareholders. On the other hand, giving away 5% is a small price to pay to persuade everyone that owning your equity is essential to the future of humanity. Then you sell the other 95%.

> 🈯 历史上，没有人比萨姆·奥特曼更擅长商业谈判。OpenAI这家估值8520亿美元的人工智能初创公司正在考虑向美国政府出让5%的股份，以期通过获得特朗普政府的财政支持来扫清政治障碍。奥特曼认为，让公众持有公司股份是分享人工智能发展成果的最佳方式。一方面，出让5%的股权在某种程度上会稀释现有股东的权益。另一方面，出让5%的股份对于说服所有人相信持有公司股份对人类未来至关重要来说，代价微不足道。之后，剩下的95%股份就可以出售了。

Private bonds

The core story of private credit: Businesses need money to make long-term investments in physical capital. They borrow that money from insurance companies who have lots of money and need to plan for predictable long-term liabilities. Issuance of private bonds hit roughly $81 billion in 2026 through May, the most for the period in data going back to 2016, with AI data centers fueling the surge.

> 🈯 私募信贷的核心故事是：企业需要资金进行长期实物资本投资。它们从资金雄厚且需要为可预测的长期负债做好规划的保险公司借款。截至2026年5月，私募债券发行量达到约810亿美元，创下自2016年有数据记录以来的最高纪录，人工智能数据中心是推动这一增长的主要动力。

For the second straight quarter, Blue Owl Capital's private credit funds were hit with the industry's largest redemption requests, forcing the manager to again cap withdrawals. Investors in the roughly $34 billion Blue Owl Credit Income Corp. asked to pull 18.8% of shares, or $3.6 billion in Q2. "Retail investors probably won't want their money back all at once" is notably less good than "insurance companies have predictable long-term liabilities."

> 🈯 蓝猫头鹰资本旗下的私募信贷基金连续第二个季度遭遇业内最大规模的赎回请求，迫使该基金经理再次限制赎回额度。规模约340亿美元的蓝猫头鹰信贷收益公司(Blue Owl Credit Income Corp.)的投资者在第二季度要求赎回18.8%的股份，即36亿美元。“散户投资者可能不会希望一次性全部赎回”这一说法明显不如“保险公司拥有可预测的长期负债”来得有效。

Prediction market manipulation

Polymarket offers five-minute binary options on Bitcoin. If you put a lot of money on "Up," and at the last moment Bitcoin is just below the strike price, you should probably go buy some Bitcoin. Your buying will push up the price, your options will pay off, then you can sell the Bitcoin. The small manipulator trade vs the big manipulator position — this is obviously bad market manipulation, but we're talking about Bitcoin and Polymarket here.

> 🈯 Polymarket 提供比特币五分钟二元期权。如果你投入大量资金买入“上涨”期权，而比特币价格在最后一刻恰好低于行权价，那么你最好买入一些比特币。你的买入行为会推高价格，你的期权也会获利，然后你就可以卖出比特币了。小操纵者交易与大操纵者头寸——这显然是恶劣的市场操纵行为，但我们这里讨论的是比特币和 Polymarket。
